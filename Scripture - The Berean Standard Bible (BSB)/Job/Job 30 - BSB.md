# Job 30 (BSB)

← [[Job 29 - BSB]] | [[Job 31 - BSB]] →

**1** “But now they mock me, men younger than I am, whose fathers I would have refused to put with my sheep dogs. ^1

**2** What use to me was the strength of their hands, since their vigor had left them? ^2

**3** Gaunt from poverty and hunger, they gnawed the dry land, and the desolate wasteland by night. ^3

**4** They plucked mallow among the shrubs, and the roots of the broom tree were their food. ^4

**5** They were banished from among men, shouted at like thieves, ^5

**6** so that they lived on the slopes of the wadis, among the rocks and in holes in the ground. ^6

**7** They cried out among the shrubs and huddled beneath the nettles. ^7

**8** A senseless and nameless brood, they were driven off the land. ^8

**9** And now they mock me in song; I have become a byword among them. ^9

**10** They abhor me and keep far from me; they do not hesitate to spit in my face. ^10

**11** Because God has unstrung my bow and afflicted me, they have cast off restraint in my presence. ^11

**12** The rabble arises at my right; they lay snares for my feet and build siege ramps against me. ^12

**13** They tear up my path; they profit from my destruction, with no one to restrain them. ^13

**14** They advance as through a wide breach; through the ruins they keep rolling in. ^14

**15** Terrors are turned loose against me; they drive away my dignity as by the wind, and my prosperity has passed like a cloud. ^15

**16** And now my soul is poured out within me; days of affliction grip me. ^16

**17** Night pierces my bones, and my gnawing pains never rest. ^17

**18** With great force He grasps my garment; He seizes me by the collar of my tunic. ^18

**19** He throws me into the mud, and I have become like dust and ashes. ^19

**20** I cry out to You for help, but You do not answer; when I stand up, You merely look at me. ^20

**21** You have ruthlessly turned on me; You oppose me with Your strong hand. ^21

**22** You snatch me up into the wind and drive me before it; You toss me about in the storm. ^22

**23** Yes, I know that You will bring me down to death, to the place appointed for all the living. ^23

**24** Yet no one stretches out his hand against a ruined man when he cries for help in his distress. ^24

**25** Have I not wept for those in trouble? Has my soul not grieved for the needy? ^25

**26** But when I hoped for good, evil came; when I looked for light, darkness fell. ^26

**27** I am churning within and cannot rest; days of affliction confront me. ^27

**28** I go about blackened, but not by the sun. I stand up in the assembly and cry for help. ^28

**29** I have become a brother of jackals, a companion of ostriches. ^29

**30** My skin grows black and peels, and my bones burn with fever. ^30

**31** My harp is tuned to mourning and my flute to the sound of weeping. ^31
