# Job 10 (BSB)

← [[Job 9 - BSB]] | [[Job 11 - BSB]] →

**1** “I loathe my own life; I will express my complaint and speak in the bitterness of my soul. ^1

**2** I will say to God: Do not condemn me! Let me know why You prosecute me. ^2

**3** Does it please You to oppress me, to reject the work of Your hands and favor the schemes of the wicked? ^3

**4** Do You have eyes of flesh? Do You see as man sees? ^4

**5** Are Your days like those of a mortal, or Your years like those of a man, ^5

**6** that You should seek my iniquity and search out my sin— ^6

**7** though You know that I am not guilty, and there is no deliverance from Your hand? ^7

**8** Your hands shaped me and altogether formed me. Would You now turn and destroy me? ^8

**9** Please remember that You molded me like clay. Would You now return me to dust? ^9

**10** Did You not pour me out like milk, and curdle me like cheese? ^10

**11** You clothed me with skin and flesh, and knit me together with bones and sinews. ^11

**12** You have granted me life and loving devotion, and Your care has preserved my spirit. ^12

**13** Yet You concealed these things in Your heart, and I know that this was in Your mind: ^13

**14** If I sinned, You would take note, and would not acquit me of my iniquity. ^14

**15** If I am guilty, woe to me! And even if I am righteous, I cannot lift my head. I am full of shame and aware of my affliction. ^15

**16** Should I hold my head high, You would hunt me like a lion, and again display Your power against me. ^16

**17** You produce new witnesses against me and multiply Your anger toward me. Hardships assault me in wave after wave. ^17

**18** Why then did You bring me from the womb? Oh, that I had died, and no eye had seen me! ^18

**19** If only I had never come to be, but had been carried from the womb to the grave. ^19

**20** Are my days not few? Withdraw from me, that I may have a little comfort, ^20

**21** before I go—never to return—to a land of darkness and gloom, ^21

**22** to a land of utter darkness, of deep shadow and disorder, where even the light is like darkness.” ^22
