# Job 16 (BSB)

← [[Job 15 - BSB]] | [[Job 17 - BSB]] →

**1** Then Job answered: ^1

**2** “I have heard many things like these; miserable comforters are you all. ^2

**3** Is there no end to your long-winded speeches? What provokes you to continue testifying? ^3

**4** I could also speak like you if you were in my place; I could heap up words against you and shake my head at you. ^4

**5** But I would encourage you with my mouth, and the consolation of my lips would bring relief. ^5

**6** Even if I speak, my pain is not relieved, and if I hold back, how will it go away? ^6

**7** Surely He has now exhausted me; You have devastated all my family. ^7

**8** You have bound me, and it has become a witness; my frailty rises up and testifies against me. ^8

**9** His anger has torn me and opposed me; He gnashes His teeth at me. My adversary pierces me with His eyes. ^9

**10** They open their mouths against me and strike my cheeks with contempt; they join together against me. ^10

**11** God has delivered me to unjust men; He has thrown me to the clutches of the wicked. ^11

**12** I was at ease, but He shattered me; He seized me by the neck and crushed me. He has set me up as His target; ^12

**13** His archers surround me. He pierces my kidneys without mercy and spills my gall on the ground. ^13

**14** He breaks me with wound upon wound; He rushes me like a mighty warrior. ^14

**15** I have sewn sackcloth over my skin; I have buried my horn in the dust. ^15

**16** My face is red with weeping, and deep shadows ring my eyes; ^16

**17** yet my hands are free of violence and my prayer is pure. ^17

**18** O earth, do not cover my blood; may my cry for help never be laid to rest. ^18

**19** Even now my witness is in heaven, and my advocate is on high. ^19

**20** My friends are my scoffers as my eyes pour out tears to God. ^20

**21** Oh, that a man might plead with God as he pleads with his neighbor! ^21

**22** For when only a few years are past I will go the way of no return. ^22
