# Job 5 (BSB)

← [[Job 4 - BSB]] | [[Job 6 - BSB]] →

**1** “Call out if you please, but who will answer? To which of the holy ones will you turn? ^1

**2** For resentment kills a fool, and envy slays the simple. ^2

**3** I have seen a fool taking root, but suddenly his house was cursed. ^3

**4** His sons are far from safety, crushed in court without a defender. ^4

**5** The hungry consume his harvest, taking it even from the thorns, and the thirsty pant after his wealth. ^5

**6** For distress does not spring from the dust, and trouble does not sprout from the ground. ^6

**7** Yet man is born to trouble as surely as sparks fly upward. ^7

**8** However, if I were you, I would appeal to God and lay my cause before Him— ^8

**9** the One who does great and unsearchable things, wonders without number. ^9

**10** He gives rain to the earth and sends water upon the fields. ^10

**11** He sets the lowly on high, so that mourners are lifted to safety. ^11

**12** He thwarts the schemes of the crafty, so that their hands find no success. ^12

**13** He catches the wise in their craftiness, and sweeps away the plans of the cunning. ^13

**14** They encounter darkness by day and grope at noon as in the night. ^14

**15** He saves the needy from the sword in their mouth and from the clutches of the powerful. ^15

**16** So the poor have hope, and injustice shuts its mouth. ^16

**17** Blessed indeed is the man whom God corrects; so do not despise the discipline of the Almighty. ^17

**18** For He wounds, but He also binds; He strikes, but His hands also heal. ^18

**19** He will rescue you from six calamities; no harm will touch you in seven. ^19

**20** In famine He will redeem you from death, and in battle from the stroke of the sword. ^20

**21** You will be hidden from the scourge of the tongue, and will not fear havoc when it comes. ^21

**22** You will laugh at destruction and famine, and need not fear the beasts of the earth. ^22

**23** For you will have a covenant with the stones of the field, and the wild animals will be at peace with you. ^23

**24** You will know that your tent is secure, and find nothing amiss when inspecting your home. ^24

**25** You will know that your offspring will be many, your descendants like the grass of the earth. ^25

**26** You will come to the grave in full vigor, like a sheaf of grain gathered in season. ^26

**27** Indeed, we have investigated, and it is true! So hear it and know for yourself.” ^27
