# Job 7 (BSB)

← [[Job 6 - BSB]] | [[Job 8 - BSB]] →

**1** “Is not man consigned to labor on earth? Are not his days like those of a hired hand? ^1

**2** Like a slave he longs for shade; like a hireling he waits for his wages. ^2

**3** So I am allotted months of futility, and nights of misery are appointed to me. ^3

**4** When I lie down I think: ‘When will I get up?’ But the night drags on, and I toss and turn until dawn. ^4

**5** My flesh is clothed with worms and encrusted with dirt; my skin is cracked and festering. ^5

**6** My days are swifter than a weaver’s shuttle; they come to an end without hope. ^6

**7** Remember that my life is but a breath. My eyes will never again see happiness. ^7

**8** The eye that beholds me will no longer see me. You will look for me, but I will be no more. ^8

**9** As a cloud vanishes and is gone, so he who goes down to Sheol does not come back up. ^9

**10** He never returns to his house; his place remembers him no more. ^10

**11** Therefore I will not restrain my mouth; I will speak in the anguish of my spirit; I will complain in the bitterness of my soul. ^11

**12** Am I the sea, or the monster of the deep, that You must keep me under guard? ^12

**13** When I think my bed will comfort me and my couch will ease my complaint, ^13

**14** then You frighten me with dreams and terrify me with visions, ^14

**15** so that I would prefer strangling and death over my life in this body. ^15

**16** I loathe my life! I would not live forever. Leave me alone, for my days are but a breath. ^16

**17** What is man that You should exalt him, that You should set Your heart upon him, ^17

**18** that You attend to him every morning, and test him every moment? ^18

**19** Will You never look away from me, or leave me alone to swallow my spittle? ^19

**20** If I have sinned, what have I done to You, O watcher of mankind? Why have You made me Your target, so that I am a burden to You? ^20

**21** Why do You not pardon my transgression and take away my iniquity? For soon I will lie down in the dust; You will seek me, but I will be no more.” ^21
