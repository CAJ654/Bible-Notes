# Job 19 (BSB)

← [[Job 18 - BSB]] | [[Job 20 - BSB]] →

**1** Then Job answered: ^1

**2** “How long will you torment me and crush me with your words? ^2

**3** Ten times now you have reproached me; you shamelessly mistreat me. ^3

**4** Even if I have truly gone astray, my error concerns me alone. ^4

**5** If indeed you would exalt yourselves above me and use my disgrace against me, ^5

**6** then understand that it is God who has wronged me and drawn His net around me. ^6

**7** Though I cry out, ‘Violence!’ I get no response; though I call for help, there is no justice. ^7

**8** He has blocked my way so I cannot pass; He has veiled my paths with darkness. ^8

**9** He has stripped me of my honor and removed the crown from my head. ^9

**10** He tears me down on every side until I am gone; He uproots my hope like a tree. ^10

**11** His anger burns against me, and He counts me among His enemies. ^11

**12** His troops advance together; they construct a ramp against me and encamp around my tent. ^12

**13** He has removed my brothers from me; my acquaintances have abandoned me. ^13

**14** My kinsmen have failed me, and my friends have forgotten me. ^14

**15** My guests and maidservants count me as a stranger; I am a foreigner in their sight. ^15

**16** I call for my servant, but he does not answer, though I implore him with my own mouth. ^16

**17** My breath is repulsive to my wife, and I am loathsome to my own family. ^17

**18** Even little boys scorn me; when I appear, they deride me. ^18

**19** All my best friends despise me, and those I love have turned against me. ^19

**20** My skin and flesh cling to my bones; I have escaped by the skin of my teeth. ^20

**21** Have pity on me, my friends, have pity, for the hand of God has struck me. ^21

**22** Why do you persecute me as God does? Will you never get enough of my flesh? ^22

**23** I wish that my words were recorded and inscribed in a book, ^23

**24** by an iron stylus on lead, or chiseled in stone forever. ^24

**25** But I know that my Redeemer lives, and in the end He will stand upon the earth. ^25

**26** Even after my skin has been destroyed, yet in my flesh I will see God. ^26

**27** I will see Him for myself; my eyes will behold Him, and not as a stranger. How my heart yearns within me! ^27

**28** If you say, ‘Let us persecute him, since the root of the matter lies with him,’ ^28

**29** then you should fear the sword yourselves, because wrath brings punishment by the sword, so that you may know there is a judgment.” ^29
