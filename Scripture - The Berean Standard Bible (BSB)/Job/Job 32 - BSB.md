# Job 32 (BSB)

← [[Job 31 - BSB]] | [[Job 33 - BSB]] →

**1** So these three men stopped answering Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes. ^1

**2** This kindled the anger of Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, of the family of Ram. He burned with anger against Job for justifying himself rather than God, ^2

**3** and he burned with anger against Job’s three friends because they had failed to refute Job, and yet had condemned him. ^3

**4** Now Elihu had waited to speak to Job because the others were older than he. ^4

**5** But when he saw that the three men had no further reply, his anger was kindled. ^5

**6** So Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite declared: “I am young in years, while you are old; that is why I was timid and afraid to tell you what I know. ^6

**7** I thought that age should speak, and many years should teach wisdom. ^7

**8** But there is a spirit in a man, the breath of the Almighty, that gives him understanding. ^8

**9** It is not only the old who are wise, or the elderly who understand justice. ^9

**10** Therefore I say, ‘Listen to me; I too will declare what I know.’ ^10

**11** Indeed, I waited while you spoke; I listened to your reasoning; as you searched for words, ^11

**12** I paid you full attention. But no one proved Job wrong; not one of you rebutted his arguments. ^12

**13** So do not claim, ‘We have found wisdom; let God, not man, refute him.’ ^13

**14** But Job has not directed his words against me, and I will not answer him with your arguments. ^14

**15** Job’s friends are dismayed, with no more to say; words have escaped them. ^15

**16** Must I wait, now that they are silent, now that they stand and no longer reply? ^16

**17** I too will answer; yes, I will declare what I know. ^17

**18** For I am full of words, and my spirit within me compels me. ^18

**19** Behold, my belly is like unvented wine; it is about to burst like a new wineskin. ^19

**20** I must speak and find relief; I must open my lips and respond. ^20

**21** I will be partial to no one, nor will I flatter any man. ^21

**22** For I do not know how to flatter, or my Maker would remove me in an instant. ^22
