# Job 33 (BSB)

← [[Job 32 - BSB]] | [[Job 34 - BSB]] →

**1** “But now, O Job, hear my speech, and listen to all my words. ^1

**2** Behold, I will open my mouth; my address is on the tip of my tongue. ^2

**3** My words are from an upright heart, and my lips speak sincerely what I know. ^3

**4** The Spirit of God has made me, and the breath of the Almighty gives me life. ^4

**5** Refute me if you can; prepare your case and confront me. ^5

**6** I am just like you before God; I was also formed from clay. ^6

**7** Surely no fear of me should terrify you; nor will my hand be heavy upon you. ^7

**8** Surely you have spoken in my hearing, and I have heard these very words: ^8

**9** ‘I am pure, without transgression; I am clean, with no iniquity in me. ^9

**10** Yet God finds occasions against me; He counts me as His enemy. ^10

**11** He puts my feet in the stocks; He watches over all my paths.’ ^11

**12** Behold, you are not right in this matter. I will answer you, for God is greater than man. ^12

**13** Why do you complain to Him that He answers nothing a man asks? ^13

**14** For God speaks in one way and in another, yet no one notices. ^14

**15** In a dream, in a vision in the night, when deep sleep falls upon men as they slumber on their beds, ^15

**16** He opens their ears and terrifies them with warnings ^16

**17** to turn a man from wrongdoing and keep him from pride, ^17

**18** to preserve his soul from the Pit and his life from perishing by the sword. ^18

**19** A man is also chastened on his bed with pain and constant distress in his bones, ^19

**20** so that he detests his bread, and his soul loathes his favorite food. ^20

**21** His flesh wastes away from sight, and his hidden bones protrude. ^21

**22** He draws near to the Pit, and his life to the messengers of death. ^22

**23** Yet if there is a messenger on his side, one mediator in a thousand, to tell a man what is right for him, ^23

**24** to be gracious to him and say, ‘Spare him from going down to the Pit; I have found his ransom,’ ^24

**25** then his flesh is refreshed like a child’s; he returns to the days of his youth. ^25

**26** He prays to God and finds favor; he sees God’s face and shouts for joy, and God restores His righteousness to that man. ^26

**27** Then he sings before men with these words: ‘I have sinned and perverted what was right; yet I did not get what I deserved. ^27

**28** He redeemed my soul from going down to the Pit, and I will live to see the light.’ ^28

**29** Behold, all these things God does to a man, two or even three times, ^29

**30** to bring back his soul from the Pit, that he may be enlightened with the light of life. ^30

**31** Pay attention, Job, and listen to me; be silent, and I will speak. ^31

**32** But if you have something to say, answer me; speak up, for I would like to vindicate you. ^32

**33** But if not, then listen to me; be quiet, and I will teach you wisdom.” ^33
