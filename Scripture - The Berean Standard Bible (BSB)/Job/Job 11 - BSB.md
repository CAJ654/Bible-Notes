# Job 11 (BSB)

← [[Job 10 - BSB]] | [[Job 12 - BSB]] →

**1** Then Zophar the Naamathite replied: ^1

**2** “Should this stream of words go unanswered and such a speaker be vindicated? ^2

**3** Should your babbling put others to silence? Will you scoff without rebuke? ^3

**4** You have said, ‘My doctrine is sound, and I am pure in Your sight.’ ^4

**5** But if only God would speak and open His lips against you, ^5

**6** and disclose to you the secrets of wisdom, for true wisdom has two sides. Know then that God exacts from you less than your iniquity deserves. ^6

**7** Can you fathom the deep things of God or discover the limits of the Almighty? ^7

**8** They are higher than the heavens—what can you do? They are deeper than Sheol—what can you know? ^8

**9** Their measure is longer than the earth and wider than the sea. ^9

**10** If He comes along to imprison you, or convenes a court, who can stop Him? ^10

**11** Surely He knows the deceit of men. If He sees iniquity, does He not take note? ^11

**12** But a witless man can no more become wise than the colt of a wild donkey can be born a man! ^12

**13** As for you, if you direct your heart and lift up your hands to Him, ^13

**14** if you put away the iniquity in your hand, and allow no injustice to dwell in your tents, ^14

**15** then indeed you will lift up your face without blemish; you will stand firm and unafraid. ^15

**16** For you will forget your misery, recalling it only as waters gone by. ^16

**17** Your life will be brighter than noonday; its darkness will be like the morning. ^17

**18** You will be secure, because there is hope, and you will look around and lie down in safety. ^18

**19** You will lie down without fear, and many will court your favor. ^19

**20** But the eyes of the wicked will fail, and escape will elude them; they will hope for their last breath.” ^20
