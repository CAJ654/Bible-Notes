# Job 4 (BSB)

← [[Job 3 - BSB]] | [[Job 5 - BSB]] →

**1** Then Eliphaz the Temanite replied: ^1

**2** “If one ventures a word with you, will you be wearied? Yet who can keep from speaking? ^2

**3** Surely you have instructed many, and have strengthened their feeble hands. ^3

**4** Your words have steadied those who stumbled; you have braced the knees that were buckling. ^4

**5** But now trouble has come upon you, and you are weary. It strikes you, and you are dismayed. ^5

**6** Is your reverence not your confidence, and the uprightness of your ways your hope? ^6

**7** Consider now, I plead: Who, being innocent, has ever perished? Or where have the upright been destroyed? ^7

**8** As I have observed, those who plow iniquity and those who sow trouble reap the same. ^8

**9** By the breath of God they perish, and by the blast of His anger they are consumed. ^9

**10** The lion may roar, and the fierce lion may growl, yet the teeth of the young lions are broken. ^10

**11** The old lion perishes for lack of prey, and the cubs of the lioness are scattered. ^11

**12** Now a word came to me secretly; my ears caught a whisper of it. ^12

**13** In disquieting visions in the night, when deep sleep falls on men, ^13

**14** fear and trembling came over me and made all my bones shudder. ^14

**15** Then a spirit glided past my face, and the hair on my body bristled. ^15

**16** It stood still, but I could not discern its appearance; a form loomed before my eyes, and I heard a whispering voice: ^16

**17** ‘Can a mortal be more righteous than God, or a man more pure than his Maker? ^17

**18** If God puts no trust in His servants, and He charges His angels with error, ^18

**19** how much more those who dwell in houses of clay, whose foundations are in the dust, who can be crushed like a moth! ^19

**20** They are smashed to pieces from dawn to dusk; unnoticed, they perish forever. ^20

**21** Are not their tent cords pulled up, so that they die without wisdom?’ ^21
