# Job 24 (BSB)

← [[Job 23 - BSB]] | [[Job 25 - BSB]] →

**1** “Why does the Almighty not reserve times for judgment? Why may those who know Him never see His days? ^1

**2** Men move boundary stones; they pasture stolen flocks. ^2

**3** They drive away the donkey of the fatherless and take the widow’s ox in pledge. ^3

**4** They push the needy off the road and force all the poor of the land into hiding. ^4

**5** Indeed, like wild donkeys in the desert, the poor go to work foraging for food; the wasteland is food for their children. ^5

**6** They gather fodder in the fields and glean the vineyards of the wicked. ^6

**7** Without clothing, they spend the night naked; they have no covering against the cold. ^7

**8** Drenched by mountain rains, they huddle against the rocks for want of shelter. ^8

**9** The fatherless infant is snatched from the breast; the nursing child of the poor is seized for a debt. ^9

**10** Without clothing, they wander about naked. They carry the sheaves, but still go hungry. ^10

**11** They crush olives within their walls; they tread the winepresses, but go thirsty. ^11

**12** From the city, men groan, and the souls of the wounded cry out, yet God charges no one with wrongdoing. ^12

**13** Then there are those who rebel against the light, not knowing its ways or staying on its paths. ^13

**14** When daylight is gone, the murderer rises to kill the poor and needy; in the night he is like a thief. ^14

**15** The eye of the adulterer watches for twilight. Thinking, ‘No eye will see me,’ he covers his face. ^15

**16** In the dark they dig through houses; by day they shut themselves in, never to experience the light. ^16

**17** For to them, deep darkness is their morning; surely they are friends with the terrors of darkness! ^17

**18** They are but foam on the surface of the water; their portion of the land is cursed, so that no one turns toward their vineyards. ^18

**19** As drought and heat consume the melting snow, so Sheol steals those who have sinned. ^19

**20** The womb forgets them; the worm feeds on them; they are remembered no more. So injustice is broken like a tree. ^20

**21** They prey on the barren and childless, and show no kindness to the widow. ^21

**22** Yet by His power, God drags away the mighty; though rising up, they have no assurance of life. ^22

**23** He gives them a sense of security, but His eyes are on their ways. ^23

**24** They are exalted for a moment, then they are gone; they are brought low and gathered up like all others; they are cut off like heads of grain. ^24

**25** If this is not so, then who can prove me a liar and reduce my words to nothing?” ^25
