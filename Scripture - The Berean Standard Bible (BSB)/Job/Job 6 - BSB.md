# Job 6 (BSB)

← [[Job 5 - BSB]] | [[Job 7 - BSB]] →

**1** Then Job replied: ^1

**2** “If only my grief could be weighed and placed with my calamity on the scales. ^2

**3** For then it would outweigh the sand of the seas—no wonder my words have been rash. ^3

**4** For the arrows of the Almighty have pierced me; my spirit drinks in their poison; the terrors of God are arrayed against me. ^4

**5** Does a wild donkey bray over fresh grass, or an ox low over its fodder? ^5

**6** Is tasteless food eaten without salt, or is there flavor in the white of an egg? ^6

**7** My soul refuses to touch them; they are loathsome food to me. ^7

**8** If only my request were granted and God would fulfill my hope: ^8

**9** that God would be willing to crush me, to unleash His hand and cut me off! ^9

**10** It still brings me comfort, and joy through unrelenting pain, that I have not denied the words of the Holy One. ^10

**11** What strength do I have, that I should still hope? What is my future, that I should be patient? ^11

**12** Is my strength like that of stone, or my flesh made of bronze? ^12

**13** Is there any help within me now that success is driven from me? ^13

**14** A despairing man should have the kindness of his friend, even if he forsakes the fear of the Almighty. ^14

**15** But my brothers are as faithless as wadis, as seasonal streams that overflow, ^15

**16** darkened because of the ice and the inflow of melting snow, ^16

**17** but ceasing in the dry season and vanishing from their channels in the heat. ^17

**18** Caravans turn aside from their routes; they go into the wasteland and perish. ^18

**19** The caravans of Tema look for water; the travelers of Sheba hope to find it. ^19

**20** They are confounded because they had hoped; their arrival brings disappointment. ^20

**21** For now you are of no help; you see terror, and you are afraid. ^21

**22** Have I ever said, ‘Give me something; offer me a bribe from your wealth; ^22

**23** deliver me from the hand of the enemy; redeem me from the grasp of the ruthless’? ^23

**24** Teach me, and I will be silent. Help me understand how I have erred. ^24

**25** How painful are honest words! But what does your argument prove? ^25

**26** Do you intend to correct my words, and treat as wind my cry of despair? ^26

**27** You would even cast lots for an orphan and barter away your friend. ^27

**28** But now, please look at me. Would I lie to your face? ^28

**29** Reconsider; do not be unjust. Reconsider, for my righteousness is at stake. ^29

**30** Is there iniquity on my tongue? Can my mouth not discern malice? ^30
