# Job 14 (BSB)

← [[Job 13 - BSB]] | [[Job 15 - BSB]] →

**1** “Man, who is born of woman, is short of days and full of trouble. ^1

**2** Like a flower, he comes forth, then withers away; like a fleeting shadow, he does not endure. ^2

**3** Do You open Your eyes to one like this? Will You bring him into judgment before You? ^3

**4** Who can bring out clean from unclean? No one! ^4

**5** Since his days are determined and the number of his months is with You, and since You have set limits that he cannot exceed, ^5

**6** look away from him and let him rest, so he can enjoy his day as a hired hand. ^6

**7** For there is hope for a tree: If it is cut down, it will sprout again, and its tender shoots will not fail. ^7

**8** If its roots grow old in the ground and its stump dies in the soil, ^8

**9** at the scent of water it will bud and put forth twigs like a sapling. ^9

**10** But a man dies and is laid low; he breathes his last, and where is he? ^10

**11** As water disappears from the sea and a river becomes parched and dry, ^11

**12** so a man lies down and does not rise. Until the heavens are no more, he will not be awakened or roused from sleep. ^12

**13** If only You would hide me in Sheol and conceal me until Your anger has passed! If only You would appoint a time for me and then remember me! ^13

**14** When a man dies, will he live again? All the days of my hard service I will wait, until my renewal comes. ^14

**15** You will call, and I will answer; You will desire the work of Your hands. ^15

**16** For then You would count my steps, but would not keep track of my sin. ^16

**17** My transgression would be sealed in a bag, and You would cover over my iniquity. ^17

**18** But as a mountain erodes and crumbles and a rock is dislodged from its place, ^18

**19** as water wears away the stones and torrents wash away the soil, so You destroy a man’s hope. ^19

**20** You forever overpower him, and he passes on; You change his countenance and send him away. ^20

**21** If his sons receive honor, he does not know it; if they are brought low, he is unaware. ^21

**22** He feels only the pain of his own body and mourns only for himself.” ^22
