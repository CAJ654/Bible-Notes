# Job 34 (BSB)

← [[Job 33 - BSB]] | [[Job 35 - BSB]] →

**1** Then Elihu continued: ^1

**2** “Hear my words, O wise men; give ear to me, O men of learning. ^2

**3** For the ear tests words as the mouth tastes food. ^3

**4** Let us choose for ourselves what is right; let us learn together what is good. ^4

**5** For Job has declared, ‘I am righteous, yet God has deprived me of justice. ^5

**6** Would I lie about my case? My wound is incurable, though I am without transgression.’ ^6

**7** What man is like Job, who drinks up derision like water? ^7

**8** He keeps company with evildoers and walks with wicked men. ^8

**9** For he has said, ‘It profits a man nothing that he should delight in God.’ ^9

**10** Therefore listen to me, O men of understanding. Far be it from God to do wrong, and from the Almighty to act unjustly. ^10

**11** For according to a man’s deeds He repays him; according to a man’s ways He brings consequences. ^11

**12** Indeed, it is true that God does not act wickedly, and the Almighty does not pervert justice. ^12

**13** Who gave Him charge over the earth? Who appointed Him over the whole world? ^13

**14** If He were to set His heart to it and withdraw His Spirit and breath, ^14

**15** all flesh would perish together and mankind would return to the dust. ^15

**16** If you have understanding, hear this; listen to my words. ^16

**17** Could one who hates justice govern? Will you condemn the just and mighty One, ^17

**18** who says to kings, ‘You are worthless!’ and to nobles, ‘You are wicked,’ ^18

**19** who is not partial to princes and does not favor rich over poor? For they are all the work of His hands. ^19

**20** They die in an instant, in the middle of the night. The people convulse and pass away; the mighty are removed without human hand. ^20

**21** For His eyes are on the ways of a man, and He sees his every step. ^21

**22** There is no darkness or deep shadow where the workers of iniquity can hide. ^22

**23** For God need not examine a man further or have him approach for judgment. ^23

**24** He shatters the mighty without inquiry and sets up others in their place. ^24

**25** Therefore, He recognizes their deeds; He overthrows them in the night and they are crushed. ^25

**26** He strikes them for their wickedness in full view, ^26

**27** because they turned aside from Him and had no regard for any of His ways. ^27

**28** They caused the cry of the poor to come before Him, and He heard the outcry of the afflicted. ^28

**29** But when He remains silent, who can condemn Him? When He hides His face, who can see Him? Yet He watches over both man and nation, ^29

**30** that godless men should not rule or lay snares for the people. ^30

**31** Suppose someone says to God, ‘I have endured my punishment; I will offend no more. ^31

**32** Teach me what I cannot see; if I have done wrong, I will not do it again.’ ^32

**33** Should God repay you on your own terms when you have disavowed Him? You must choose, not I; so tell me what you know. ^33

**34** Men of understanding will declare to me, and the wise men who hear me will say: ^34

**35** ‘Job speaks without knowledge; his words lack insight.’ ^35

**36** If only Job were tried to the utmost for answering like a wicked man. ^36

**37** For he adds rebellion to his sin; he claps his hands among us and multiplies his words against God.” ^37
