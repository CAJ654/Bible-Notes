# Job 17 (BSB)

← [[Job 16 - BSB]] | [[Job 18 - BSB]] →

**1** “My spirit is broken; my days are extinguished; the grave awaits me. ^1

**2** Surely mockers surround me, and my eyes must gaze at their rebellion. ^2

**3** Give me, I pray, the pledge You demand. Who else will be my guarantor? ^3

**4** You have closed their minds to understanding; therefore You will not exalt them. ^4

**5** If a man denounces his friends for a price, the eyes of his children will fail. ^5

**6** He has made me a byword among the people, a man in whose face they spit. ^6

**7** My eyes have grown dim with grief, and my whole body is but a shadow. ^7

**8** The upright are appalled at this, and the innocent are stirred against the godless. ^8

**9** Yet a righteous one holds to his way, and the one with clean hands grows stronger. ^9

**10** But come back and try again, all of you. For I will not find a wise man among you. ^10

**11** My days have passed; my plans are broken off—even the desires of my heart. ^11

**12** They have turned night into day, making light seem near in the face of darkness. ^12

**13** If I look for Sheol as my home, if I spread out my bed in darkness, ^13

**14** and say to corruption, ‘You are my father,’ and to the worm, ‘My mother,’ or ‘My sister,’ ^14

**15** where then is my hope? Who can see any hope for me? ^15

**16** Will it go down to the gates of Sheol? Will we go down together into the dust?” ^16
