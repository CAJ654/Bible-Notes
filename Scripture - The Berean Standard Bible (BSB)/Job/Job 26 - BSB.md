# Job 26 (BSB)

← [[Job 25 - BSB]] | [[Job 27 - BSB]] →

**1** Then Job answered: ^1

**2** “How you have helped the powerless and saved the arm that is feeble! ^2

**3** How you have counseled the unwise and provided fully sound insight! ^3

**4** To whom have you uttered these words? And whose spirit spoke through you? ^4

**5** The dead tremble—those beneath the waters and those who dwell in them. ^5

**6** Sheol is naked before God, and Abaddon has no covering. ^6

**7** He stretches out the north over empty space; He hangs the earth upon nothing. ^7

**8** He wraps up the waters in His clouds, yet the clouds do not burst under their own weight. ^8

**9** He covers the face of the full moon, spreading over it His cloud. ^9

**10** He has inscribed a horizon on the face of the waters at the boundary between light and darkness. ^10

**11** The foundations of heaven quake, astounded at His rebuke. ^11

**12** By His power He stirred the sea; by His understanding He shattered Rahab. ^12

**13** By His breath the skies were cleared; His hand pierced the fleeing serpent. ^13

**14** Indeed, these are but the fringes of His ways; how faint is the whisper we hear of Him! Who then can understand the thunder of His power?” ^14
