# Job 39 (BSB)

← [[Job 38 - BSB]] | [[Job 40 - BSB]] →

**1** “Do you know when mountain goats give birth? Have you watched the doe bear her fawn? ^1

**2** Can you count the months they are pregnant? Do you know the time they give birth? ^2

**3** They crouch down and bring forth their young; they deliver their newborn. ^3

**4** Their young ones thrive and grow up in the open field; they leave and do not return. ^4

**5** Who set the wild donkey free? Who released the swift donkey from the harness? ^5

**6** I made the wilderness his home and the salt flats his dwelling. ^6

**7** He scorns the tumult of the city and never hears the shouts of a driver. ^7

**8** He roams the mountains for pasture, searching for any green thing. ^8

**9** Will the wild ox consent to serve you? Will he stay by your manger at night? ^9

**10** Can you hold him to the furrow with a harness? Will he plow the valleys behind you? ^10

**11** Can you rely on his great strength? Will you leave your hard work to him? ^11

**12** Can you trust him to bring in your grain and gather it to your threshing floor? ^12

**13** The wings of the ostrich flap joyfully, but cannot match the pinions and feathers of the stork. ^13

**14** For she leaves her eggs on the ground and lets them warm in the sand. ^14

**15** She forgets that a foot may crush them, or a wild animal may trample them. ^15

**16** She treats her young harshly, as if not her own, with no concern that her labor was in vain. ^16

**17** For God has deprived her of wisdom; He has not endowed her with understanding. ^17

**18** Yet when she proudly spreads her wings, she laughs at the horse and its rider. ^18

**19** Do you give strength to the horse or adorn his neck with a mane? ^19

**20** Do you make him leap like a locust, striking terror with his proud snorting? ^20

**21** He paws in the valley and rejoices in his strength; he charges into battle. ^21

**22** He laughs at fear, frightened of nothing; he does not turn back from the sword. ^22

**23** A quiver rattles at his side, along with a flashing spear and lance. ^23

**24** Trembling with excitement, he devours the distance; he cannot stand still when the ram’s horn sounds. ^24

**25** At the blast of the horn, he snorts with fervor. He catches the scent of battle from afar, the shouts of captains and the cry of war. ^25

**26** Does the hawk take flight by your understanding and spread his wings toward the south? ^26

**27** Does the eagle soar at your command and make his nest on high? ^27

**28** He dwells on a cliff and lodges there; his stronghold is on a rocky crag. ^28

**29** From there he spies out food; his eyes see it from afar. ^29

**30** His young ones feast on blood; and where the slain are, there he is.” ^30
