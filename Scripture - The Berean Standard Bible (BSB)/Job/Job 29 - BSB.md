# Job 29 (BSB)

← [[Job 28 - BSB]] | [[Job 30 - BSB]] →

**1** And Job continued his discourse: ^1

**2** “How I long for the months gone by, for the days when God watched over me, ^2

**3** when His lamp shone above my head, and by His light I walked through the darkness, ^3

**4** when I was in my prime, when the friendship of God rested on my tent, ^4

**5** when the Almighty was still with me and my children were around me, ^5

**6** when my steps were bathed in cream and the rock poured out for me streams of oil! ^6

**7** When I went out to the city gate and took my seat in the public square, ^7

**8** the young men saw me and withdrew, and the old men rose to their feet. ^8

**9** The princes refrained from speaking and covered their mouths with their hands. ^9

**10** The voices of the nobles were hushed, and their tongues stuck to the roofs of their mouths. ^10

**11** For those who heard me called me blessed, and those who saw me commended me, ^11

**12** because I rescued the poor who cried out and the fatherless who had no helper. ^12

**13** The dying man blessed me, and I made the widow’s heart sing for joy. ^13

**14** I put on righteousness, and it clothed me; justice was my robe and my turban. ^14

**15** I served as eyes to the blind and as feet to the lame. ^15

**16** I was a father to the needy, and I took up the case of the stranger. ^16

**17** I shattered the fangs of the unjust and snatched the prey from his teeth. ^17

**18** So I thought: ‘I will die in my nest and multiply my days as the sand. ^18

**19** My roots will spread out to the waters, and the dew will rest nightly on my branches. ^19

**20** My glory is ever new within me, and my bow is renewed in my hand.’ ^20

**21** Men listened to me with expectation, waiting silently for my counsel. ^21

**22** After my words, they spoke no more; my speech settled on them like dew. ^22

**23** They waited for me as for rain and drank in my words like spring showers. ^23

**24** If I smiled at them, they did not believe it; the light of my countenance was precious. ^24

**25** I chose their course and presided as chief. So I dwelt as a king among his troops, as a comforter of the mourners. ^25
