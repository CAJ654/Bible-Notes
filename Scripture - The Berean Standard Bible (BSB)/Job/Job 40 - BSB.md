# Job 40 (BSB)

← [[Job 39 - BSB]] | [[Job 41 - BSB]] →

**1** And the LORD said to Job: ^1

**2** “Will the faultfinder contend with the Almighty? Let him who argues with God give an answer.” ^2

**3** Then Job answered the LORD: ^3

**4** “Behold, I am insignificant. How can I reply to You? I place my hand over my mouth. ^4

**5** I have spoken once, but I have no answer—twice, but I have nothing to add.” ^5

**6** Then the LORD answered Job out of the whirlwind and said: ^6

**7** “Now brace yourself like a man; I will question you, and you shall inform Me. ^7

**8** Would you really annul My justice? Would you condemn Me to justify yourself? ^8

**9** Do you have an arm like God’s? Can you thunder with a voice like His? ^9

**10** Then adorn yourself with majesty and splendor, and clothe yourself with honor and glory. ^10

**11** Unleash the fury of your wrath; look on every proud man and bring him low. ^11

**12** Look on every proud man and humble him; trample the wicked where they stand. ^12

**13** Bury them together in the dust; imprison them in the grave. ^13

**14** Then I will confess to you that your own right hand can save you. ^14

**15** Look at Behemoth, which I made along with you. He feeds on grass like an ox. ^15

**16** See the strength of his loins and the power in the muscles of his belly. ^16

**17** His tail sways like a cedar; the sinews of his thighs are tightly knit. ^17

**18** His bones are tubes of bronze; his limbs are rods of iron. ^18

**19** He is the foremost of God’s works; only his Maker can draw the sword against him. ^19

**20** The hills yield him their produce, while all the beasts of the field play nearby. ^20

**21** He lies under the lotus plants, hidden among the reeds of the marsh. ^21

**22** The lotus plants conceal him in their shade; the willows of the brook surround him. ^22

**23** Though the river rages, Behemoth is unafraid; he remains secure, though the Jordan surges to his mouth. ^23

**24** Can anyone capture him as he looks on, or pierce his nose with a snare? ^24
