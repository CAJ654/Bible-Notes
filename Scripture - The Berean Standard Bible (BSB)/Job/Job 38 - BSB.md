# Job 38 (BSB)

← [[Job 37 - BSB]] | [[Job 39 - BSB]] →

**1** Then the LORD answered Job out of the whirlwind and said: ^1

**2** “Who is this who obscures My counsel by words without knowledge? ^2

**3** Now brace yourself like a man; I will question you, and you shall inform Me. ^3

**4** Where were you when I laid the foundations of the earth? Tell Me, if you have understanding. ^4

**5** Who fixed its measurements? Surely you know! Or who stretched a measuring line across it? ^5

**6** On what were its foundations set, or who laid its cornerstone, ^6

**7** while the morning stars sang together and all the sons of God shouted for joy? ^7

**8** Who enclosed the sea behind doors when it burst forth from the womb, ^8

**9** when I made the clouds its garment and thick darkness its blanket, ^9

**10** when I fixed its boundaries and set in place its bars and doors, ^10

**11** and I declared: ‘You may come this far, but no farther; here your proud waves must stop’? ^11

**12** In your days, have you commanded the morning or assigned the dawn its place, ^12

**13** that it might spread to the ends of the earth and shake the wicked out of it? ^13

**14** The earth takes shape like clay under a seal; its hills stand out like the folds of a garment. ^14

**15** Light is withheld from the wicked, and their upraised arm is broken. ^15

**16** Have you journeyed to the vents of the sea or walked in the trenches of the deep? ^16

**17** Have the gates of death been revealed to you? Have you seen the gates of the shadow of death? ^17

**18** Have you surveyed the extent of the earth? Tell Me, if you know all this. ^18

**19** Where is the way to the home of light? Do you know where darkness resides, ^19

**20** so you can lead it back to its border? Do you know the paths to its home? ^20

**21** Surely you know, for you were already born! And the number of your days is great! ^21

**22** Have you entered the storehouses of snow or observed the storehouses of hail, ^22

**23** which I hold in reserve for times of trouble, for the day of war and battle? ^23

**24** In which direction is the lightning dispersed, or the east wind scattered over the earth? ^24

**25** Who cuts a channel for the flood or clears a path for the thunderbolt, ^25

**26** to bring rain on a barren land, on a desert where no man lives, ^26

**27** to satisfy the parched wasteland and make it sprout with tender grass? ^27

**28** Does the rain have a father? Who has begotten the drops of dew? ^28

**29** From whose womb does the ice emerge? Who gives birth to the frost from heaven, ^29

**30** when the waters become hard as stone and the surface of the deep is frozen? ^30

**31** Can you bind the chains of the Pleiades or loosen the belt of Orion? ^31

**32** Can you bring forth the constellations in their seasons or lead out the Bear and her cubs? ^32

**33** Do you know the laws of the heavens? Can you set their dominion over the earth? ^33

**34** Can you command the clouds so that a flood of water covers you? ^34

**35** Can you send the lightning bolts on their way? Do they report to you, ‘Here we are’? ^35

**36** Who has put wisdom in the heart or given understanding to the mind? ^36

**37** Who has the wisdom to count the clouds? Or who can tilt the water jars of the heavens ^37

**38** when the dust hardens into a mass and the clods of earth stick together? ^38

**39** Can you hunt the prey for a lioness or satisfy the hunger of young lions ^39

**40** when they crouch in their dens and lie in wait in the thicket? ^40

**41** Who provides food for the raven when its young cry out to God as they wander about for lack of food? ^41
