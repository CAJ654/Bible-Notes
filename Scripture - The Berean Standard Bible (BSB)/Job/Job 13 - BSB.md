# Job 13 (BSB)

← [[Job 12 - BSB]] | [[Job 14 - BSB]] →

**1** “Indeed, my eyes have seen all this; my ears have heard and understood. ^1

**2** What you know, I also know; I am not inferior to you. ^2

**3** Yet I desire to speak to the Almighty and argue my case before God. ^3

**4** You, however, smear with lies; you are all worthless physicians. ^4

**5** If only you would remain silent; for that would be your wisdom! ^5

**6** Hear now my argument, and listen to the plea of my lips. ^6

**7** Will you speak wickedly on God’s behalf or speak deceitfully for Him? ^7

**8** Would you show Him partiality or argue in His defense? ^8

**9** Would it be well when He examined you? Could you deceive Him as you would deceive a man? ^9

**10** Surely He would rebuke you if you secretly showed partiality. ^10

**11** Would His majesty not terrify you? Would the dread of Him not fall upon you? ^11

**12** Your maxims are proverbs of ashes; your defenses are defenses of clay. ^12

**13** Be silent, and I will speak. Then let come to me what may. ^13

**14** Why do I put myself at risk and take my life in my own hands? ^14

**15** Though He slay me, I will hope in Him. I will still defend my ways to His face. ^15

**16** Moreover, this will be my salvation, for no godless man can appear before Him. ^16

**17** Listen carefully to my words; let my declaration ring in your ears. ^17

**18** See now, I have prepared my case; I know that I will be vindicated. ^18

**19** Can anyone indict me? If so, I will be silent and die. ^19

**20** Only grant these two things to me, so that I need not hide from You: ^20

**21** Withdraw Your hand from me, and do not let Your terror frighten me. ^21

**22** Then call me, and I will answer, or let me speak, and You can reply. ^22

**23** How many are my iniquities and sins? Reveal to me my transgression and sin. ^23

**24** Why do You hide Your face and consider me as Your enemy? ^24

**25** Would You frighten a windblown leaf? Would You chase after dry chaff? ^25

**26** For You record bitter accusations against me and bequeath to me the iniquities of my youth. ^26

**27** You put my feet in the stocks and stand watch over all my paths; You set a limit for the soles of my feet. ^27

**28** So man wastes away like something rotten, like a moth-eaten garment. ^28
