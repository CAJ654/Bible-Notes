# Job 25 (BSB)

← [[Job 24 - BSB]] | [[Job 26 - BSB]] →

**1** Then Bildad the Shuhite replied: ^1

**2** “Dominion and awe belong to God; He establishes harmony in the heights of heaven. ^2

**3** Can His troops be numbered? On whom does His light not rise? ^3

**4** How then can a man be just before God? How can one born of woman be pure? ^4

**5** If even the moon does not shine, and the stars are not pure in His sight, ^5

**6** how much less man, who is but a maggot, and the son of man, who is but a worm!” ^6
