# Job 31 (BSB)

← [[Job 30 - BSB]] | [[Job 32 - BSB]] →

**1** “I have made a covenant with my eyes. How then could I gaze with desire at a virgin? ^1

**2** For what is the allotment of God from above, or the heritage from the Almighty on high? ^2

**3** Does not disaster come to the unjust and calamity to the workers of iniquity? ^3

**4** Does He not see my ways and count my every step? ^4

**5** If I have walked in falsehood or my foot has rushed to deceit, ^5

**6** let God weigh me with honest scales, that He may know my integrity. ^6

**7** If my steps have turned from the path, if my heart has followed my eyes, or if impurity has stuck to my hands, ^7

**8** then may another eat what I have sown, and may my crops be uprooted. ^8

**9** If my heart has been enticed by my neighbor’s wife, or I have lurked at his door, ^9

**10** then may my own wife grind grain for another, and may other men sleep with her. ^10

**11** For that would be a heinous crime, an iniquity to be judged. ^11

**12** For it is a fire that burns down to Abaddon; it would root out my entire harvest. ^12

**13** If I have rejected the cause of my manservant or maidservant when they made a complaint against me, ^13

**14** what will I do when God rises to judge? How will I answer when called to account? ^14

**15** Did not He who made me in the womb also make them? Did not the same One form us in the womb? ^15

**16** If I have denied the desires of the poor or allowed the widow’s eyes to fail, ^16

**17** if I have eaten my morsel alone, not sharing it with the fatherless— ^17

**18** though from my youth I reared him as would a father, and from my mother’s womb I guided the widow— ^18

**19** if I have seen one perish for lack of clothing, or a needy man without a cloak, ^19

**20** if his heart has not blessed me for warming him with the fleece of my sheep, ^20

**21** if I have lifted up my hand against the fatherless because I saw that I had support in the gate, ^21

**22** then may my arm fall from my shoulder and be torn from its socket. ^22

**23** For calamity from God terrifies me, and His splendor I cannot overpower. ^23

**24** If I have put my trust in gold or called pure gold my security, ^24

**25** if I have rejoiced in my great wealth because my hand had gained so much, ^25

**26** if I have beheld the sun in its radiance or the moon moving in splendor, ^26

**27** so that my heart was secretly enticed and my hand threw a kiss from my mouth, ^27

**28** this would also be an iniquity to be judged, for I would have denied God on high. ^28

**29** If I have rejoiced in my enemy’s ruin, or exulted when evil befell him— ^29

**30** I have not allowed my mouth to sin by asking for his life with a curse— ^30

**31** if the men of my house have not said, ‘Who is there who has not had his fill?’— ^31

**32** but no stranger had to lodge on the street, for my door has been open to the traveler— ^32

**33** if I have covered my transgressions like Adam by hiding my guilt in my heart, ^33

**34** because I greatly feared the crowds and the contempt of the clans terrified me, so that I kept silent and would not go outside— ^34

**35** (Oh, that I had one to hear me! Here is my signature. Let the Almighty answer me; let my accuser compose an indictment. ^35

**36** Surely I would carry it on my shoulder and wear it like a crown. ^36

**37** I would give account of all my steps; I would approach Him like a prince.)— ^37

**38** if my land cries out against me and its furrows weep together, ^38

**39** if I have devoured its produce without payment or broken the spirit of its tenants, ^39

**40** then let briers grow instead of wheat and stinkweed instead of barley.” Thus conclude the words of Job. ^40
