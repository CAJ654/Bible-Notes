# Job 20 (BSB)

← [[Job 19 - BSB]] | [[Job 21 - BSB]] →

**1** Then Zophar the Naamathite replied: ^1

**2** “So my anxious thoughts compel me to answer, because of the turmoil within me. ^2

**3** I have heard a rebuke that insults me, and my understanding prompts a reply. ^3

**4** Do you not know that from antiquity, since man was placed on the earth, ^4

**5** the triumph of the wicked has been brief and the joy of the godless momentary? ^5

**6** Though his arrogance reaches the heavens, and his head touches the clouds, ^6

**7** he will perish forever, like his own dung; those who had seen him will ask, ‘Where is he?’ ^7

**8** He will fly away like a dream, never to be found; he will be chased away like a vision in the night. ^8

**9** The eye that saw him will see him no more, and his place will no longer behold him. ^9

**10** His sons will seek the favor of the poor, for his own hands must return his wealth. ^10

**11** The youthful vigor that fills his bones will lie down with him in the dust. ^11

**12** Though evil is sweet in his mouth and he conceals it under his tongue, ^12

**13** though he cannot bear to let it go and keeps it in his mouth, ^13

**14** yet in his stomach his food sours into the venom of cobras within him. ^14

**15** He swallows wealth but vomits it out; God will force it from his stomach. ^15

**16** He will suck the poison of cobras; the fangs of a viper will kill him. ^16

**17** He will not enjoy the streams, the rivers flowing with honey and cream. ^17

**18** He must return the fruit of his labor without consuming it; he cannot enjoy the profits of his trading. ^18

**19** For he has oppressed and forsaken the poor; he has seized houses he did not build. ^19

**20** Because his appetite is never satisfied, he cannot escape with his treasure. ^20

**21** Nothing is left for him to consume; thus his prosperity will not endure. ^21

**22** In the midst of his plenty, he will be distressed; the full force of misery will come upon him. ^22

**23** When he has filled his stomach, God will vent His fury upon him, raining it down on him as he eats. ^23

**24** Though he flees from an iron weapon, a bronze-tipped arrow will pierce him. ^24

**25** It is drawn out of his back, the gleaming point from his liver. Terrors come over him. ^25

**26** Total darkness is reserved for his treasures. A fire unfanned will consume him and devour what is left in his tent. ^26

**27** The heavens will expose his iniquity, and the earth will rise up against him. ^27

**28** The possessions of his house will be removed, flowing away on the day of God’s wrath. ^28

**29** This is the wicked man’s portion from God, the inheritance God has appointed him.” ^29
