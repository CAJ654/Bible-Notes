# Job 36 (BSB)

← [[Job 35 - BSB]] | [[Job 37 - BSB]] →

**1** And Elihu continued: ^1

**2** “Bear with me a little longer, and I will show you that there is more to be said on God’s behalf. ^2

**3** I get my knowledge from afar, and I will ascribe justice to my Maker. ^3

**4** For truly my words are free of falsehood; one perfect in knowledge is with you. ^4

**5** Indeed, God is mighty, but He despises no one; He is mighty in strength of understanding. ^5

**6** He does not keep the wicked alive, but He grants justice to the afflicted. ^6

**7** He does not take His eyes off the righteous, but He enthrones them with kings and exalts them forever. ^7

**8** And if men are bound with chains, caught in cords of affliction, ^8

**9** then He tells them their deeds and how arrogantly they have transgressed. ^9

**10** He opens their ears to correction and commands that they turn from iniquity. ^10

**11** If they obey and serve Him, then they end their days in prosperity and their years in happiness. ^11

**12** But if they do not obey, then they perish by the sword and die without knowledge. ^12

**13** The godless in heart harbor resentment; even when He binds them, they do not cry for help. ^13

**14** They die in their youth, among the male shrine prostitutes. ^14

**15** God rescues the afflicted by their affliction and opens their ears in oppression. ^15

**16** Indeed, He drew you from the jaws of distress to a spacious and broad place, to a table full of richness. ^16

**17** But now you are laden with the judgment due the wicked; judgment and justice have seized you. ^17

**18** Be careful that no one lures you with riches; do not let a large bribe lead you astray. ^18

**19** Can your wealth or all your mighty effort keep you from distress? ^19

**20** Do not long for the night, when people vanish from their homes. ^20

**21** Be careful not to turn to iniquity, for this you have preferred to affliction. ^21

**22** Behold, God is exalted in His power. Who is a teacher like Him? ^22

**23** Who has appointed His way for Him, or told Him, ‘You have done wrong’? ^23

**24** Remember to magnify His work, which men have praised in song. ^24

**25** All mankind has seen it; men behold it from afar. ^25

**26** Indeed, God is great—beyond our knowledge; the number of His years is unsearchable. ^26

**27** For He draws up drops of water which distill the rain from the mist, ^27

**28** which the clouds pour out and shower abundantly on mankind. ^28

**29** Furthermore, who can understand how the clouds spread out, how the thunder roars from His pavilion? ^29

**30** See how He scatters His lightning around Him and covers the depths of the sea. ^30

**31** For by these He judges the nations and provides food in abundance. ^31

**32** He fills His hands with lightning and commands it to strike its mark. ^32

**33** The thunder declares His presence; even the cattle regard the rising storm. ^33
