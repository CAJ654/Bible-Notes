# Job 21 (BSB)

← [[Job 20 - BSB]] | [[Job 22 - BSB]] →

**1** Then Job answered: ^1

**2** “Listen carefully to my words; let this be your consolation to me. ^2

**3** Bear with me while I speak; then, after I have spoken, you may go on mocking. ^3

**4** Is my complaint against a man? Then why should I not be impatient? ^4

**5** Look at me and be appalled; put your hand over your mouth. ^5

**6** When I remember, terror takes hold, and my body trembles in horror. ^6

**7** Why do the wicked live on, growing old and increasing in power? ^7

**8** Their descendants are established around them, and their offspring before their eyes. ^8

**9** Their homes are safe from fear; no rod of punishment from God is upon them. ^9

**10** Their bulls breed without fail; their cows bear calves and do not miscarry. ^10

**11** They send forth their little ones like a flock; their children skip about, ^11

**12** singing to the tambourine and lyre and making merry at the sound of the flute. ^12

**13** They spend their days in prosperity and go down to Sheol in peace. ^13

**14** Yet they say to God: ‘Leave us alone! For we have no desire to know Your ways. ^14

**15** Who is the Almighty, that we should serve Him, and what would we gain if we pray to Him?’ ^15

**16** Still, their prosperity is not in their own hands, so I stay far from the counsel of the wicked. ^16

**17** How often is the lamp of the wicked put out? Does disaster come upon them? Does God, in His anger, apportion destruction? ^17

**18** Are they like straw before the wind, like chaff swept away by a storm? ^18

**19** It is said that God lays up one’s punishment for his children. Let God repay the man himself, so he will know it. ^19

**20** Let his eyes see his own destruction; let him drink for himself the wrath of the Almighty. ^20

**21** For what does he care about his household after him, when the number of his months has run out? ^21

**22** Can anyone teach knowledge to God, since He judges those on high? ^22

**23** One man dies full of vigor, completely secure and at ease. ^23

**24** His body is well nourished, and his bones are rich with marrow. ^24

**25** Yet another man dies in the bitterness of his soul, having never tasted prosperity. ^25

**26** But together they lie down in the dust, and worms cover them both. ^26

**27** Behold, I know your thoughts full well, the schemes by which you would wrong me. ^27

**28** For you say, ‘Where now is the nobleman’s house, and where are the tents in which the wicked dwell?’ ^28

**29** Have you never asked those who travel the roads? Do you not accept their reports? ^29

**30** Indeed, the evil man is spared from the day of calamity, delivered from the day of wrath. ^30

**31** Who denounces his behavior to his face? Who repays him for what he has done? ^31

**32** He is carried to the grave, and watch is kept over his tomb. ^32

**33** The clods of the valley are sweet to him; everyone follows behind him, and those before him are without number. ^33

**34** So how can you comfort me with empty words? For your answers remain full of falsehood.” ^34
