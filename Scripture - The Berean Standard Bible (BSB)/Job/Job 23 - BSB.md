# Job 23 (BSB)

← [[Job 22 - BSB]] | [[Job 24 - BSB]] →

**1** Then Job answered: ^1

**2** “Even today my complaint is bitter. His hand is heavy despite my groaning. ^2

**3** If only I knew where to find Him, so that I could go to His seat. ^3

**4** I would plead my case before Him and fill my mouth with arguments. ^4

**5** I would learn how He would answer, and consider what He would say. ^5

**6** Would He contend with me in His great power? No, He would certainly take note of me. ^6

**7** Then an upright man could reason with Him, and I would be delivered forever from my Judge. ^7

**8** If I go east, He is not there, and if I go west, I cannot find Him. ^8

**9** When He is at work in the north, I cannot behold Him; when He turns to the south, I cannot see Him. ^9

**10** Yet He knows the way I have taken; when He has tested me, I will come forth as gold. ^10

**11** My feet have followed in His tracks; I have kept His way without turning aside. ^11

**12** I have not departed from the command of His lips; I have treasured the words of His mouth more than my daily bread. ^12

**13** But He is unchangeable, and who can oppose Him? He does what He desires. ^13

**14** For He carries out His decree against me, and He has many such plans. ^14

**15** Therefore I am terrified in His presence; when I consider this, I fear Him. ^15

**16** God has made my heart faint; the Almighty has terrified me. ^16

**17** Yet I am not silenced by the darkness, by the thick darkness that covers my face. ^17
