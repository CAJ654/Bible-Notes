# Job 27 (BSB)

← [[Job 26 - BSB]] | [[Job 28 - BSB]] →

**1** Job continued his discourse: ^1

**2** “As surely as God lives, who has deprived me of justice—the Almighty, who has embittered my soul— ^2

**3** as long as my breath is still within me and the breath of God remains in my nostrils, ^3

**4** my lips will not speak wickedness, and my tongue will not utter deceit. ^4

**5** I will never say that you are right; I will maintain my integrity until I die. ^5

**6** I will cling to my righteousness and never let go. As long as I live, my conscience will not accuse me. ^6

**7** May my enemy be like the wicked and my opponent like the unjust. ^7

**8** For what is the hope of the godless when he is cut off, when God takes away his life? ^8

**9** Will God hear his cry when distress comes upon him? ^9

**10** Will he delight in the Almighty? Will he call upon God at all times? ^10

**11** I will instruct you in the power of God. I will not conceal the ways of the Almighty. ^11

**12** Surely all of you have seen it for yourselves. Why then do you keep up this empty talk? ^12

**13** This is the wicked man’s portion from God—the heritage the ruthless receive from the Almighty. ^13

**14** Though his sons are many, they are destined for the sword; and his offspring will never have enough food. ^14

**15** His survivors will be buried by the plague, and their widows will not weep for them. ^15

**16** Though he heaps up silver like dust and piles up a wardrobe like clay, ^16

**17** what he lays up, the righteous will wear, and his silver will be divided by the innocent. ^17

**18** The house he built is like a moth’s cocoon, like a hut set up by a watchman. ^18

**19** He lies down wealthy, but will do so no more; when he opens his eyes, all is gone. ^19

**20** Terrors overtake him like a flood; a tempest sweeps him away in the night. ^20

**21** The east wind carries him away, and he is gone; it sweeps him out of his place. ^21

**22** It hurls itself against him without mercy as he flees headlong from its power. ^22

**23** It claps its hands at him and hisses him out of his place. ^23
