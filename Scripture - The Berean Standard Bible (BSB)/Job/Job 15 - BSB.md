# Job 15 (BSB)

← [[Job 14 - BSB]] | [[Job 16 - BSB]] →

**1** Then Eliphaz the Temanite replied: ^1

**2** “Does a wise man answer with empty counsel or fill his belly with the hot east wind? ^2

**3** Should he argue with useless words or speeches that serve no purpose? ^3

**4** But you even undermine the fear of God and hinder meditation before Him. ^4

**5** For your iniquity instructs your mouth, and you choose the language of the crafty. ^5

**6** Your own mouth, not mine, condemns you; your own lips testify against you. ^6

**7** Were you the first man ever born? Were you brought forth before the hills? ^7

**8** Do you listen in on the council of God or limit wisdom to yourself? ^8

**9** What do you know that we do not? What do you understand that is not clear to us? ^9

**10** Both the gray-haired and the aged are on our side—men much older than your father. ^10

**11** Are the consolations of God not enough for you, even words spoken gently to you? ^11

**12** Why has your heart carried you away, and why do your eyes flash, ^12

**13** so that you turn your spirit against God and pour such words from your mouth? ^13

**14** What is man, that he should be pure, or one born of woman, that he should be righteous? ^14

**15** If God puts no trust in His holy ones, if even the heavens are not pure in His eyes, ^15

**16** how much less man, who is vile and corrupt, who drinks injustice like water? ^16

**17** Listen to me and I will inform you. I will describe what I have seen, ^17

**18** what was declared by wise men and was not concealed from their fathers, ^18

**19** to whom alone the land was given when no foreigner passed among them. ^19

**20** A wicked man writhes in pain all his days; only a few years are reserved for the ruthless. ^20

**21** Sounds of terror fill his ears; in his prosperity the destroyer attacks him. ^21

**22** He despairs of his return from darkness; he is marked for the sword. ^22

**23** He wanders about as food for vultures; he knows the day of darkness is at hand. ^23

**24** Distress and anguish terrify him, overwhelming him like a king poised to attack. ^24

**25** For he has stretched out his hand against God and has vaunted himself against the Almighty, ^25

**26** rushing headlong at Him with a thick, studded shield. ^26

**27** Though his face is covered with fat and his waistline bulges with flesh, ^27

**28** he will dwell in ruined cities, in abandoned houses destined to become rubble. ^28

**29** He will no longer be rich; his wealth will not endure. His possessions will not overspread the land. ^29

**30** He will not escape from the darkness; the flame will wither his shoots, and the breath of God’s mouth will carry him away. ^30

**31** Let him not deceive himself with trust in emptiness, for emptiness will be his reward. ^31

**32** It will be paid in full before his time, and his branch will not flourish. ^32

**33** He will be like a vine stripped of its unripe grapes, like an olive tree that sheds its blossoms. ^33

**34** For the company of the godless will be barren, and fire will consume the tents of bribery. ^34

**35** They conceive trouble and give birth to evil; their womb is pregnant with deceit.” ^35
