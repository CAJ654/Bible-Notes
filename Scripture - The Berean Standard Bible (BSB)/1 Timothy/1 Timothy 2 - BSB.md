# 1 Timothy 2 (BSB)

← [[1 Timothy 1 - BSB]] | [[1 Timothy 3 - BSB]] →

**1** First of all, then, I urge that petitions, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgiving be offered for everyone— ^1

**2** for kings and all those in authority—so that we may lead tranquil and quiet lives in all godliness and dignity. ^2

**3** This is good and pleasing in the sight of God our Savior, ^3

**4** who wants everyone to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth. ^4

**5** For there is one God, and there is one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus, ^5

**6** who gave Himself as a ransom for all—the testimony that was given at just the right time. ^6

**7** For this reason I was appointed as a preacher, an apostle, and a faithful and true teacher of the Gentiles. I am telling the truth; I am not lying about anything. ^7

**8** Therefore I want the men everywhere to pray, lifting up holy hands, without anger or dissension. ^8

**9** Likewise, I want the women to adorn themselves with respectable apparel, with modesty, and with self-control, not with braided hair or gold or pearls or expensive clothes, ^9

**10** but with good deeds, as is proper for women who profess to worship God. ^10

**11** A woman must learn in quietness and full submissiveness. ^11

**12** I do not permit a woman to teach or to exercise authority over a man; she is to remain quiet. ^12

**13** For Adam was formed first, and then Eve. ^13

**14** And it was not Adam who was deceived, but the woman who was deceived and fell into transgression. ^14

**15** Women, however, will be saved through childbearing, if they continue in faith, love, and holiness, with self-control. ^15
