# Ephesians 1 (BSB)

[[Ephesians 2 - BSB]] →

**1** Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, To the saints in Ephesus, the faithful in Christ Jesus: ^1

**2** Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. ^2

**3** Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly realms. ^3

**4** For He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world to be holy and blameless in His presence. In love ^4

**5** He predestined us for adoption as His sons through Jesus Christ, according to the good pleasure of His will, ^5

**6** to the praise of His glorious grace, which He has freely given us in the Beloved One. ^6

**7** In Him we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of our trespasses, according to the riches of His grace ^7

**8** that He lavished on us with all wisdom and understanding. ^8

**9** And He has made known to us the mystery of His will according to His good pleasure, which He purposed in Christ ^9

**10** as a plan for the fullness of time, to bring all things in heaven and on earth together in Christ. ^10

**11** In Him we were also chosen as God’s own, having been predestined according to the plan of Him who works out everything by the counsel of His will, ^11

**12** in order that we, who were the first to hope in Christ, would be for the praise of His glory. ^12

**13** And in Him, having heard and believed the word of truth—the gospel of your salvation—you were sealed with the promised Holy Spirit, ^13

**14** who is the pledge of our inheritance until the redemption of those who are God’s possession, to the praise of His glory. ^14

**15** For this reason, ever since I heard about your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all the saints, ^15

**16** I have not stopped giving thanks for you, remembering you in my prayers, ^16

**17** that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the glorious Father, may give you a spirit of wisdom and revelation in your knowledge of Him. ^17

**18** I ask that the eyes of your heart may be enlightened, so that you may know the hope of His calling, the riches of His glorious inheritance in the saints, ^18

**19** and the surpassing greatness of His power to us who believe. These are in accordance with the working of His mighty strength, ^19

**20** which He exerted in Christ when He raised Him from the dead and seated Him at His right hand in the heavenly realms, ^20

**21** far above all rule and authority, power and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in the present age but also in the one to come. ^21

**22** And God put everything under His feet and made Him head over everything for the church, ^22

**23** which is His body, the fullness of Him who fills all in all. ^23
