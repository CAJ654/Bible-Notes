# Luke 24 (BSB)

← [[Luke 23 - BSB]]

**1** On the first day of the week, very early in the morning, the women came to the tomb, bringing the spices they had prepared. ^1

**2** They found the stone rolled away from the tomb, ^2

**3** but when they entered, they did not find the body of the Lord Jesus. ^3

**4** While they were puzzling over this, suddenly two men in radiant apparel stood beside them. ^4

**5** As the women bowed their faces to the ground in terror, the two men asked them, “Why do you look for the living among the dead? ^5

**6** He is not here; He has risen! Remember how He told you while He was still in Galilee: ^6

**7** ‘The Son of Man must be delivered into the hands of sinful men, and be crucified, and on the third day rise again.’” ^7

**8** Then they remembered His words. ^8

**9** And when they returned from the tomb, they reported all these things to the Eleven and to all the others. ^9

**10** It was Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, and the other women with them who told this to the apostles. ^10

**11** But their words seemed like nonsense to them, and they did not believe the women. ^11

**12** Peter, however, got up and ran to the tomb. And after bending down and seeing only the linen cloths, he went away, wondering to himself what had happened. ^12

**13** That same day two of them were going to a village called Emmaus, about seven miles from Jerusalem. ^13

**14** They were talking with each other about everything that had happened. ^14

**15** And as they talked and deliberated, Jesus Himself came up and walked along with them. ^15

**16** But their eyes were kept from recognizing Him. ^16

**17** He asked them, “What are you discussing so intently as you walk along?” They stood still, with sadness on their faces. ^17

**18** One of them, named Cleopas, asked Him, “Are You the only visitor to Jerusalem who does not know the things that have happened there in recent days?” ^18

**19** “What things?” He asked. “The events involving Jesus of Nazareth,” they answered. “This man was a prophet, powerful in speech and action before God and all the people. ^19

**20** Our chief priests and rulers delivered Him up to the sentence of death, and they crucified Him. ^20

**21** But we were hoping He was the One who would redeem Israel. And besides all this, it is the third day since these things took place. ^21

**22** Furthermore, some of our women astounded us. They were at the tomb early this morning, ^22

**23** but they did not find His body. They came and told us they had seen a vision of angels, who said that Jesus was alive. ^23

**24** Then some of our companions went to the tomb and found it just as the women had described. But Him they did not see.” ^24

**25** Then Jesus said to them, “O foolish ones, and slow of heart to believe all that the prophets have spoken! ^25

**26** Was it not necessary for the Christ to suffer these things and then to enter His glory?” ^26

**27** And beginning with Moses and all the Prophets, He explained to them what was written in all the Scriptures about Himself. ^27

**28** As they approached the village where they were headed, He seemed to be going farther. ^28

**29** But they pleaded with Him, “Stay with us, for it is nearly evening and the day is almost over.” So He went in to stay with them. ^29

**30** While He was reclining at the table with them, He took bread, spoke a blessing and broke it, and gave it to them. ^30

**31** Then their eyes were opened and they recognized Jesus—and He disappeared from their sight. ^31

**32** They asked each other, “Were not our hearts burning within us as He spoke with us on the road and opened the Scriptures to us?” ^32

**33** And they got up that very hour and returned to Jerusalem. There they found the Eleven and those with them, gathered together ^33

**34** and saying, “The Lord has indeed risen and has appeared to Simon!” ^34

**35** Then the two told what had happened on the road, and how they had recognized Jesus in the breaking of the bread. ^35

**36** While they were describing these events, Jesus Himself stood among them and said, “Peace be with you.” ^36

**37** But they were startled and frightened, thinking they had seen a spirit. ^37

**38** “Why are you troubled,” Jesus asked, “and why do doubts arise in your hearts? ^38

**39** Look at My hands and My feet. It is I Myself. Touch Me and see—for a spirit does not have flesh and bones, as you see I have.” ^39

**40** And when He had said this, He showed them His hands and feet. ^40

**41** While they were still in disbelief because of their joy and amazement, He asked them, “Do you have anything here to eat?” ^41

**42** So they gave Him a piece of broiled fish, ^42

**43** and He took it and ate it in front of them. ^43

**44** Jesus said to them, “These are the words I spoke to you while I was still with you: Everything must be fulfilled that is written about Me in the Law of Moses, the Prophets, and the Psalms.” ^44

**45** Then He opened their minds to understand the Scriptures. ^45

**46** And He told them, “This is what is written: The Christ will suffer and rise from the dead on the third day, ^46

**47** and in His name repentance and forgiveness of sins will be proclaimed to all nations, beginning in Jerusalem. ^47

**48** You are witnesses of these things. ^48

**49** And behold, I am sending the promise of My Father upon you. But remain in the city until you have been clothed with power from on high.” ^49

**50** When Jesus had led them out as far as Bethany, He lifted up His hands and blessed them. ^50

**51** While He was blessing them, He left them and was carried up into heaven. ^51

**52** And they worshiped Him and returned to Jerusalem with great joy, ^52

**53** praising God continually in the temple. ^53
