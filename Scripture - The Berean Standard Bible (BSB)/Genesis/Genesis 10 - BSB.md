# Genesis 10 (BSB)

← [[Genesis 9 - BSB]] | [[Genesis 11 - BSB]] →

**1** This is the account of Noah’s sons Shem, Ham, and Japheth, who also had sons after the flood. ^1

**2** The sons of Japheth: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. ^2

**3** The sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah. ^3

**4** And the sons of Javan: Elishah, Tarshish, the Kittites, and the Rodanites. ^4

**5** From these, the maritime peoples separated into their territories, according to their languages, by clans within their nations. ^5

**6** The sons of Ham: Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan. ^6

**7** The sons of Cush: Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca. And the sons of Raamah: Sheba and Dedan. ^7

**8** Cush was the father of Nimrod, who began to be a mighty one on the earth. ^8

**9** He was a mighty hunter before the LORD; so it is said, “Like Nimrod, a mighty hunter before the LORD.” ^9

**10** His kingdom began in Babylon, Erech, Accad, and Calneh, in the land of Shinar. ^10

**11** From that land he went forth into Assyria, where he built Nineveh, Rehoboth-Ir, Calah, ^11

**12** and Resen, which is between Nineveh and the great city of Calah. ^12

**13** Mizraim was the father of the Ludites, the Anamites, the Lehabites, the Naphtuhites, ^13

**14** the Pathrusites, the Casluhites (from whom the Philistines came), and the Caphtorites. ^14

**15** And Canaan was the father of Sidon his firstborn, and of the Hittites, ^15

**16** the Jebusites, the Amorites, the Girgashites, ^16

**17** the Hivites, the Arkites, the Sinites, ^17

**18** the Arvadites, the Zemarites, and the Hamathites. Later the Canaanite clans were scattered, ^18

**19** and the borders of Canaan extended from Sidon toward Gerar as far as Gaza, and then toward Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, and Zeboiim, as far as Lasha. ^19

**20** These are the sons of Ham according to their clans, languages, lands, and nations. ^20

**21** And sons were also born to Shem, the older brother of Japheth; Shem was the forefather of all the sons of Eber. ^21

**22** The sons of Shem: Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram. ^22

**23** The sons of Aram: Uz, Hul, Gether, and Mash. ^23

**24** Arphaxad was the father of Shelah, and Shelah was the father of Eber. ^24

**25** Two sons were born to Eber: One was named Peleg, because in his days the earth was divided, and his brother was named Joktan. ^25

**26** And Joktan was the father of Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah, ^26

**27** Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah, ^27

**28** Obal, Abimael, Sheba, ^28

**29** Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. All these were sons of Joktan. ^29

**30** Their territory extended from Mesha to Sephar, in the eastern hill country. ^30

**31** These are the sons of Shem, according to their clans, languages, lands, and nations. ^31

**32** All these are the clans of Noah’s sons, according to their generations and nations. From these the nations of the earth spread out after the flood. ^32
