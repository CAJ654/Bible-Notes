# Genesis 5 (BSB)

← [[Genesis 4 - BSB]] | [[Genesis 6 - BSB]] →

**1** This is the book of the generations of Adam. In the day that God created man, He made him in His own likeness. ^1

**2** Male and female He created them, and He blessed them. And in the day they were created, He called them “man.” ^2

**3** When Adam was 130 years old, he had a son in his own likeness, after his own image; and he named him Seth. ^3

**4** And after he had become the father of Seth, Adam lived 800 years and had other sons and daughters. ^4

**5** So Adam lived a total of 930 years, and then he died. ^5

**6** When Seth was 105 years old, he became the father of Enosh. ^6

**7** And after he had become the father of Enosh, Seth lived 807 years and had other sons and daughters. ^7

**8** So Seth lived a total of 912 years, and then he died. ^8

**9** When Enosh was 90 years old, he became the father of Kenan. ^9

**10** And after he had become the father of Kenan, Enosh lived 815 years and had other sons and daughters. ^10

**11** So Enosh lived a total of 905 years, and then he died. ^11

**12** When Kenan was 70 years old, he became the father of Mahalalel. ^12

**13** And after he had become the father of Mahalalel, Kenan lived 840 years and had other sons and daughters. ^13

**14** So Kenan lived a total of 910 years, and then he died. ^14

**15** When Mahalalel was 65 years old, he became the father of Jared. ^15

**16** And after he had become the father of Jared, Mahalalel lived 830 years and had other sons and daughters. ^16

**17** So Mahalalel lived a total of 895 years, and then he died. ^17

**18** When Jared was 162 years old, he became the father of Enoch. ^18

**19** And after he had become the father of Enoch, Jared lived 800 years and had other sons and daughters. ^19

**20** So Jared lived a total of 962 years, and then he died. ^20

**21** When Enoch was 65 years old, he became the father of Methuselah. ^21

**22** And after he had become the father of Methuselah, Enoch walked with God 300 years and had other sons and daughters. ^22

**23** So Enoch lived a total of 365 years. ^23

**24** Enoch walked with God, and then he was no more, because God had taken him away. ^24

**25** When Methuselah was 187 years old, he became the father of Lamech. ^25

**26** And after he had become the father of Lamech, Methuselah lived 782 years and had other sons and daughters. ^26

**27** So Methuselah lived a total of 969 years, and then he died. ^27

**28** When Lamech was 182 years old, he had a son. ^28

**29** And he named him Noah, saying, “May this one comfort us in the labor and toil of our hands caused by the ground that the LORD has cursed.” ^29

**30** And after he had become the father of Noah, Lamech lived 595 years and had other sons and daughters. ^30

**31** So Lamech lived a total of 777 years, and then he died. ^31

**32** After Noah was 500 years old, he became the father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth. ^32
