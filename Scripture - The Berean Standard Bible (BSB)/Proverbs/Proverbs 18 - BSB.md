# Proverbs 18 (BSB)

← [[Proverbs 17 - BSB]] | [[Proverbs 19 - BSB]] →

**1** He who isolates himself pursues selfish desires; he rebels against all sound judgment. ^1

**2** A fool does not delight in understanding, but only in airing his opinions. ^2

**3** With a wicked man comes contempt as well, and shame is accompanied by disgrace. ^3

**4** The words of a man’s mouth are deep waters; the fountain of wisdom is a bubbling brook. ^4

**5** Showing partiality to the wicked is not good, nor is depriving the innocent of justice. ^5

**6** A fool’s lips bring him strife, and his mouth invites a beating. ^6

**7** A fool’s mouth is his ruin, and his lips are a snare to his soul. ^7

**8** The words of a gossip are like choice morsels that go down into the inmost being. ^8

**9** Whoever is slothful in his work is brother to him who destroys. ^9

**10** The name of the LORD is a strong tower; the righteous run to it and are safe. ^10

**11** A rich man’s wealth is his fortified city; it is like a high wall in his imagination. ^11

**12** Before his downfall a man’s heart is proud, but humility comes before honor. ^12

**13** He who answers a matter before he hears it—this is folly and disgrace to him. ^13

**14** The spirit of a man can endure his sickness, but who can survive a broken spirit? ^14

**15** The heart of the discerning acquires knowledge, and the ear of the wise seeks it out. ^15

**16** A man’s gift opens doors for him, and brings him before great men. ^16

**17** The first to state his case seems right until another comes and cross-examines him. ^17

**18** Casting the lot ends quarrels and separates strong opponents. ^18

**19** An offended brother is harder to win than a fortified city, and disputes are like the bars of a castle. ^19

**20** From the fruit of his mouth a man’s belly is filled; with the harvest from his lips he is satisfied. ^20

**21** Life and death are in the power of the tongue, and those who love it will eat its fruit. ^21

**22** He who finds a wife finds a good thing and obtains favor from the LORD. ^22

**23** The poor man pleads for mercy, but the rich man answers harshly. ^23

**24** A man of many companions may come to ruin, but there is a friend who stays closer than a brother. ^24
