# Proverbs 20 (BSB)

← [[Proverbs 19 - BSB]] | [[Proverbs 21 - BSB]] →

**1** Wine is a mocker, strong drink is a brawler, and whoever is led astray by them is not wise. ^1

**2** The terror of a king is like the roar of a lion; whoever provokes him forfeits his own life. ^2

**3** It is honorable for a man to resolve a dispute, but any fool will quarrel. ^3

**4** The slacker does not plow in season; at harvest time he looks, but nothing is there. ^4

**5** The intentions of a man’s heart are deep waters, but a man of understanding draws them out. ^5

**6** Many a man proclaims his loving devotion, but who can find a trustworthy man? ^6

**7** The righteous man walks with integrity; blessed are his children after him. ^7

**8** A king who sits on a throne to judge sifts out all evil with his eyes. ^8

**9** Who can say, “I have kept my heart pure; I am cleansed from my sin”? ^9

**10** Differing weights and unequal measures—both are detestable to the LORD. ^10

**11** Even a young man is known by his actions—whether his conduct is pure and upright. ^11

**12** Ears that hear and eyes that see—the LORD has made them both. ^12

**13** Do not love sleep, or you will grow poor; open your eyes, and you will have plenty of food. ^13

**14** “Worthless, worthless!” says the buyer, but on the way out, he gloats. ^14

**15** There is an abundance of gold and rubies, but lips of knowledge are a rare treasure. ^15

**16** Take the garment of the one who posts security for a stranger; get collateral if it is for a foreigner. ^16

**17** Food gained by fraud is sweet to a man, but later his mouth is full of gravel. ^17

**18** Set plans by consultation, and wage war under sound guidance. ^18

**19** He who reveals secrets is a constant gossip; avoid the one who babbles with his lips. ^19

**20** Whoever curses his father or mother, his lamp will be extinguished in deepest darkness. ^20

**21** An inheritance gained quickly will not be blessed in the end. ^21

**22** Do not say, “I will avenge this evil!” Wait on the LORD, and He will save you. ^22

**23** Unequal weights are detestable to the LORD, and dishonest scales are no good. ^23

**24** A man’s steps are from the LORD, so how can anyone understand his own way? ^24

**25** It is a trap for a man to dedicate something rashly, only later to reconsider his vows. ^25

**26** A wise king separates out the wicked and drives the threshing wheel over them. ^26

**27** The spirit of a man is the lamp of the LORD, searching out his inmost being. ^27

**28** Loving devotion and faithfulness preserve a king; by these he maintains his throne. ^28

**29** The glory of young men is their strength, and gray hair is the splendor of the old. ^29

**30** Lashes and wounds scour evil, and beatings cleanse the inmost parts. ^30
