# Proverbs 14 (BSB)

← [[Proverbs 13 - BSB]] | [[Proverbs 15 - BSB]] →

**1** Every wise woman builds her house, but a foolish one tears it down with her own hands. ^1

**2** He who walks in uprightness fears the LORD, but the one who is devious in his ways despises Him. ^2

**3** The proud speech of a fool brings a rod to his back, but the lips of the wise protect them. ^3

**4** Where there are no oxen, the manger is empty, but an abundant harvest comes through the strength of the ox. ^4

**5** An honest witness does not deceive, but a dishonest witness pours forth lies. ^5

**6** A mocker seeks wisdom and finds none, but knowledge comes easily to the discerning. ^6

**7** Stay away from a foolish man; you will gain no knowledge from his speech. ^7

**8** The wisdom of the prudent is to discern his way, but the folly of fools deceives them. ^8

**9** Fools mock the making of amends, but goodwill is found among the upright. ^9

**10** The heart knows its own bitterness, and no stranger shares in its joy. ^10

**11** The house of the wicked will be destroyed, but the tent of the upright will flourish. ^11

**12** There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is the way of death. ^12

**13** Even in laughter the heart may ache, and joy may end in sorrow. ^13

**14** The backslider in heart receives the fill of his own ways, but a good man is rewarded for his ways. ^14

**15** The simple man believes every word, but the prudent man watches his steps. ^15

**16** A wise man fears and turns from evil, but a fool is careless and reckless. ^16

**17** A quick-tempered man acts foolishly, and a devious man is hated. ^17

**18** The simple inherit folly, but the prudent are crowned with knowledge. ^18

**19** The evil bow before the good, and the wicked at the gates of the righteous. ^19

**20** The poor man is hated even by his neighbor, but many are those who love the rich. ^20

**21** He who despises his neighbor sins, but blessed is he who shows kindness to the poor. ^21

**22** Do not those who contrive evil go astray? But those who plan goodness find loving devotion and faithfulness. ^22

**23** There is profit in all labor, but mere talk leads only to poverty. ^23

**24** The crown of the wise is their wealth, but the effort of fools is folly. ^24

**25** A truthful witness saves lives, but one who utters lies is deceitful. ^25

**26** He who fears the LORD is secure in confidence, and his children shall have a place of refuge. ^26

**27** The fear of the LORD is a fountain of life, turning a man from the snares of death. ^27

**28** A large population is a king’s splendor, but a lack of subjects is a prince’s ruin. ^28

**29** A patient man has great understanding, but a quick-tempered man promotes folly. ^29

**30** A tranquil heart is life to the body, but envy rots the bones. ^30

**31** Whoever oppresses the poor taunts their Maker, but whoever is kind to the needy honors Him. ^31

**32** The wicked man is thrown down by his own sin, but the righteous man has a refuge even in death. ^32

**33** Wisdom rests in the heart of the discerning; even among fools she is known. ^33

**34** Righteousness exalts a nation, but sin is a disgrace to any people. ^34

**35** A king delights in a wise servant, but his anger falls on the shameful. ^35
