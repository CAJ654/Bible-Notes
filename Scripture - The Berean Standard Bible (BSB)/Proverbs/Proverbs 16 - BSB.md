# Proverbs 16 (BSB)

← [[Proverbs 15 - BSB]] | [[Proverbs 17 - BSB]] →

**1** The plans of the heart belong to man, but the reply of the tongue is from the LORD. ^1

**2** All a man’s ways are pure in his own eyes, but his motives are weighed out by the LORD. ^2

**3** Commit your works to the LORD and your plans will be achieved. ^3

**4** The LORD has made everything for His purpose—even the wicked for the day of disaster. ^4

**5** Everyone who is proud in heart is detestable to the LORD; be assured that he will not go unpunished. ^5

**6** By loving devotion and faithfulness iniquity is atoned for, and by the fear of the LORD one turns aside from evil. ^6

**7** When a man’s ways please the LORD, He makes even the man’s enemies live at peace with him. ^7

**8** Better a little with righteousness than great gain with injustice. ^8

**9** A man’s heart plans his course, but the LORD determines his steps. ^9

**10** A divine verdict is on the lips of a king; his mouth must not betray justice. ^10

**11** Honest scales and balances are from the LORD; all the weights in the bag are His concern. ^11

**12** Wicked behavior is detestable for kings, for a throne is established through righteousness. ^12

**13** Righteous lips are a king’s delight, and he who speaks honestly is beloved. ^13

**14** The wrath of a king is a messenger of death, but a wise man will pacify it. ^14

**15** When a king’s face brightens, there is life; his favor is like a rain cloud in spring. ^15

**16** How much better to acquire wisdom than gold! To gain understanding is more desirable than silver. ^16

**17** The highway of the upright leads away from evil; he who guards his way protects his life. ^17

**18** Pride goes before destruction, and a haughty spirit before a fall. ^18

**19** It is better to be lowly in spirit among the humble than to divide the spoil with the proud. ^19

**20** Whoever heeds instruction will find success, and blessed is he who trusts in the LORD. ^20

**21** The wise in heart are called discerning, and pleasant speech promotes instruction. ^21

**22** Understanding is a fountain of life to its possessor, but the discipline of fools is folly. ^22

**23** The heart of the wise man instructs his mouth and adds persuasiveness to his lips. ^23

**24** Pleasant words are a honeycomb, sweet to the soul and healing to the bones. ^24

**25** There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is the way of death. ^25

**26** A worker’s appetite works for him because his hunger drives him onward. ^26

**27** A worthless man digs up evil, and his speech is like a scorching fire. ^27

**28** A perverse man spreads dissension, and a gossip divides close friends. ^28

**29** A violent man entices his neighbor and leads him down a path that is not good. ^29

**30** He who winks his eye devises perversity; he who purses his lips is bent on evil. ^30

**31** Gray hair is a crown of glory; it is attained along the path of righteousness. ^31

**32** He who is slow to anger is better than a warrior, and he who controls his temper is greater than one who captures a city. ^32

**33** The lot is cast into the lap, but its every decision is from the LORD. ^33
