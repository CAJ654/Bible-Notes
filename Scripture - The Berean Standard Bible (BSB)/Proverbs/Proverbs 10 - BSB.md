# Proverbs 10 (BSB)

← [[Proverbs 9 - BSB]] | [[Proverbs 11 - BSB]] →

**1** The proverbs of Solomon: A wise son brings joy to his father, but a foolish son grief to his mother. ^1

**2** Ill-gotten treasures profit nothing, but righteousness brings deliverance from death. ^2

**3** The LORD does not let the righteous go hungry, but He denies the craving of the wicked. ^3

**4** Idle hands make one poor, but diligent hands bring wealth. ^4

**5** He who gathers in summer is a wise son, but he who sleeps during harvest is a disgraceful son. ^5

**6** Blessings are on the head of the righteous, but the mouth of the wicked conceals violence. ^6

**7** The memory of the righteous is a blessing, but the name of the wicked will rot. ^7

**8** A wise heart will receive commandments, but foolish lips will come to ruin. ^8

**9** He who walks in integrity walks securely, but he who perverts his ways will be found out. ^9

**10** He who winks the eye causes grief, and foolish lips will come to ruin. ^10

**11** The mouth of the righteous is a fountain of life, but the mouth of the wicked conceals violence. ^11

**12** Hatred stirs up dissension, but love covers all transgressions. ^12

**13** Wisdom is found on the lips of the discerning, but a rod is for the back of him who lacks judgment. ^13

**14** The wise store up knowledge, but the mouth of the fool invites destruction. ^14

**15** The wealth of the rich man is his fortified city, but poverty is the ruin of the poor. ^15

**16** The labor of the righteous leads to life, but the gain of the wicked brings punishment. ^16

**17** Whoever heeds instruction is on the path to life, but he who ignores reproof goes astray. ^17

**18** The one who conceals hatred has lying lips, and whoever spreads slander is a fool. ^18

**19** When words are many, sin is unavoidable, but he who restrains his lips is wise. ^19

**20** The tongue of the righteous is choice silver, but the heart of the wicked has little worth. ^20

**21** The lips of the righteous feed many, but fools die for lack of judgment. ^21

**22** The blessing of the LORD enriches, and He adds no sorrow to it. ^22

**23** The fool delights in shameful conduct, but a man of understanding has wisdom. ^23

**24** What the wicked man dreads will overtake him, but the desire of the righteous will be granted. ^24

**25** When the whirlwind passes, the wicked are no more, but the righteous are secure forever. ^25

**26** Like vinegar to the teeth and smoke to the eyes, so is the slacker to those who send him. ^26

**27** The fear of the LORD prolongs life, but the years of the wicked will be cut short. ^27

**28** The hope of the righteous is joy, but the expectations of the wicked will perish. ^28

**29** The way of the LORD is a refuge to the upright, but destruction awaits those who do evil. ^29

**30** The righteous will never be shaken, but the wicked will not inhabit the land. ^30

**31** The mouth of the righteous brings forth wisdom, but a perverse tongue will be cut out. ^31

**32** The lips of the righteous know what is fitting, but the mouth of the wicked is perverse. ^32
