# Proverbs 9 (BSB)

← [[Proverbs 8 - BSB]] | [[Proverbs 10 - BSB]] →

**1** Wisdom has built her house; she has carved out her seven pillars. ^1

**2** She has prepared her meat and mixed her wine; she has also set her table. ^2

**3** She has sent out her maidservants; she calls out from the heights of the city. ^3

**4** “Whoever is simple, let him turn in here!” she says to him who lacks judgment. ^4

**5** “Come, eat my bread and drink the wine I have mixed. ^5

**6** Leave your folly behind, and you will live; walk in the way of understanding.” ^6

**7** He who corrects a mocker brings shame on himself; he who rebukes a wicked man taints himself. ^7

**8** Do not rebuke a mocker, or he will hate you; rebuke a wise man, and he will love you. ^8

**9** Instruct a wise man, and he will be wiser still; teach a righteous man, and he will increase his learning. ^9

**10** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom, and knowledge of the Holy One is understanding. ^10

**11** For through wisdom your days will be multiplied, and years will be added to your life. ^11

**12** If you are wise, you are wise to your own advantage; but if you scoff, you alone will bear the consequences. ^12

**13** The woman named Folly is loud; she is naive and knows nothing. ^13

**14** She sits at the door of her house, on a seat in the heights of the city, ^14

**15** calling out to those who pass by, who make their paths straight. ^15

**16** “Whoever is simple, let him turn in here!” she says to him who lacks judgment. ^16

**17** “Stolen water is sweet, and bread eaten in secret is tasty!” ^17

**18** But they do not know that the dead are there, that her guests are in the depths of Sheol. ^18
