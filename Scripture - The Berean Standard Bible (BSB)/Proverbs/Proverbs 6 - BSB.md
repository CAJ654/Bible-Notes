# Proverbs 6 (BSB)

← [[Proverbs 5 - BSB]] | [[Proverbs 7 - BSB]] →

**1** My son, if you have put up security for your neighbor, if you have struck hands in pledge with a stranger, ^1

**2** if you have been trapped by the words of your lips, ensnared by the words of your mouth, ^2

**3** then do this, my son, to free yourself, for you have fallen into your neighbor’s hands: Go, humble yourself, and press your plea with your neighbor. ^3

**4** Allow no sleep to your eyes or slumber to your eyelids. ^4

**5** Free yourself, like a gazelle from the hand of the hunter, like a bird from the snare of the fowler. ^5

**6** Walk in the manner of the ant, O slacker; observe its ways and become wise. ^6

**7** Without a commander, without an overseer or ruler, ^7

**8** it prepares its provisions in summer; it gathers its food at harvest. ^8

**9** How long will you lie there, O slacker? When will you get up from your sleep? ^9

**10** A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest, ^10

**11** and poverty will come upon you like a robber, and need like a bandit. ^11

**12** A worthless person, a wicked man, walks with a perverse mouth, ^12

**13** winking his eyes, speaking with his feet, and pointing with his fingers. ^13

**14** With deceit in his heart he devises evil; he continually sows discord. ^14

**15** Therefore calamity will come upon him suddenly; in an instant he will be shattered beyond recovery. ^15

**16** There are six things that the LORD hates, seven that are detestable to Him: ^16

**17** haughty eyes, a lying tongue, hands that shed innocent blood, ^17

**18** a heart that devises wicked schemes, feet that run swiftly to evil, ^18

**19** a false witness who gives false testimony, and one who stirs up discord among brothers. ^19

**20** My son, keep your father’s commandment, and do not forsake your mother’s teaching. ^20

**21** Bind them always upon your heart; tie them around your neck. ^21

**22** When you walk, they will guide you; when you lie down, they will watch over you; when you awake, they will speak to you. ^22

**23** For this commandment is a lamp, this teaching is a light, and the reproofs of discipline are the way to life, ^23

**24** to keep you from the evil woman, from the smooth tongue of the adulteress. ^24

**25** Do not lust in your heart for her beauty or let her captivate you with her eyes. ^25

**26** For the levy of the prostitute is poverty, and the adulteress preys upon your very life. ^26

**27** Can a man embrace fire and his clothes not be burned? ^27

**28** Can a man walk on hot coals without scorching his feet? ^28

**29** So is he who sleeps with another man’s wife; no one who touches her will go unpunished. ^29

**30** Men do not despise the thief if he steals to satisfy his hunger. ^30

**31** Yet if caught, he must pay sevenfold; he must give up all the wealth of his house. ^31

**32** He who commits adultery lacks judgment; whoever does so destroys himself. ^32

**33** Wounds and dishonor will befall him, and his reproach will never be wiped away. ^33

**34** For jealousy enrages a husband, and he will show no mercy in the day of vengeance. ^34

**35** He will not be appeased by any ransom, or persuaded by lavish gifts. ^35
