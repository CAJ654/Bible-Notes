# Proverbs 7 (BSB)

← [[Proverbs 6 - BSB]] | [[Proverbs 8 - BSB]] →

**1** My son, keep my words and treasure my commandments within you. ^1

**2** Keep my commandments and live; guard my teachings as the apple of your eye. ^2

**3** Tie them to your fingers; write them on the tablet of your heart. ^3

**4** Say to wisdom, “You are my sister,” and call understanding your kinsman, ^4

**5** that they may keep you from the adulteress, from the stranger with seductive words. ^5

**6** For at the window of my house I looked through the lattice. ^6

**7** I saw among the simple, I noticed among the youths, a young man lacking judgment, ^7

**8** crossing the street near her corner, strolling down the road to her house, ^8

**9** at twilight, as the day was fading into the dark of the night. ^9

**10** Then a woman came out to meet him, with the attire of a harlot and cunning of heart. ^10

**11** She is loud and defiant; her feet do not remain at home. ^11

**12** Now in the street, now in the squares, she lurks at every corner. ^12

**13** She seizes him and kisses him; she brazenly says to him: ^13

**14** “I have made my peace offerings; today I have paid my vows. ^14

**15** So I came out to meet you; I sought you, and I have found you. ^15

**16** I have decked my bed with coverings, with colored linen from Egypt. ^16

**17** I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, with aloes, and with cinnamon. ^17

**18** Come, let us take our fill of love till morning. Let us delight in loving caresses! ^18

**19** For my husband is not at home; he has gone on a long journey. ^19

**20** He took with him a bag of money and will not return till the moon is full.” ^20

**21** With her great persuasion she entices him; with her flattering lips she lures him. ^21

**22** He follows her on impulse, like an ox going to the slaughter, like a deer bounding into a trap, ^22

**23** until an arrow pierces his liver, like a bird darting into a snare—not knowing it will cost him his life. ^23

**24** Now, my sons, listen to me, and attend to the words of my mouth. ^24

**25** Do not let your heart turn aside to her ways; do not stray into her paths. ^25

**26** For she has brought many down to death; her slain are many in number. ^26

**27** Her house is the road to Sheol, descending to the chambers of death. ^27
