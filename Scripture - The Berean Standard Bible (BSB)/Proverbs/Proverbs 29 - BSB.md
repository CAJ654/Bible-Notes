# Proverbs 29 (BSB)

← [[Proverbs 28 - BSB]] | [[Proverbs 30 - BSB]] →

**1** A man who remains stiff-necked after much reproof will suddenly be shattered beyond recovery. ^1

**2** When the righteous flourish, the people rejoice, but when the wicked rule, the people groan. ^2

**3** A man who loves wisdom brings joy to his father, but a companion of prostitutes squanders his wealth. ^3

**4** By justice a king brings stability to the land, but a man who exacts tribute demolishes it. ^4

**5** A man who flatters his neighbor spreads a net for his feet. ^5

**6** An evil man is caught by his own sin, but a righteous one sings and rejoices. ^6

**7** The righteous consider the cause of the poor, but the wicked have no regard for such concerns. ^7

**8** Mockers inflame a city, but the wise turn away anger. ^8

**9** If a wise man goes to court with a fool, there will be raving and laughing with no resolution. ^9

**10** Men of bloodshed hate a blameless man, but the upright care for his life. ^10

**11** A fool vents all his anger, but a wise man holds it back. ^11

**12** If a ruler listens to lies, all his officials will be wicked. ^12

**13** The poor man and the oppressor have this in common: The LORD gives light to the eyes of both. ^13

**14** A king who judges the poor with fairness—his throne will be established forever. ^14

**15** A rod of correction imparts wisdom, but a child left to himself disgraces his mother. ^15

**16** When the wicked thrive, rebellion increases; but the righteous will see their downfall. ^16

**17** Discipline your son, and he will give you rest; he will bring delight to your soul. ^17

**18** Where there is no vision, the people cast off restraint; but blessed is he who keeps the Law. ^18

**19** A servant cannot be corrected by words alone; though he understands, he will not respond. ^19

**20** Do you see a man who speaks in haste? There is more hope for a fool than for him. ^20

**21** A servant pampered from his youth will bring grief in the end. ^21

**22** An angry man stirs up dissension, and a hot-tempered man abounds in transgression. ^22

**23** A man’s pride will bring him low, but a humble spirit will obtain honor. ^23

**24** A partner to a thief hates his own soul; he receives the oath but does not testify. ^24

**25** The fear of man is a snare, but whoever trusts in the LORD is set securely on high. ^25

**26** Many seek the ruler’s favor, but a man receives justice from the LORD. ^26

**27** An unjust man is detestable to the righteous, and one whose way is upright is detestable to the wicked. ^27
