# Proverbs 1 (BSB)

[[Proverbs 2 - BSB]] →

**1** These are the proverbs of Solomon son of David, king of Israel, ^1

**2** for gaining wisdom and discipline, for comprehending words of insight, ^2

**3** and for receiving instruction in wise living and in righteousness, justice, and equity. ^3

**4** To impart prudence to the simple and knowledge and discretion to the young, ^4

**5** let the wise listen and gain instruction, and the discerning acquire wise counsel ^5

**6** by understanding the proverbs and parables, the sayings and riddles of the wise. ^6

**7** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge, but fools despise wisdom and discipline. ^7

**8** Listen, my son, to your father’s instruction, and do not forsake the teaching of your mother. ^8

**9** For they are a garland of grace on your head and a pendant around your neck. ^9

**10** My son, if sinners entice you, do not yield to them. ^10

**11** If they say, “Come along, let us lie in wait for blood, let us ambush the innocent without cause, ^11

**12** let us swallow them alive like Sheol, and whole like those descending into the Pit. ^12

**13** We will find all manner of precious goods; we will fill our houses with plunder. ^13

**14** Throw in your lot with us; let us all share one purse”— ^14

**15** my son, do not walk the road with them or set foot upon their path. ^15

**16** For their feet run to evil, and they are swift to shed blood. ^16

**17** How futile it is to spread the net where any bird can see it! ^17

**18** But they lie in wait for their own blood; they ambush their own lives. ^18

**19** Such is the fate of all who are greedy, whose unjust gain takes the lives of its possessors. ^19

**20** Wisdom calls out in the street, she lifts her voice in the square; ^20

**21** in the main concourse she cries aloud, at the city gates she makes her speech: ^21

**22** “How long, O simple ones, will you love your simple ways? How long will scoffers delight in their scorn and fools hate knowledge? ^22

**23** If you had repented at my rebuke, then surely I would have poured out my spirit on you; I would have made my words known to you. ^23

**24** Because you refused my call, and no one took my outstretched hand, ^24

**25** because you neglected all my counsel, and wanted none of my correction, ^25

**26** in turn I will mock your calamity; I will sneer when terror strikes you, ^26

**27** when your dread comes like a storm, and your destruction like a whirlwind, when distress and anguish overwhelm you. ^27

**28** Then they will call on me, but I will not answer; they will earnestly seek me, but will not find me. ^28

**29** For they hated knowledge and chose not to fear the LORD. ^29

**30** They accepted none of my counsel; they despised all my reproof. ^30

**31** So they will eat the fruit of their own way, and be filled with their own devices. ^31

**32** For the waywardness of the simple will slay them, and the complacency of fools will destroy them. ^32

**33** But whoever listens to me will dwell in safety, secure from the fear of evil.” ^33
