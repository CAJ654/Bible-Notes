# Proverbs 31 (BSB)

← [[Proverbs 30 - BSB]]

**1** These are the words of King Lemuel—the burden that his mother taught him: ^1

**2** What shall I say, O my son? What, O son of my womb? What, O son of my vows? ^2

**3** Do not spend your strength on women or your vigor on those who ruin kings. ^3

**4** It is not for kings, O Lemuel, it is not for kings to drink wine, or for rulers to crave strong drink, ^4

**5** lest they drink and forget what is decreed, depriving all the oppressed of justice. ^5

**6** Give strong drink to one who is perishing, and wine to the bitter in soul. ^6

**7** Let him drink and forget his poverty, and remember his misery no more. ^7

**8** Open your mouth for those with no voice, for the cause of all the dispossessed. ^8

**9** Open your mouth, judge righteously, and defend the cause of the poor and needy. ^9

**10** A wife of noble character, who can find? She is far more precious than rubies. ^10

**11** The heart of her husband trusts in her, and he lacks nothing of value. ^11

**12** She brings him good and not harm all the days of her life. ^12

**13** She selects wool and flax and works with eager hands. ^13

**14** She is like the merchant ships, bringing her food from afar. ^14

**15** She rises while it is still night to provide food for her household and portions for her maidservants. ^15

**16** She appraises a field and buys it; from her earnings she plants a vineyard. ^16

**17** She girds herself with strength and shows that her arms are strong. ^17

**18** She sees that her gain is good, and her lamp is not extinguished at night. ^18

**19** She stretches out her hands to the distaff and grasps the spindle with her fingers. ^19

**20** She opens her arms to the poor and reaches out her hands to the needy. ^20

**21** When it snows, she has no fear for her household, for they are all clothed in scarlet. ^21

**22** She makes coverings for her bed; her clothing is fine linen and purple. ^22

**23** Her husband is known at the city gates, where he sits among the elders of the land. ^23

**24** She makes linen garments and sells them; she delivers sashes to the merchants. ^24

**25** Strength and honor are her clothing, and she can laugh at the days to come. ^25

**26** She opens her mouth with wisdom, and faithful instruction is on her tongue. ^26

**27** She watches over the affairs of her household and does not eat the bread of idleness. ^27

**28** Her children rise up and call her blessed; her husband praises her as well: ^28

**29** “Many daughters have done noble things, but you surpass them all!” ^29

**30** Charm is deceptive and beauty is fleeting, but a woman who fears the LORD is to be praised. ^30

**31** Give her the fruit of her hands, and let her works praise her at the gates. ^31
