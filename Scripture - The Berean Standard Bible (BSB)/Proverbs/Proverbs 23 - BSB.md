# Proverbs 23 (BSB)

← [[Proverbs 22 - BSB]] | [[Proverbs 24 - BSB]] →

**1** When you sit down to dine with a ruler, consider carefully what is set before you, ^1

**2** and put a knife to your throat if you possess a great appetite. ^2

**3** Do not crave his delicacies, for that food is deceptive. ^3

**4** Do not wear yourself out to get rich; be wise enough to restrain yourself. ^4

**5** When you glance at wealth, it disappears, for it makes wings for itself and flies like an eagle to the sky. ^5

**6** Do not eat the bread of a stingy man, and do not crave his delicacies; ^6

**7** for he is keeping track, inwardly counting the cost. “Eat and drink,” he says to you, but his heart is not with you. ^7

**8** You will vomit up what little you have eaten and waste your pleasant words. ^8

**9** Do not speak to a fool, for he will despise the wisdom of your words. ^9

**10** Do not move an ancient boundary stone or encroach on the fields of the fatherless, ^10

**11** for their Redeemer is strong; He will take up their case against you. ^11

**12** Apply your heart to instruction and your ears to words of knowledge. ^12

**13** Do not withhold discipline from a child; although you strike him with a rod, he will not die. ^13

**14** Strike him with a rod, and you will deliver his soul from Sheol. ^14

**15** My son, if your heart is wise, my own heart will indeed rejoice. ^15

**16** My inmost being will rejoice when your lips speak what is right. ^16

**17** Do not let your heart envy sinners, but always continue in the fear of the LORD. ^17

**18** For surely there is a future, and your hope will not be cut off. ^18

**19** Listen, my son, and be wise, and guide your heart on the right course. ^19

**20** Do not join those who drink too much wine or gorge themselves on meat. ^20

**21** For the drunkard and the glutton will come to poverty, and drowsiness will clothe them in rags. ^21

**22** Listen to your father who gave you life, and do not despise your mother when she is old. ^22

**23** Invest in truth and never sell it—in wisdom and instruction and understanding. ^23

**24** The father of a righteous man will greatly rejoice, and he who fathers a wise son will delight in him. ^24

**25** May your father and mother be glad, and may she who gave you birth rejoice! ^25

**26** My son, give me your heart, and let your eyes delight in my ways. ^26

**27** For a prostitute is a deep pit, and an adulteress is a narrow well. ^27

**28** Like a robber she lies in wait and multiplies the faithless among men. ^28

**29** Who has woe? Who has sorrow? Who has contentions? Who has complaints? Who has needless wounds? Who has bloodshot eyes? ^29

**30** Those who linger over wine, who go to taste mixed drinks. ^30

**31** Do not gaze at wine while it is red, when it sparkles in the cup and goes down smoothly. ^31

**32** In the end it bites like a snake and stings like a viper. ^32

**33** Your eyes will see strange things, and your mind will utter perversities. ^33

**34** You will be like one sleeping on the high seas or lying on the top of a mast: ^34

**35** “They struck me, but I feel no pain! They beat me, but I did not know it! When can I wake up to search for another drink?” ^35
