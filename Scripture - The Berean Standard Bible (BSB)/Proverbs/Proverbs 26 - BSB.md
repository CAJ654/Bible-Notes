# Proverbs 26 (BSB)

← [[Proverbs 25 - BSB]] | [[Proverbs 27 - BSB]] →

**1** Like snow in summer and rain at harvest, honor does not befit a fool. ^1

**2** Like a fluttering sparrow or darting swallow, an undeserved curse does not come to rest. ^2

**3** A whip for the horse, a bridle for the donkey, and a rod for the backs of fools! ^3

**4** Do not answer a fool according to his folly, or you yourself will be like him. ^4

**5** Answer a fool according to his folly, lest he become wise in his own eyes. ^5

**6** Like cutting off one’s own feet or drinking violence is the sending of a message by the hand of a fool. ^6

**7** Like lame legs hanging limp is a proverb in the mouth of a fool. ^7

**8** Like binding a stone into a sling is the giving of honor to a fool. ^8

**9** Like a thorn that goes into the hand of a drunkard is a proverb in the mouth of a fool. ^9

**10** Like an archer who wounds at random is he who hires a fool or passerby. ^10

**11** As a dog returns to its vomit, so a fool repeats his folly. ^11

**12** Do you see a man who is wise in his own eyes? There is more hope for a fool than for him. ^12

**13** The slacker says, “A lion is in the road! A fierce lion roams the public square!” ^13

**14** As a door turns on its hinges, so the slacker turns on his bed. ^14

**15** The slacker buries his hand in the dish; it wearies him to bring it back to his mouth. ^15

**16** The slacker is wiser in his own eyes than seven men who answer discreetly. ^16

**17** Like one who grabs a dog by the ears is a passerby who meddles in a quarrel not his own. ^17

**18** Like a madman shooting firebrands and deadly arrows, ^18

**19** so is the man who deceives his neighbor and says, “I was only joking!” ^19

**20** Without wood, a fire goes out; without gossip, a conflict ceases. ^20

**21** Like charcoal for embers and wood for fire, so is a quarrelsome man for kindling strife. ^21

**22** The words of a gossip are like choice morsels that go down into the inmost being. ^22

**23** Like glaze covering an earthen vessel are burning lips and a wicked heart. ^23

**24** A hateful man disguises himself with his speech, but he lays up deceit in his heart. ^24

**25** When he speaks graciously, do not believe him, for seven abominations fill his heart. ^25

**26** Though his hatred is concealed by deception, his wickedness will be exposed in the assembly. ^26

**27** He who digs a pit will fall into it, and he who rolls a stone will have it roll back on him. ^27

**28** A lying tongue hates those it crushes, and a flattering mouth causes ruin. ^28
