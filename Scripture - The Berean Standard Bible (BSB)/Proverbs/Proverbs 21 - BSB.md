# Proverbs 21 (BSB)

← [[Proverbs 20 - BSB]] | [[Proverbs 22 - BSB]] →

**1** The king’s heart is a waterway in the hand of the LORD; He directs it where He pleases. ^1

**2** All a man’s ways seem right to him, but the LORD weighs the heart. ^2

**3** To do righteousness and justice is more desirable to the LORD than sacrifice. ^3

**4** Haughty eyes and a proud heart—the guides of the wicked—are sin. ^4

**5** The plans of the diligent bring plenty, as surely as haste leads to poverty. ^5

**6** Making a fortune by a lying tongue is a vanishing mist, a deadly pursuit. ^6

**7** The violence of the wicked will sweep them away because they refuse to do what is just. ^7

**8** The way of a guilty man is crooked, but the conduct of the innocent is upright. ^8

**9** Better to live on a corner of the roof than to share a house with a quarrelsome wife. ^9

**10** The soul of the wicked man craves evil; his neighbor finds no favor in his eyes. ^10

**11** When a mocker is punished, the simple gain wisdom; and when a wise man is instructed, he acquires knowledge. ^11

**12** The Righteous One considers the house of the wicked and brings the wicked to ruin. ^12

**13** Whoever shuts his ears to the cry of the poor, he too shall cry out and receive no answer. ^13

**14** A gift in secret soothes anger, and a covert bribe pacifies great wrath. ^14

**15** Justice executed is a joy to the righteous, but a terror to the workers of iniquity. ^15

**16** The man who strays from the path of understanding will rest in the assembly of the dead. ^16

**17** He who loves pleasure will become poor; the one who loves wine and oil will never be rich. ^17

**18** The wicked become a ransom for the righteous, and the faithless for the upright. ^18

**19** Better to live in the desert than with a contentious and ill-tempered wife. ^19

**20** Precious treasures and oil are in the dwelling of the wise, but a foolish man consumes them. ^20

**21** He who pursues righteousness and loving devotion finds life, righteousness, and honor. ^21

**22** A wise man scales the city of the mighty and pulls down the stronghold in which they trust. ^22

**23** He who guards his mouth and tongue keeps his soul from distress. ^23

**24** Mocker is the name of the proud and arrogant man—of him who acts with excessive pride. ^24

**25** The craving of the slacker kills him because his hands refuse to work. ^25

**26** All day long he covets more, but the righteous give without restraint. ^26

**27** The sacrifice of the wicked is detestable—how much more so when brought with ill intent! ^27

**28** A lying witness will perish, but the man who listens to truth will speak forever. ^28

**29** A wicked man hardens his face, but the upright man makes his way sure. ^29

**30** There is no wisdom, no understanding, no counsel that can prevail against the LORD. ^30

**31** A horse is prepared for the day of battle, but victory is of the LORD. ^31
