# Proverbs 15 (BSB)

← [[Proverbs 14 - BSB]] | [[Proverbs 16 - BSB]] →

**1** A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger. ^1

**2** The tongue of the wise commends knowledge, but the mouth of the fool spouts folly. ^2

**3** The eyes of the LORD are in every place, observing the evil and the good. ^3

**4** A soothing tongue is a tree of life, but a perverse tongue crushes the spirit. ^4

**5** A fool rejects his father’s discipline, but whoever heeds correction is prudent. ^5

**6** The house of the righteous has great treasure, but the income of the wicked is trouble. ^6

**7** The lips of the wise spread knowledge, but not so the hearts of fools. ^7

**8** The sacrifice of the wicked is detestable to the LORD, but the prayer of the upright is His delight. ^8

**9** The LORD detests the way of the wicked, but He loves those who pursue righteousness. ^9

**10** Discipline is harsh for him who leaves the path; he who hates correction will die. ^10

**11** Sheol and Abaddon lie open before the LORD—how much more the hearts of men! ^11

**12** A mocker does not love to be reproved, nor will he consult the wise. ^12

**13** A joyful heart makes a cheerful countenance, but sorrow of the heart crushes the spirit. ^13

**14** A discerning heart seeks knowledge, but the mouth of a fool feeds on folly. ^14

**15** All the days of the oppressed are bad, but a cheerful heart has a continual feast. ^15

**16** Better a little with the fear of the LORD than great treasure with turmoil. ^16

**17** Better a dish of vegetables where there is love than a fattened ox with hatred. ^17

**18** A hot-tempered man stirs up strife, but he who is slow to anger calms dispute. ^18

**19** The way of the slacker is like a hedge of thorns, but the path of the upright is a highway. ^19

**20** A wise son brings joy to his father, but a foolish man despises his mother. ^20

**21** Folly is joy to one who lacks judgment, but a man of understanding walks a straight path. ^21

**22** Plans fail for lack of counsel, but with many advisers they succeed. ^22

**23** A man takes joy in a fitting reply—and how good is a timely word! ^23

**24** The path of life leads upward for the wise, that he may avoid going down to Sheol. ^24

**25** The LORD tears down the house of the proud, but He protects the boundaries of the widow. ^25

**26** The LORD detests the thoughts of the wicked, but the words of the pure are pleasant to Him. ^26

**27** He who is greedy for unjust gain brings trouble on his household, but he who hates bribes will live. ^27

**28** The heart of the righteous ponders how to answer, but the mouth of the wicked blurts out evil. ^28

**29** The LORD is far from the wicked, but He hears the prayer of the righteous. ^29

**30** The light of the eyes cheers the heart, and good news nourishes the bones. ^30

**31** He who listens to life-giving reproof will dwell among the wise. ^31

**32** He who ignores discipline despises himself, but whoever heeds correction gains understanding. ^32

**33** The fear of the LORD is the instruction of wisdom, and humility comes before honor. ^33
