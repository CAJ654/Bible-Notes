# Psalm 114 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 113 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 115 - BSB]] →

**1** When Israel departed from Egypt, the house of Jacob from a people of foreign tongue, ^1

**2** Judah became God’s sanctuary, Israel His dominion. ^2

**3** The sea observed and fled; the Jordan turned back; ^3

**4** the mountains skipped like rams, the hills like lambs. ^4

**5** Why was it, O sea, that you fled, O Jordan, that you turned back, ^5

**6** O mountains, that you skipped like rams, O hills, like lambs? ^6

**7** Tremble, O earth, at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the God of Jacob, ^7

**8** who turned the rock into a pool, the flint into a fountain of water! ^8
