# Psalm 104 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 103 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 105 - BSB]] →

**1** Bless the LORD, O my soul! O LORD my God, You are very great; You are clothed with splendor and majesty. ^1

**2** He wraps Himself in light as with a garment; He stretches out the heavens like a tent, ^2

**3** laying the beams of His chambers in the waters above, making the clouds His chariot, walking on the wings of the wind. ^3

**4** He makes the winds His messengers, flames of fire His servants. ^4

**5** He set the earth on its foundations, never to be moved. ^5

**6** You covered it with the deep like a garment; the waters stood above the mountains. ^6

**7** At Your rebuke the waters fled; at the sound of Your thunder they hurried away— ^7

**8** the mountains rose and the valleys sank to the place You assigned for them— ^8

**9** You set a boundary they cannot cross, that they may never again cover the earth. ^9

**10** He sends forth springs in the valleys; they flow between the mountains. ^10

**11** They give drink to every beast of the field; the wild donkeys quench their thirst. ^11

**12** The birds of the air nest beside the springs; they sing among the branches. ^12

**13** He waters the mountains from His chambers; the earth is satisfied by the fruit of His works. ^13

**14** He makes the grass grow for the livestock and provides crops for man to cultivate, bringing forth food from the earth: ^14

**15** wine that gladdens the heart of man, oil that makes his face to shine, and bread that sustains his heart. ^15

**16** The trees of the LORD have their fill, the cedars of Lebanon that He planted, ^16

**17** where the birds build their nests; the stork makes her home in the cypresses. ^17

**18** The high mountains are for the wild goats, the cliffs a refuge for the rock badgers. ^18

**19** He made the moon to mark the seasons; the sun knows when to set. ^19

**20** You bring darkness, and it becomes night, when all the beasts of the forest prowl. ^20

**21** The young lions roar for their prey and seek their food from God. ^21

**22** The sun rises, and they withdraw; they lie down in their dens. ^22

**23** Man goes forth to his work and to his labor until evening. ^23

**24** How many are Your works, O LORD! In wisdom You have made them all; the earth is full of Your creatures. ^24

**25** Here is the sea, vast and wide, teeming with creatures beyond number, living things both great and small. ^25

**26** There the ships pass, and Leviathan, which You formed to frolic there. ^26

**27** All creatures look to You to give them their food in due season. ^27

**28** When You give it to them, they gather it up; when You open Your hand, they are satisfied with good things. ^28

**29** When You hide Your face, they are terrified; when You take away their breath, they die and return to dust. ^29

**30** When You send Your Spirit, they are created, and You renew the face of the earth. ^30

**31** May the glory of the LORD endure forever; may the LORD rejoice in His works. ^31

**32** He looks on the earth, and it trembles; He touches the mountains, and they smolder. ^32

**33** I will sing to the LORD all my life; I will sing praise to my God while I have my being. ^33

**34** May my meditation be pleasing to Him, for I rejoice in the LORD. ^34

**35** May sinners vanish from the earth and the wicked be no more. Bless the LORD, O my soul. Hallelujah! ^35
