# Psalm 144 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 143 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 145 - BSB]] →

**1** Of David. Blessed be the LORD, my Rock, who trains my hands for war, my fingers for battle. ^1

**2** He is my steadfast love and my fortress, my stronghold and my deliverer. He is my shield, in whom I take refuge, who subdues peoples under me. ^2

**3** O LORD, what is man, that You regard him, the son of man that You think of him? ^3

**4** Man is like a breath; his days are like a passing shadow. ^4

**5** Part Your heavens, O LORD, and come down; touch the mountains, that they may smoke. ^5

**6** Flash forth Your lightning and scatter them; shoot Your arrows and rout them. ^6

**7** Reach down from on high; set me free and rescue me from the deep waters, from the grasp of foreigners, ^7

**8** whose mouths speak falsehood, whose right hands are deceitful. ^8

**9** I will sing to You a new song, O God; on a harp of ten strings I will make music to You— ^9

**10** to Him who gives victory to kings, who frees His servant David from the deadly sword. ^10

**11** Set me free and rescue me from the grasp of foreigners, whose mouths speak falsehood, whose right hands are deceitful. ^11

**12** Then our sons will be like plants nurtured in their youth, our daughters like corner pillars carved to adorn a palace. ^12

**13** Our storehouses will be full, supplying all manner of produce; our flocks will bring forth thousands, tens of thousands in our fields. ^13

**14** Our oxen will bear great loads. There will be no breach in the walls, no going into captivity, and no cry of lament in our streets. ^14

**15** Blessed are the people of whom this is so; blessed are the people whose God is the LORD. ^15
