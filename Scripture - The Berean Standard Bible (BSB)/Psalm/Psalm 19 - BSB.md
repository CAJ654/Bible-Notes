# Psalm 19 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 18 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 20 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. The heavens declare the glory of God; the skies proclaim the work of His hands. ^1

**2** Day after day they pour forth speech; night after night they reveal knowledge. ^2

**3** Without speech or language, without a sound to be heard, ^3

**4** their voice has gone out into all the earth, their words to the ends of the world. In the heavens He has pitched a tent for the sun. ^4

**5** Like a bridegroom emerging from his chamber, like a champion rejoicing to run his course, ^5

**6** it rises at one end of the heavens and runs its circuit to the other; nothing is deprived of its warmth. ^6

**7** The Law of the LORD is perfect, reviving the soul; the testimony of the LORD is trustworthy, making wise the simple. ^7

**8** The precepts of the LORD are right, bringing joy to the heart; the commandments of the LORD are radiant, giving light to the eyes. ^8

**9** The fear of the LORD is pure, enduring forever; the judgments of the LORD are true, being altogether righteous. ^9

**10** They are more precious than gold, than much pure gold; they are sweeter than honey, than honey from the comb. ^10

**11** By them indeed Your servant is warned; in keeping them is great reward. ^11

**12** Who can discern his own errors? Cleanse me from my hidden faults. ^12

**13** Keep Your servant also from willful sins; may they not rule over me. Then I will be blameless and cleansed of great transgression. ^13

**14** May the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be pleasing in Your sight, O LORD, my Rock and my Redeemer. ^14
