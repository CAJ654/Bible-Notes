# Psalm 71 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 70 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 72 - BSB]] →

**1** In You, O LORD, I have taken refuge; let me never be put to shame. ^1

**2** In Your justice, rescue and deliver me; incline Your ear and save me. ^2

**3** Be my rock of refuge, where I can always go. Give the command to save me, for You are my rock and my fortress. ^3

**4** Deliver me, O my God, from the hand of the wicked, from the grasp of the unjust and ruthless. ^4

**5** For You are my hope, O Lord GOD, my confidence from my youth. ^5

**6** I have leaned on You since birth; You pulled me from my mother’s womb. My praise is always for You. ^6

**7** I have become a portent to many, but You are my strong refuge. ^7

**8** My mouth is filled with Your praise and with Your splendor all day long. ^8

**9** Do not discard me in my old age; do not forsake me when my strength fails. ^9

**10** For my enemies speak against me, and those who lie in wait for my life conspire, ^10

**11** saying, “God has forsaken him; pursue him and seize him, for there is no one to rescue him.” ^11

**12** Be not far from me, O God. Hurry, O my God, to help me. ^12

**13** May the accusers of my soul be ashamed and consumed; may those who seek my harm be covered with scorn and disgrace. ^13

**14** But I will always hope and will praise You more and more. ^14

**15** My mouth will declare Your righteousness and Your salvation all day long, though I cannot know their full measure. ^15

**16** I will come in the strength of the Lord GOD; I will proclaim Your righteousness—Yours alone. ^16

**17** O God, You have taught me from my youth, and to this day I proclaim Your marvelous deeds. ^17

**18** Even when I am old and gray, do not forsake me, O God, until I proclaim Your power to the next generation, Your might to all who are to come. ^18

**19** Your righteousness reaches to the heavens, O God, You who have done great things. Who, O God, is like You? ^19

**20** Though You have shown me many troubles and misfortunes, You will revive me once again. Even from the depths of the earth You will bring me back up. ^20

**21** You will increase my honor and comfort me once again. ^21

**22** So I will praise You with the harp for Your faithfulness, O my God; I will sing praise to You with the lyre, O Holy One of Israel. ^22

**23** When I sing praise to You my lips will shout for joy, along with my soul, which You have redeemed. ^23

**24** My tongue will indeed proclaim Your righteousness all day long, for those who seek my harm are disgraced and confounded. ^24
