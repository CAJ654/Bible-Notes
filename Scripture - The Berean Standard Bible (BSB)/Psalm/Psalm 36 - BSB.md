# Psalm 36 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 35 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 37 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David, the servant of the LORD. An oracle is in my heart regarding the transgression of the wicked man: There is no fear of God before his eyes. ^1

**2** For his eyes are too full of conceit to detect or hate his own sin. ^2

**3** The words of his mouth are wicked and deceitful; he has ceased to be wise and well-doing. ^3

**4** Even on his bed he plots wickedness; he sets himself on a path that is not good; he fails to reject evil. ^4

**5** Your loving devotion, O LORD, reaches to the heavens, Your faithfulness to the clouds. ^5

**6** Your righteousness is like the highest mountains; Your judgments are like the deepest sea. O LORD, You preserve man and beast. ^6

**7** How precious is Your loving devotion, O God, that the children of men take refuge in the shadow of Your wings! ^7

**8** They feast on the abundance of Your house, and You give them drink from Your river of delights. ^8

**9** For with You is the fountain of life; in Your light we see light. ^9

**10** Extend Your loving devotion to those who know You, and Your righteousness to the upright in heart. ^10

**11** Let not the foot of the proud come against me, nor the hand of the wicked drive me away. ^11

**12** There the evildoers lie fallen, thrown down and unable to rise. ^12
