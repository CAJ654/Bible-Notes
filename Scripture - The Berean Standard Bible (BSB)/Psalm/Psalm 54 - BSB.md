# Psalm 54 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 53 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 55 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. A Maskil of David. When the Ziphites went to Saul and said, “Is David not hiding among us?” Save me, O God, by Your name, and vindicate me by Your might! ^1

**2** Hear my prayer, O God; listen to the words of my mouth. ^2

**3** For strangers rise up against me, and ruthless men seek my life—men with no regard for God. Selah ^3

**4** Surely God is my helper; the Lord is the sustainer of my soul. ^4

**5** He will reward my enemies with evil. In Your faithfulness, destroy them. ^5

**6** Freely I will sacrifice to You; I will praise Your name, O LORD, for it is good. ^6

**7** For He has delivered me from every trouble, and my eyes have looked in triumph on my foes. ^7
