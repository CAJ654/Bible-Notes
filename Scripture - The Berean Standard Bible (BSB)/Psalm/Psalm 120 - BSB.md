# Psalm 120 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 119 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 121 - BSB]] →

**1** A song of ascents. In my distress I cried to the LORD, and He answered me. ^1

**2** Deliver my soul, O LORD, from lying lips and a deceitful tongue. ^2

**3** What will He do to you, and what will be added to you, O deceitful tongue? ^3

**4** Sharp arrows will come from the warrior, with burning coals of the broom tree! ^4

**5** Woe to me that I dwell in Meshech, that I live among the tents of Kedar! ^5

**6** Too long have I dwelt among those who hate peace. ^6

**7** I am in favor of peace; but when I speak, they want war. ^7
