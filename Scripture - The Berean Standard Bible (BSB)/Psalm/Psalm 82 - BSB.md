# Psalm 82 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 81 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 83 - BSB]] →

**1** A Psalm of Asaph. God presides in the divine assembly; He renders judgment among the gods: ^1

**2** “How long will you judge unjustly and show partiality to the wicked? Selah ^2

**3** Defend the cause of the weak and fatherless; uphold the rights of the afflicted and oppressed. ^3

**4** Rescue the weak and needy; save them from the hand of the wicked. ^4

**5** They do not know or understand; they wander in the darkness; all the foundations of the earth are shaken. ^5

**6** I have said, ‘You are gods; you are all sons of the Most High.’ ^6

**7** But like mortals you will die, and like rulers you will fall.” ^7

**8** Arise, O God, judge the earth, for all the nations are Your inheritance. ^8
