# Psalm 28 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 27 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 29 - BSB]] →

**1** Of David. To You, O LORD, I call; be not deaf to me, O my Rock. For if You remain silent, I will be like those descending to the Pit. ^1

**2** Hear my cry for mercy when I call to You for help, when I lift up my hands toward Your holy sanctuary. ^2

**3** Do not drag me away with the wicked, and with the workers of iniquity, who speak peace to their neighbors while malice is in their hearts. ^3

**4** Repay them according to their deeds and for their works of evil. Repay them for what their hands have done; bring back on them what they deserve. ^4

**5** Since they show no regard for the works of the LORD or what His hands have done, He will tear them down and never rebuild them. ^5

**6** Blessed be the LORD, for He has heard my cry for mercy. ^6

**7** The LORD is my strength and my shield; my heart trusts in Him, and I am helped. Therefore my heart rejoices, and I give thanks to Him with my song. ^7

**8** The LORD is the strength of His people, a stronghold of salvation for His anointed. ^8

**9** Save Your people and bless Your inheritance; shepherd them and carry them forever. ^9
