# Psalm 89 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 88 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 90 - BSB]] →

**1** A Maskil of Ethan the Ezrahite. I will sing of the loving devotion of the LORD forever; with my mouth I will proclaim Your faithfulness to all generations. ^1

**2** For I have said, “Loving devotion is built up forever; in the heavens You establish Your faithfulness.” ^2

**3** You said, “I have made a covenant with My chosen one, I have sworn to David My servant: ^3

**4** ‘I will establish your offspring forever and build up your throne for all generations.’” Selah ^4

**5** The heavens praise Your wonders, O LORD—Your faithfulness as well—in the assembly of the holy ones. ^5

**6** For who in the skies can compare with the LORD? Who among the heavenly beings is like the LORD? ^6

**7** In the council of the holy ones, God is greatly feared, and awesome above all who surround Him. ^7

**8** O LORD God of Hosts, who is like You? O mighty LORD, Your faithfulness surrounds You. ^8

**9** You rule the raging sea; when its waves mount up, You still them. ^9

**10** You crushed Rahab like a carcass; You scattered Your enemies with Your mighty arm. ^10

**11** The heavens are Yours, and also the earth. The earth and its fullness You founded. ^11

**12** North and south You created; Tabor and Hermon shout for joy at Your name. ^12

**13** Mighty is Your arm; strong is Your hand. Your right hand is exalted. ^13

**14** Righteousness and justice are the foundation of Your throne; loving devotion and faithfulness go before You. ^14

**15** Blessed are those who know the joyful sound, who walk, O LORD, in the light of Your presence. ^15

**16** They rejoice in Your name all day long, and in Your righteousness they exult. ^16

**17** For You are the glory of their strength, and by Your favor our horn is exalted. ^17

**18** Surely our shield belongs to the LORD, and our king to the Holy One of Israel. ^18

**19** You once spoke in a vision; to Your godly ones You said, “I have bestowed help on a warrior; I have exalted one chosen from the people. ^19

**20** I have found My servant David; with My sacred oil I have anointed him. ^20

**21** My hand will sustain him; surely My arm will strengthen him. ^21

**22** No enemy will exact tribute; no wicked man will oppress him. ^22

**23** I will crush his foes before him and strike down those who hate him. ^23

**24** My faithfulness and loving devotion will be with him, and through My name his horn will be exalted. ^24

**25** I will set his hand over the sea, and his right hand upon the rivers. ^25

**26** He will call to Me, ‘You are my Father, my God, the Rock of my salvation.’ ^26

**27** I will indeed appoint him as My firstborn, the highest of the kings of the earth. ^27

**28** I will forever preserve My loving devotion for him, and My covenant with him will stand fast. ^28

**29** I will establish his line forever, his throne as long as the heavens endure. ^29

**30** If his sons forsake My law and do not walk in My judgments, ^30

**31** if they violate My statutes and fail to keep My commandments, ^31

**32** I will attend to their transgression with the rod, and to their iniquity with stripes. ^32

**33** But I will not withdraw My loving devotion from him, nor ever betray My faithfulness. ^33

**34** I will not violate My covenant or alter the utterance of My lips. ^34

**35** Once and for all I have sworn by My holiness—I will not lie to David— ^35

**36** his offspring shall endure forever, and his throne before Me like the sun, ^36

**37** like the moon, established forever, a faithful witness in the sky.” Selah ^37

**38** Now, however, You have spurned and rejected him; You are enraged by Your anointed one. ^38

**39** You have renounced the covenant with Your servant and sullied his crown in the dust. ^39

**40** You have broken down all his walls; You have reduced his strongholds to rubble. ^40

**41** All who pass by plunder him; he has become a reproach to his neighbors. ^41

**42** You have exalted the right hand of his foes; You have made all his enemies rejoice. ^42

**43** You have bent the edge of his sword and have not sustained him in battle. ^43

**44** You have ended his splendor and cast his throne to the ground. ^44

**45** You have cut short the days of his youth; You have covered him with shame. Selah ^45

**46** How long, O LORD? Will You hide Yourself forever? Will Your wrath keep burning like fire? ^46

**47** Remember the briefness of my lifespan! For what futility You have created all men! ^47

**48** What man can live and never see death? Can he deliver his soul from the power of Sheol? Selah ^48

**49** Where, O Lord, is Your loving devotion of old, which You faithfully swore to David? ^49

**50** Remember, O Lord, the reproach of Your servants, which I bear in my heart from so many people— ^50

**51** how Your enemies have taunted, O LORD, and have mocked every step of Your anointed one! ^51

**52** Blessed be the LORD forever! Amen and amen. ^52
