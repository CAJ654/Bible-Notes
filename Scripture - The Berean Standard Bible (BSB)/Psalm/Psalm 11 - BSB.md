# Psalm 11 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 10 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 12 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. Of David. In the LORD I take refuge. How then can you say to me: “Flee like a bird to your mountain! ^1

**2** For behold, the wicked bend their bows. They set their arrow on the string to shoot from the shadows at the upright in heart. ^2

**3** If the foundations are destroyed, what can the righteous do?” ^3

**4** The LORD is in His holy temple; the LORD is on His heavenly throne. His eyes are watching closely; they examine the sons of men. ^4

**5** The LORD tests the righteous and the wicked; His soul hates the lover of violence. ^5

**6** On the wicked He will rain down fiery coals and sulfur; a scorching wind will be their portion. ^6

**7** For the LORD is righteous; He loves justice. The upright will see His face. ^7
