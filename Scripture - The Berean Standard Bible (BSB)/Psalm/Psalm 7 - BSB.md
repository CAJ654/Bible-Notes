# Psalm 7 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 6 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 8 - BSB]] →

**1** A Shiggaion of David, which he sang to the LORD concerning the words of Cush, a Benjamite. O LORD my God, I take refuge in You; save me and deliver me from all my pursuers, ^1

**2** or they will shred my soul like a lion and tear me to pieces with no one to rescue me. ^2

**3** O LORD my God, if I have done this, if injustice is on my hands, ^3

**4** if I have rewarded my ally with evil, if I have plundered my foe without cause, ^4

**5** then may my enemy pursue me and overtake me; may he trample me to the ground and leave my honor in the dust. Selah ^5

**6** Arise, O LORD, in Your anger; rise up against the fury of my enemies. Awake, my God, and ordain judgment. ^6

**7** Let the assembled peoples gather around You; take Your seat over them on high. ^7

**8** The LORD judges the peoples; vindicate me, O LORD, according to my righteousness and integrity. ^8

**9** Put an end to the evil of the wicked, but establish the righteous, O righteous God who searches hearts and minds. ^9

**10** My shield is with God, who saves the upright in heart. ^10

**11** God is a righteous judge and a God who feels indignation each day. ^11

**12** If one does not repent, God will sharpen His sword; He has bent and strung His bow. ^12

**13** He has prepared His deadly weapons; He ordains His arrows with fire. ^13

**14** Behold, the wicked man travails with evil; he conceives trouble and births falsehood. ^14

**15** He has dug a hole and hollowed it out; he has fallen into a pit of his own making. ^15

**16** His trouble recoils on himself, and his violence falls on his own head. ^16

**17** I will thank the LORD for His righteousness and sing praise to the name of the LORD Most High. ^17
