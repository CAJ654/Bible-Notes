# Psalm 136 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 135 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 137 - BSB]] →

**1** Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good. His loving devotion endures forever. ^1

**2** Give thanks to the God of gods. His loving devotion endures forever. ^2

**3** Give thanks to the Lord of lords. His loving devotion endures forever. ^3

**4** He alone does great wonders. His loving devotion endures forever. ^4

**5** By His insight He made the heavens. His loving devotion endures forever. ^5

**6** He spread out the earth upon the waters. His loving devotion endures forever. ^6

**7** He made the great lights—His loving devotion endures forever. ^7

**8** the sun to rule the day, His loving devotion endures forever. ^8

**9** the moon and stars to govern the night. His loving devotion endures forever. ^9

**10** He struck down the firstborn of Egypt His loving devotion endures forever. ^10

**11** and brought Israel out from among them His loving devotion endures forever. ^11

**12** with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm. His loving devotion endures forever. ^12

**13** He divided the Red Sea in two His loving devotion endures forever. ^13

**14** and led Israel through the midst, His loving devotion endures forever. ^14

**15** but swept Pharaoh and his army into the Red Sea. His loving devotion endures forever. ^15

**16** He led His people through the wilderness. His loving devotion endures forever. ^16

**17** He struck down great kings His loving devotion endures forever. ^17

**18** and slaughtered mighty kings—His loving devotion endures forever. ^18

**19** Sihon king of the Amorites His loving devotion endures forever. ^19

**20** and Og king of Bashan—His loving devotion endures forever. ^20

**21** and He gave their land as an inheritance, His loving devotion endures forever. ^21

**22** a heritage to His servant Israel. His loving devotion endures forever. ^22

**23** He remembered us in our low estate His loving devotion endures forever. ^23

**24** and freed us from our enemies. His loving devotion endures forever. ^24

**25** He gives food to every creature. His loving devotion endures forever. ^25

**26** Give thanks to the God of heaven! His loving devotion endures forever. ^26
