# Psalm 64 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 63 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 65 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. Hear, O God, my voice of complaint; preserve my life from dread of the enemy. ^1

**2** Hide me from the scheming of the wicked, from the mob of workers of iniquity, ^2

**3** who sharpen their tongues like swords and aim their bitter words like arrows, ^3

**4** ambushing the innocent in seclusion, shooting suddenly, without fear. ^4

**5** They hold fast to their evil purpose; they speak of hiding their snares. “Who will see them?” they say. ^5

**6** They devise injustice and say, “We have perfected a secret plan.” For the inner man and the heart are mysterious. ^6

**7** But God will shoot them with arrows; suddenly they will be wounded. ^7

**8** They will be made to stumble, their own tongues turned against them. All who see will shake their heads. ^8

**9** Then all mankind will fear and proclaim the work of God; so they will ponder what He has done. ^9

**10** Let the righteous rejoice in the LORD and take refuge in Him; let all the upright in heart exult. ^10
