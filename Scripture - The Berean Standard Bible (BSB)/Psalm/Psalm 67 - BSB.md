# Psalm 67 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 66 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 68 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. A Psalm. A song. May God be gracious to us and bless us, and cause His face to shine upon us, Selah ^1

**2** that Your ways may be known on earth, Your salvation among all nations. ^2

**3** Let the peoples praise You, O God; let all the peoples praise You. ^3

**4** Let the nations be glad and sing for joy, for You judge the peoples justly and lead the nations of the earth. Selah ^4

**5** Let the peoples praise You, O God; let all the peoples praise You. ^5

**6** The earth has yielded its harvest; God, our God, blesses us. ^6

**7** God blesses us, that all the ends of the earth shall fear Him. ^7
