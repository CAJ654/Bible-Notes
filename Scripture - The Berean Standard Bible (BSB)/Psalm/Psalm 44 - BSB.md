# Psalm 44 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 43 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 45 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. A Maskil of the sons of Korah. We have heard with our ears, O God; our fathers have told us the work You did in their days, in the days of old. ^1

**2** With Your hand You drove out the nations and planted our fathers there; You crushed the peoples and cast them out. ^2

**3** For it was not by their sword that they took the land; their arm did not bring them victory. It was by Your right hand, Your arm, and the light of Your face, because You favored them. ^3

**4** You are my King, O God, who ordains victories for Jacob. ^4

**5** Through You we repel our foes; through Your name we trample our enemies. ^5

**6** For I do not trust in my bow, nor does my sword save me. ^6

**7** For You save us from our enemies; You put those who hate us to shame. ^7

**8** In God we have boasted all day long, and Your name we will praise forever. Selah ^8

**9** But You have rejected and humbled us; You no longer go forth with our armies. ^9

**10** You have made us retreat from the foe, and those who hate us have plundered us. ^10

**11** You have given us up as sheep to be devoured; You have scattered us among the nations. ^11

**12** You sell Your people for nothing; no profit do You gain from their sale. ^12

**13** You have made us a reproach to our neighbors, a mockery and derision to those around us. ^13

**14** You have made us a byword among the nations, a laughingstock among the peoples. ^14

**15** All day long my disgrace is before me, and shame has covered my face, ^15

**16** at the voice of the scorner and reviler, because of the enemy, bent on revenge. ^16

**17** All this has come upon us, though we have not forgotten You or betrayed Your covenant. ^17

**18** Our hearts have not turned back; our steps have not strayed from Your path. ^18

**19** But You have crushed us in the lair of jackals; You have covered us with deepest darkness. ^19

**20** If we had forgotten the name of our God or spread out our hands to a foreign god, ^20

**21** would not God have discovered, since He knows the secrets of the heart? ^21

**22** Yet for Your sake we face death all day long; we are considered as sheep to be slaughtered. ^22

**23** Wake up, O Lord! Why are You sleeping? Arise! Do not reject us forever. ^23

**24** Why do You hide Your face and forget our affliction and oppression? ^24

**25** For our soul has sunk to the dust; our bodies cling to the earth. ^25

**26** Rise up; be our help! Redeem us on account of Your loving devotion. ^26
