# Psalm 50 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 49 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 51 - BSB]] →

**1** A Psalm of Asaph. The Mighty One, God the LORD, speaks and summons the earth from where the sun rises to where it sets. ^1

**2** From Zion, perfect in beauty, God shines forth. ^2

**3** Our God approaches and will not be silent! Consuming fire precedes Him, and a tempest rages around Him. ^3

**4** He summons the heavens above, and the earth, that He may judge His people: ^4

**5** “Gather to Me My saints, who made a covenant with Me by sacrifice.” ^5

**6** And the heavens proclaim His righteousness, for God Himself is Judge. Selah ^6

**7** “Hear, O My people, and I will speak, O Israel, and I will testify against you: I am God, your God. ^7

**8** I do not rebuke you for your sacrifices, and your burnt offerings are ever before Me. ^8

**9** I have no need for a bull from your stall or goats from your pens, ^9

**10** for every beast of the forest is Mine—the cattle on a thousand hills. ^10

**11** I know every bird in the mountains, and the creatures of the field are Mine. ^11

**12** If I were hungry, I would not tell you, for the world is Mine, and the fullness thereof. ^12

**13** Do I eat the flesh of bulls, or drink the blood of goats? ^13

**14** Sacrifice a thank offering to God, and fulfill your vows to the Most High. ^14

**15** Call upon Me in the day of trouble; I will deliver you, and you will honor Me.” ^15

**16** To the wicked, however, God says, “What right have you to recite My statutes and to bear My covenant on your lips? ^16

**17** For you hate My instruction and cast My words behind you. ^17

**18** When you see a thief, you befriend him, and throw in your lot with adulterers. ^18

**19** You unleash your mouth for evil and harness your tongue to deceit. ^19

**20** You sit and malign your brother; you slander your own mother’s son. ^20

**21** You have done these things, and I kept silent; you thought I was just like you. But now I rebuke you and accuse you to your face. ^21

**22** Now consider this, you who forget God, lest I tear you to pieces, with no one to rescue you: ^22

**23** He who sacrifices a thank offering honors Me, and to him who rightly orders his way, I will show the salvation of God.” ^23
