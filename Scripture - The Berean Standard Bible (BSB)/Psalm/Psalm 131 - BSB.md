# Psalm 131 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 130 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 132 - BSB]] →

**1** A song of ascents. Of David. My heart is not proud, O LORD, my eyes are not haughty. I do not aspire to great things or matters too lofty for me. ^1

**2** Surely I have stilled and quieted my soul; like a weaned child with his mother, like a weaned child is my soul within me. ^2

**3** O Israel, put your hope in the LORD, both now and forevermore. ^3
