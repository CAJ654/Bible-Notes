# Psalm 30 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 29 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 31 - BSB]] →

**1** A Psalm. A song for the dedication of the temple. Of David. I will exalt You, O LORD, for You have lifted me up and have not allowed my foes to rejoice over me. ^1

**2** O LORD my God, I cried to You for help, and You healed me. ^2

**3** O LORD, You pulled me up from Sheol; You spared me from descending into the Pit. ^3

**4** Sing to the LORD, O you His saints, and praise His holy name. ^4

**5** For His anger is fleeting, but His favor lasts a lifetime. Weeping may stay the night, but joy comes in the morning. ^5

**6** In prosperity I said, “I will never be shaken.” ^6

**7** O LORD, You favored me; You made my mountain stand strong. When You hid Your face, I was dismayed. ^7

**8** To You, O LORD, I called, and I begged my Lord for mercy: ^8

**9** “What gain is there in my bloodshed, in my descent to the Pit? Will the dust praise You? Will it proclaim Your faithfulness? ^9

**10** Hear me, O LORD, and have mercy; O LORD, be my helper.” ^10

**11** You turned my mourning into dancing; You peeled off my sackcloth and clothed me with joy, ^11

**12** that my heart may sing Your praises and not be silent. O LORD my God, I will give thanks forever. ^12
