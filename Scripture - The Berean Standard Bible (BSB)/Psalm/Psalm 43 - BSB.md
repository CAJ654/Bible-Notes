# Psalm 43 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 42 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 44 - BSB]] →

**1** Vindicate me, O God, and plead my case against an ungodly nation; deliver me from deceitful and unjust men. ^1

**2** For You are the God of my refuge. Why have You rejected me? Why must I walk in sorrow because of the enemy’s oppression? ^2

**3** Send out Your light and Your truth; let them lead me. Let them bring me to Your holy mountain and to the place where You dwell. ^3

**4** Then I will go to the altar of God, to God, my greatest joy. I will praise You with the harp, O God, my God. ^4

**5** Why are you downcast, O my soul? Why the unease within me? Put your hope in God, for I will yet praise Him, my Savior and my God. ^5
