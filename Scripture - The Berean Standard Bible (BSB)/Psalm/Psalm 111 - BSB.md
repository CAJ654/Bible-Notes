# Psalm 111 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 110 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 112 - BSB]] →

**1** Hallelujah! I will give thanks to the LORD with all my heart in the council of the upright and in the assembly. ^1

**2** Great are the works of the LORD; they are pondered by all who delight in them. ^2

**3** Splendid and majestic is His work; His righteousness endures forever. ^3

**4** He has caused His wonders to be remembered; the LORD is gracious and compassionate. ^4

**5** He provides food for those who fear Him; He remembers His covenant forever. ^5

**6** He has shown His people the power of His works by giving them the inheritance of the nations. ^6

**7** The works of His hands are truth and justice; all His precepts are trustworthy. ^7

**8** They are upheld forever and ever, enacted in truth and uprightness. ^8

**9** He has sent redemption to His people; He has ordained His covenant forever; holy and awesome is His name. ^9

**10** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom; all who follow His precepts gain rich understanding. His praise endures forever! ^10
