# Psalm 134 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 133 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 135 - BSB]] →

**1** A song of ascents. Come, bless the LORD, all you servants of the LORD who serve by night in the house of the LORD! ^1

**2** Lift up your hands to the sanctuary and bless the LORD! ^2

**3** May the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth, bless you from Zion. ^3
