# Psalm 126 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 125 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 127 - BSB]] →

**1** A song of ascents. When the LORD restored the captives of Zion, we were like dreamers. ^1

**2** Then our mouths were filled with laughter, our tongues with shouts of joy. Then it was said among the nations, “The LORD has done great things for them.” ^2

**3** The LORD has done great things for us; we are filled with joy. ^3

**4** Restore our captives, O LORD, like streams in the Negev. ^4

**5** Those who sow in tears will reap with shouts of joy. ^5

**6** He who goes out weeping, bearing a trail of seed, will surely return with shouts of joy, carrying sheaves of grain. ^6
