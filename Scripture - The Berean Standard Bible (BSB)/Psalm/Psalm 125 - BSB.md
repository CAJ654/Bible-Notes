# Psalm 125 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 124 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 126 - BSB]] →

**1** A song of ascents. Those who trust in the LORD are like Mount Zion. It cannot be moved; it abides forever. ^1

**2** As the mountains surround Jerusalem, so the LORD surrounds His people, both now and forevermore. ^2

**3** For the scepter of the wicked will not rest upon the land allotted to the righteous, so that the righteous will not put forth their hands to injustice. ^3

**4** Do good, O LORD, to those who are good, and to the upright in heart. ^4

**5** But those who turn to crooked ways the LORD will banish with the evildoers. Peace be upon Israel. ^5
