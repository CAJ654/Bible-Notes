# Psalm 74 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 73 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 75 - BSB]] →

**1** A Maskil of Asaph. Why have You rejected us forever, O God? Why does Your anger smolder against the sheep of Your pasture? ^1

**2** Remember Your congregation, which You purchased long ago and redeemed as the tribe of Your inheritance—Mount Zion, where You dwell. ^2

**3** Turn Your steps to the everlasting ruins, to everything in the sanctuary the enemy has destroyed. ^3

**4** Your foes have roared within Your meeting place; they have unfurled their banners as signs, ^4

**5** like men wielding axes in a thicket of trees ^5

**6** and smashing all the carvings with hatchets and picks. ^6

**7** They have burned Your sanctuary to the ground; they have defiled the dwelling place of Your Name. ^7

**8** They said in their hearts, “We will crush them completely.” They burned down every place where God met us in the land. ^8

**9** There are no signs for us to see. There is no longer any prophet. And none of us knows how long this will last. ^9

**10** How long, O God, will the enemy taunt You? Will the foe revile Your name forever? ^10

**11** Why do You withdraw Your strong right hand? Stretch it out to destroy them! ^11

**12** Yet God is my King from ancient times, working salvation on the earth. ^12

**13** You divided the sea by Your strength; You smashed the heads of the dragons of the sea; ^13

**14** You crushed the heads of Leviathan; You fed him to the creatures of the desert. ^14

**15** You broke open the fountain and the flood; You dried up the ever-flowing rivers. ^15

**16** The day is Yours, and also the night; You established the moon and the sun. ^16

**17** You set all the boundaries of the earth; You made the summer and winter. ^17

**18** Remember how the enemy has mocked You, O LORD, how a foolish people has spurned Your name. ^18

**19** Do not deliver the soul of Your dove to beasts; do not forget the lives of Your afflicted forever. ^19

**20** Consider Your covenant, for haunts of violence fill the dark places of the land. ^20

**21** Do not let the oppressed retreat in shame; may the poor and needy praise Your name. ^21

**22** Rise up, O God; defend Your cause! Remember how the fool mocks You all day long. ^22

**23** Do not disregard the clamor of Your adversaries, the uproar of Your enemies that ascends continually. ^23
