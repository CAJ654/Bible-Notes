# Psalm 29 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 28 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 30 - BSB]] →

**1** A Psalm of David. Ascribe to the LORD, O heavenly beings, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength. ^1

**2** Ascribe to the LORD the glory due His name; worship the LORD in the splendor of His holiness. ^2

**3** The voice of the LORD is over the waters; the God of glory thunders; the LORD is heard over many waters. ^3

**4** The voice of the LORD is powerful; the voice of the LORD is majestic. ^4

**5** The voice of the LORD breaks the cedars; the LORD shatters the cedars of Lebanon. ^5

**6** He makes Lebanon skip like a calf, and Sirion like a young wild ox. ^6

**7** The voice of the LORD strikes with flames of fire. ^7

**8** The voice of the LORD shakes the wilderness; the LORD shakes the Wilderness of Kadesh. ^8

**9** The voice of the LORD twists the oaks and strips the forests bare. And in His temple all cry, “Glory!” ^9

**10** The LORD sits enthroned over the flood; the LORD is enthroned as King forever. ^10

**11** The LORD gives His people strength; the LORD blesses His people with peace. ^11
