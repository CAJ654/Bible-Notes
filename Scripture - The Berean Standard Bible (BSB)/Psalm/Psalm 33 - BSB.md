# Psalm 33 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 32 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 34 - BSB]] →

**1** Rejoice in the LORD, O righteous ones; it is fitting for the upright to praise Him. ^1

**2** Praise the LORD with the harp; make music to Him with ten strings. ^2

**3** Sing to Him a new song; play skillfully with a shout of joy. ^3

**4** For the word of the LORD is upright, and all His work is trustworthy. ^4

**5** The LORD loves righteousness and justice; the earth is full of His loving devotion. ^5

**6** By the word of the LORD the heavens were made, and all the stars by the breath of His mouth. ^6

**7** He piles up the waters of the sea; He puts the depths into storehouses. ^7

**8** Let all the earth fear the LORD; let all the people of the world revere Him. ^8

**9** For He spoke, and it came to be; He commanded, and it stood firm. ^9

**10** The LORD frustrates the plans of the nations; He thwarts the devices of the peoples. ^10

**11** The counsel of the LORD stands forever, the purposes of His heart to all generations. ^11

**12** Blessed is the nation whose God is the LORD, the people He has chosen as His inheritance! ^12

**13** The LORD looks down from heaven; He sees all the sons of men. ^13

**14** From His dwelling place He gazes on all who inhabit the earth. ^14

**15** He shapes the hearts of each; He considers all their works. ^15

**16** No king is saved by his vast army; no warrior is delivered by his great strength. ^16

**17** A horse is a vain hope for salvation; even its great strength cannot save. ^17

**18** Surely the eyes of the LORD are on those who fear Him, on those whose hope is in His loving devotion ^18

**19** to deliver them from death and keep them alive in famine. ^19

**20** Our soul waits for the LORD; He is our help and our shield. ^20

**21** For our hearts rejoice in Him, since we trust in His holy name. ^21

**22** May Your loving devotion rest on us, O LORD, as we put our hope in You. ^22
