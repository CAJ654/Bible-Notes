# Psalm 56 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 55 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 57 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. To the tune of “A Dove on Distant Oaks.” A Miktam of David, when the Philistines seized him in Gath. Be merciful to me, O God, for men are hounding me; all day they press their attack. ^1

**2** My enemies pursue me all day long, for many proudly assail me. ^2

**3** When I am afraid, I put my trust in You. ^3

**4** In God, whose word I praise—in God I trust. I will not be afraid. What can man do to me? ^4

**5** All day long they twist my words; all their thoughts are on my demise. ^5

**6** They conspire, they lurk, they watch my steps while they wait to take my life. ^6

**7** In spite of such sin, will they escape? In Your anger, O God, cast down the nations. ^7

**8** You have taken account of my wanderings. Put my tears in Your bottle—are they not in Your book? ^8

**9** Then my enemies will retreat on the day I cry for help. By this I will know that God is on my side. ^9

**10** In God, whose word I praise, in the LORD, whose word I praise, ^10

**11** in God I trust; I will not be afraid. What can man do to me? ^11

**12** Your vows are upon me, O God; I will render thank offerings to You. ^12

**13** For You have delivered my soul from death, and my feet from stumbling, that I may walk before God in the light of life. ^13
