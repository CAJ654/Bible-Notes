# Psalm 34 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 33 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 35 - BSB]] →

**1** Of David, when he pretended to be insane before Abimelech, so that the king drove him away. I will bless the LORD at all times; His praise will always be on my lips. ^1

**2** My soul boasts in the LORD; let the oppressed hear and rejoice. ^2

**3** Magnify the LORD with me; let us exalt His name together. ^3

**4** I sought the LORD, and He answered me; He delivered me from all my fears. ^4

**5** Those who look to Him are radiant with joy; their faces shall never be ashamed. ^5

**6** This poor man called out, and the LORD heard him; He saved him from all his troubles. ^6

**7** The angel of the LORD encamps around those who fear Him, and he delivers them. ^7

**8** Taste and see that the LORD is good; blessed is the man who takes refuge in Him! ^8

**9** Fear the LORD, you His saints, for those who fear Him lack nothing. ^9

**10** Young lions go lacking and hungry, but those who seek the LORD lack no good thing. ^10

**11** Come, children, listen to me; I will teach you the fear of the LORD. ^11

**12** Who is the man who delights in life, who desires to see good days? ^12

**13** Keep your tongue from evil and your lips from deceitful speech. ^13

**14** Turn away from evil and do good; seek peace and pursue it. ^14

**15** The eyes of the LORD are on the righteous, and His ears are inclined to their cry. ^15

**16** But the face of the LORD is against those who do evil, to wipe out all memory of them from the earth. ^16

**17** The righteous cry out, and the LORD hears; He delivers them from all their troubles. ^17

**18** The LORD is near to the brokenhearted; He saves the contrite in spirit. ^18

**19** Many are the afflictions of the righteous, but the LORD delivers him from them all. ^19

**20** He protects all his bones; not one of them will be broken. ^20

**21** Evil will slay the wicked, and the haters of the righteous will be condemned. ^21

**22** The LORD redeems His servants, and none who take refuge in Him will be condemned. ^22
