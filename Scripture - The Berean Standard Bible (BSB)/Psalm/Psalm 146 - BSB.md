# Psalm 146 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 145 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 147 - BSB]] →

**1** Hallelujah! Praise the LORD, O my soul. ^1

**2** I will praise the LORD all my life; I will sing praises to my God while I have my being. ^2

**3** Put not your trust in princes, in mortal man, who cannot save. ^3

**4** When his spirit departs, he returns to the ground; on that very day his plans perish. ^4

**5** Blessed is he whose help is the God of Jacob, whose hope is in the LORD his God, ^5

**6** the Maker of heaven and earth, the sea, and everything in them. He remains faithful forever. ^6

**7** He executes justice for the oppressed and gives food to the hungry. The LORD sets the prisoners free, ^7

**8** the LORD opens the eyes of the blind, the LORD lifts those who are weighed down, the LORD loves the righteous. ^8

**9** The LORD protects foreigners; He sustains the fatherless and the widow, but the ways of the wicked He frustrates. ^9

**10** The LORD reigns forever, your God, O Zion, for all generations. Hallelujah! ^10
