# Psalm 16 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 15 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 17 - BSB]] →

**1** A Miktam of David. Preserve me, O God, for in You I take refuge. ^1

**2** I said to the LORD, “You are my Lord; apart from You I have no good thing.” ^2

**3** As for the saints in the land, they are the excellence in whom all my delight resides. ^3

**4** Sorrows will multiply to those who chase other gods. I will not pour out their libations of blood, or speak their names with my lips. ^4

**5** The LORD is my chosen portion and my cup; You have made my lot secure. ^5

**6** The lines of my boundary have fallen in pleasant places; surely my inheritance is delightful. ^6

**7** I will bless the LORD who counsels me; even at night my conscience instructs me. ^7

**8** I have set the LORD always before me. Because He is at my right hand, I will not be shaken. ^8

**9** Therefore my heart is glad and my tongue rejoices; my body also will dwell securely. ^9

**10** For You will not abandon my soul to Sheol, nor will You let Your Holy One see decay. ^10

**11** You have made known to me the path of life; You will fill me with joy in Your presence, with eternal pleasures at Your right hand. ^11
