# Psalm 106 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 105 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 107 - BSB]] →

**1** Hallelujah! Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever. ^1

**2** Who can describe the mighty acts of the LORD or fully proclaim His praise? ^2

**3** Blessed are those who uphold justice, who practice righteousness at all times. ^3

**4** Remember me, O LORD, in Your favor to Your people; visit me with Your salvation, ^4

**5** that I may see the prosperity of Your chosen ones, and rejoice in the gladness of Your nation, and give glory with Your inheritance. ^5

**6** We have sinned like our fathers; we have done wrong and acted wickedly. ^6

**7** Our fathers in Egypt did not grasp Your wonders or remember Your abundant kindness; but they rebelled by the sea, there at the Red Sea. ^7

**8** Yet He saved them for the sake of His name, to make His power known. ^8

**9** He rebuked the Red Sea, and it dried up; He led them through the depths as through a desert. ^9

**10** He saved them from the hand that hated them; He redeemed them from the hand of the enemy. ^10

**11** The waters covered their foes; not one of them remained. ^11

**12** Then they believed His promises and sang His praise. ^12

**13** Yet they soon forgot His works and failed to wait for His counsel. ^13

**14** They craved intensely in the wilderness and tested God in the desert. ^14

**15** So He granted their request, but sent a wasting disease upon them. ^15

**16** In the camp they envied Moses, as well as Aaron, the holy one of the LORD. ^16

**17** The earth opened up and swallowed Dathan; it covered the assembly of Abiram. ^17

**18** Then fire blazed through their company; flames consumed the wicked. ^18

**19** At Horeb they made a calf and worshiped a molten image. ^19

**20** They exchanged their Glory for the image of a grass-eating ox. ^20

**21** They forgot God their Savior, who did great things in Egypt, ^21

**22** wondrous works in the land of Ham, and awesome deeds by the Red Sea. ^22

**23** So He said He would destroy them—had not Moses His chosen one stood before Him in the breach to divert His wrath from destroying them. ^23

**24** They despised the pleasant land; they did not believe His promise. ^24

**25** They grumbled in their tents and did not listen to the voice of the LORD. ^25

**26** So He raised His hand and swore to cast them down in the wilderness, ^26

**27** to disperse their offspring among the nations and scatter them throughout the lands. ^27

**28** They yoked themselves to Baal of Peor and ate sacrifices offered to lifeless gods. ^28

**29** So they provoked the LORD to anger with their deeds, and a plague broke out among them. ^29

**30** But Phinehas stood and intervened, and the plague was restrained. ^30

**31** It was credited to him as righteousness for endless generations to come. ^31

**32** At the waters of Meribah they angered the LORD, and trouble came to Moses because of them. ^32

**33** For they rebelled against His Spirit, and Moses spoke rashly with his lips. ^33

**34** They did not destroy the peoples as the LORD had commanded them, ^34

**35** but they mingled with the nations and adopted their customs. ^35

**36** They worshiped their idols, which became a snare to them. ^36

**37** They sacrificed their sons and their daughters to demons. ^37

**38** They shed innocent blood—the blood of their sons and daughters, whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan, and the land was polluted with blood. ^38

**39** They defiled themselves by their actions and prostituted themselves by their deeds. ^39

**40** So the anger of the LORD burned against His people, and He abhorred His own inheritance. ^40

**41** He delivered them into the hand of the nations, and those who hated them ruled over them. ^41

**42** Their enemies oppressed them and subdued them under their hand. ^42

**43** Many times He rescued them, but they were bent on rebellion and sank down in their iniquity. ^43

**44** Nevertheless He heard their cry; He took note of their distress. ^44

**45** And He remembered His covenant with them, and relented by the abundance of His loving devotion. ^45

**46** He made them objects of compassion to all who held them captive. ^46

**47** Save us, O LORD our God, and gather us from the nations, that we may give thanks to Your holy name, that we may glory in Your praise. ^47

**48** Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Let all the people say, “Amen!” Hallelujah! ^48
