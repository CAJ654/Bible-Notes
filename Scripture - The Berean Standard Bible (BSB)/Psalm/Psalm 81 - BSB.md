# Psalm 81 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 80 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 82 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. According to Gittith. Of Asaph. Sing for joy to God our strength; make a joyful noise to the God of Jacob. ^1

**2** Lift up a song, strike the tambourine, play the sweet-sounding harp and lyre. ^2

**3** Sound the ram’s horn at the New Moon, and at the full moon on the day of our Feast. ^3

**4** For this is a statute for Israel, an ordinance of the God of Jacob. ^4

**5** He ordained it as a testimony for Joseph when he went out over the land of Egypt, where I heard an unfamiliar language: ^5

**6** “I relieved his shoulder of the burden; his hands were freed from the basket. ^6

**7** You called out in distress, and I rescued you; I answered you from the cloud of thunder; I tested you at the waters of Meribah. Selah ^7

**8** Hear, O My people, and I will warn you: O Israel, if only you would listen to Me! ^8

**9** There must be no strange god among you, nor shall you bow to a foreign god. ^9

**10** I am the LORD your God, who brought you up out of Egypt. Open wide your mouth, and I will fill it. ^10

**11** But My people would not listen to Me, and Israel would not obey Me. ^11

**12** So I gave them up to their stubborn hearts to follow their own devices. ^12

**13** If only My people would listen to Me, if Israel would follow My ways, ^13

**14** how soon I would subdue their enemies and turn My hand against their foes! ^14

**15** Those who hate the LORD would feign obedience, and their doom would last forever. ^15

**16** But I would feed you the finest wheat; with honey from the rock I would satisfy you.” ^16
