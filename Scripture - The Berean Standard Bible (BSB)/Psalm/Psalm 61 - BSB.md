# Psalm 61 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 60 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 62 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. Of David. Hear my cry, O God; attend to my prayer. ^1

**2** From the ends of the earth I call out to You whenever my heart is faint. Lead me to the rock that is higher than I. ^2

**3** For You have been my refuge, a tower of strength against the enemy. ^3

**4** Let me dwell in Your tent forever and take refuge in the shelter of Your wings. Selah ^4

**5** For You have heard my vows, O God; You have given me the inheritance reserved for those who fear Your name. ^5

**6** Increase the days of the king’s life; may his years span many generations. ^6

**7** May he sit enthroned in God’s presence forever; appoint Your loving devotion and Your faithfulness to guard him. ^7

**8** Then I will ever sing praise to Your name and fulfill my vows day by day. ^8
