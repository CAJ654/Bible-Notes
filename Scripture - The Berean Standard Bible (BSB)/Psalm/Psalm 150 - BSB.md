# Psalm 150 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 149 - BSB]]

**1** Hallelujah! Praise God in His sanctuary. Praise Him in His mighty heavens. ^1

**2** Praise Him for His mighty acts; praise Him for His excellent greatness. ^2

**3** Praise Him with the sound of the horn; praise Him with the harp and lyre. ^3

**4** Praise Him with tambourine and dancing; praise Him with strings and flute. ^4

**5** Praise Him with clashing cymbals; praise Him with resounding cymbals. ^5

**6** Let everything that has breath praise the LORD! Hallelujah! ^6
