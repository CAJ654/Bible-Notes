# Psalm 147 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 146 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 148 - BSB]] →

**1** Hallelujah! How good it is to sing praises to our God, how pleasant and lovely to praise Him! ^1

**2** The LORD builds up Jerusalem; He gathers the exiles of Israel. ^2

**3** He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds. ^3

**4** He determines the number of the stars; He calls them each by name. ^4

**5** Great is our Lord, and mighty in power; His understanding has no limit. ^5

**6** The LORD sustains the humble, but casts the wicked to the ground. ^6

**7** Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving; make music on the harp to our God, ^7

**8** who covers the sky with clouds, who prepares rain for the earth, who makes grass to grow on the hills. ^8

**9** He provides food for the animals, and for the young ravens when they call. ^9

**10** He does not delight in the strength of the horse; He takes no pleasure in the legs of a man. ^10

**11** The LORD is pleased with those who fear Him, who hope in His loving devotion. ^11

**12** Exalt the LORD, O Jerusalem; praise your God, O Zion! ^12

**13** For He strengthens the bars of your gates and blesses the children within you. ^13

**14** He makes peace at your borders; He fills you with the finest wheat. ^14

**15** He sends forth His command to the earth; His word runs swiftly. ^15

**16** He spreads the snow like wool; He scatters the frost like ashes; ^16

**17** He casts forth His hail like pebbles. Who can withstand His icy blast? ^17

**18** He sends forth His word and melts them; He unleashes His winds, and the waters flow. ^18

**19** He declares His word to Jacob, His statutes and judgments to Israel. ^19

**20** He has done this for no other nation; they do not know His judgments. Hallelujah! ^20
