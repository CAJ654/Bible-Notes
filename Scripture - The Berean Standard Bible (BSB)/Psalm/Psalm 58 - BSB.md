# Psalm 58 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 57 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 59 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A Miktam of David. Do you indeed speak justly, O rulers? Do you judge uprightly, O sons of men? ^1

**2** No, in your hearts you devise injustice; with your hands you mete out violence on the earth. ^2

**3** The wicked are estranged from the womb; the liars go astray from birth. ^3

**4** Their venom is like the venom of a snake, like a cobra that shuts its ears, ^4

**5** refusing to hear the tune of the charmer who skillfully weaves his spell. ^5

**6** O God, shatter their teeth in their mouths; O LORD, tear out the fangs of the lions. ^6

**7** May they vanish like water that runs off; when they draw the bow, may their arrows be blunted. ^7

**8** Like a slug that dissolves in its slime, like a woman’s stillborn child, may they never see the sun. ^8

**9** Before your pots can feel the burning thorns—whether green or dry—He will sweep them away. ^9

**10** The righteous will rejoice when they see they are avenged; they will wash their feet in the blood of the wicked. ^10

**11** Then men will say, “There is surely a reward for the righteous! There is surely a God who judges the earth!” ^11
