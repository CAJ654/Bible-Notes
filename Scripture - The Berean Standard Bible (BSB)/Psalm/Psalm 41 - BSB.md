# Psalm 41 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 40 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 42 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. Blessed is the one who cares for the poor; the LORD will deliver him in the day of trouble. ^1

**2** The LORD will protect and preserve him; He will bless him in the land and refuse to give him over to the will of his foes. ^2

**3** The LORD will sustain him on his bed of illness and restore him from his bed of sickness. ^3

**4** I said, “O LORD, be gracious to me; heal me, for I have sinned against You.” ^4

**5** My enemies say with malice: “When will he die and be forgotten?” ^5

**6** My visitor speaks falsehood; he gathers slander in his heart; he goes out and spreads it abroad. ^6

**7** All who hate me whisper against me; they imagine the worst for me: ^7

**8** “A vile disease has been poured into him; he will never get up from where he lies!” ^8

**9** Even my close friend whom I trusted, the one who shared my bread, has lifted up his heel against me. ^9

**10** But You, O LORD, be gracious to me and raise me up, that I may repay them. ^10

**11** By this I know that You delight in me, for my enemy does not triumph over me. ^11

**12** In my integrity You uphold me and set me in Your presence forever. ^12

**13** Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Amen and Amen. ^13
