# Psalm 141 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 140 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 142 - BSB]] →

**1** A Psalm of David. I call upon You, O LORD; come quickly to me. Hear my voice when I call to You. ^1

**2** May my prayer be set before You like incense; my uplifted hands, like the evening offering. ^2

**3** Set a guard, O LORD, over my mouth; keep watch at the door of my lips. ^3

**4** Do not let my heart be drawn to any evil thing or take part in works of wickedness with men who do iniquity; let me not feast on their delicacies. ^4

**5** Let the righteous man strike me; let his rebuke be an act of loving devotion. It is oil for my head; let me not refuse it. For my prayer is ever against the deeds of the wicked. ^5

**6** When their rulers are thrown down from the cliffs, the people will listen to my words, for they are pleasant. ^6

**7** As when one plows and breaks up the soil, so our bones have been scattered at the mouth of Sheol. ^7

**8** But my eyes are fixed on You, O GOD the Lord. In You I seek refuge; do not leave my soul defenseless. ^8

**9** Keep me from the snares they have laid for me, and from the lures of evildoers. ^9

**10** Let the wicked fall into their own nets, while I pass by in safety. ^10
