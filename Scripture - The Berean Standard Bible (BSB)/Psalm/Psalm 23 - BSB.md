# Psalm 23 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 22 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 24 - BSB]] →

**1** A Psalm of David. The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want. ^1

**2** He makes me lie down in green pastures; He leads me beside quiet waters. ^2

**3** He restores my soul; He guides me in the paths of righteousness for the sake of His name. ^3

**4** Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for You are with me; Your rod and Your staff, they comfort me. ^4

**5** You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies. You anoint my head with oil; my cup overflows. ^5

**6** Surely goodness and mercy will follow me all the days of my life, and I will dwell in the house of the LORD forever. ^6
