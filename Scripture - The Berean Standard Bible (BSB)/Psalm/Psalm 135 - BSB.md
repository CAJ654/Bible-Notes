# Psalm 135 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 134 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 136 - BSB]] →

**1** Hallelujah! Praise the name of the LORD. Give praise, O servants of the LORD, ^1

**2** who stand in the house of the LORD, in the courts of the house of our God. ^2

**3** Hallelujah, for the LORD is good; sing praises to His name, for it is lovely. ^3

**4** For the LORD has chosen Jacob as His own, Israel as His treasured possession. ^4

**5** For I know that the LORD is great; our Lord is above all gods. ^5

**6** The LORD does all that pleases Him in the heavens and on the earth, in the seas and in all their depths. ^6

**7** He causes the clouds to rise from the ends of the earth. He generates the lightning with the rain and brings forth the wind from His storehouses. ^7

**8** He struck down the firstborn of Egypt, of both man and beast. ^8

**9** He sent signs and wonders into your midst, O Egypt, against Pharaoh and all his servants. ^9

**10** He struck down many nations and slaughtered mighty kings: ^10

**11** Sihon king of the Amorites, Og king of Bashan, and all the kings of Canaan. ^11

**12** He gave their land as an inheritance, as a heritage to His people Israel. ^12

**13** Your name, O LORD, endures forever, Your renown, O LORD, through all generations. ^13

**14** For the LORD will vindicate His people and will have compassion on His servants. ^14

**15** The idols of the nations are silver and gold, made by the hands of men. ^15

**16** They have mouths, but cannot speak; they have eyes, but cannot see; ^16

**17** they have ears, but cannot hear; nor is there breath in their mouths. ^17

**18** Those who make them become like them, as do all who trust in them. ^18

**19** O house of Israel, bless the LORD; O house of Aaron, bless the LORD; ^19

**20** O house of Levi, bless the LORD; you who fear the LORD, bless the LORD! ^20

**21** Blessed be the LORD from Zion—He who dwells in Jerusalem. Hallelujah! ^21
