# Psalm 24 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 23 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 25 - BSB]] →

**1** A Psalm of David. The earth is the LORD’s, and the fullness thereof, the world and all who dwell therein. ^1

**2** For He has founded it upon the seas and established it upon the waters. ^2

**3** Who may ascend the hill of the LORD? Who may stand in His holy place? ^3

**4** He who has clean hands and a pure heart, who does not lift up his soul to an idol or swear deceitfully. ^4

**5** He will receive blessing from the LORD and vindication from the God of his salvation. ^5

**6** Such is the generation of those who seek Him, who seek Your face, O God of Jacob. Selah ^6

**7** Lift up your heads, O gates! Be lifted up, O ancient doors, that the King of Glory may enter! ^7

**8** Who is this King of Glory? The LORD strong and mighty, the LORD mighty in battle. ^8

**9** Lift up your heads, O gates! Be lifted up, O ancient doors, that the King of Glory may enter! ^9

**10** Who is He, this King of Glory? The LORD of Hosts—He is the King of Glory. Selah ^10
