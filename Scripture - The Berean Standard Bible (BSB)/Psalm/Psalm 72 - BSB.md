# Psalm 72 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 71 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 73 - BSB]] →

**1** Of Solomon. Endow the king with Your justice, O God, and the son of the king with Your righteousness. ^1

**2** May he judge Your people with righteousness and Your afflicted with justice. ^2

**3** May the mountains bring peace to the people, and the hills bring righteousness. ^3

**4** May he vindicate the afflicted among the people; may he save the children of the needy and crush the oppressor. ^4

**5** May they fear him as long as the sun shines, as long as the moon remains, through all generations. ^5

**6** May he be like rain that falls on freshly cut grass, like spring showers that water the earth. ^6

**7** May the righteous flourish in his days and prosperity abound until the moon is no more. ^7

**8** May he rule from sea to sea, and from the Euphrates to the ends of the earth. ^8

**9** May the nomads bow before him, and his enemies lick the dust. ^9

**10** May the kings of Tarshish and distant shores bring tribute; may the kings of Sheba and Seba offer gifts. ^10

**11** May all kings bow down to him and all nations serve him. ^11

**12** For he will deliver the needy who cry out and the afflicted who have no helper. ^12

**13** He will take pity on the poor and needy and save the lives of the oppressed. ^13

**14** He will redeem them from oppression and violence, for their blood is precious in his sight. ^14

**15** Long may he live! May gold from Sheba be given him. May people ever pray for him; may they bless him all day long. ^15

**16** May there be an abundance of grain in the land; may it sway atop the hills. May its fruit trees flourish like the forests of Lebanon, the people of its cities like the grass of the field. ^16

**17** May his name endure forever; may his name continue as long as the sun shines. In him may all nations be blessed; may they call him blessed. ^17

**18** Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel, who alone does marvelous deeds. ^18

**19** And blessed be His glorious name forever; may all the earth be filled with His glory. Amen and amen. ^19

**20** Thus conclude the prayers of David son of Jesse. ^20
