# Psalm 80 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 79 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 81 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Lilies of the Covenant.” A Psalm of Asaph. Hear us, O Shepherd of Israel, who leads Joseph like a flock; You who sit enthroned between the cherubim, shine forth ^1

**2** before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh. Rally Your mighty power and come to save us. ^2

**3** Restore us, O God, and cause Your face to shine upon us, that we may be saved. ^3

**4** O LORD God of Hosts, how long will Your anger smolder against the prayers of Your people? ^4

**5** You fed them with the bread of tears and made them drink the full measure of their tears. ^5

**6** You make us contend with our neighbors; our enemies mock us. ^6

**7** Restore us, O God of Hosts, and cause Your face to shine upon us, that we may be saved. ^7

**8** You uprooted a vine from Egypt; You drove out the nations and transplanted it. ^8

**9** You cleared the ground for it, and it took root and filled the land. ^9

**10** The mountains were covered by its shade, and the mighty cedars with its branches. ^10

**11** It sent out its branches to the Sea, and its shoots toward the River. ^11

**12** Why have You broken down its walls, so that all who pass by pick its fruit? ^12

**13** The boar from the forest ravages it, and the creatures of the field feed upon it. ^13

**14** Return, O God of Hosts, we pray! Look down from heaven and see! Attend to this vine— ^14

**15** the root Your right hand has planted, the son You have raised up for Yourself. ^15

**16** Your vine has been cut down and burned; they perish at the rebuke of Your countenance. ^16

**17** Let Your hand be upon the man at Your right hand, on the son of man You have raised up for Yourself. ^17

**18** Then we will not turn away from You; revive us, and we will call on Your name. ^18

**19** Restore us, O LORD God of Hosts; cause Your face to shine upon us, that we may be saved. ^19
