# Psalm 130 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 129 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 131 - BSB]] →

**1** A song of ascents. Out of the depths I cry to You, O LORD! ^1

**2** O Lord, hear my voice; let Your ears be attentive to my plea for mercy. ^2

**3** If You, O LORD, kept track of iniquities, then who, O Lord, could stand? ^3

**4** But with You there is forgiveness, so that You may be feared. ^4

**5** I wait for the LORD; my soul does wait, and in His word I put my hope. ^5

**6** My soul waits for the Lord more than watchmen wait for the morning—more than watchmen wait for the morning. ^6

**7** O Israel, put your hope in the LORD, for with the LORD is loving devotion, and with Him is redemption in abundance. ^7

**8** And He will redeem Israel from all iniquity. ^8
