# Psalm 73 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 72 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 74 - BSB]] →

**1** A Psalm of Asaph. Surely God is good to Israel, to those who are pure in heart. ^1

**2** But as for me, my feet had almost stumbled; my steps had nearly slipped. ^2

**3** For I envied the arrogant when I saw the prosperity of the wicked. ^3

**4** They have no struggle in their death; their bodies are well-fed. ^4

**5** They are free of the burdens others carry; they are not afflicted like other men. ^5

**6** Therefore pride is their necklace; a garment of violence covers them. ^6

**7** From their prosperity proceeds iniquity; the imaginations of their hearts run wild. ^7

**8** They mock and speak with malice; with arrogance they threaten oppression. ^8

**9** They set their mouths against the heavens, and their tongues strut across the earth. ^9

**10** So their people return to this place and drink up waters in abundance. ^10

**11** The wicked say, “How can God know? Does the Most High have knowledge?” ^11

**12** Behold, these are the wicked—always carefree as they increase their wealth. ^12

**13** Surely in vain I have kept my heart pure; in innocence I have washed my hands. ^13

**14** For I am afflicted all day long and punished every morning. ^14

**15** If I had said, “I will speak this way,” then I would have betrayed Your children. ^15

**16** When I tried to understand all this, it was troublesome in my sight ^16

**17** until I entered God’s sanctuary; then I discerned their end. ^17

**18** Surely You set them on slick ground; You cast them down into ruin. ^18

**19** How suddenly they are laid waste, completely swept away by terrors! ^19

**20** Like one waking from a dream, so You, O Lord, awaken and despise their form. ^20

**21** When my heart was grieved and I was pierced within, ^21

**22** I was senseless and ignorant; I was a brute beast before You. ^22

**23** Yet I am always with You; You hold my right hand. ^23

**24** You guide me with Your counsel, and later receive me in glory. ^24

**25** Whom have I in heaven but You? And on earth I desire no one besides You. ^25

**26** My flesh and my heart may fail, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever. ^26

**27** Those far from You will surely perish; You destroy all who are unfaithful to You. ^27

**28** But as for me, it is good to draw near to God. I have made the Lord GOD my refuge, that I may proclaim all Your works. ^28
