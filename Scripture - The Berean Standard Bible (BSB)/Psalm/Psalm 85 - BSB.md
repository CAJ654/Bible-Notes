# Psalm 85 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 84 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 86 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. A Psalm of the sons of Korah. You showed favor to Your land, O LORD; You restored Jacob from captivity. ^1

**2** You forgave the iniquity of Your people; You covered all their sin. Selah ^2

**3** You withheld all Your fury; You turned from Your burning anger. ^3

**4** Restore us, O God of our salvation, and put away Your displeasure toward us. ^4

**5** Will You be angry with us forever? Will You draw out Your anger to all generations? ^5

**6** Will You not revive us again, that Your people may rejoice in You? ^6

**7** Show us Your loving devotion, O LORD, and grant us Your salvation. ^7

**8** I will listen to what God the LORD will say; for He will surely speak peace to His people and His saints; He will not let them return to folly. ^8

**9** Surely His salvation is near to those who fear Him, that His glory may dwell in our land. ^9

**10** Loving devotion and faithfulness have joined together; righteousness and peace have kissed. ^10

**11** Faithfulness sprouts from the earth, and righteousness looks down from heaven. ^11

**12** The LORD will indeed provide what is good, and our land will yield its increase. ^12

**13** Righteousness will go before Him to prepare the way for His steps. ^13
