# Psalm 8 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 7 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 9 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. According to Gittith. A Psalm of David. O LORD, our Lord, how majestic is Your name in all the earth! You have set Your glory above the heavens. ^1

**2** From the mouths of children and infants You have ordained praise on account of Your adversaries, to silence the enemy and avenger. ^2

**3** When I behold Your heavens, the work of Your fingers, the moon and the stars, which You have set in place— ^3

**4** what is man that You are mindful of him, or the son of man that You care for him? ^4

**5** You made him a little lower than the angels; You crowned him with glory and honor. ^5

**6** You made him ruler of the works of Your hands; You have placed everything under his feet: ^6

**7** all sheep and oxen, and even the beasts of the field, ^7

**8** the birds of the air and the fish of the sea, all that swim the paths of the seas. ^8

**9** O LORD, our Lord, how majestic is Your name in all the earth! ^9
