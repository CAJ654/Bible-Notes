# Psalm 124 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 123 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 125 - BSB]] →

**1** A song of ascents. Of David. If the LORD had not been on our side—let Israel now declare— ^1

**2** if the LORD had not been on our side when men attacked us, ^2

**3** when their anger flared against us, then they would have swallowed us alive, ^3

**4** then the floods would have engulfed us, then the torrent would have overwhelmed us, ^4

**5** then the raging waters would have swept us away. ^5

**6** Blessed be the LORD, who has not given us as prey to their teeth. ^6

**7** We have escaped like a bird from the snare of the fowler; the net is torn, and we have slipped away. ^7

**8** Our help is in the name of the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth. ^8
