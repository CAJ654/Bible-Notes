# Psalm 47 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 46 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 48 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. A Psalm of the sons of Korah. Clap your hands, all you peoples; shout unto God with a voice of triumph. ^1

**2** How awesome is the LORD Most High, the great King over all the earth! ^2

**3** He subdues nations beneath us, and peoples under our feet. ^3

**4** He chooses our inheritance for us, the pride of Jacob, whom He loves. Selah ^4

**5** God has ascended amid shouts of joy, the LORD with the sound of the horn. ^5

**6** Sing praises to God, sing praises; sing praises to our King, sing praises! ^6

**7** For God is King of all the earth; sing to Him a psalm of praise. ^7

**8** God reigns over the nations; God is seated on His holy throne. ^8

**9** The nobles of the nations have assembled as the people of the God of Abraham; for the shields of the earth belong to God; He is highly exalted. ^9
