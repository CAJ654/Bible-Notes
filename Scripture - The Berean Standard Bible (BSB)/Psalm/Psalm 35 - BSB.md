# Psalm 35 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 34 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 36 - BSB]] →

**1** Of David. Contend with my opponents, O LORD; fight against those who fight against me. ^1

**2** Take up Your shield and buckler; arise and come to my aid. ^2

**3** Draw the spear and javelin against my pursuers; say to my soul: “I am your salvation.” ^3

**4** May those who seek my life be disgraced and put to shame; may those who plan to harm me be driven back and confounded. ^4

**5** May they be like chaff in the wind, as the angel of the LORD drives them away. ^5

**6** May their path be dark and slick, as the angel of the LORD pursues. ^6

**7** For without cause they laid their net for me; without reason they dug a pit for my soul. ^7

**8** May ruin befall them by surprise; may the net they hid ensnare them; may they fall into the hazard they created. ^8

**9** Then my soul will rejoice in the LORD and exult in His salvation. ^9

**10** All my bones will exclaim, “Who is like You, O LORD, who delivers the afflicted from the aggressor, the poor and needy from the robber?” ^10

**11** Hostile witnesses come forward; they make charges I know nothing about. ^11

**12** They repay me evil for good, to the bereavement of my soul. ^12

**13** Yet when they were ill, I put on sackcloth; I humbled myself with fasting, but my prayers returned unanswered. ^13

**14** I paced about as for my friend or brother; I was bowed down with grief, like one mourning for his mother. ^14

**15** But when I stumbled, they assembled in glee; they gathered together against me. Assailants I did not know slandered me without ceasing. ^15

**16** Like godless jesters at a feast, they gnashed their teeth at me. ^16

**17** How long, O Lord, will You look on? Rescue my soul from their ravages, my precious life from these lions. ^17

**18** Then I will give You thanks in the great assembly; I will praise You among many people. ^18

**19** Let not my enemies gloat over me without cause, nor those who hate me without reason wink in malice. ^19

**20** For they do not speak peace, but they devise deceitful schemes against those who live quietly in the land. ^20

**21** They gape at me and say, “Aha, aha! Our eyes have seen!” ^21

**22** O LORD, You have seen it; be not silent. O Lord, be not far from me. ^22

**23** Awake and rise to my defense, to my cause, my God and my Lord! ^23

**24** Vindicate me by Your righteousness, O LORD my God, and do not let them gloat over me. ^24

**25** Let them not say in their hearts, “Aha, just what we wanted!” Let them not say, “We have swallowed him up!” ^25

**26** May those who gloat in my distress be ashamed and confounded; may those who exalt themselves over me be clothed in shame and reproach. ^26

**27** May those who favor my vindication shout for joy and gladness; may they always say, “Exalted be the LORD who delights in His servant’s well-being.” ^27

**28** Then my tongue will proclaim Your righteousness and Your praises all day long. ^28
