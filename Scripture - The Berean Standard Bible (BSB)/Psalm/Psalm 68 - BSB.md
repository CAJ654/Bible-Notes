# Psalm 68 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 67 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 69 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. A song. God arises. His enemies are scattered, and those who hate Him flee His presence. ^1

**2** As smoke is blown away, You will drive them out; as wax melts before the fire, the wicked will perish in the presence of God. ^2

**3** But the righteous will be glad and rejoice before God; they will celebrate with joy. ^3

**4** Sing to God! Sing praises to His name. Exalt Him who rides on the clouds—His name is the LORD—and rejoice before Him. ^4

**5** A father of the fatherless and a defender of widows is God in His holy habitation. ^5

**6** God settles the lonely in families; He leads the prisoners out to prosperity, but the rebellious dwell in a sun-scorched land. ^6

**7** O God, when You went out before Your people, when You marched through the wasteland, Selah ^7

**8** the earth shook and the heavens poured down rain before God, the One on Sinai, before God, the God of Israel. ^8

**9** You sent abundant rain, O God; You refreshed Your weary inheritance. ^9

**10** Your flock settled therein; O God, from Your bounty You provided for the poor. ^10

**11** The Lord gives the command; a great company of women proclaim it: ^11

**12** “Kings and their armies flee in haste; she who waits at home divides the plunder. ^12

**13** Though you lie down among the sheepfolds, the wings of the dove are covered with silver, and her feathers with shimmering gold.” ^13

**14** When the Almighty scattered the kings in the land, it was like the snow falling on Zalmon. ^14

**15** A mountain of God is Mount Bashan; a mountain of many peaks is Mount Bashan. ^15

**16** Why do you gaze in envy, O mountains of many peaks? This is the mountain God chose for His dwelling, where the LORD will surely dwell forever. ^16

**17** The chariots of God are tens of thousands—thousands of thousands are they; the Lord is in His sanctuary as He was at Sinai. ^17

**18** You have ascended on high; You have led captives away. You have received gifts from men, even from the rebellious, that the LORD God may dwell there. ^18

**19** Blessed be the Lord, who daily bears our burden, the God of our salvation. Selah ^19

**20** Our God is a God of deliverance; the Lord GOD is our rescuer from death. ^20

**21** Surely God will crush the heads of His enemies, the hairy crowns of those who persist in guilty ways. ^21

**22** The Lord said, “I will retrieve them from Bashan, I will bring them up from the depths of the sea, ^22

**23** that your foot may be dipped in the blood of your foes—the tongues of your dogs in the same.” ^23

**24** They have seen Your procession, O God—the march of my God and King into the sanctuary. ^24

**25** The singers lead the way, the musicians follow after, among the maidens playing tambourines. ^25

**26** Bless God in the great congregation; bless the LORD from the fountain of Israel. ^26

**27** There is Benjamin, the youngest, ruling them, the princes of Judah in their company, the princes of Zebulun and of Naphtali. ^27

**28** Summon Your power, O God; show Your strength, O God, which You have exerted on our behalf. ^28

**29** Because of Your temple at Jerusalem kings will bring You gifts. ^29

**30** Rebuke the beast in the reeds, the herd of bulls among the calves of the nations, until it submits, bringing bars of silver. Scatter the nations who delight in war. ^30

**31** Envoys will arrive from Egypt; Cush will stretch out her hands to God. ^31

**32** Sing to God, O kingdoms of the earth; sing praises to the Lord—Selah ^32

**33** to Him who rides upon the highest heavens of old; behold, His mighty voice resounds. ^33

**34** Ascribe the power to God, whose majesty is over Israel, whose strength is in the skies. ^34

**35** O God, You are awesome in Your sanctuary; the God of Israel Himself gives strength and power to His people. Blessed be God! ^35
