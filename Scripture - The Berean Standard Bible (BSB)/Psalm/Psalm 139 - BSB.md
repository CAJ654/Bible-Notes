# Psalm 139 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 138 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 140 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. O LORD, You have searched me and known me. ^1

**2** You know when I sit and when I rise; You understand my thoughts from afar. ^2

**3** You search out my path and my lying down; You are aware of all my ways. ^3

**4** Even before a word is on my tongue, You know all about it, O LORD. ^4

**5** You hem me in behind and before; You have laid Your hand upon me. ^5

**6** Such knowledge is too wonderful for me, too lofty for me to attain. ^6

**7** Where can I go to escape Your Spirit? Where can I flee from Your presence? ^7

**8** If I ascend to the heavens, You are there; if I make my bed in Sheol, You are there. ^8

**9** If I rise on the wings of the dawn, if I settle by the farthest sea, ^9

**10** even there Your hand will guide me; Your right hand will hold me fast. ^10

**11** If I say, “Surely the darkness will hide me, and the light become night around me”— ^11

**12** even the darkness is not dark to You, but the night shines like the day, for darkness is as light to You. ^12

**13** For You formed my inmost being; You knit me together in my mother’s womb. ^13

**14** I praise You, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Marvelous are Your works, and I know this very well. ^14

**15** My frame was not hidden from You when I was made in secret, when I was woven together in the depths of the earth. ^15

**16** Your eyes saw my unformed body; all my days were written in Your book and ordained for me before one of them came to be. ^16

**17** How precious to me are Your thoughts, O God, how vast is their sum! ^17

**18** If I were to count them, they would outnumber the grains of sand; and when I awake, I am still with You. ^18

**19** O God, that You would slay the wicked—away from me, you bloodthirsty men— ^19

**20** who speak of You deceitfully; Your enemies take Your name in vain. ^20

**21** Do I not hate those who hate You, O LORD, and detest those who rise against You? ^21

**22** I hate them with perfect hatred; I count them as my enemies. ^22

**23** Search me, O God, and know my heart; test me and know my concerns. ^23

**24** See if there is any offensive way in me; lead me in the way everlasting. ^24
