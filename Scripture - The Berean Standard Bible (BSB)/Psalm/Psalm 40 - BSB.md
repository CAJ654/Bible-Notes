# Psalm 40 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 39 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 41 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. I waited patiently for the LORD; He inclined to me and heard my cry. ^1

**2** He lifted me up from the pit of despair, out of the miry clay; He set my feet upon a rock, and made my footsteps firm. ^2

**3** He put a new song in my mouth, a hymn of praise to our God. Many will see and fear and put their trust in the LORD. ^3

**4** Blessed is the man who has made the LORD his trust, who has not turned to the proud, nor to those who lapse into falsehood. ^4

**5** Many, O LORD my God, are the wonders You have done, and the plans You have for us—none can compare to You—if I proclaim and declare them, they are more than I can count. ^5

**6** Sacrifice and offering You did not desire, but my ears You have opened. Burnt offerings and sin offerings You did not require. ^6

**7** Then I said, “Here I am, I have come—it is written about me in the scroll: ^7

**8** I delight to do Your will, O my God; Your law is within my heart.” ^8

**9** I proclaim righteousness in the great assembly; behold, I do not seal my lips, as You, O LORD, do know. ^9

**10** I have not covered up Your righteousness in my heart; I have declared Your faithfulness and salvation; I have not concealed Your loving devotion and faithfulness from the great assembly. ^10

**11** O LORD, do not withhold Your mercy from me; Your loving devotion and faithfulness will always guard me. ^11

**12** For evils without number surround me; my sins have overtaken me, so that I cannot see. They are more than the hairs of my head, and my heart has failed within me. ^12

**13** Be pleased, O LORD, to deliver me; hurry, O LORD, to help me. ^13

**14** May those who seek my life be ashamed and confounded; may those who wish me harm be repelled and humiliated. ^14

**15** May those who say to me, “Aha, aha!” be appalled at their own shame. ^15

**16** May all who seek You rejoice and be glad in You; may those who love Your salvation always say, “The LORD be magnified!” ^16

**17** But I am poor and needy; may the Lord think of me. You are my helper and deliverer; O my God, do not delay. ^17
