# Psalm 105 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 104 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 106 - BSB]] →

**1** Give thanks to the LORD, call upon His name; make known His deeds among the nations. ^1

**2** Sing to Him, sing praises to Him; tell of all His wonders. ^2

**3** Glory in His holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice. ^3

**4** Seek out the LORD and His strength; seek His face always. ^4

**5** Remember the wonders He has done, His marvels, and the judgments He has pronounced, ^5

**6** O offspring of His servant Abraham, O sons of Jacob, His chosen ones. ^6

**7** He is the LORD our God; His judgments carry throughout the earth. ^7

**8** He remembers His covenant forever, the word He ordained for a thousand generations— ^8

**9** the covenant He made with Abraham, and the oath He swore to Isaac. ^9

**10** He confirmed it to Jacob as a decree, to Israel as an everlasting covenant: ^10

**11** “I will give you the land of Canaan as the portion of your inheritance.” ^11

**12** When they were few in number, few indeed, and strangers in the land, ^12

**13** they wandered from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another. ^13

**14** He let no man oppress them; He rebuked kings on their behalf: ^14

**15** “Do not touch My anointed ones! Do no harm to My prophets!” ^15

**16** He called down famine on the land and cut off all their supplies of food. ^16

**17** He sent a man before them—Joseph, sold as a slave. ^17

**18** They bruised his feet with shackles and placed his neck in irons, ^18

**19** until his prediction came true and the word of the LORD proved him right. ^19

**20** The king sent and released him; the ruler of peoples set him free. ^20

**21** He made him master of his household, ruler over all his substance, ^21

**22** to instruct his princes as he pleased and teach his elders wisdom. ^22

**23** Then Israel entered Egypt; Jacob dwelt in the land of Ham. ^23

**24** And the LORD made His people very fruitful, more numerous than their foes, ^24

**25** whose hearts He turned to hate His people, to conspire against His servants. ^25

**26** He sent Moses His servant, and Aaron, whom He had chosen. ^26

**27** They performed His miraculous signs among them, and wonders in the land of Ham. ^27

**28** He sent darkness, and it became dark—yet they defied His words. ^28

**29** He turned their waters to blood and caused their fish to die. ^29

**30** Their land teemed with frogs, even in their royal chambers. ^30

**31** He spoke, and insects swarmed—gnats throughout their country. ^31

**32** He gave them hail for rain, with lightning throughout their land. ^32

**33** He struck their vines and fig trees and shattered the trees of their country. ^33

**34** He spoke, and the locusts came—young locusts without number. ^34

**35** They devoured every plant in their land and consumed the produce of their soil. ^35

**36** Then He struck all the firstborn in their land, the firstfruits of all their vigor. ^36

**37** He brought Israel out with silver and gold, and none among His tribes stumbled. ^37

**38** Egypt was glad when they departed, for the dread of Israel had fallen on them. ^38

**39** He spread a cloud as a covering and a fire to light up the night. ^39

**40** They asked, and He brought quail and satisfied them with the bread of heaven. ^40

**41** He opened a rock, and water gushed out; it flowed like a river in the desert. ^41

**42** For He remembered His holy promise to Abraham His servant. ^42

**43** He brought forth His people with rejoicing, His chosen with shouts of joy. ^43

**44** He gave them the lands of the nations, that they might inherit the fruit of others’ labor, ^44

**45** that they might keep His statutes and obey His laws. Hallelujah! ^45
