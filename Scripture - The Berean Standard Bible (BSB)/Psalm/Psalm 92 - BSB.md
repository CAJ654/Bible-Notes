# Psalm 92 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 91 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 93 - BSB]] →

**1** A Psalm. A song for the Sabbath day. It is good to praise the LORD, and to sing praises to Your name, O Most High, ^1

**2** to proclaim Your loving devotion in the morning and Your faithfulness at night ^2

**3** with the ten-stringed harp and the melody of the lyre. ^3

**4** For You, O LORD, have made me glad by Your deeds; I sing for joy at the works of Your hands. ^4

**5** How great are Your works, O LORD, how deep are Your thoughts! ^5

**6** A senseless man does not know, and a fool does not understand, ^6

**7** that though the wicked sprout like grass, and all evildoers flourish, they will be forever destroyed. ^7

**8** But You, O LORD, are exalted forever! ^8

**9** For surely Your enemies, O LORD, surely Your enemies will perish; all evildoers will be scattered. ^9

**10** But You have exalted my horn like that of a wild ox; with fine oil I have been anointed. ^10

**11** My eyes see the downfall of my enemies; my ears hear the wailing of my wicked foes. ^11

**12** The righteous will flourish like a palm tree, and grow like a cedar in Lebanon. ^12

**13** Planted in the house of the LORD, they will flourish in the courts of our God. ^13

**14** In old age they will still bear fruit; healthy and green they will remain, ^14

**15** to proclaim, “The LORD is upright; He is my Rock, and in Him there is no unrighteousness.” ^15
