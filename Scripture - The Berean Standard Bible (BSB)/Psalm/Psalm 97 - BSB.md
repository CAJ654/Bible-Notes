# Psalm 97 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 96 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 98 - BSB]] →

**1** The LORD reigns, let the earth rejoice; let the distant shores be glad. ^1

**2** Clouds and darkness surround Him; righteousness and justice are His throne’s foundation. ^2

**3** Fire goes before Him and consumes His foes on every side. ^3

**4** His lightning illuminates the world; the earth sees and trembles. ^4

**5** The mountains melt like wax at the presence of the LORD, before the Lord of all the earth. ^5

**6** The heavens proclaim His righteousness; all the peoples see His glory. ^6

**7** All worshipers of images are put to shame—those who boast in idols. Worship Him, all you gods! ^7

**8** Zion hears and rejoices, and the towns of Judah exult because of Your judgments, O LORD. ^8

**9** For You, O LORD, are Most High over all the earth; You are exalted far above all gods. ^9

**10** Hate evil, O you who love the LORD! He preserves the souls of His saints; He delivers them from the hand of the wicked. ^10

**11** Light shines on the righteous, gladness on the upright in heart. ^11

**12** Rejoice in the LORD, you righteous ones, and praise His holy name. ^12
