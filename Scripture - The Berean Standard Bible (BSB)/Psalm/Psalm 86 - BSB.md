# Psalm 86 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 85 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 87 - BSB]] →

**1** A prayer of David. Incline Your ear, O LORD, and answer me, for I am poor and needy. ^1

**2** Preserve my soul, for I am godly. You are my God; save Your servant who trusts in You. ^2

**3** Be merciful to me, O Lord, for I call to You all day long. ^3

**4** Bring joy to Your servant, for to You, O Lord, I lift up my soul. ^4

**5** For You, O Lord, are kind and forgiving, rich in loving devotion to all who call on You. ^5

**6** Hear my prayer, O LORD, and attend to my plea for mercy. ^6

**7** In the day of my distress I call on You, because You answer me. ^7

**8** O Lord, there is none like You among the gods, nor any works like Yours. ^8

**9** All the nations You have made will come and bow before You, O Lord, and they will glorify Your name. ^9

**10** For You are great and perform wonders; You alone are God. ^10

**11** Teach me Your way, O LORD, that I may walk in Your truth. Give me an undivided heart, that I may fear Your name. ^11

**12** I will praise You, O Lord my God, with all my heart; I will glorify Your name forever. ^12

**13** For great is Your loving devotion to me; You have delivered me from the depths of Sheol. ^13

**14** The arrogant rise against me, O God; a band of ruthless men seeks my life; they have no regard for You. ^14

**15** But You, O Lord, are a compassionate and gracious God, slow to anger, abounding in loving devotion and faithfulness. ^15

**16** Turn to me and have mercy; grant Your strength to Your servant; save the son of Your maidservant. ^16

**17** Show me a sign of Your goodness, that my enemies may see and be ashamed; for You, O LORD, have helped me and comforted me. ^17
