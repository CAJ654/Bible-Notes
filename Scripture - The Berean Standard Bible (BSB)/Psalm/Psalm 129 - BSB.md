# Psalm 129 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 128 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 130 - BSB]] →

**1** A song of ascents. Many a time they have persecuted me from my youth—let Israel now declare— ^1

**2** many a time they have persecuted me from my youth, but they have not prevailed against me. ^2

**3** The plowmen plowed over my back; they made their furrows long. ^3

**4** The LORD is righteous; He has cut me from the cords of the wicked. ^4

**5** May all who hate Zion be turned back in shame. ^5

**6** May they be like grass on the rooftops, which withers before it can grow, ^6

**7** unable to fill the hands of the reaper, or the arms of the binder of sheaves. ^7

**8** May none who pass by say to them, “The blessing of the LORD be on you; we bless you in the name of the LORD.” ^8
