# Psalm 87 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 86 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 88 - BSB]] →

**1** A Psalm of the sons of Korah. A song. He has founded His city on the holy mountains. ^1

**2** The LORD loves the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob. ^2

**3** Glorious things are ascribed to you, O city of God. Selah ^3

**4** “I will mention Rahab and Babylon among those who know Me—along with Philistia, Tyre, and Cush—when I say, ‘This one was born in Zion.’” ^4

**5** And it will be said of Zion: “This one and that one were born in her, and the Most High Himself will establish her.” ^5

**6** The LORD will record in the register of the peoples: “This one was born in Zion.” Selah ^6

**7** Singers and pipers will proclaim, “All my springs of joy are in You.” ^7
