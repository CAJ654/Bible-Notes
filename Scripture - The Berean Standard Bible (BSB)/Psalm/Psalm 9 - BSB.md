# Psalm 9 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 8 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 10 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Death of the Son.” A Psalm of David. I will give thanks to the LORD with all my heart; I will recount all Your wonders. ^1

**2** I will be glad and rejoice in You; I will sing praise to Your name, O Most High. ^2

**3** When my enemies retreat, they stumble and perish before You. ^3

**4** For You have upheld my just cause; You sit on Your throne judging righteously. ^4

**5** You have rebuked the nations; You have destroyed the wicked; You have erased their name forever and ever. ^5

**6** The enemy has come to eternal ruin, and You have uprooted their cities; the very memory of them has vanished. ^6

**7** But the LORD abides forever; He has established His throne for judgment. ^7

**8** He judges the world with justice; He governs the people with equity. ^8

**9** The LORD is a refuge for the oppressed, a stronghold in times of trouble. ^9

**10** Those who know Your name trust in You, for You, O LORD, have not forsaken those who seek You. ^10

**11** Sing praises to the LORD, who dwells in Zion; proclaim His deeds among the nations. ^11

**12** For the Avenger of bloodshed remembers; He does not ignore the cry of the afflicted. ^12

**13** Be merciful to me, O LORD; see how my enemies afflict me! Lift me up from the gates of death, ^13

**14** that I may declare all Your praises—that within the gates of Daughter Zion I may rejoice in Your salvation. ^14

**15** The nations have fallen into a pit of their making; their feet are caught in the net they have hidden. ^15

**16** The LORD is known by the justice He brings; the wicked are ensnared by the work of their hands. Higgaion Selah ^16

**17** The wicked will return to Sheol—all the nations who forget God. ^17

**18** For the needy will not always be forgotten; nor the hope of the oppressed forever dashed. ^18

**19** Rise up, O LORD, do not let man prevail; let the nations be judged in Your presence. ^19

**20** Lay terror upon them, O LORD; let the nations know they are but men. Selah ^20
