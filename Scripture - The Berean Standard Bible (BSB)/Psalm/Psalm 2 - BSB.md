# Psalm 2 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 1 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 3 - BSB]] →

**1** Why do the nations rage and the peoples plot in vain? ^1

**2** The kings of the earth take their stand and the rulers gather together, against the LORD and against His Anointed One: ^2

**3** “Let us break Their chains and cast away Their cords.” ^3

**4** The One enthroned in heaven laughs; the Lord taunts them. ^4

**5** Then He rebukes them in His anger, and terrifies them in His fury: ^5

**6** “I have installed My King on Zion, upon My holy mountain.” ^6

**7** I will proclaim the decree spoken to Me by the LORD: “You are My Son; today I have become Your Father. ^7

**8** Ask Me, and I will make the nations Your inheritance, the ends of the earth Your possession. ^8

**9** You will break them with an iron scepter; You will shatter them like pottery.” ^9

**10** Therefore be wise, O kings; be admonished, O judges of the earth. ^10

**11** Serve the LORD with fear, and rejoice with trembling. ^11

**12** Kiss the Son, lest He be angry and you perish in your rebellion, when His wrath ignites in an instant. Blessed are all who take refuge in Him. ^12
