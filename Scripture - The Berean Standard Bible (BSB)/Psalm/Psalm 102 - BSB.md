# Psalm 102 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 101 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 103 - BSB]] →

**1** A prayer of one who is afflicted, when he grows faint and pours out his lament before the LORD. Hear my prayer, O LORD; let my cry for help come before You. ^1

**2** Do not hide Your face from me in my day of distress. Incline Your ear to me; answer me quickly when I call. ^2

**3** For my days vanish like smoke, and my bones burn like glowing embers. ^3

**4** My heart is afflicted, and withered like grass; I even forget to eat my bread. ^4

**5** Through my loud groaning my skin hangs on my bones. ^5

**6** I am like a desert owl, like an owl among the ruins. ^6

**7** I lie awake; I am like a lone bird on a housetop. ^7

**8** All day long my enemies taunt me; they ridicule me and curse me. ^8

**9** For I have eaten ashes like bread and mixed my drink with tears ^9

**10** because of Your indignation and wrath, for You have picked me up and cast me aside. ^10

**11** My days are like lengthening shadows, and I wither away like grass. ^11

**12** But You, O LORD, sit enthroned forever; Your renown endures to all generations. ^12

**13** You will rise up and have compassion on Zion, for it is time to show her favor—the appointed time has come. ^13

**14** For Your servants delight in her stones and take pity on her dust. ^14

**15** So the nations will fear the name of the LORD, and all the kings of the earth will fear Your glory. ^15

**16** For the LORD will rebuild Zion; He has appeared in His glory. ^16

**17** He will turn toward the prayer of the destitute; He will not despise their prayer. ^17

**18** Let this be written for the generation to come, so that a people not yet created may praise the LORD. ^18

**19** For He looked down from the heights of His sanctuary; the LORD gazed out from heaven to earth ^19

**20** to hear a prisoner’s groaning, to release those condemned to death, ^20

**21** that they may proclaim the name of the LORD in Zion and praise Him in Jerusalem, ^21

**22** when peoples and kingdoms assemble to serve the LORD. ^22

**23** He has broken my strength on the way; He has cut short my days. ^23

**24** I say: “O my God, do not take me in the midst of my days! Your years go on through all generations. ^24

**25** In the beginning You laid the foundations of the earth, and the heavens are the work of Your hands. ^25

**26** They will perish, but You remain; they will all wear out like a garment. Like clothing You will change them, and they will be passed on. ^26

**27** But You remain the same, and Your years will never end. ^27

**28** The children of Your servants will dwell securely, and their descendants will be established before You.” ^28
