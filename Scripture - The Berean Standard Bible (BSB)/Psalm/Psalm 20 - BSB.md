# Psalm 20 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 19 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 21 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. May the LORD answer you in the day of trouble; may the name of the God of Jacob protect you. ^1

**2** May He send you help from the sanctuary and sustain you from Zion. ^2

**3** May He remember all your gifts and look favorably on your burnt offerings. Selah ^3

**4** May He give you the desires of your heart and make all your plans succeed. ^4

**5** May we shout for joy at your victory and raise a banner in the name of our God. May the LORD grant all your petitions. ^5

**6** Now I know that the LORD saves His anointed; He answers him from His holy heaven with the saving power of His right hand. ^6

**7** Some trust in chariots and others in horses, but we trust in the name of the LORD our God. ^7

**8** They collapse and fall, but we rise up and stand firm. ^8

**9** O LORD, save the king. Answer us on the day we call. ^9
