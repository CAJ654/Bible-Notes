# Psalm 57 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 56 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 58 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A Miktam of David, when he fled from Saul into the cave. Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy, for in You my soul takes refuge. In the shadow of Your wings I will take shelter until the danger has passed. ^1

**2** I cry out to God Most High, to God who fulfills His purpose for me. ^2

**3** He reaches down from heaven and saves me; He rebukes those who trample me. Selah God sends forth His loving devotion and His truth. ^3

**4** My soul is among the lions; I lie down with ravenous beasts—with men whose teeth are spears and arrows, whose tongues are sharp swords. ^4

**5** Be exalted, O God, above the heavens; may Your glory cover all the earth. ^5

**6** They spread a net for my feet; my soul was despondent. They dug a pit before me, but they themselves have fallen into it! Selah ^6

**7** My heart is steadfast, O God, my heart is steadfast. I will sing and make music. ^7

**8** Awake, my glory! Awake, O harp and lyre! I will awaken the dawn. ^8

**9** I will praise You, O Lord, among the nations; I will sing Your praises among the peoples. ^9

**10** For Your loving devotion reaches to the heavens, and Your faithfulness to the clouds. ^10

**11** Be exalted, O God, above the heavens; may Your glory cover all the earth. ^11
