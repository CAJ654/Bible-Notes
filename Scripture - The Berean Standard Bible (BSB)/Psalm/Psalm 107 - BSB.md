# Psalm 107 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 106 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 108 - BSB]] →

**1** Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever. ^1

**2** Let the redeemed of the LORD say so, whom He has redeemed from the hand of the enemy ^2

**3** and gathered from the lands, from east and west, from north and south. ^3

**4** Some wandered in desert wastelands, finding no path to a city in which to dwell. ^4

**5** They were hungry and thirsty; their soul fainted within them. ^5

**6** Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He delivered them from their distress. ^6

**7** He led them on a straight path to reach a city where they could live. ^7

**8** Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men. ^8

**9** For He satisfies the thirsty and fills the hungry with good things. ^9

**10** Some sat in darkness and in the shadow of death, prisoners in affliction and chains, ^10

**11** because they rebelled against the words of God and despised the counsel of the Most High. ^11

**12** He humbled their hearts with hard labor; they stumbled, and there was no one to help. ^12

**13** Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He saved them from their distress. ^13

**14** He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death and broke away their chains. ^14

**15** Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men. ^15

**16** For He has broken down the gates of bronze and cut through the bars of iron. ^16

**17** Fools, in their rebellious ways, and through their iniquities, suffered affliction. ^17

**18** They loathed all food and drew near to the gates of death. ^18

**19** Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He saved them from their distress. ^19

**20** He sent forth His word and healed them; He rescued them from the Pit. ^20

**21** Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men. ^21

**22** Let them offer sacrifices of thanksgiving and declare His works with rejoicing. ^22

**23** Others went out to sea in ships, conducting trade on the mighty waters. ^23

**24** They saw the works of the LORD, and His wonders in the deep. ^24

**25** For He spoke and raised a tempest that lifted the waves of the sea. ^25

**26** They mounted up to the heavens, then sunk to the depths; their courage melted in their anguish. ^26

**27** They reeled and staggered like drunkards, and all their skill was useless. ^27

**28** Then they cried out to the LORD in their trouble, and He brought them out of their distress. ^28

**29** He calmed the storm to a whisper, and the waves of the sea were hushed. ^29

**30** They rejoiced in the silence, and He guided them to the harbor they desired. ^30

**31** Let them give thanks to the LORD for His loving devotion and His wonders to the sons of men. ^31

**32** Let them exalt Him in the assembly of the people and praise Him in the council of the elders. ^32

**33** He turns rivers into deserts, springs of water into thirsty ground, ^33

**34** and fruitful land into fields of salt, because of the wickedness of its dwellers. ^34

**35** He turns a desert into pools of water and a dry land into flowing springs. ^35

**36** He causes the hungry to settle there, that they may establish a city in which to dwell. ^36

**37** They sow fields and plant vineyards that yield a fruitful harvest. ^37

**38** He blesses them, and they multiply greatly; He does not let their herds diminish. ^38

**39** When they are decreased and humbled by oppression, evil, and sorrow, ^39

**40** He pours out contempt on the nobles and makes them wander in a trackless wasteland. ^40

**41** But He lifts the needy from affliction and increases their families like flocks. ^41

**42** The upright see and rejoice, and all iniquity shuts its mouth. ^42

**43** Let him who is wise pay heed to these things and consider the loving devotion of the LORD. ^43
