# Psalm 15 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 14 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 16 - BSB]] →

**1** A Psalm of David. O LORD, who may abide in Your tent? Who may dwell on Your holy mountain? ^1

**2** He who walks with integrity and practices righteousness, who speaks the truth from his heart, ^2

**3** who has no slander on his tongue, who does no harm to his neighbor, who casts no scorn on his friend, ^3

**4** who despises the vile but honors those who fear the LORD, who does not revise a costly oath, ^4

**5** who lends his money without interest and refuses a bribe against the innocent. He who does these things will never be shaken. ^5
