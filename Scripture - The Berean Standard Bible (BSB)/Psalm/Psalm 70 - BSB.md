# Psalm 70 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 69 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 71 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. Of David. To bring remembrance. Make haste, O God, to deliver me! Hurry, O LORD, to help me! ^1

**2** May those who seek my life be ashamed and confounded; may those who wish me harm be repelled and humiliated. ^2

**3** May those who say, “Aha, aha!” retreat because of their shame. ^3

**4** May all who seek You rejoice and be glad in You; may those who love Your salvation always say, “Let God be magnified!” ^4

**5** But I am poor and needy; hurry to me, O God. You are my help and my deliverer; O LORD, do not delay. ^5
