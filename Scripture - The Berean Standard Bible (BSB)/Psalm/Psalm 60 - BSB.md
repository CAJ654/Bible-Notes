# Psalm 60 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 59 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 61 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Lily of the Covenant.” A Miktam of David for instruction. When he fought Aram-naharaim and Aram-zobah, and Joab returned and struck down 12,000 Edomites in the Valley of Salt. You have rejected us, O God; You have broken us; You have been angry; restore us! ^1

**2** You have shaken the land and torn it open. Heal its fractures, for it is quaking. ^2

**3** You have shown Your people hardship; we are staggered from the wine You made us drink. ^3

**4** You have raised a banner for those who fear You, that they may flee the bow. Selah ^4

**5** Respond and save us with Your right hand, that Your beloved may be delivered. ^5

**6** God has spoken from His sanctuary: “I will triumph! I will parcel out Shechem and apportion the Valley of Succoth. ^6

**7** Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine; Ephraim is My helmet, Judah is My scepter. ^7

**8** Moab is My washbasin; upon Edom I toss My sandal; over Philistia I shout in triumph.” ^8

**9** Who will bring me to the fortified city? Who will lead me to Edom? ^9

**10** Have You not rejected us, O God? Will You no longer march out, O God, with our armies? ^10

**11** Give us aid against the enemy, for the help of man is worthless. ^11

**12** With God we will perform with valor, and He will trample our enemies. ^12
