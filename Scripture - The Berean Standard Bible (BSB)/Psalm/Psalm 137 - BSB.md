# Psalm 137 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 136 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 138 - BSB]] →

**1** By the rivers of Babylon we sat and wept when we remembered Zion. ^1

**2** There on the willows we hung our harps, ^2

**3** for there our captors requested a song; our tormentors demanded songs of joy: “Sing us a song of Zion.” ^3

**4** How can we sing a song of the LORD in a foreign land? ^4

**5** If I forget you, O Jerusalem, may my right hand cease to function. ^5

**6** May my tongue cling to the roof of my mouth if I do not remember you, if I do not exalt Jerusalem as my greatest joy! ^6

**7** Remember, O LORD, the sons of Edom on the day Jerusalem fell: “Destroy it,” they said, “tear it down to its foundations!” ^7

**8** O Daughter of Babylon, doomed to destruction, blessed is he who repays you as you have done to us. ^8

**9** Blessed is he who seizes your infants and dashes them against the rocks. ^9
