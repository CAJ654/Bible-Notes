# Psalm 133 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 132 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 134 - BSB]] →

**1** A song of ascents. Of David. Behold, how good and pleasant it is when brothers live together in harmony! ^1

**2** It is like fine oil on the head, running down on the beard, running down Aaron’s beard over the collar of his robes. ^2

**3** It is like the dew of Hermon falling on the mountains of Zion. For there the LORD has bestowed the blessing of life forevermore. ^3
