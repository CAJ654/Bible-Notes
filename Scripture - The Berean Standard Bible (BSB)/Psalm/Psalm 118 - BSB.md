# Psalm 118 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 117 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 119 - BSB]] →

**1** Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever. ^1

**2** Let Israel say, “His loving devotion endures forever.” ^2

**3** Let the house of Aaron say, “His loving devotion endures forever.” ^3

**4** Let those who fear the LORD say, “His loving devotion endures forever.” ^4

**5** In my distress I called to the LORD, and He answered and set me free. ^5

**6** The LORD is on my side; I will not be afraid. What can man do to me? ^6

**7** The LORD is on my side; He is my helper. Therefore I will look in triumph on those who hate me. ^7

**8** It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to trust in man. ^8

**9** It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to trust in princes. ^9

**10** All the nations surrounded me, but in the name of the LORD I cut them off. ^10

**11** They surrounded me on every side, but in the name of the LORD I cut them off. ^11

**12** They swarmed around me like bees, but they were extinguished like burning thorns; in the name of the LORD I cut them off. ^12

**13** I was pushed so hard I was falling, but the LORD helped me. ^13

**14** The LORD is my strength and my song, and He has become my salvation. ^14

**15** Shouts of joy and salvation resound in the tents of the righteous: “The right hand of the LORD performs with valor! ^15

**16** The right hand of the LORD is exalted! The right hand of the LORD performs with valor!” ^16

**17** I will not die, but I will live and proclaim what the LORD has done. ^17

**18** The LORD disciplined me severely, but He has not given me over to death. ^18

**19** Open to me the gates of righteousness, that I may enter and give thanks to the LORD. ^19

**20** This is the gate of the LORD; the righteous shall enter through it. ^20

**21** I will give You thanks, for You have answered me, and You have become my salvation. ^21

**22** The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone. ^22

**23** This is from the LORD, and it is marvelous in our eyes. ^23

**24** This is the day that the LORD has made; we will rejoice and be glad in it. ^24

**25** O LORD, save us, we pray. We beseech You, O LORD, cause us to prosper! ^25

**26** Blessed is he who comes in the name of the LORD. From the house of the LORD we bless you. ^26

**27** The LORD is God; He has made His light to shine upon us. Bind the festal sacrifice with cords to the horns of the altar. ^27

**28** You are my God, and I will give You thanks. You are my God, and I will exalt You. ^28

**29** Give thanks to the LORD, for He is good; His loving devotion endures forever. ^29
