# Psalm 99 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 98 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 100 - BSB]] →

**1** The LORD reigns; let the nations tremble! He is enthroned above the cherubim; let the earth quake! ^1

**2** Great is the LORD in Zion; He is exalted above all the peoples. ^2

**3** Let them praise Your great and awesome name—He is holy! ^3

**4** The mighty King loves justice. You have established equity; You have exercised justice and righteousness in Jacob. ^4

**5** Exalt the LORD our God, and worship at His footstool; He is holy! ^5

**6** Moses and Aaron were among His priests; Samuel was among those who called on His name. They called to the LORD and He answered. ^6

**7** He spoke to them from the pillar of cloud; they kept His decrees and the statutes He gave them. ^7

**8** O LORD our God, You answered them. You were a forgiving God to them, yet an avenger of their misdeeds. ^8

**9** Exalt the LORD our God and worship at His holy mountain, for the LORD our God is holy. ^9
