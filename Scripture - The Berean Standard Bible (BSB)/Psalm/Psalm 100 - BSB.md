# Psalm 100 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 99 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 101 - BSB]] →

**1** A Psalm of thanksgiving. Make a joyful noise to the LORD, all the earth. ^1

**2** Serve the LORD with gladness; come into His presence with joyful songs. ^2

**3** Know that the LORD is God. It is He who made us, and we are His; we are His people, and the sheep of His pasture. ^3

**4** Enter His gates with thanksgiving and His courts with praise; give thanks to Him and bless His name. ^4

**5** For the LORD is good, and His loving devotion endures forever; His faithfulness continues to all generations. ^5
