# Psalm 94 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 93 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 95 - BSB]] →

**1** O LORD, God of vengeance, O God of vengeance, shine forth. ^1

**2** Rise up, O Judge of the earth; render a reward to the proud. ^2

**3** How long will the wicked, O LORD, how long will the wicked exult? ^3

**4** They pour out arrogant words; all workers of iniquity boast. ^4

**5** They crush Your people, O LORD; they oppress Your heritage. ^5

**6** They kill the widow and the foreigner; they murder the fatherless. ^6

**7** They say, “The LORD does not see; the God of Jacob pays no heed.” ^7

**8** Take notice, O senseless among the people! O fools, when will you be wise? ^8

**9** He who affixed the ear, can He not hear? He who formed the eye, can He not see? ^9

**10** He who admonishes the nations, does He not discipline? He who teaches man, does He lack knowledge? ^10

**11** The LORD knows the thoughts of man, that they are futile. ^11

**12** Blessed is the man You discipline, O LORD, and teach from Your law, ^12

**13** to grant him relief from days of trouble, until a pit is dug for the wicked. ^13

**14** For the LORD will not forsake His people; He will never abandon His heritage. ^14

**15** Surely judgment will again be righteous, and all the upright in heart will follow it. ^15

**16** Who will rise up for me against the wicked? Who will stand for me against the workers of iniquity? ^16

**17** Unless the LORD had been my helper, I would soon have dwelt in the abode of silence. ^17

**18** If I say, “My foot is slipping,” Your loving devotion, O LORD, supports me. ^18

**19** When anxiety overwhelms me, Your consolation delights my soul. ^19

**20** Can a corrupt throne be Your ally—one devising mischief by decree? ^20

**21** They band together against the righteous and condemn the innocent to death. ^21

**22** But the LORD has been my stronghold, and my God is my rock of refuge. ^22

**23** He will bring upon them their own iniquity and destroy them for their wickedness. The LORD our God will destroy them. ^23
