# Psalm 138 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 137 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 139 - BSB]] →

**1** Of David. I give You thanks with all my heart; before the gods I sing Your praises. ^1

**2** I bow down toward Your holy temple and give thanks to Your name for Your loving devotion and Your faithfulness; You have exalted Your name and Your word above all else. ^2

**3** On the day I called, You answered me; You emboldened me and strengthened my soul. ^3

**4** All the kings of the earth will give You thanks, O LORD, when they hear the words of Your mouth. ^4

**5** They will sing of the ways of the LORD, for the glory of the LORD is great. ^5

**6** Though the LORD is on high, He attends to the lowly; but the proud He knows from afar. ^6

**7** If I walk in the midst of trouble, You preserve me from the anger of my foes; You extend Your hand, and Your right hand saves me. ^7

**8** The LORD will fulfill His purpose for me. O LORD, Your loving devotion endures forever—do not abandon the works of Your hands. ^8
