# Psalm 39 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 38 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 40 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. For Jeduthun. A Psalm of David. I said, “I will watch my ways so that I will not sin with my tongue; I will guard my mouth with a muzzle as long as the wicked are present.” ^1

**2** I was speechless and still; I remained silent, even from speaking good, and my sorrow was stirred. ^2

**3** My heart grew hot within me; as I mused, the fire burned. Then I spoke with my tongue: ^3

**4** “Show me, O LORD, my end and the measure of my days. Let me know how fleeting my life is. ^4

**5** You, indeed, have made my days as handbreadths, and my lifetime as nothing before You. Truly each man at his best exists as but a breath. Selah ^5

**6** Surely every man goes about like a phantom; surely he bustles in vain; he heaps up riches not knowing who will haul them away. ^6

**7** And now, O Lord, for what do I wait? My hope is in You. ^7

**8** Deliver me from all my transgressions; do not make me the reproach of fools. ^8

**9** I have become mute; I do not open my mouth because of what You have done. ^9

**10** Remove Your scourge from me; I am perishing by the force of Your hand. ^10

**11** You discipline and correct a man for his iniquity, consuming like a moth what he holds dear; surely each man is but a vapor. Selah ^11

**12** Hear my prayer, O LORD, and give ear to my cry for help; do not be deaf to my weeping. For I am a foreigner dwelling with You, a stranger like all my fathers. ^12

**13** Turn Your gaze away from me, that I may again be cheered before I depart and am no more.” ^13
