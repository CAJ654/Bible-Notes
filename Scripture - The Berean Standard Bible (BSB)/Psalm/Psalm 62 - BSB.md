# Psalm 62 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 61 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 63 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. According to Jeduthun. A Psalm of David. In God alone my soul finds rest; my salvation comes from Him. ^1

**2** He alone is my rock and my salvation. He is my fortress; I will never be shaken. ^2

**3** How long will you threaten a man? Will all of you throw him down like a leaning wall or a tottering fence? ^3

**4** They fully intend to cast him down from his lofty perch; they delight in lies; with their mouths they bless, but inwardly they curse. Selah ^4

**5** Rest in God alone, O my soul, for my hope comes from Him. ^5

**6** He alone is my rock and my salvation; He is my fortress; I will not be shaken. ^6

**7** My salvation and my honor rest on God, my strong rock; my refuge is in God. ^7

**8** Trust in Him at all times, O people; pour out your hearts before Him. God is our refuge. Selah ^8

**9** Lowborn men are but a vapor; the exalted are but a lie. Weighed on the scale, they go up; together they are but a vapor. ^9

**10** Place no trust in extortion or false hope in stolen goods. If your riches increase, do not set your heart upon them. ^10

**11** God has spoken once; I have heard this twice: that power belongs to God, ^11

**12** and loving devotion to You, O Lord. For You will repay each man according to his deeds. ^12
