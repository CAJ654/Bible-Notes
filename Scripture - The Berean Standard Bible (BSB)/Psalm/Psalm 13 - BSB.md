# Psalm 13 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 12 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 14 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. How long, O LORD? Will You forget me forever? How long will You hide Your face from me? ^1

**2** How long must I wrestle in my soul, with sorrow in my heart each day? How long will my enemy dominate me? ^2

**3** Consider me and respond, O LORD my God. Give light to my eyes, lest I sleep in death, ^3

**4** lest my enemy say, “I have overcome him,” and my foes rejoice when I fall. ^4

**5** But I have trusted in Your loving devotion; my heart will rejoice in Your salvation. ^5

**6** I will sing to the LORD, for He has been good to me. ^6
