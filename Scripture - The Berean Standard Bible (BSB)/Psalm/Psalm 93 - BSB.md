# Psalm 93 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 92 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 94 - BSB]] →

**1** The LORD reigns! He is robed in majesty; the LORD has clothed and armed Himself with strength. The world indeed is firmly established; it cannot be moved. ^1

**2** Your throne was established long ago; You are from all eternity. ^2

**3** The floodwaters have risen, O LORD; the rivers have raised their voice; the seas lift up their pounding waves. ^3

**4** Above the roar of many waters—the mighty breakers of the sea—the LORD on high is majestic. ^4

**5** Your testimonies are fully confirmed; holiness adorns Your house, O LORD, for all the days to come. ^5
