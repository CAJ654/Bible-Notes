# Psalm 103 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 102 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 104 - BSB]] →

**1** Of David. Bless the LORD, O my soul; all that is within me, bless His holy name. ^1

**2** Bless the LORD, O my soul, and do not forget all His kind deeds— ^2

**3** He who forgives all your iniquities and heals all your diseases, ^3

**4** who redeems your life from the Pit and crowns you with loving devotion and compassion, ^4

**5** who satisfies you with good things, so that your youth is renewed like the eagle’s. ^5

**6** The LORD executes righteousness and justice for all the oppressed. ^6

**7** He made known His ways to Moses, His deeds to the people of Israel. ^7

**8** The LORD is compassionate and gracious, slow to anger, abounding in loving devotion. ^8

**9** He will not always accuse us, nor harbor His anger forever. ^9

**10** He has not dealt with us according to our sins or repaid us according to our iniquities. ^10

**11** For as high as the heavens are above the earth, so great is His loving devotion for those who fear Him. ^11

**12** As far as the east is from the west, so far has He removed our transgressions from us. ^12

**13** As a father has compassion on his children, so the LORD has compassion on those who fear Him. ^13

**14** For He knows our frame; He is mindful that we are dust. ^14

**15** As for man, his days are like grass—he blooms like a flower of the field; ^15

**16** when the wind passes over, it vanishes, and its place remembers it no more. ^16

**17** But from everlasting to everlasting the loving devotion of the LORD extends to those who fear Him, and His righteousness to their children’s children— ^17

**18** to those who keep His covenant and remember to obey His precepts. ^18

**19** The LORD has established His throne in heaven, and His kingdom rules over all. ^19

**20** Bless the LORD, all His angels mighty in strength who carry out His word, who hearken to the voice of His command. ^20

**21** Bless the LORD, all His hosts, you servants who do His will. ^21

**22** Bless the LORD, all His works in all places of His dominion. Bless the LORD, O my soul! ^22
