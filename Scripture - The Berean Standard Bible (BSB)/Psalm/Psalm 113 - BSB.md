# Psalm 113 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 112 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 114 - BSB]] →

**1** Hallelujah! Give praise, O servants of the LORD; praise the name of the LORD. ^1

**2** Blessed be the name of the LORD both now and forevermore. ^2

**3** From where the sun rises to where it sets, the name of the LORD is praised. ^3

**4** The LORD is exalted over all the nations, His glory above the heavens. ^4

**5** Who is like the LORD our God, the One enthroned on high? ^5

**6** He humbles Himself to behold the heavens and the earth. ^6

**7** He raises the poor from the dust and lifts the needy from the dump ^7

**8** to seat them with nobles, with the princes of His people. ^8

**9** He settles the barren woman in her home as a joyful mother to her children. Hallelujah! ^9
