# Psalm 98 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 97 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 99 - BSB]] →

**1** A Psalm. Sing to the LORD a new song, for He has done wonders; His right hand and holy arm have gained Him the victory. ^1

**2** The LORD has proclaimed His salvation and revealed His righteousness to the nations. ^2

**3** He has remembered His love and faithfulness to the house of Israel; all the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God. ^3

**4** Make a joyful noise to the LORD, all the earth; break forth—let your cry ring out, and sing praises! ^4

**5** Sing praises to the LORD with the lyre, in melodious song with the harp. ^5

**6** With trumpets and the blast of the ram’s horn shout for joy before the LORD, the King. ^6

**7** Let the sea resound, and all that fills it, the world, and all who dwell in it. ^7

**8** Let the rivers clap their hands, let the mountains sing together for joy ^8

**9** before the LORD, for He comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world with righteousness and the peoples with equity. ^9
