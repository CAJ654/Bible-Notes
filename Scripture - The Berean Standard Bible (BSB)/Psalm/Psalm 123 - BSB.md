# Psalm 123 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 122 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 124 - BSB]] →

**1** A song of ascents. I lift up my eyes to You, the One enthroned in heaven. ^1

**2** As the eyes of servants look to the hand of their master, as the eyes of a maidservant look to the hand of her mistress, so our eyes are on the LORD our God until He shows us mercy. ^2

**3** Have mercy on us, O LORD, have mercy, for we have endured much contempt. ^3

**4** We have endured much scorn from the arrogant, much contempt from the proud. ^4
