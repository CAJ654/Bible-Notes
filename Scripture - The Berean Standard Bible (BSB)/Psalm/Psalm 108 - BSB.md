# Psalm 108 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 107 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 109 - BSB]] →

**1** A song. A Psalm of David. My heart is steadfast, O God; I will sing and make music with all my being. ^1

**2** Awake, O harp and lyre! I will awaken the dawn. ^2

**3** I will praise You, O LORD, among the nations; I will sing Your praises among the peoples. ^3

**4** For Your loving devotion extends beyond the heavens, and Your faithfulness reaches to the clouds. ^4

**5** Be exalted, O God, above the heavens; may Your glory cover all the earth. ^5

**6** Respond and save us with Your right hand, that Your beloved may be delivered. ^6

**7** God has spoken from His sanctuary: “I will triumph! I will parcel out Shechem and apportion the Valley of Succoth. ^7

**8** Gilead is Mine, and Manasseh is Mine; Ephraim is My helmet, Judah is My scepter. ^8

**9** Moab is My washbasin; upon Edom I toss My sandal; over Philistia I shout in triumph.” ^9

**10** Who will bring me to the fortified city? Who will lead me to Edom? ^10

**11** Have You not rejected us, O God? Will You no longer march out, O God, with our armies? ^11

**12** Give us aid against the enemy, for the help of man is worthless. ^12

**13** With God we will perform with valor, and He will trample our enemies. ^13
