# Psalm 10 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 9 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 11 - BSB]] →

**1** Why, O LORD, do You stand far off? Why do You hide in times of trouble? ^1

**2** In pride the wicked pursue the needy; let them be caught in the schemes they devise. ^2

**3** For the wicked man boasts in the cravings of his heart; he blesses the greedy and reviles the LORD. ^3

**4** In his pride the wicked man does not seek Him; in all his schemes there is no God. ^4

**5** He is secure in his ways at all times; Your lofty judgments are far from him; he sneers at all his foes. ^5

**6** He says to himself, “I will not be moved; from age to age I am free of distress.” ^6

**7** His mouth is full of cursing, deceit, and violence; trouble and malice are under his tongue. ^7

**8** He lies in wait near the villages; in ambush he slays the innocent; his eyes watch in stealth for the helpless. ^8

**9** He lies in wait like a lion in a thicket; he lurks to seize the oppressed; he catches the lowly in his net. ^9

**10** They are crushed and beaten down; the helpless fall prey to his strength. ^10

**11** He says to himself, “God has forgotten; He hides His face and never sees.” ^11

**12** Arise, O LORD! Lift up Your hand, O God! Do not forget the helpless. ^12

**13** Why has the wicked man renounced God? He says to himself, “You will never call me to account.” ^13

**14** But You have regarded trouble and grief; You see to repay it by Your hand. The victim entrusts himself to You; You are the helper of the fatherless. ^14

**15** Break the arm of the wicked and evildoer; call him to account for his wickedness until none is left to be found. ^15

**16** The LORD is King forever and ever; the nations perish from His land. ^16

**17** You have heard, O LORD, the desire of the humble; You will strengthen their hearts. You will incline Your ear, ^17

**18** to vindicate the fatherless and oppressed, that the men of the earth may strike terror no more. ^18
