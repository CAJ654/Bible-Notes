# Psalm 12 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 11 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 13 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. According to Sheminith. A Psalm of David. Help, O LORD, for the godly are no more; the faithful have vanished from among men. ^1

**2** They lie to one another; they speak with flattering lips and a double heart. ^2

**3** May the LORD cut off all flattering lips and every boastful tongue. ^3

**4** They say, “With our tongues we will prevail. We own our lips—who can be our master?” ^4

**5** “For the cause of the oppressed and for the groaning of the needy, I will now arise,” says the LORD. “I will bring safety to him who yearns.” ^5

**6** The words of the LORD are flawless, like silver refined in a furnace, like gold purified sevenfold. ^6

**7** You, O LORD, will keep us; You will forever guard us from this generation. ^7

**8** The wicked wander freely, and vileness is exalted among men. ^8
