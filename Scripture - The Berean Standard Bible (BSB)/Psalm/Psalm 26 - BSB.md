# Psalm 26 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 25 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 27 - BSB]] →

**1** Of David. Vindicate me, O LORD! For I have walked with integrity; I have trusted in the LORD without wavering. ^1

**2** Test me, O LORD, and try me; examine my heart and mind. ^2

**3** For Your loving devotion is before my eyes, and I have walked in Your truth. ^3

**4** I do not sit with deceitful men, nor keep company with hypocrites. ^4

**5** I hate the mob of evildoers, and refuse to sit with the wicked. ^5

**6** I wash my hands in innocence that I may go about Your altar, O LORD, ^6

**7** to raise my voice in thanksgiving and declare all Your wonderful works. ^7

**8** O LORD, I love the house where You dwell, the place where Your glory resides. ^8

**9** Do not take my soul away with sinners, or my life with men of bloodshed, ^9

**10** in whose hands are wicked schemes, whose right hands are full of bribes. ^10

**11** But I will walk with integrity; redeem me and be merciful to me. ^11

**12** My feet stand on level ground; in the congregations I will bless the LORD. ^12
