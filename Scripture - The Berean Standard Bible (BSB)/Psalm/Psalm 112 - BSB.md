# Psalm 112 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 111 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 113 - BSB]] →

**1** Hallelujah! Blessed is the man who fears the LORD, who greatly delights in His commandments. ^1

**2** His descendants will be mighty in the land; the generation of the upright will be blessed. ^2

**3** Wealth and riches are in his house, and his righteousness endures forever. ^3

**4** Light dawns in the darkness for the upright—for the gracious, compassionate, and righteous. ^4

**5** It is well with the man who is generous and lends freely, whose affairs are guided by justice. ^5

**6** Surely he will never be shaken; the righteous man will be remembered forever. ^6

**7** He does not fear bad news; his heart is steadfast, trusting in the LORD. ^7

**8** His heart is assured; he does not fear, until he looks in triumph on his foes. ^8

**9** He has scattered abroad his gifts to the poor; his righteousness endures forever; his horn will be lifted high in honor. ^9

**10** The wicked man will see and be grieved; he will gnash his teeth and waste away; the desires of the wicked will perish. ^10
