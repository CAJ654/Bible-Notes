# Psalm 46 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 45 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 47 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. Of the sons of Korah. According to Alamoth. A song. God is our refuge and strength, an ever-present help in times of trouble. ^1

**2** Therefore we will not fear, though the earth is transformed and the mountains are toppled into the depths of the seas, ^2

**3** though their waters roar and foam and the mountains quake in the surge. Selah ^3

**4** There is a river whose streams delight the city of God, the holy place where the Most High dwells. ^4

**5** God is within her; she will not be moved. God will help her when morning dawns. ^5

**6** Nations rage, kingdoms crumble; the earth melts when He lifts His voice. ^6

**7** The LORD of Hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress. Selah ^7

**8** Come, see the works of the LORD, who brings devastation upon the earth. ^8

**9** He makes wars to cease throughout the earth; He breaks the bow and shatters the spear; He burns the shields in the fire. ^9

**10** “Be still and know that I am God; I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted over the earth.” ^10

**11** The LORD of Hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our fortress. Selah ^11
