# Psalm 149 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 148 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 150 - BSB]] →

**1** Hallelujah! Sing to the LORD a new song—His praise in the assembly of the godly. ^1

**2** Let Israel rejoice in their Maker; let the children of Zion rejoice in their King. ^2

**3** Let them praise His name with dancing, and make music to Him with tambourine and harp. ^3

**4** For the LORD takes pleasure in His people; He adorns the afflicted with salvation. ^4

**5** Let the saints exult in glory; let them shout for joy upon their beds. ^5

**6** May the high praises of God be in their mouths, and a double-edged sword in their hands, ^6

**7** to inflict vengeance on the nations and punishment on the peoples, ^7

**8** to bind their kings with chains and their nobles with shackles of iron, ^8

**9** to execute the judgment written against them. This honor is for all His saints. Hallelujah! ^9
