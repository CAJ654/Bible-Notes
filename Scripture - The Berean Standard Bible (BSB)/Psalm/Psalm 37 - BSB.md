# Psalm 37 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 36 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 38 - BSB]] →

**1** Of David. Do not fret over those who do evil; do not envy those who do wrong. ^1

**2** For they wither quickly like grass and wilt like tender plants. ^2

**3** Trust in the LORD and do good; dwell in the land and cultivate faithfulness. ^3

**4** Delight yourself in the LORD, and He will give you the desires of your heart. ^4

**5** Commit your way to the LORD; trust in Him, and He will do it. ^5

**6** He will bring forth your righteousness like the dawn, your justice like the noonday sun. ^6

**7** Be still before the LORD and wait patiently for Him; do not fret when men prosper in their ways, when they carry out wicked schemes. ^7

**8** Refrain from anger and abandon wrath; do not fret—it can only bring harm. ^8

**9** For the evildoers will be cut off, but those who hope in the LORD will inherit the land. ^9

**10** Yet a little while, and the wicked will be no more; though you look for them, they will not be found. ^10

**11** But the meek will inherit the land and delight in abundant prosperity. ^11

**12** The wicked scheme against the righteous and gnash their teeth at them, ^12

**13** but the Lord laughs, seeing that their day is coming. ^13

**14** The wicked have drawn the sword and bent the bow to bring down the poor and needy, to slay those whose ways are upright. ^14

**15** But their swords will pierce their own hearts, and their bows will be broken. ^15

**16** Better is the little of the righteous than the abundance of many who are wicked. ^16

**17** For the arms of the wicked will be broken, but the LORD upholds the righteous. ^17

**18** The LORD knows the days of the blameless, and their inheritance will last forever. ^18

**19** In the time of evil they will not be ashamed, and in the days of famine they will be satisfied. ^19

**20** But the wicked and enemies of the LORD will perish like the glory of the fields. They will vanish; like smoke they will fade away. ^20

**21** The wicked borrow and do not repay, but the righteous are gracious and giving. ^21

**22** Surely those He blesses will inherit the land, but the cursed will be destroyed. ^22

**23** The steps of a man are ordered by the LORD who takes delight in his journey. ^23

**24** Though he falls, he will not be overwhelmed, for the LORD is holding his hand. ^24

**25** I once was young and now am old, yet never have I seen the righteous abandoned or their children begging for bread. ^25

**26** They are ever generous and quick to lend, and their children are a blessing. ^26

**27** Turn away from evil and do good, so that you will abide forever. ^27

**28** For the LORD loves justice and will not forsake His saints. They are preserved forever, but the offspring of the wicked will be cut off. ^28

**29** The righteous will inherit the land and dwell in it forever. ^29

**30** The mouth of the righteous man utters wisdom, and his tongue speaks justice. ^30

**31** The law of his God is in his heart; his steps do not falter. ^31

**32** Though the wicked lie in wait for the righteous, and seek to slay them, ^32

**33** the LORD will not leave them in their power or let them be condemned under judgment. ^33

**34** Wait for the LORD and keep His way, and He will raise you up to inherit the land. When the wicked are cut off, you will see it. ^34

**35** I have seen a wicked, ruthless man flourishing like a well-rooted native tree, ^35

**36** yet he passed away and was no more; though I searched, he could not be found. ^36

**37** Consider the blameless and observe the upright, for posterity awaits the man of peace. ^37

**38** But the transgressors will all be destroyed; the future of the wicked will be cut off. ^38

**39** The salvation of the righteous is from the LORD; He is their stronghold in time of trouble. ^39

**40** The LORD helps and delivers them; He rescues and saves them from the wicked, because they take refuge in Him. ^40
