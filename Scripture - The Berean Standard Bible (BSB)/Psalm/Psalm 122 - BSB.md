# Psalm 122 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 121 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 123 - BSB]] →

**1** A song of ascents. Of David. I was glad when they said to me, “Let us go to the house of the LORD.” ^1

**2** Our feet are standing in your gates, O Jerusalem. ^2

**3** Jerusalem is built up as a city united together, ^3

**4** where the tribes go up, the tribes of the LORD, as a testimony for Israel, to give thanks to the name of the LORD. ^4

**5** For there the thrones of judgment stand, the thrones of the house of David. ^5

**6** Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: “May those who love you prosper. ^6

**7** May there be peace within your walls, and prosperity inside your fortresses.” ^7

**8** For the sake of my brothers and friends, I will say, “Peace be within you.” ^8

**9** For the sake of the house of the LORD our God, I will seek your prosperity. ^9
