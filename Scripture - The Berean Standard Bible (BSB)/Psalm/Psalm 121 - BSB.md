# Psalm 121 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 120 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 122 - BSB]] →

**1** A song of ascents. I lift up my eyes to the hills. From where does my help come? ^1

**2** My help comes from the LORD, the Maker of heaven and earth. ^2

**3** He will not allow your foot to slip; your Protector will not slumber. ^3

**4** Behold, the Protector of Israel will neither slumber nor sleep. ^4

**5** The LORD is your keeper; the LORD is the shade on your right hand. ^5

**6** The sun will not strike you by day, nor the moon by night. ^6

**7** The LORD will guard you from all evil; He will preserve your soul. ^7

**8** The LORD will watch over your coming and going, both now and forevermore. ^8
