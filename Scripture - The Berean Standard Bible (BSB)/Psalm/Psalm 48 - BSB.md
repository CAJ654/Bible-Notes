# Psalm 48 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 47 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 49 - BSB]] →

**1** A song. A Psalm of the sons of Korah. Great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised in the city of our God, His holy mountain. ^1

**2** Beautiful in loftiness, the joy of all the earth, like the peaks of Zaphon is Mount Zion, the city of the great King. ^2

**3** God is in her citadels; He has shown Himself to be a fortress. ^3

**4** For behold, the kings assembled; they all advanced together. ^4

**5** They saw and were astounded; they fled in terror. ^5

**6** Trembling seized them there, anguish like a woman in labor. ^6

**7** With a wind from the east You wrecked the ships of Tarshish. ^7

**8** As we have heard, so we have seen in the city of the LORD of Hosts, in the city of our God: God will establish her forever. Selah ^8

**9** Within Your temple, O God, we contemplate Your loving devotion. ^9

**10** Your name, O God, like Your praise, reaches to the ends of the earth; Your right hand is full of righteousness. ^10

**11** Mount Zion is glad, the daughters of Judah rejoice, on account of Your judgments. ^11

**12** March around Zion, encircle her, count her towers, ^12

**13** consider her ramparts, tour her citadels, that you may tell the next generation. ^13

**14** For this God is our God forever and ever; He will be our guide even till death. ^14
