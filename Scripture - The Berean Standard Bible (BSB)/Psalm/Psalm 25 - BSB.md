# Psalm 25 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 24 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 26 - BSB]] →

**1** Of David. To You, O LORD, I lift up my soul; ^1

**2** in You, my God, I trust. Do not let me be put to shame; do not let my enemies exult over me. ^2

**3** Surely none who wait for You will be put to shame; but those who engage in treachery without cause will be disgraced. ^3

**4** Show me Your ways, O LORD; teach me Your paths. ^4

**5** Guide me in Your truth and teach me, for You are the God of my salvation; all day long I wait for You. ^5

**6** Remember, O LORD, Your compassion and loving devotion, for they are from age to age. ^6

**7** Remember not the sins of my youth, nor my rebellious acts; remember me according to Your loving devotion, because of Your goodness, O LORD. ^7

**8** Good and upright is the LORD; therefore He shows sinners the way. ^8

**9** He guides the humble in what is right and teaches them His way. ^9

**10** All the LORD’s ways are loving and faithful to those who keep His covenant and His decrees. ^10

**11** For the sake of Your name, O LORD, forgive my iniquity, for it is great. ^11

**12** Who is the man who fears the LORD? He will instruct him in the path chosen for him. ^12

**13** His soul will dwell in prosperity, and his descendants will inherit the land. ^13

**14** The LORD confides in those who fear Him, and reveals His covenant to them. ^14

**15** My eyes are always on the LORD, for He will free my feet from the mesh. ^15

**16** Turn to me and be gracious, for I am lonely and afflicted. ^16

**17** The troubles of my heart increase; free me from my distress. ^17

**18** Consider my affliction and trouble, and take away all my sins. ^18

**19** Consider my enemies, for they are many, and they hate me with vicious hatred. ^19

**20** Guard my soul and deliver me; let me not be put to shame, for I take refuge in You. ^20

**21** May integrity and uprightness preserve me, because I wait for You. ^21

**22** Redeem Israel, O God, from all its distress. ^22
