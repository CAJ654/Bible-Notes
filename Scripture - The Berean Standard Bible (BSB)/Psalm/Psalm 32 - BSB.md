# Psalm 32 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 31 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 33 - BSB]] →

**1** Of David. A Maskil. Blessed is he whose transgressions are forgiven, whose sins are covered. ^1

**2** Blessed is the man whose iniquity the LORD does not count against him, in whose spirit there is no deceit. ^2

**3** When I kept silent, my bones became brittle from my groaning all day long. ^3

**4** For day and night Your hand was heavy upon me; my strength was drained as in the summer heat. Selah ^4

**5** Then I acknowledged my sin to You and did not hide my iniquity. I said, “I will confess my transgressions to the LORD,” and You forgave the guilt of my sin. Selah ^5

**6** Therefore let all the godly pray to You while You may be found. Surely when great waters rise, they will not come near. ^6

**7** You are my hiding place. You protect me from trouble; You surround me with songs of deliverance. Selah ^7

**8** I will instruct you and teach you the way you should go; I will give you counsel and watch over you. ^8

**9** Do not be like the horse or mule, which have no understanding; they must be controlled with bit and bridle to make them come to you. ^9

**10** Many are the sorrows of the wicked, but loving devotion surrounds him who trusts in the LORD. ^10

**11** Be glad in the LORD and rejoice, O righteous ones; shout for joy, all you upright in heart. ^11
