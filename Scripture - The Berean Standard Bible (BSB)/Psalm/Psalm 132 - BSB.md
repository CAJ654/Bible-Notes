# Psalm 132 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 131 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 133 - BSB]] →

**1** A song of ascents. O LORD, remember on behalf of David all the hardships he endured, ^1

**2** how he swore an oath to the LORD, and vowed to the Mighty One of Jacob: ^2

**3** “I will not enter my house or get into my bed, ^3

**4** I will not give sleep to my eyes or slumber to my eyelids, ^4

**5** until I find a place for the LORD, a dwelling for the Mighty One of Jacob.” ^5

**6** We heard that the ark was in Ephrathah; we found it in the fields of Jaar. ^6

**7** Let us go to His dwelling place; let us worship at His footstool. ^7

**8** Arise, O LORD, to Your resting place, You and the ark of Your strength. ^8

**9** May Your priests be clothed with righteousness, and Your saints shout for joy. ^9

**10** For the sake of Your servant David, do not reject Your anointed one. ^10

**11** The LORD swore an oath to David, a promise He will not revoke: “One of your descendants I will place on your throne. ^11

**12** If your sons keep My covenant and the testimony I will teach them, then their sons will also sit on your throne forever and ever.” ^12

**13** For the LORD has chosen Zion; He has desired it for His home: ^13

**14** “This is My resting place forever and ever; here I will dwell, for I have desired this home. ^14

**15** I will bless her with abundant provisions; I will satisfy her poor with bread. ^15

**16** I will clothe her priests with salvation, and her saints will sing out in joy. ^16

**17** There I will make a horn grow for David; I have prepared a lamp for My anointed one. ^17

**18** I will clothe his enemies with shame, but the crown upon him will gleam.” ^18
