# Psalm 42 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 41 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 43 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. A Maskil of the sons of Korah. As the deer pants for streams of water, so my soul longs after You, O God. ^1

**2** My soul thirsts for God, the living God. When shall I come and appear in God’s presence? ^2

**3** My tears have been my food both day and night, while men ask me all day long, “Where is your God?” ^3

**4** These things come to mind as I pour out my soul: how I walked with the multitude, leading the festive procession to the house of God with shouts of joy and praise. ^4

**5** Why are you downcast, O my soul? Why the unease within me? Put your hope in God, for I will yet praise Him for the salvation of His presence. ^5

**6** O my God, my soul despairs within me. Therefore I remember You from the land of Jordan and the peaks of Hermon—even from Mount Mizar. ^6

**7** Deep calls to deep in the roar of Your waterfalls; all Your breakers and waves have rolled over me. ^7

**8** The LORD decrees His loving devotion by day, and at night His song is with me as a prayer to the God of my life. ^8

**9** I say to God my Rock, “Why have You forgotten me? Why must I walk in sorrow because of the enemy’s oppression?” ^9

**10** Like the crushing of my bones, my enemies taunt me, while they say to me all day long, “Where is your God?” ^10

**11** Why are you downcast, O my soul? Why the unease within me? Put your hope in God, for I will yet praise Him, my Savior and my God. ^11
