# Psalm 101 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 100 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 102 - BSB]] →

**1** A Psalm of David. I will sing of Your loving devotion and justice; to You, O LORD, I will sing praises. ^1

**2** I will ponder the way that is blameless—when will You come to me? I will walk in my house with integrity of heart. ^2

**3** I will set no worthless thing before my eyes. I hate the work of those who fall away; it shall not cling to me. ^3

**4** A perverse heart shall depart from me; I will know nothing of evil. ^4

**5** Whoever slanders his neighbor in secret, I will put to silence; the one with haughty eyes and a proud heart, I will not endure. ^5

**6** My eyes favor the faithful of the land, that they may dwell with me; he who walks in the way of integrity shall minister to me. ^6

**7** No one who practices deceit shall dwell in my house; no one who tells lies shall stand in my presence. ^7

**8** Every morning I will remove all the wicked of the land, that I may cut off every evildoer from the city of the LORD. ^8
