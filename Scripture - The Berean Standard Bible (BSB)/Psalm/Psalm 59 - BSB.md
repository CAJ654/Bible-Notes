# Psalm 59 (BSB)

← [[Psalm 58 - BSB]] | [[Psalm 60 - BSB]] →

**1** For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A Miktam of David, when Saul sent men to watch David’s house in order to kill him. Deliver me from my enemies, O my God; protect me from those who rise against me. ^1

**2** Deliver me from workers of iniquity, and save me from men of bloodshed. ^2

**3** See how they lie in wait for me. Fierce men conspire against me for no transgression or sin of my own, O LORD. ^3

**4** For no fault of my own, they move swiftly to attack me. Arise to help me, and take notice. ^4

**5** O LORD God of Hosts, the God of Israel, rouse Yourself to punish all the nations; show no mercy to the wicked traitors. Selah ^5

**6** They return in the evening, snarling like dogs and prowling around the city. ^6

**7** See what they spew from their mouths—sharp words from their lips: “For who can hear us?” ^7

**8** But You, O LORD, laugh at them; You scoff at all the nations. ^8

**9** I will keep watch for You, O my strength, because You, O God, are my fortress. ^9

**10** My God of loving devotion will come to meet me; God will let me stare down my foes. ^10

**11** Do not kill them, or my people will forget. Scatter them by Your power, and bring them down, O Lord, our shield. ^11

**12** By the sins of their mouths and the words of their lips, let them be trapped in their pride, in the curses and lies they utter. ^12

**13** Consume them in wrath; consume them till they are no more, so it may be known to the ends of the earth that God rules over Jacob. Selah ^13

**14** They return in the evening, snarling like dogs and prowling around the city. ^14

**15** They scavenge for food, and growl if they are not satisfied. ^15

**16** But I will sing of Your strength and proclaim Your loving devotion in the morning. For You are my fortress, my refuge in times of trouble. ^16

**17** To You, O my strength, I sing praises, for You, O God, are my fortress, my God of loving devotion. ^17
