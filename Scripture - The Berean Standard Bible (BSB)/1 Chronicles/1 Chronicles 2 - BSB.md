# 1 Chronicles 2 (BSB)

← [[1 Chronicles 1 - BSB]] | [[1 Chronicles 3 - BSB]] →

**1** These were the sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun, ^1

**2** Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher. ^2

**3** The sons of Judah: Er, Onan, and Shelah. These three were born to him by Bath-shua the Canaanite. Er, Judah’s firstborn, was wicked in the sight of the LORD. So the LORD put him to death. ^3

**4** Tamar, Judah’s daughter-in-law, bore to him Perez and Zerah. Judah had five sons in all. ^4

**5** The sons of Perez: Hezron and Hamul. ^5

**6** The sons of Zerah: Zimri, Ethan, Heman, Calcol, and Dara—five in all. ^6

**7** The son of Carmi: Achar, who brought trouble upon Israel by violating the ban on devoted things. ^7

**8** The son of Ethan: Azariah. ^8

**9** The sons who were born to Hezron: Jerahmeel, Ram, and Caleb. ^9

**10** Ram was the father of Amminadab, and Amminadab was the father of Nahshon, a leader of the descendants of Judah. ^10

**11** Nahshon was the father of Salmon, and Salmon was the father of Boaz. ^11

**12** Boaz was the father of Obed, and Obed was the father of Jesse. ^12

**13** Jesse was the father of Eliab his firstborn; Abinadab was born second, Shimea third, ^13

**14** Nethanel fourth, Raddai fifth, ^14

**15** Ozem sixth, and David seventh. ^15

**16** Their sisters were Zeruiah and Abigail. And the three sons of Zeruiah were Abishai, Joab, and Asahel. ^16

**17** Abigail was the mother of Amasa, whose father was Jether the Ishmaelite. ^17

**18** Caleb son of Hezron had children by his wife Azubah and by Jerioth. These were the sons of Azubah: Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon. ^18

**19** When Azubah died, Caleb married Ephrath, who bore to him Hur. ^19

**20** Hur was the father of Uri, and Uri was the father of Bezalel. ^20

**21** Later, Hezron slept with the daughter of Machir the father of Gilead. He had married her when he was sixty years old, and she bore to him Segub. ^21

**22** Segub was the father of Jair, who had twenty-three cities in the land of Gilead. ^22

**23** But Geshur and Aram captured Havvoth-jair, along with Kenath and its sixty surrounding villages. All these were descendants of Machir the father of Gilead. ^23

**24** After Hezron died in Caleb-ephrathah, his wife Abijah bore to him Ashhur the father of Tekoa. ^24

**25** The sons of Jerahmeel the firstborn of Hezron: Ram his firstborn, Bunah, Oren, Ozem, and Ahijah. ^25

**26** Jerahmeel had another wife named Atarah, who was the mother of Onam. ^26

**27** The sons of Ram the firstborn of Jerahmeel: Maaz, Jamin, and Eker. ^27

**28** The sons of Onam: Shammai and Jada. The sons of Shammai: Nadab and Abishur. ^28

**29** Abishur’s wife was named Abihail, and she bore to him Ahban and Molid. ^29

**30** The sons of Nadab: Seled and Appaim. Seled died without children. ^30

**31** The son of Appaim: Ishi. The son of Ishi: Sheshan. The son of Sheshan: Ahlai. ^31

**32** The sons of Jada the brother of Shammai: Jether and Jonathan. Jether died without children. ^32

**33** The sons of Jonathan: Peleth and Zaza. These were the descendants of Jerahmeel. ^33

**34** Sheshan had no sons, but only daughters. He also had an Egyptian servant named Jarha. ^34

**35** Sheshan gave his daughter in marriage to his servant Jarha, and she bore to him Attai. ^35

**36** Attai was the father of Nathan, Nathan was the father of Zabad, ^36

**37** Zabad was the father of Ephlal, Ephlal was the father of Obed, ^37

**38** Obed was the father of Jehu, Jehu was the father of Azariah, ^38

**39** Azariah was the father of Helez, Helez was the father of Elasah, ^39

**40** Elasah was the father of Sismai, Sismai was the father of Shallum, ^40

**41** Shallum was the father of Jekamiah, and Jekamiah was the father of Elishama. ^41

**42** The sons of Caleb the brother of Jerahmeel: Mesha his firstborn, who was the father of Ziph, and Mareshah his second son, who was the father of Hebron. ^42

**43** The sons of Hebron: Korah, Tappuah, Rekem, and Shema. ^43

**44** Shema was the father of Raham the father of Jorkeam, and Rekem was the father of Shammai. ^44

**45** The son of Shammai was Maon, and Maon was the father of Beth-zur. ^45

**46** Caleb’s concubine Ephah was the mother of Haran, Moza, and Gazez. Haran was the father of Gazez. ^46

**47** The sons of Jahdai: Regem, Jotham, Geshan, Pelet, Ephah, and Shaaph. ^47

**48** Caleb’s concubine Maacah was the mother of Sheber and Tirhanah. ^48

**49** She was also the mother of Shaaph father of Madmannah, and of Sheva father of Machbenah and Gibea. Caleb’s daughter was Acsah. ^49

**50** These were the descendants of Caleb. The sons of Hur the firstborn of Ephrathah: Shobal the father of Kiriath-jearim, ^50

**51** Salma the father of Bethlehem, and Hareph the father of Beth-gader. ^51

**52** These were the descendants of Shobal the father of Kiriath-jearim: Haroeh, half the Manahathites, ^52

**53** and the clans of Kiriath-jearim—the Ithrites, Puthites, Shumathites, and Mishraites. From these descended the Zorathites and Eshtaolites. ^53

**54** The descendants of Salma: Bethlehem, the Netophathites, Atroth-beth-joab, half the Manahathites, the Zorites, ^54

**55** and the clans of the scribes who lived at Jabez—the Tirathites, Shimeathites, and Sucathites. These are the Kenites who came from Hammath, the father of the house of Rechab. ^55
