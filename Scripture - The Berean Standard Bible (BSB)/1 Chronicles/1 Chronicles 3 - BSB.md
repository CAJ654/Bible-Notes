# 1 Chronicles 3 (BSB)

← [[1 Chronicles 2 - BSB]] | [[1 Chronicles 4 - BSB]] →

**1** These were the sons of David who were born to him in Hebron: The firstborn was Amnon by Ahinoam of Jezreel; the second was Daniel by Abigail of Carmel; ^1

**2** the third was Absalom the son of Maacah daughter of King Talmai of Geshur; the fourth was Adonijah the son of Haggith; ^2

**3** the fifth was Shephatiah by Abital; and the sixth was Ithream by his wife Eglah. ^3

**4** These six sons were born to David in Hebron, where he reigned seven years and six months. And David reigned in Jerusalem thirty-three years, ^4

**5** and these sons were born to him in Jerusalem: Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon. These four were born to him by Bathsheba daughter of Ammiel. ^5

**6** David’s other sons were Ibhar, Elishua, Eliphelet, ^6

**7** Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia, ^7

**8** Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet—nine in all. ^8

**9** These were all the sons of David, besides the sons by his concubines. And Tamar was their sister. ^9

**10** Solomon’s son was Rehoboam: Abijah was his son, Asa his son, Jehoshaphat his son, ^10

**11** Joram his son, Ahaziah his son, Joash his son, ^11

**12** Amaziah his son, Azariah his son, Jotham his son, ^12

**13** Ahaz his son, Hezekiah his son, Manasseh his son, ^13

**14** Amon his son, and Josiah his son. ^14

**15** The sons of Josiah: Johanan was the firstborn, Jehoiakim the second, Zedekiah the third, and Shallum the fourth. ^15

**16** The successors of Jehoiakim: Jeconiah his son, and Zedekiah. ^16

**17** The descendants of Jeconiah the captive: Shealtiel his son, ^17

**18** Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah. ^18

**19** The sons of Pedaiah: Zerubbabel and Shimei. The children of Zerubbabel: Meshullam and Hananiah, their sister Shelomith, ^19

**20** and five others: Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah, and Jushab-hesed. ^20

**21** The descendants of Hananiah: Pelatiah, Jeshaiah, and the sons of Rephaiah, of Arnan, of Obadiah, and of Shecaniah. ^21

**22** The six descendants of Shecaniah were Shemaiah and his sons: Hattush, Igal, Bariah, Neariah, and Shaphat. ^22

**23** The sons of Neariah: Elioenai, Hizkiah, and Azrikam—three in all. ^23

**24** The sons of Elioenai: Hodaviah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan, Delaiah, and Anani—seven in all. ^24
