# 1 Chronicles 8 (BSB)

← [[1 Chronicles 7 - BSB]] | [[1 Chronicles 9 - BSB]] →

**1** Benjamin was the father of Bela his firstborn, Ashbel the second, Aharah the third, ^1

**2** Nohah the fourth, and Rapha the fifth. ^2

**3** The sons of Bela: Addar, Gera, Abihud, ^3

**4** Abishua, Naaman, Ahoah, ^4

**5** Gera, Shephuphan, and Huram. ^5

**6** These were the descendants of Ehud who were the heads of the families living in Geba and were exiled to Manahath: ^6

**7** Naaman, Ahijah, and Gera, who carried them into exile and who was the father of Uzza and Ahihud. ^7

**8** Shaharaim had sons in the country of Moab after he had divorced his wives Hushim and Baara. ^8

**9** His sons by his wife Hodesh: Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, Malcam, ^9

**10** Jeuz, Sachia, and Mirmah. These were his sons, heads of families. ^10

**11** He also had sons by Hushim: Abitub and Elpaal. ^11

**12** The sons of Elpaal: Eber, Misham, Shemed (who built Ono and Lod with its villages), ^12

**13** and Beriah and Shema (who were the heads of families living in Aijalon and who drove out the inhabitants of Gath). ^13

**14** Ahio, Shashak, Jeremoth, ^14

**15** Zebadiah, Arad, Eder, ^15

**16** Michael, Ishpah, and Joha were the sons of Beriah. ^16

**17** Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hizki, Heber, ^17

**18** Ishmerai, Izliah, and Jobab were the sons of Elpaal. ^18

**19** Jakim, Zichri, Zabdi, ^19

**20** Elienai, Zillethai, Eliel, ^20

**21** Adaiah, Beraiah, and Shimrath were the sons of Shimei. ^21

**22** Ishpan, Eber, Eliel, ^22

**23** Abdon, Zichri, Hanan, ^23

**24** Hananiah, Elam, Anthothijah, ^24

**25** Iphdeiah, and Penuel were the sons of Shashak. ^25

**26** Shamsherai, Shehariah, Athaliah, ^26

**27** Jaareshiah, Elijah, and Zichri were the sons of Jeroham. ^27

**28** All these were heads of families, the chiefs according to their genealogies, and they lived in Jerusalem. ^28

**29** Jeiel the father of Gibeon lived in Gibeon. His wife’s name was Maacah, ^29

**30** and Abdon was his firstborn son, then Zur, Kish, Baal, Nadab, ^30

**31** Gedor, Ahio, Zecher, ^31

**32** and Mikloth, who was the father of Shimeah. They too lived alongside their relatives in Jerusalem. ^32

**33** Ner was the father of Kish, Kish was the father of Saul, and Saul was the father of Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Esh-baal. ^33

**34** The son of Jonathan: Merib-baal, and Merib-baal was the father of Micah. ^34

**35** The sons of Micah: Pithon, Melech, Tarea, and Ahaz. ^35

**36** Ahaz was the father of Jehoaddah, Jehoaddah was the father of Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri, and Zimri was the father of Moza. ^36

**37** Moza was the father of Binea. Raphah was his son, Eleasah his son, and Azel his son. ^37

**38** Azel had six sons, and these were their names: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. All these were the sons of Azel. ^38

**39** The sons of his brother Eshek: Ulam was his firstborn, Jeush second, and Eliphelet third. ^39

**40** The sons of Ulam were mighty men of valor, archers, and they had many sons and grandsons—150 in all. All these were the descendants of Benjamin. ^40
