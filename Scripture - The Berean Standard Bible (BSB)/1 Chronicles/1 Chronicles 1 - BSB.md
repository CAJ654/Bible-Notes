# 1 Chronicles 1 (BSB)

[[1 Chronicles 2 - BSB]] →

**1** Adam, Seth, Enosh, ^1

**2** Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared, ^2

**3** Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech, Noah. ^3

**4** The sons of Noah: Shem, Ham, and Japheth. ^4

**5** The sons of Japheth: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. ^5

**6** The sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah. ^6

**7** And the sons of Javan: Elishah, Tarshish, the Kittites, and the Rodanites. ^7

**8** The sons of Ham: Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan. ^8

**9** The sons of Cush: Seba, Havilah, Sabta, Raamah, and Sabteca. The sons of Raamah: Sheba and Dedan. ^9

**10** Cush was the father of Nimrod, who began to be a mighty one on the earth. ^10

**11** Mizraim was the father of the Ludites, the Anamites, the Lehabites, the Naphtuhites, ^11

**12** the Pathrusites, the Casluhites (from whom the Philistines came), and the Caphtorites. ^12

**13** And Canaan was the father of Sidon his firstborn, and of the Hittites, ^13

**14** the Jebusites, the Amorites, the Girgashites, ^14

**15** the Hivites, the Arkites, the Sinites, ^15

**16** the Arvadites, the Zemarites, and the Hamathites. ^16

**17** The sons of Shem: Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram. The sons of Aram: Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech. ^17

**18** Arphaxad was the father of Shelah, and Shelah was the father of Eber. ^18

**19** Two sons were born to Eber: One was named Peleg, because in his days the earth was divided, and his brother was named Joktan. ^19

**20** And Joktan was the father of Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah, ^20

**21** Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah, ^21

**22** Obal, Abimael, Sheba, ^22

**23** Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. All these were sons of Joktan. ^23

**24** So from Shem came Arphaxad, Shelah, ^24

**25** Eber, Peleg, Reu, ^25

**26** Serug, Nahor, Terah, ^26

**27** and Abram (that is, Abraham). ^27

**28** The sons of Abraham were Isaac and Ishmael. ^28

**29** These are their genealogies: Nebaioth the firstborn of Ishmael, then Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam, ^29

**30** Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema, ^30

**31** Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These were the sons of Ishmael. ^31

**32** The sons born to Keturah, Abraham’s concubine: Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. The sons of Jokshan: Sheba and Dedan. ^32

**33** The sons of Midian: Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All of these were Keturah’s sons. ^33

**34** Abraham was the father of Isaac. The sons of Isaac: Esau and Israel. ^34

**35** The sons of Esau: Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. ^35

**36** The sons of Eliphaz: Teman, Omar, Zepho, Gatam, and Kenaz; and by Timna, Amalek. ^36

**37** The sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. ^37

**38** The sons of Seir: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. ^38

**39** The sons of Lotan: Hori and Homam. Timna was Lotan’s sister. ^39

**40** The sons of Shobal: Alvan, Manahath, Ebal, Shepho, and Onam. The sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Anah. ^40

**41** The son of Anah: Dishon. The sons of Dishon: Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran. ^41

**42** The sons of Ezer: Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan. The sons of Dishan: Uz and Aran. ^42

**43** These are the kings who reigned in the land of Edom before any king reigned over the Israelites: Bela son of Beor. His city was named Dinhabah. ^43

**44** When Bela died, Jobab son of Zerah from Bozrah reigned in his place. ^44

**45** When Jobab died, Husham from the land of the Temanites reigned in his place. ^45

**46** When Husham died, Hadad son of Bedad, who defeated Midian in the country of Moab, reigned in his place. And the name of his city was Avith. ^46

**47** When Hadad died, Samlah from Masrekah reigned in his place. ^47

**48** When Samlah died, Shaul from Rehoboth on the Euphrates reigned in his place. ^48

**49** When Shaul died, Baal-hanan son of Achbor reigned in his place. ^49

**50** When Baal-hanan died, Hadad reigned in his place. His city was named Pau, and his wife’s name was Mehetabel daughter of Matred, the daughter of Me-zahab. ^50

**51** Then Hadad died. Now the chiefs of Edom were Timna, Alvah, Jetheth, ^51

**52** Oholibamah, Elah, Pinon, ^52

**53** Kenaz, Teman, Mibzar, ^53

**54** Magdiel, and Iram. These were the chiefs of Edom. ^54
