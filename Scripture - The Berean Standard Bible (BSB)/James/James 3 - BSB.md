# James 3 (BSB)

← [[James 2 - BSB]] | [[James 4 - BSB]] →

**1** Not many of you should become teachers, my brothers, because you know that we who teach will be judged more strictly. ^1

**2** We all stumble in many ways. If anyone is never at fault in what he says, he is a perfect man, able to control his whole body. ^2

**3** When we put bits into the mouths of horses to make them obey us, we can guide the whole animal. ^3

**4** Consider ships as well. Although they are so large and are driven by strong winds, they are steered by a very small rudder wherever the pilot is inclined. ^4

**5** In the same way, the tongue is a small part of the body, but it boasts of great things. Consider how small a spark sets a great forest ablaze. ^5

**6** The tongue also is a fire, a world of wickedness among the parts of the body. It pollutes the whole person, sets the course of his life on fire, and is itself set on fire by hell. ^6

**7** All kinds of animals, birds, reptiles, and creatures of the sea are being tamed and have been tamed by man, ^7

**8** but no man can tame the tongue. It is a restless evil, full of deadly poison. ^8

**9** With the tongue we bless our Lord and Father, and with it we curse men, who have been made in God’s likeness. ^9

**10** Out of the same mouth come blessing and cursing. My brothers, this should not be! ^10

**11** Can both fresh water and salt water flow from the same spring? ^11

**12** My brothers, can a fig tree grow olives, or a grapevine bear figs? Neither can a salt spring produce fresh water. ^12

**13** Who is wise and understanding among you? Let him show it by his good conduct, by deeds done in the humility that comes from wisdom. ^13

**14** But if you harbor bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your hearts, do not boast in it or deny the truth. ^14

**15** Such wisdom does not come from above, but is earthly, unspiritual, demonic. ^15

**16** For where jealousy and selfish ambition exist, there will be disorder and every evil practice. ^16

**17** But the wisdom from above is first of all pure, then peace-loving, gentle, accommodating, full of mercy and good fruit, impartial, and sincere. ^17

**18** Peacemakers who sow in peace reap the fruit of righteousness. ^18
