# 1 Corinthians 16 (BSB)

← [[1 Corinthians 15 - BSB]]

**1** Now about the collection for the saints, you are to do as I directed the churches of Galatia: ^1

**2** On the first day of every week, each of you should set aside a portion of his income, saving it up, so that when I come no collections will be needed. ^2

**3** Then, on my arrival, I will send letters with those you recommend to carry your gift to Jerusalem. ^3

**4** And if it is advisable for me to go also, they can travel with me. ^4

**5** After I go through Macedonia, however, I will come to you; for I will be going through Macedonia. ^5

**6** Perhaps I will stay with you awhile, or even spend the winter, so that you can help me on my journey, wherever I go. ^6

**7** For I do not want to see you now only in passing; I hope to spend some time with you, if the Lord permits. ^7

**8** But I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost, ^8

**9** because a great door for effective work has opened to me, even though many oppose me. ^9

**10** If Timothy comes, see to it that he has nothing to fear while he is with you, for he is doing the work of the Lord, just as I am. ^10

**11** No one, then, should treat him with contempt. Send him on his way in peace so that he can return to me, for I am expecting him along with the brothers. ^11

**12** Now about our brother Apollos: I strongly urged him to go to you with the brothers. He was not at all inclined to go now, but he will go when he has the opportunity. ^12

**13** Be on the alert. Stand firm in the faith. Be men of courage. Be strong. ^13

**14** Do everything in love. ^14

**15** You know that Stephanas and his household were the first converts in Achaia, and they have devoted themselves to the service of the saints. Now I urge you, brothers, ^15

**16** to submit to such as these, and to every fellow worker and laborer. ^16

**17** I am glad that Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus have arrived, because they have supplied what was lacking from you. ^17

**18** For they refreshed my spirit and yours as well. Show your appreciation, therefore, to such men. ^18

**19** The churches in the province of Asia send you greetings. Aquila and Prisca greet you warmly in the Lord, and so does the church that meets at their house. ^19

**20** All the brothers here send you greetings. Greet one another with a holy kiss. ^20

**21** This greeting is in my own hand—Paul. ^21

**22** If anyone does not love the Lord, let him be under a curse. Come, O Lord! ^22

**23** The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you. ^23

**24** My love be with all of you in Christ Jesus. Amen. ^24
