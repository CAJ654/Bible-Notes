# 1 Corinthians 12 (BSB)

← [[1 Corinthians 11 - BSB]] | [[1 Corinthians 13 - BSB]] →

**1** Now about spiritual gifts, brothers, I do not want you to be uninformed. ^1

**2** You know that when you were pagans, you were influenced and led astray to mute idols. ^2

**3** Therefore I inform you that no one who is speaking by the Spirit of God says, “Jesus be cursed,” and no one can say, “Jesus is Lord,” except by the Holy Spirit. ^3

**4** There are different gifts, but the same Spirit. ^4

**5** There are different ministries, but the same Lord. ^5

**6** There are different ways of working, but the same God works all things in all people. ^6

**7** Now to each one the manifestation of the Spirit is given for the common good. ^7

**8** To one there is given through the Spirit the message of wisdom, to another the message of knowledge by the same Spirit, ^8

**9** to another faith by the same Spirit, to another gifts of healing by that one Spirit, ^9

**10** to another the working of miracles, to another prophecy, to another distinguishing between spirits, to another speaking in various tongues, and to still another the interpretation of tongues. ^10

**11** All these are the work of one and the same Spirit, who apportions them to each one as He determines. ^11

**12** The body is a unit, though it is composed of many parts. And although its parts are many, they all form one body. So it is with Christ. ^12

**13** For in one Spirit we were all baptized into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, slave or free, and we were all given one Spirit to drink. ^13

**14** For the body does not consist of one part, but of many. ^14

**15** If the foot should say, “Because I am not a hand, I do not belong to the body,” that would not make it any less a part of the body. ^15

**16** And if the ear should say, “Because I am not an eye, I do not belong to the body,” that would not make it any less a part of the body. ^16

**17** If the whole body were an eye, where would the sense of hearing be? If the whole body were an ear, where would the sense of smell be? ^17

**18** But in fact, God has arranged the members of the body, every one of them, according to His design. ^18

**19** If they were all one part, where would the body be? ^19

**20** As it is, there are many parts, but one body. ^20

**21** The eye cannot say to the hand, “I do not need you.” Nor can the head say to the feet, “I do not need you.” ^21

**22** On the contrary, the parts of the body that seem to be weaker are indispensable, ^22

**23** and the parts we consider less honorable, we treat with greater honor. And our unpresentable parts are treated with special modesty, ^23

**24** whereas our presentable parts have no such need. But God has composed the body and has given greater honor to the parts that lacked it, ^24

**25** so that there should be no division in the body, but that its members should have mutual concern for one another. ^25

**26** If one part suffers, every part suffers with it; if one part is honored, every part rejoices with it. ^26

**27** Now you are the body of Christ, and each of you is a member of it. ^27

**28** And in the church God has appointed first of all apostles, second prophets, third teachers, then workers of miracles, and those with gifts of healing, helping, administration, and various tongues. ^28

**29** Are all apostles? Are all prophets? Are all teachers? Do all work miracles? ^29

**30** Do all have gifts of healing? Do all speak in tongues? Do all interpret? ^30

**31** But eagerly desire the greater gifts. And now I will show you the most excellent way. ^31
