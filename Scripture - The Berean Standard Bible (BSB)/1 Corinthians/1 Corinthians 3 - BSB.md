# 1 Corinthians 3 (BSB)

← [[1 Corinthians 2 - BSB]] | [[1 Corinthians 4 - BSB]] →

**1** Brothers, I could not address you as spiritual, but as worldly—as infants in Christ. ^1

**2** I gave you milk, not solid food, for you were not yet ready for solid food. In fact, you are still not ready, ^2

**3** for you are still worldly. For since there is jealousy and dissension among you, are you not worldly? Are you not walking in the way of man? ^3

**4** For when one of you says, “I follow Paul,” and another, “I follow Apollos,” are you not mere men? ^4

**5** What then is Apollos? And what is Paul? They are servants through whom you believed, as the Lord has assigned to each his role. ^5

**6** I planted the seed and Apollos watered it, but God made it grow. ^6

**7** So neither he who plants nor he who waters is anything, but only God, who makes things grow. ^7

**8** He who plants and he who waters are one in purpose, and each will be rewarded according to his own labor. ^8

**9** For we are God’s fellow workers; you are God’s field, God’s building. ^9

**10** By the grace God has given me, I laid a foundation as an expert builder, and someone else is building on it. But each one must be careful how he builds. ^10

**11** For no one can lay a foundation other than the one already laid, which is Jesus Christ. ^11

**12** If anyone builds on this foundation using gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, or straw, ^12

**13** his workmanship will be evident, because the Day will bring it to light. It will be revealed with fire, and the fire will prove the quality of each man’s work. ^13

**14** If what he has built survives, he will receive a reward. ^14

**15** If it is burned up, he will suffer loss. He himself will be saved, but only as if through the flames. ^15

**16** Do you not know that you yourselves are God’s temple, and that God’s Spirit dwells in you? ^16

**17** If anyone destroys God’s temple, God will destroy him; for God’s temple is holy, and you are that temple. ^17

**18** Let no one deceive himself. If any of you thinks he is wise in this age, he should become a fool, so that he may become wise. ^18

**19** For the wisdom of this world is foolishness in God’s sight. As it is written: “He catches the wise in their craftiness.” ^19

**20** And again, “The Lord knows that the thoughts of the wise are futile.” ^20

**21** Therefore, stop boasting in men. All things are yours, ^21

**22** whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas or the world or life or death or the present or the future. All of them belong to you, ^22

**23** and you belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God. ^23
