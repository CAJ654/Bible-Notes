# 2 Timothy 3 (BSB)

← [[2 Timothy 2 - BSB]] | [[2 Timothy 4 - BSB]] →

**1** But understand this: In the last days terrible times will come. ^1

**2** For men will be lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boastful, arrogant, abusive, disobedient to their parents, ungrateful, unholy, ^2

**3** unloving, unforgiving, slanderous, without self-control, brutal, without love of good, ^3

**4** traitorous, reckless, conceited, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God, ^4

**5** having a form of godliness but denying its power. Turn away from such as these! ^5

**6** They are the kind who worm their way into households and captivate vulnerable women who are weighed down with sins and led astray by various passions, ^6

**7** who are always learning but never able to come to a knowledge of the truth. ^7

**8** Just as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so also these men oppose the truth. They are depraved in mind and disqualified from the faith. ^8

**9** But they will not advance much further. For just like Jannes and Jambres, their folly will be plain to everyone. ^9

**10** You, however, have observed my teaching, my conduct, my purpose, my faith, my patience, my love, my perseverance, ^10

**11** my persecutions, and the sufferings that came upon me in Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra. What persecutions I endured! Yet the Lord rescued me from all of them. ^11

**12** Indeed, all who desire to live godly lives in Christ Jesus will be persecuted, ^12

**13** while evil men and imposters go from bad to worse, deceiving and being deceived. ^13

**14** But as for you, continue in the things you have learned and firmly believed, since you know from whom you have learned them. ^14

**15** From infancy you have known the Holy Scriptures, which are able to make you wise for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus. ^15

**16** All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for instruction, for conviction, for correction, and for training in righteousness, ^16

**17** so that the man of God may be complete, fully equipped for every good work. ^17
