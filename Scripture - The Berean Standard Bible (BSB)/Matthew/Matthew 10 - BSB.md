# Matthew 10 (BSB)

← [[Matthew 9 - BSB]] | [[Matthew 11 - BSB]] →

**1** And calling His twelve disciples to Him, Jesus gave them authority over unclean spirits, so that they could drive them out and heal every disease and sickness. ^1

**2** These are the names of the twelve apostles: first Simon, called Peter, and his brother Andrew; James son of Zebedee, and his brother John; ^2

**3** Philip and Bartholomew; Thomas and Matthew the tax collector; James son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus; ^3

**4** Simon the Zealot, and Judas Iscariot, who betrayed Jesus. ^4

**5** These twelve Jesus sent out with the following instructions: “Do not go onto the road of the Gentiles or enter any town of the Samaritans. ^5

**6** Go rather to the lost sheep of Israel. ^6

**7** As you go, preach this message: ‘The kingdom of heaven is near.’ ^7

**8** Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse the lepers, drive out demons. Freely you have received; freely give. ^8

**9** Do not take along any gold or silver or copper in your belts. ^9

**10** Take no bag for the road, or second tunic, or sandals, or staff; for the worker is worthy of his provisions. ^10

**11** Whatever town or village you enter, find out who is worthy there and stay at his house until you move on. ^11

**12** As you enter the home, greet its occupants. ^12

**13** If the home is worthy, let your peace rest on it, but if it is not, let your peace return to you. ^13

**14** And if anyone will not welcome you or heed your words, shake the dust off your feet when you leave that home or town. ^14

**15** Truly I tell you, it will be more bearable for Sodom and Gomorrah on the day of judgment than for that town. ^15

**16** Behold, I am sending you out like sheep among wolves; therefore be as shrewd as snakes and as innocent as doves. ^16

**17** But beware of men, for they will hand you over to their councils and flog you in their synagogues. ^17

**18** On My account you will be brought before governors and kings as witnesses to them and to the Gentiles. ^18

**19** But when they hand you over, do not worry about how to respond or what to say. In that hour you will be given what to say. ^19

**20** For it will not be you speaking, but the Spirit of your Father speaking through you. ^20

**21** Brother will betray brother to death, and a father his child; children will rise against their parents and have them put to death. ^21

**22** You will be hated by everyone because of My name, but the one who perseveres to the end will be saved. ^22

**23** When they persecute you in one town, flee to the next. Truly I tell you, you will not reach all the towns of Israel before the Son of Man comes. ^23

**24** A disciple is not above his teacher, nor a servant above his master. ^24

**25** It is enough for a disciple to be like his teacher, and a servant like his master. If the head of the house has been called Beelzebul, how much more the members of his household! ^25

**26** So do not be afraid of them. For there is nothing concealed that will not be disclosed, and nothing hidden that will not be made known. ^26

**27** What I tell you in the dark, speak in the daylight; what is whispered in your ear, proclaim from the housetops. ^27

**28** Do not be afraid of those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul. Instead, fear the One who can destroy both soul and body in hell. ^28

**29** Are not two sparrows sold for a penny? Yet not one of them will fall to the ground apart from the will of your Father. ^29

**30** And even the very hairs of your head are all numbered. ^30

**31** So do not be afraid; you are worth more than many sparrows. ^31

**32** Therefore everyone who confesses Me before men, I will also confess him before My Father in heaven. ^32

**33** But whoever denies Me before men, I will also deny him before My Father in heaven. ^33

**34** Do not assume that I have come to bring peace to the earth; I have not come to bring peace, but a sword. ^34

**35** For I have come to turn ‘a man against his father, a daughter against her mother, a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law. ^35

**36** A man’s enemies will be the members of his own household.’ ^36

**37** Anyone who loves his father or mother more than Me is not worthy of Me; anyone who loves his son or daughter more than Me is not worthy of Me; ^37

**38** and anyone who does not take up his cross and follow Me is not worthy of Me. ^38

**39** Whoever finds his life will lose it, and whoever loses his life for My sake will find it. ^39

**40** He who receives you receives Me, and he who receives Me receives the One who sent Me. ^40

**41** Whoever receives a prophet because he is a prophet will receive a prophet’s reward, and whoever receives a righteous man because he is a righteous man will receive a righteous man’s reward. ^41

**42** And if anyone gives even a cup of cold water to one of these little ones because he is My disciple, truly I tell you, he will never lose his reward.” ^42
