# Matthew 4 (BSB)

← [[Matthew 3 - BSB]] | [[Matthew 5 - BSB]] →

**1** Then Jesus was led by the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted by the devil. ^1

**2** After fasting forty days and forty nights, He was hungry. ^2

**3** The tempter came to Him and said, “If You are the Son of God, tell these stones to become bread.” ^3

**4** But Jesus answered, “It is written: ‘Man shall not live on bread alone, but on every word that comes from the mouth of God.’” ^4

**5** Then the devil took Him to the holy city and set Him on the pinnacle of the temple. ^5

**6** “If You are the Son of God,” he said, “throw Yourself down. For it is written: ‘He will command His angels concerning You, and they will lift You up in their hands, so that You will not strike Your foot against a stone.’” ^6

**7** Jesus replied, “It is also written: ‘Do not put the Lord your God to the test.’” ^7

**8** Again, the devil took Him to a very high mountain and showed Him all the kingdoms of the world and their glory. ^8

**9** “All this I will give You,” he said, “if You will fall down and worship me.” ^9

**10** “Away from Me, Satan!” Jesus told him. “For it is written: ‘Worship the Lord your God and serve Him only.’” ^10

**11** Then the devil left Him, and angels came and ministered to Him. ^11

**12** When Jesus heard that John had been imprisoned, He withdrew to Galilee. ^12

**13** Leaving Nazareth, He went and lived in Capernaum, which is by the sea in the region of Zebulun and Naphtali, ^13

**14** to fulfill what was spoken through the prophet Isaiah: ^14

**15** “Land of Zebulun and land of Naphtali, the Way of the Sea, beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles— ^15

**16** the people living in darkness have seen a great light; on those living in the land of the shadow of death, a light has dawned.” ^16

**17** From that time on Jesus began to preach, “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is near.” ^17

**18** As Jesus was walking beside the Sea of Galilee, He saw two brothers, Simon called Peter and his brother Andrew. They were casting a net into the sea, for they were fishermen. ^18

**19** “Come, follow Me,” Jesus said, “and I will make you fishers of men.” ^19

**20** And at once they left their nets and followed Him. ^20

**21** Going on from there, He saw two other brothers, James son of Zebedee and his brother John. They were in a boat with their father Zebedee, mending their nets. Jesus called them, ^21

**22** and immediately they left the boat and their father and followed Him. ^22

**23** Jesus went throughout Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every disease and sickness among the people. ^23

**24** News about Him spread all over Syria, and people brought to Him all who were ill with various diseases, those suffering acute pain, the demon-possessed, those having seizures, and the paralyzed, and He healed them. ^24

**25** Large crowds followed Him, having come from Galilee, the Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judea, and beyond the Jordan. ^25
