# Matthew 15 (BSB)

← [[Matthew 14 - BSB]] | [[Matthew 16 - BSB]] →

**1** Then some Pharisees and scribes came to Jesus from Jerusalem and asked, ^1

**2** “Why do Your disciples break the tradition of the elders? They do not wash their hands before they eat.” ^2

**3** Jesus replied, “And why do you break the command of God for the sake of your tradition? ^3

**4** For God said, ‘Honor your father and mother’ and ‘Anyone who curses his father or mother must be put to death.’ ^4

**5** But you say that if anyone says to his father or mother, ‘Whatever you would have received from me is a gift devoted to God,’ ^5

**6** he need not honor his father or mother with it. Thus you nullify the word of God for the sake of your tradition. ^6

**7** You hypocrites! Isaiah prophesied correctly about you: ^7

**8** ‘These people honor Me with their lips, but their hearts are far from Me. ^8

**9** They worship Me in vain; they teach as doctrine the precepts of men.’” ^9

**10** Jesus called the crowd to Him and said, “Listen and understand. ^10

**11** A man is not defiled by what enters his mouth, but by what comes out of it.” ^11

**12** Then the disciples came to Him and said, “Are You aware that the Pharisees were offended when they heard this?” ^12

**13** But Jesus replied, “Every plant that My heavenly Father has not planted will be pulled up by its roots. ^13

**14** Disregard them! They are blind guides. If a blind man leads a blind man, both will fall into a pit.” ^14

**15** Peter said to Him, “Explain this parable to us.” ^15

**16** “Do you still not understand?” Jesus asked. ^16

**17** “Do you not yet realize that whatever enters the mouth goes into the stomach and then is eliminated? ^17

**18** But the things that come out of the mouth come from the heart, and these things defile a man. ^18

**19** For out of the heart come evil thoughts, murder, adultery, sexual immorality, theft, false testimony, and slander. ^19

**20** These are what defile a man, but eating with unwashed hands does not defile him.” ^20

**21** Leaving that place, Jesus withdrew to the district of Tyre and Sidon. ^21

**22** And a Canaanite woman from that region came to Him, crying out, “Lord, Son of David, have mercy on me! My daughter is miserably possessed by a demon.” ^22

**23** But Jesus did not answer a word. So His disciples came and urged Him, “Send her away, for she keeps crying out after us.” ^23

**24** He answered, “I was sent only to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.” ^24

**25** The woman came and knelt before Him. “Lord, help me!” she said. ^25

**26** But Jesus replied, “It is not right to take the children’s bread and toss it to the dogs.” ^26

**27** “Yes, Lord,” she said, “even the dogs eat the crumbs that fall from their master’s table.” ^27

**28** “O woman,” Jesus answered, “your faith is great! Let it be done for you as you desire.” And her daughter was healed from that very hour. ^28

**29** Moving on from there, Jesus went along the Sea of Galilee. Then He went up on a mountain and sat down. ^29

**30** Large crowds came to Him, bringing the lame, the blind, the crippled, the mute, and many others, and laid them at His feet, and He healed them. ^30

**31** The crowd was amazed when they saw the mute speaking, the crippled restored, the lame walking, and the blind seeing. And they glorified the God of Israel. ^31

**32** Then Jesus called His disciples to Him and said, “I have compassion for this crowd, because they have already been with Me three days and have nothing to eat. I do not want to send them away hungry, or they may faint along the way.” ^32

**33** The disciples replied, “Where in this desolate place could we find enough bread to feed such a large crowd?” ^33

**34** “How many loaves do you have?” Jesus asked. “Seven,” they replied, “and a few small fish.” ^34

**35** And He instructed the crowd to sit down on the ground. ^35

**36** Taking the seven loaves and the fish, He gave thanks and broke them. Then He gave them to the disciples, and the disciples gave them to the people. ^36

**37** They all ate and were satisfied, and the disciples picked up seven basketfuls of broken pieces that were left over. ^37

**38** A total of four thousand men were fed, besides women and children. ^38

**39** After Jesus had dismissed the crowds, He got into the boat and went to the region of Magadan. ^39
