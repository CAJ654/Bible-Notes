# Numbers 34 (BSB)

← [[Numbers 33 - BSB]] | [[Numbers 35 - BSB]] →

**1** Then the LORD said to Moses, ^1

**2** “Command the Israelites and say to them: When you enter the land of Canaan, it will be allotted to you as an inheritance with these boundaries: ^2

**3** Your southern border will extend from the Wilderness of Zin along the border of Edom. On the east, your southern border will run from the end of the Salt Sea, ^3

**4** cross south of the Ascent of Akrabbim, continue to Zin, and go south of Kadesh-barnea. Then it will go on to Hazar-addar and proceed to Azmon, ^4

**5** where it will turn from Azmon, join the Brook of Egypt, and end at the Sea. ^5

**6** Your western border will be the coastline of the Great Sea; this will be your boundary on the west. ^6

**7** Your northern border will run from the Great Sea directly to Mount Hor, ^7

**8** and from Mount Hor to Lebo-hamath, then extend to Zedad, ^8

**9** continue to Ziphron, and end at Hazar-enan. This will be your boundary on the north. ^9

**10** And your eastern border will run straight from Hazar-enan to Shepham, ^10

**11** then go down from Shepham to Riblah on the east side of Ain and continue along the slopes east of the Sea of Chinnereth. ^11

**12** Then the border will go down along the Jordan and end at the Salt Sea. This will be your land, defined by its borders on all sides.” ^12

**13** So Moses commanded the Israelites, “Apportion this land by lot as an inheritance. The LORD has commanded that it be given to the nine and a half tribes. ^13

**14** For the tribes of the Reubenites and Gadites, along with the half-tribe of Manasseh, have already received their inheritance. ^14

**15** These two and a half tribes have received their inheritance across the Jordan from Jericho, toward the sunrise.” ^15

**16** Then the LORD said to Moses, ^16

**17** “These are the names of the men who are to assign the land as an inheritance for you: Eleazar the priest and Joshua son of Nun. ^17

**18** Appoint one leader from each tribe to distribute the land. ^18

**19** These are their names: Caleb son of Jephunneh from the tribe of Judah; ^19

**20** Shemuel son of Ammihud from the tribe of Simeon; ^20

**21** Elidad son of Chislon from the tribe of Benjamin; ^21

**22** Bukki son of Jogli, a leader from the tribe of Dan; ^22

**23** Hanniel son of Ephod, a leader from the tribe of Manasseh son of Joseph; ^23

**24** Kemuel son of Shiphtan, a leader from the tribe of Ephraim; ^24

**25** Eli-zaphan son of Parnach, a leader from the tribe of Zebulun; ^25

**26** Paltiel son of Azzan, a leader from the tribe of Issachar; ^26

**27** Ahihud son of Shelomi, a leader from the tribe of Asher; ^27

**28** and Pedahel son of Ammihud, a leader from the tribe of Naphtali.” ^28

**29** These are the ones whom the LORD commanded to apportion the inheritance to the Israelites in the land of Canaan. ^29
