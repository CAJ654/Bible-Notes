# Isaiah 6 (BSB)

← [[Isaiah 5 - BSB]] | [[Isaiah 7 - BSB]] →

**1** In the year that King Uzziah died, I saw the Lord seated on a throne, high and exalted; and the train of His robe filled the temple. ^1

**2** Above Him stood seraphim, each having six wings: With two wings they covered their faces, with two they covered their feet, and with two they were flying. ^2

**3** And they were calling out to one another: “Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of Hosts; all the earth is full of His glory.” ^3

**4** At the sound of their voices the doorposts and thresholds shook, and the temple was filled with smoke. ^4

**5** Then I said: “Woe is me, for I am ruined, because I am a man of unclean lips dwelling among a people of unclean lips; for my eyes have seen the King, the LORD of Hosts.” ^5

**6** Then one of the seraphim flew to me, and in his hand was a glowing coal that he had taken with tongs from the altar. ^6

**7** And with it he touched my mouth and said: “Now that this has touched your lips, your iniquity is removed and your sin is atoned for.” ^7

**8** Then I heard the voice of the Lord saying: “Whom shall I send? Who will go for Us?” And I said: “Here am I. Send me!” ^8

**9** And He replied: “Go and tell this people, ‘Be ever hearing, but never understanding; be ever seeing, but never perceiving.’ ^9

**10** Make the hearts of this people calloused; deafen their ears and close their eyes. Otherwise they might see with their eyes, hear with their ears, understand with their hearts, and turn and be healed.” ^10

**11** Then I asked: “How long, O Lord?” And He replied: “Until the cities lie ruined and without inhabitant, until the houses are left unoccupied and the land is desolate and ravaged, ^11

**12** until the LORD has driven men far away and the land is utterly forsaken. ^12

**13** And though a tenth remains in the land, it will be burned again. As the terebinth and oak leave stumps when felled, so the holy seed will be a stump in the land.” ^13
