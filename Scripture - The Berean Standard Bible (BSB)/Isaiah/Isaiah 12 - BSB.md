# Isaiah 12 (BSB)

← [[Isaiah 11 - BSB]] | [[Isaiah 13 - BSB]] →

**1** In that day you will say: “O LORD, I will praise You. Although You were angry with me, Your anger has turned away, and You have comforted me. ^1

**2** Surely God is my salvation; I will trust and not be afraid. For the LORD GOD is my strength and my song, and He also has become my salvation.” ^2

**3** With joy you will draw water from the springs of salvation, ^3

**4** and on that day you will say: “Give praise to the LORD; proclaim His name! Make His works known among the peoples; declare that His name is exalted. ^4

**5** Sing to the LORD, for He has done glorious things. Let this be known in all the earth. ^5

**6** Cry out and sing, O citizen of Zion, for great among you is the Holy One of Israel.” ^6
