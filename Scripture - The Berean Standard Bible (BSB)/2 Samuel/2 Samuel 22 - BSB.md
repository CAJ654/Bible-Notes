# 2 Samuel 22 (BSB)

← [[2 Samuel 21 - BSB]] | [[2 Samuel 23 - BSB]] →

**1** And David sang this song to the LORD on the day the LORD had delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul. ^1

**2** He said: “The LORD is my rock, my fortress, and my deliverer. ^2

**3** My God is my rock, in whom I take refuge, my shield, and the horn of my salvation. My stronghold, my refuge, and my Savior, You save me from violence. ^3

**4** I will call upon the LORD, who is worthy to be praised; so shall I be saved from my enemies. ^4

**5** For the waves of death engulfed me; the torrents of chaos overwhelmed me. ^5

**6** The cords of Sheol entangled me; the snares of death confronted me. ^6

**7** In my distress I called upon the LORD; I cried out to my God. And from His temple He heard my voice, and my cry for help reached His ears. ^7

**8** Then the earth shook and quaked; the foundations of the heavens trembled; they were shaken because He burned with anger. ^8

**9** Smoke rose from His nostrils, and consuming fire came from His mouth; glowing coals blazed forth. ^9

**10** He parted the heavens and came down with dark clouds beneath His feet. ^10

**11** He mounted a cherub and flew; He soared on the wings of the wind. ^11

**12** He made darkness a canopy around Him, a gathering of water and thick clouds. ^12

**13** From the brightness of His presence coals of fire blazed forth. ^13

**14** The LORD thundered from heaven; the voice of the Most High resounded. ^14

**15** He shot His arrows and scattered the foes; He hurled lightning and routed them. ^15

**16** The channels of the sea appeared, and the foundations of the world were exposed at the rebuke of the LORD, at the blast of the breath of His nostrils. ^16

**17** He reached down from on high and took hold of me; He drew me out of deep waters. ^17

**18** He rescued me from my powerful enemy, from foes too mighty for me. ^18

**19** They confronted me in my day of calamity, but the LORD was my support. ^19

**20** He brought me out into the open; He rescued me because He delighted in me. ^20

**21** The LORD has rewarded me according to my righteousness; He has repaid me according to the cleanness of my hands. ^21

**22** For I have kept the ways of the LORD and have not wickedly departed from my God. ^22

**23** For all His ordinances are before me; I have not disregarded His statutes. ^23

**24** And I have been blameless before Him and kept myself from iniquity. ^24

**25** So the LORD has repaid me according to my righteousness, according to my cleanness in His sight. ^25

**26** To the faithful You show Yourself faithful, to the blameless You show Yourself blameless; ^26

**27** to the pure You show Yourself pure, but to the crooked You show Yourself shrewd. ^27

**28** You save an afflicted people, but Your eyes are on the haughty to bring them down. ^28

**29** For You, O LORD, are my lamp; the LORD lights up my darkness. ^29

**30** For in You I can charge an army; with my God I can scale a wall. ^30

**31** As for God, His way is perfect; the word of the LORD is flawless. He is a shield to all who take refuge in Him. ^31

**32** For who is God besides the LORD? And who is the Rock except our God? ^32

**33** God is my strong fortress, and He makes my way clear. ^33

**34** He makes my feet like those of a deer and stations me upon the heights. ^34

**35** He trains my hands for battle; my arms can bend a bow of bronze. ^35

**36** You have given me Your shield of salvation, and Your gentleness exalts me. ^36

**37** You broaden the path beneath me so that my ankles do not give way. ^37

**38** I pursued my enemies and destroyed them; I did not turn back until they were consumed. ^38

**39** I devoured and crushed them so they could not rise; they have fallen under my feet. ^39

**40** You have armed me with strength for battle; You have subdued my foes beneath me. ^40

**41** You have made my enemies retreat before me; I destroyed those who hated me. ^41

**42** They looked, but there was no one to save them—to the LORD, but He did not answer. ^42

**43** I ground them as the dust of the earth; I crushed and trampled them like mud in the streets. ^43

**44** You have delivered me from the strife of my people; You have preserved me as the head of nations; a people I had not known shall serve me. ^44

**45** Foreigners cower before me; when they hear me, they obey me. ^45

**46** Foreigners lose heart and come trembling from their strongholds. ^46

**47** The LORD lives, and blessed be my Rock! And may God, the Rock of my salvation, be exalted— ^47

**48** the God who avenges me and brings down nations beneath me, ^48

**49** who frees me from my enemies. You exalt me above my foes; You rescue me from violent men. ^49

**50** Therefore I will praise You, O LORD, among the nations; I will sing praises to Your name. ^50

**51** Great salvation He brings to His king. He shows loving devotion to His anointed, to David and his descendants forever.” ^51
