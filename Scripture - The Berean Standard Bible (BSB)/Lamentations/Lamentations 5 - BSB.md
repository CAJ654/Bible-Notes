# Lamentations 5 (BSB)

← [[Lamentations 4 - BSB]]

**1** Remember, O LORD, what has happened to us. Look and see our disgrace! ^1

**2** Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers, our houses to foreigners. ^2

**3** We have become fatherless orphans; our mothers are widows. ^3

**4** We must buy the water we drink; our wood comes at a price. ^4

**5** We are closely pursued; we are weary and find no rest. ^5

**6** We submitted to Egypt and Assyria to get enough bread. ^6

**7** Our fathers sinned and are no more, but we bear their punishment. ^7

**8** Slaves rule over us; there is no one to deliver us from their hands. ^8

**9** We get our bread at the risk of our lives because of the sword in the wilderness. ^9

**10** Our skin is as hot as an oven with fever from our hunger. ^10

**11** Women have been ravished in Zion, virgins in the cities of Judah. ^11

**12** Princes have been hung up by their hands; elders receive no respect. ^12

**13** Young men toil at millstones; boys stagger under loads of wood. ^13

**14** The elders have left the city gate; the young men have stopped their music. ^14

**15** Joy has left our hearts; our dancing has turned to mourning. ^15

**16** The crown has fallen from our head. Woe to us, for we have sinned! ^16

**17** Because of this, our hearts are faint; because of these, our eyes grow dim— ^17

**18** because of Mount Zion, which lies desolate, patrolled by foxes. ^18

**19** You, O LORD, reign forever; Your throne endures from generation to generation. ^19

**20** Why have You forgotten us forever? Why have You forsaken us for so long? ^20

**21** Restore us to Yourself, O LORD, so we may return; renew our days as of old, ^21

**22** unless You have utterly rejected us and remain angry with us beyond measure. ^22
