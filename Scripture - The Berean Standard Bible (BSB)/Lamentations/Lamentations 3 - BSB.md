# Lamentations 3 (BSB)

← [[Lamentations 2 - BSB]] | [[Lamentations 4 - BSB]] →

**1** I am the man who has seen affliction under the rod of God’s wrath. ^1

**2** He has driven me away and made me walk in darkness instead of light. ^2

**3** Indeed, He keeps turning His hand against me all day long. ^3

**4** He has worn away my flesh and skin; He has shattered my bones. ^4

**5** He has besieged me and surrounded me with bitterness and hardship. ^5

**6** He has made me dwell in darkness like those dead for ages. ^6

**7** He has walled me in so I cannot escape; He has weighed me down with chains. ^7

**8** Even when I cry out and plead for help, He shuts out my prayer. ^8

**9** He has barred my ways with cut stones; He has made my paths crooked. ^9

**10** He is a bear lying in wait, a lion hiding in ambush. ^10

**11** He forced me off my path and tore me to pieces; He left me without help. ^11

**12** He bent His bow and set me as the target for His arrow. ^12

**13** He pierced my kidneys with His arrows. ^13

**14** I am a laughingstock to all my people; they mock me in song all day long. ^14

**15** He has filled me with bitterness; He has intoxicated me with wormwood. ^15

**16** He has ground my teeth with gravel and trampled me in the dust. ^16

**17** My soul has been deprived of peace; I have forgotten what prosperity is. ^17

**18** So I say, “My strength has perished, along with my hope from the LORD.” ^18

**19** Remember my affliction and wandering, the wormwood and the gall. ^19

**20** Surely my soul remembers and is humbled within me. ^20

**21** Yet I call this to mind, and therefore I have hope: ^21

**22** Because of the loving devotion of the LORD we are not consumed, for His mercies never fail. ^22

**23** They are new every morning; great is Your faithfulness! ^23

**24** “The LORD is my portion,” says my soul, “therefore I will hope in Him.” ^24

**25** The LORD is good to those who wait for Him, to the soul who seeks Him. ^25

**26** It is good to wait quietly for the salvation of the LORD. ^26

**27** It is good for a man to bear the yoke while he is still young. ^27

**28** Let him sit alone in silence, for the LORD has laid it upon him. ^28

**29** Let him bury his face in the dust—perhaps there is still hope. ^29

**30** Let him offer his cheek to the one who would strike him; let him be filled with reproach. ^30

**31** For the Lord will not cast us off forever. ^31

**32** Even if He causes grief, He will show compassion according to His abundant loving devotion. ^32

**33** For He does not willingly afflict or grieve the sons of men. ^33

**34** To crush underfoot all the prisoners of the land, ^34

**35** to deny a man justice before the Most High, ^35

**36** to subvert a man in his lawsuit—of these the Lord does not approve. ^36

**37** Who has spoken and it came to pass, unless the Lord has ordained it? ^37

**38** Do not both adversity and good come from the mouth of the Most High? ^38

**39** Why should any mortal man complain, in view of his sins? ^39

**40** Let us examine and test our ways, and turn back to the LORD. ^40

**41** Let us lift up our hearts and hands to God in heaven: ^41

**42** “We have sinned and rebelled; You have not forgiven.” ^42

**43** You have covered Yourself in anger and pursued us; You have killed without pity. ^43

**44** You have covered Yourself with a cloud that no prayer can pass through. ^44

**45** You have made us scum and refuse among the nations. ^45

**46** All our enemies open their mouths against us. ^46

**47** Panic and pitfall have come upon us—devastation and destruction. ^47

**48** Streams of tears flow from my eyes over the destruction of the daughter of my people. ^48

**49** My eyes overflow unceasingly, without relief, ^49

**50** until the LORD looks down from heaven and sees. ^50

**51** My eyes bring grief to my soul because of all the daughters of my city. ^51

**52** Without cause my enemies hunted me like a bird. ^52

**53** They dropped me alive into a pit and cast stones upon me. ^53

**54** The waters flowed over my head, and I thought I was going to die. ^54

**55** I called on Your name, O LORD, out of the depths of the Pit. ^55

**56** You heard my plea: “Do not ignore my cry for relief.” ^56

**57** You drew near when I called on You; You said, “Do not be afraid.” ^57

**58** You defend my cause, O Lord; You redeem my life. ^58

**59** You have seen, O LORD, the wrong done to me; vindicate my cause! ^59

**60** You have seen all their malice, all their plots against me. ^60

**61** O LORD, You have heard their insults, all their plots against me— ^61

**62** the slander and murmuring of my assailants against me all day long. ^62

**63** When they sit and when they rise, see how they mock me in song. ^63

**64** You will pay them back what they deserve, O LORD, according to the work of their hands. ^64

**65** Put a veil of anguish over their hearts; may Your curse be upon them! ^65

**66** You will pursue them in anger and exterminate them from under Your heavens, O LORD. ^66
