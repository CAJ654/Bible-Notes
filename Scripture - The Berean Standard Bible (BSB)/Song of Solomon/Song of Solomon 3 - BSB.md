# Song of Solomon 3 (BSB)

← [[Song of Solomon 2 - BSB]] | [[Song of Solomon 4 - BSB]] →

**1** On my bed at night I sought the one I love; I sought him, but did not find him. ^1

**2** I will arise now and go about the city, through the streets and squares. I will seek the one I love. So I sought him but did not find him. ^2

**3** I encountered the watchmen on their rounds of the city: “Have you seen the one I love?” ^3

**4** I had just passed them when I found the one I love. I held him and would not let go until I had brought him to my mother’s house, to the chamber of the one who conceived me. ^4

**5** O daughters of Jerusalem, I adjure you by the gazelles and does of the field: Do not arouse or awaken love until the time is right. ^5

**6** Who is this coming up from the wilderness like a column of smoke, scented with myrrh and frankincense from all the spices of the merchant? ^6

**7** Behold, it is Solomon’s carriage, escorted by sixty of the mightiest men of Israel. ^7

**8** All are skilled with the sword, experienced in warfare. Each has his sword at his side prepared for the terror of the night. ^8

**9** King Solomon has made his carriage out of the timber of Lebanon. ^9

**10** He has made its posts of silver, its base of gold, its seat of purple fabric. Its interior is inlaid with love by the daughters of Jerusalem. ^10

**11** Come out, O daughters of Zion, and gaze at King Solomon, wearing the crown with which his mother crowned him on the day of his wedding—the day of his heart’s rejoicing. ^11
