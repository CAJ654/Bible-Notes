# 1 Corinthians 16 (WEBU)

← [[1 Corinthians 15 - WEBU]]

**1** Now concerning the collection for the saints: as I commanded the assemblies of Galatia, you do likewise. ^1

**2** On the first day of every week, let each one of you save as he may prosper, that no collections are made when I come. ^2

**3** When I arrive, I will send whoever you approve with letters to carry your gracious gift to Jerusalem. ^3

**4** If it is appropriate for me to go also, they will go with me. ^4

**5** I will come to you when I have passed through Macedonia, for I am passing through Macedonia. ^5

**6** But with you it may be that I will stay with you, or even winter with you, that you may send me on my journey wherever I go. ^6

**7** For I do not wish to see you now in passing, but I hope to stay a while with you, if the Lord permits. ^7

**8** But I will stay at Ephesus until Pentecost, ^8

**9** for a great and effective door has opened to me, and there are many adversaries. ^9

**10** Now if Timothy comes, see that he is with you without fear, for he does the work of the Lord, as I also do. ^10

**11** Therefore let no one despise him. But set him forward on his journey in peace, that he may come to me; for I expect him with the brothers. ^11

**12** Now concerning Apollos the brother, I strongly urged him to come to you with the brothers, but it was not at all his desire to come now; but he will come when he has an opportunity. ^12

**13** Watch! Stand firm in the faith! Be courageous! Be strong! ^13

**14** Let all that you do be done in love. ^14

**15** Now I beg you, brothers—you know the house of Stephanas, that it is the first fruits of Achaia, and that they have set themselves to serve the saints— ^15

**16** that you also be in subjection to such, and to everyone who helps in the work and labors. ^16

**17** I rejoice at the coming of Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus; for that which was lacking on your part, they supplied. ^17

**18** For they refreshed my spirit and yours. Therefore acknowledge those who are like that. ^18

**19** The assemblies of Asia greet you. Aquila and Priscilla greet you warmly in the Lord, together with the assembly that is in their house. ^19

**20** All the brothers greet you. Greet one another with a holy kiss. ^20

**21** This greeting is by me, Paul, with my own hand. ^21

**22** If any man doesn’t love the Lord Jesus Christ, let him be cursed. Come, Lord! ^22

**23** The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with you. ^23

**24** My love to all of you in Christ Jesus. Amen. ^24
