# 1 Timothy 3 (WEBU)

← [[1 Timothy 2 - WEBU]] | [[1 Timothy 4 - WEBU]] →

**1** This is a faithful saying: someone who seeks to be an overseer desires a good work. ^1

**2** The overseer therefore must be without reproach, the husband of one wife, temperate, sensible, modest, hospitable, good at teaching; ^2

**3** not a drinker, not violent, not greedy for money, but gentle, not quarrelsome, not covetous; ^3

**4** one who rules his own house well, having children in subjection with all reverence; ^4

**5** (for how could someone who doesn’t know how to rule his own house take care of God’s assembly?) ^5

**6** not a new convert, lest being puffed up he fall into the same condemnation as the devil. ^6

**7** Moreover he must have good testimony from those who are outside, to avoid falling into reproach and the snare of the devil. ^7

**8** Servants, in the same way, must be reverent, not double-tongued, not addicted to much wine, not greedy for money, ^8

**9** holding the mystery of the faith in a pure conscience. ^9

**10** Let them also first be tested; then let them serve if they are blameless. ^10

**11** Their wives in the same way must be reverent, not slanderers, temperate, and faithful in all things. ^11

**12** Let servants be husbands of one wife, ruling their children and their own houses well. ^12

**13** For those who have served well gain for themselves a good standing and great boldness in the faith which is in Christ Jesus. ^13

**14** These things I write to you, hoping to come to you shortly, ^14

**15** but if I wait long, that you may know how men ought to behave themselves in God’s house, which is the assembly of the living God, the pillar and ground of the truth. ^15

**16** Without controversy, the mystery of godliness is great: God was revealed in the flesh, justified in the spirit, seen by angels, preached among the nations, believed on in the world, and received up in glory. ^16
