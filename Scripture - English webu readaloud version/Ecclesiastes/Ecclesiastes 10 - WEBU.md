# Ecclesiastes 10 (WEBU)

← [[Ecclesiastes 9 - WEBU]] | [[Ecclesiastes 11 - WEBU]] →

**1** Dead flies cause the oil of the perfumer to produce an evil odor; so does a little folly outweigh wisdom and honor. ^1

**2** A wise man’s heart is at his right hand, but a fool’s heart at his left. ^2

**3** Yes also when the fool walks by the way, his understanding fails him, and he says to everyone that he is a fool. ^3

**4** If the spirit of the ruler rises up against you, don’t leave your place; for gentleness lays great offenses to rest. ^4

**5** There is an evil which I have seen under the sun, the sort of error which proceeds from the ruler. ^5

**6** Folly is set in great dignity, and the rich sit in a low place. ^6

**7** I have seen servants on horses, and princes walking like servants on the earth. ^7

**8** He who digs a pit may fall into it; and whoever breaks through a wall may be bitten by a snake. ^8

**9** Whoever carves out stones may be injured by them. Whoever splits wood may be endangered by it. ^9

**10** If the ax is blunt, and one doesn’t sharpen the edge, then he must use more strength; but skill brings success. ^10

**11** If the snake bites before it is charmed, then is there no profit for the charmer’s tongue. ^11

**12** The words of a wise man’s mouth are gracious; but a fool is swallowed by his own lips. ^12

**13** The beginning of the words of his mouth is foolishness; and the end of his talk is mischievous madness. ^13

**14** A fool also multiplies words. Man doesn’t know what will be; and that which will be after him, who can tell him? ^14

**15** The labor of fools wearies every one of them; for he doesn’t know how to go to the city. ^15

**16** Woe to you, land, when your king is a child, and your princes eat in the morning! ^16

**17** Happy are you, land, when your king is the son of nobles, and your princes eat in due season, for strength, and not for drunkenness! ^17

**18** By slothfulness the roof sinks in; and through idleness of the hands the house leaks. ^18

**19** A feast is made for laughter, and wine makes the life glad; and money is the answer for all things. ^19

**20** Don’t curse the king, no, not in your thoughts; and don’t curse the rich in your bedroom, for a bird of the sky may carry your voice, and that which has wings may tell the matter. ^20
