# Genesis 5 (WEBU)

← [[Genesis 4 - WEBU]] | [[Genesis 6 - WEBU]] →

**1** This is the book of the generations of Adam. In the day that God created man, he made him in God’s likeness. ^1

**2** He created them male and female, and blessed them. On the day they were created, he named them Adam. ^2

**3** Adam lived one hundred thirty years, and became the father of a son in his own likeness, after his image, and named him Seth. ^3

**4** The days of Adam after he became the father of Seth were eight hundred years, and he became the father of other sons and daughters. ^4

**5** All the days that Adam lived were nine hundred thirty years, then he died. ^5

**6** Seth lived one hundred five years, then became the father of Enosh. ^6

**7** Seth lived after he became the father of Enosh eight hundred seven years, and became the father of other sons and daughters. ^7

**8** All of the days of Seth were nine hundred twelve years, then he died. ^8

**9** Enosh lived ninety years, and became the father of Kenan. ^9

**10** Enosh lived after he became the father of Kenan eight hundred fifteen years, and became the father of other sons and daughters. ^10

**11** All of the days of Enosh were nine hundred five years, then he died. ^11

**12** Kenan lived seventy years, then became the father of Mahalalel. ^12

**13** Kenan lived after he became the father of Mahalalel eight hundred forty years, and became the father of other sons and daughters ^13

**14** and all of the days of Kenan were nine hundred ten years, then he died. ^14

**15** Mahalalel lived sixty-five years, then became the father of Jared. ^15

**16** Mahalalel lived after he became the father of Jared eight hundred thirty years, and became the father of other sons and daughters. ^16

**17** All of the days of Mahalalel were eight hundred ninety-five years, then he died. ^17

**18** Jared lived one hundred sixty-two years, then became the father of Enoch. ^18

**19** Jared lived after he became the father of Enoch eight hundred years, and became the father of other sons and daughters. ^19

**20** All of the days of Jared were nine hundred sixty-two years, then he died. ^20

**21** Enoch lived sixty-five years, then became the father of Methuselah. ^21

**22** After Methuselah’s birth, Enoch walked with God for three hundred years, and became the father of more sons and daughters. ^22

**23** All the days of Enoch were three hundred sixty-five years. ^23

**24** Enoch walked with God, and he was not found, for God took him. ^24

**25** Methuselah lived one hundred eighty-seven years, then became the father of Lamech. ^25

**26** Methuselah lived after he became the father of Lamech seven hundred eighty-two years, and became the father of other sons and daughters. ^26

**27** All the days of Methuselah were nine hundred sixty-nine years, then he died. ^27

**28** Lamech lived one hundred eighty-two years, then became the father of a son. ^28

**29** He named him Noah, saying, “This one will comfort us in our work and in the toil of our hands, caused by the ground which the LORD has cursed.” ^29

**30** Lamech lived after he became the father of Noah five hundred ninety-five years, and became the father of other sons and daughters. ^30

**31** All the days of Lamech were seven hundred seventy-seven years, then he died. ^31

**32** Noah was five hundred years old, then Noah became the father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth. ^32
