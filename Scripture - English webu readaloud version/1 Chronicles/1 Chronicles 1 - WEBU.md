# 1 Chronicles 1 (WEBU)

[[1 Chronicles 2 - WEBU]] →

**1** Adam, Seth, Enosh, ^1

**2** Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared, ^2

**3** Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech, ^3

**4** Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth. ^4

**5** The sons of Japheth: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. ^5

**6** The sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, Diphath, and Togarmah. ^6

**7** The sons of Javan: Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Rodanim. ^7

**8** The sons of Ham: Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan. ^8

**9** The sons of Cush: Seba, Havilah, Sabta, Raama, Sabteca. The sons of Raamah: Sheba and Dedan. ^9

**10** Cush became the father of Nimrod. He began to be a mighty one on the earth. ^10

**11** Mizraim became the father of Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, Naphtuhim, ^11

**12** Pathrusim, Casluhim (where the Philistines came from), and Caphtorim. ^12

**13** Canaan became the father of Sidon his firstborn, Heth, ^13

**14** the Jebusite, the Amorite, the Girgashite, ^14

**15** the Hivite, the Arkite, the Sinite, ^15

**16** the Arvadite, the Zemarite, and the Hamathite. ^16

**17** The sons of Shem: Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, Aram, Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech. ^17

**18** Arpachshad became the father of Shelah, and Shelah became the father of Eber. ^18

**19** To Eber were born two sons: the name of the one was Peleg, for in his days the earth was divided; and his brother’s name was Joktan. ^19

**20** Joktan became the father of Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah, ^20

**21** Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah, ^21

**22** Ebal, Abimael, Sheba, ^22

**23** Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan. ^23

**24** Shem, Arpachshad, Shelah, ^24

**25** Eber, Peleg, Reu, ^25

**26** Serug, Nahor, Terah, ^26

**27** Abram (also called Abraham). ^27

**28** The sons of Abraham: Isaac and Ishmael. ^28

**29** These are their generations: the firstborn of Ishmael, Nebaioth; then Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam, ^29

**30** Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema, ^30

**31** Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These are the sons of Ishmael. ^31

**32** The sons of Keturah, Abraham’s concubine: she bore Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. The sons of Jokshan: Sheba and Dedan. ^32

**33** The sons of Midian: Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All these were the sons of Keturah. ^33

**34** Abraham became the father of Isaac. The sons of Isaac: Esau and Israel. ^34

**35** The sons of Esau: Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam, and Korah. ^35

**36** The sons of Eliphaz: Teman, Omar, Zephi, Gatam, Kenaz, Timna, and Amalek. ^36

**37** The sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. ^37

**38** The sons of Seir: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan. ^38

**39** The sons of Lotan: Hori and Homam; and Timna was Lotan’s sister. ^39

**40** The sons of Shobal: Alian, Manahath, Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. The sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Anah. ^40

**41** The son of Anah: Dishon. The sons of Dishon: Hamran, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran. ^41

**42** The sons of Ezer: Bilhan, Zaavan, and Jaakan. The sons of Dishan: Uz and Aran. ^42

**43** Now these are the kings who reigned in the land of Edom, before any king reigned over the children of Israel: Bela the son of Beor; and the name of his city was Dinhabah. ^43

**44** Bela died, and Jobab the son of Zerah of Bozrah reigned in his place. ^44

**45** Jobab died, and Husham of the land of the Temanites reigned in his place. ^45

**46** Husham died, and Hadad the son of Bedad, who struck Midian in the field of Moab, reigned in his place; and the name of his city was Avith. ^46

**47** Hadad died, and Samlah of Masrekah reigned in his place. ^47

**48** Samlah died, and Shaul of Rehoboth by the River reigned in his place. ^48

**49** Shaul died, and Baal Hanan the son of Achbor reigned in his place. ^49

**50** Baal Hanan died, and Hadad reigned in his place; and the name of his city was Pai. His wife’s name was Mehetabel, the daughter of Matred, the daughter of Mezahab. ^50

**51** Then Hadad died. The chiefs of Edom were: chief Timna, chief Aliah, chief Jetheth, ^51

**52** chief Oholibamah, chief Elah, chief Pinon, ^52

**53** chief Kenaz, chief Teman, chief Mibzar, ^53

**54** chief Magdiel, and chief Iram. These are the chiefs of Edom. ^54
