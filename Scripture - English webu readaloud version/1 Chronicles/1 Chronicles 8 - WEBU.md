# 1 Chronicles 8 (WEBU)

← [[1 Chronicles 7 - WEBU]] | [[1 Chronicles 9 - WEBU]] →

**1** Benjamin became the father of Bela his firstborn, Ashbel the second, Aharah the third, ^1

**2** Nohah the fourth, and Rapha the fifth. ^2

**3** Bela had sons: Addar, Gera, Abihud, ^3

**4** Abishua, Naaman, Ahoah, ^4

**5** Gera, Shephuphan, and Huram. ^5

**6** These are the sons of Ehud. These are the heads of fathers’ households of the inhabitants of Geba, who were carried captive to Manahath: ^6

**7** Naaman, Ahijah, and Gera, who carried them captive; and he became the father of Uzza and Ahihud. ^7

**8** Shaharaim became the father of children in the field of Moab, after he had sent them away. Hushim and Baara were his wives. ^8

**9** By Hodesh his wife, he became the father of Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, Malcam, ^9

**10** Jeuz, Shachia, and Mirmah. These were his sons, heads of fathers’ households. ^10

**11** By Hushim, he became the father of Abitub and Elpaal. ^11

**12** The sons of Elpaal: Eber, Misham, and Shemed, who built Ono and Lod, with its towns; ^12

**13** and Beriah and Shema, who were heads of fathers’ households of the inhabitants of Aijalon, who put to flight the inhabitants of Gath; ^13

**14** and Ahio, Shashak, Jeremoth, ^14

**15** Zebadiah, Arad, Eder, ^15

**16** Michael, Ishpah, Joha, the sons of Beriah, ^16

**17** Zebadiah, Meshullam, Hizki, Heber, ^17

**18** Ishmerai, Izliah, Jobab, the sons of Elpaal, ^18

**19** Jakim, Zichri, Zabdi, ^19

**20** Elienai, Zillethai, Eliel, ^20

**21** Adaiah, Beraiah, Shimrath, the sons of Shimei, ^21

**22** Ishpan, Eber, Eliel, ^22

**23** Abdon, Zichri, Hanan, ^23

**24** Hananiah, Elam, Anthothijah, ^24

**25** Iphdeiah, Penuel, the sons of Shashak, ^25

**26** Shamsherai, Shehariah, Athaliah, ^26

**27** Jaareshiah, Elijah, Zichri, and the sons of Jeroham. ^27

**28** These were heads of fathers’ households throughout their generations, chief men. These lived in Jerusalem. ^28

**29** The father of Gibeon, whose wife’s name was Maacah, lived in Gibeon ^29

**30** with his firstborn son Abdon, Zur, Kish, Baal, Nadab, ^30

**31** Gedor, Ahio, Zecher, ^31

**32** and Mikloth, who became the father of Shimeah. They also lived with their families in Jerusalem, near their relatives. ^32

**33** Ner became the father of Kish. Kish became the father of Saul. Saul became the father of Jonathan, Malchishua, Abinadab, and Eshbaal. ^33

**34** The son of Jonathan was Merib-baal. Merib-baal became the father of Micah. ^34

**35** The sons of Micah: Pithon, Melech, Tarea, and Ahaz. ^35

**36** Ahaz became the father of Jehoaddah. Jehoaddah became the father of Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri. Zimri became the father of Moza. ^36

**37** Moza became the father of Binea. Raphah was his son, Eleasah his son, and Azel his son. ^37

**38** Azel had six sons, whose names are these: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. All these were the sons of Azel. ^38

**39** The sons of Eshek his brother: Ulam his firstborn, Jeush the second, and Eliphelet the third. ^39

**40** The sons of Ulam were mighty men of valor, archers, and had many sons, and grandsons, one hundred fifty. All these were of the sons of Benjamin. ^40
