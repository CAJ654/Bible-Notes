# 2 Corinthians 6 (WEBU)

← [[2 Corinthians 5 - WEBU]] | [[2 Corinthians 7 - WEBU]] →

**1** Working together, we entreat also that you do not receive the grace of God in vain. ^1

**2** For he says, “At an acceptable time I listened to you. In a day of salvation I helped you.” Behold, now is the acceptable time. Behold, now is the day of salvation. ^2

**3** We give no occasion of stumbling in anything, that our service may not be blamed, ^3

**4** but in everything commending ourselves as servants of God: in great endurance, in afflictions, in hardships, in distresses, ^4

**5** in beatings, in imprisonments, in riots, in labors, in watchings, in fastings, ^5

**6** in pureness, in knowledge, in perseverance, in kindness, in the Holy Spirit, in sincere love, ^6

**7** in the word of truth, in the power of God, by the armor of righteousness on the right hand and on the left, ^7

**8** by glory and dishonor, by evil report and good report, as deceivers and yet true, ^8

**9** as unknown and yet well known, as dying and behold—we live, as punished and not killed, ^9

**10** as sorrowful yet always rejoicing, as poor yet making many rich, as having nothing and yet possessing all things. ^10

**11** Our mouth is open to you, Corinthians. Our heart is enlarged. ^11

**12** You are not restricted by us, but you are restricted by your own affections. ^12

**13** Now in return—I speak as to my children—you also open your hearts. ^13

**14** Don’t be unequally yoked with unbelievers, for what fellowship do righteousness and iniquity have? Or what fellowship does light have with darkness? ^14

**15** What agreement does Christ have with Belial? Or what portion does a believer have with an unbeliever? ^15

**16** What agreement does a temple of God have with idols? For you are a temple of the living God. Even as God said, “I will dwell in them and walk in them. I will be their God and they will be my people.” ^16

**17** Therefore “‘Come out from among them, and be separate,’ says the Lord. ‘Touch no unclean thing. I will receive you. ^17

**18** I will be to you a Father. You will be to me sons and daughters,’ says the Lord Almighty.” ^18
