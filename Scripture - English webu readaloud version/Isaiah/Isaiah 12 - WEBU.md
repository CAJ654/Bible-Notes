# Isaiah 12 (WEBU)

← [[Isaiah 11 - WEBU]] | [[Isaiah 13 - WEBU]] →

**1** In that day you will say, “I will give thanks to you, the LORD; for though you were angry with me, your anger has turned away and you comfort me. ^1

**2** Behold, God is my salvation. I will trust, and will not be afraid; for the LORD, the LORD, is my strength and song; and he has become my salvation.” ^2

**3** Therefore with joy you will draw water out of the wells of salvation. ^3

**4** In that day you will say, “Give thanks to the LORD! Call on his name! Declare his doings among the peoples! Proclaim that his name is exalted! ^4

**5** Sing to the LORD, for he has done excellent things! Let this be known in all the earth! ^5

**6** Cry aloud and shout, you inhabitant of Zion, for the Holy One of Israel is great among you!” ^6
