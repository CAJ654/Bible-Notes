# Isaiah 64 (WEBU)

← [[Isaiah 63 - WEBU]] | [[Isaiah 65 - WEBU]] →

**1** Oh that you would tear the heavens, that you would come down, that the mountains might quake at your presence— ^1

**2** as when fire kindles the brushwood, and the fire causes the water to boil. Make your name known to your adversaries, that the nations may tremble at your presence! ^2

**3** When you did awesome things which we didn’t look for, you came down, and the mountains quaked at your presence. ^3

**4** For from of old men have not heard, nor perceived by the ear, nor has the eye seen a God besides you, who works for him who waits for him. ^4

**5** You meet him who rejoices and does righteousness, those who remember you in your ways. Behold, you were angry, and we sinned. We have been in sin for a long time. Shall we be saved? ^5

**6** For we have all become like one who is unclean, and all our righteousness is like a polluted garment. We all fade like a leaf; and our iniquities, like the wind, take us away. ^6

**7** There is no one who calls on your name, who stirs himself up to take hold of you; for you have hidden your face from us, and have consumed us by means of our iniquities. ^7

**8** But now, LORD, you are our Father. We are the clay and you our potter. We all are the work of your hand. ^8

**9** Don’t be furious, LORD. Don’t remember iniquity forever. Look and see, we beg you, we are all your people. ^9

**10** Your holy cities have become a wilderness. Zion has become a wilderness, Jerusalem a desolation. ^10

**11** Our holy and our beautiful house where our fathers praised you is burned with fire. All our pleasant places are laid waste. ^11

**12** Will you hold yourself back for these things, LORD? Will you keep silent and punish us very severely? ^12
