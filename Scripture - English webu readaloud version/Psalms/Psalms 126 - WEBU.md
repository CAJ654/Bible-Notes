# Psalms 126 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 125 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 127 - WEBU]] →

**1** A Song of Ascents. When the LORD brought back those who returned to Zion, we were like those who dream. ^1

**2** Then our mouth was filled with laughter, and our tongue with singing. Then they said among the nations, “The LORD has done great things for them.” ^2

**3** The LORD has done great things for us, and we are glad. ^3

**4** Restore our fortunes again, LORD, like the streams in the Negev. ^4

**5** Those who sow in tears will reap in joy. ^5

**6** He who goes out weeping, carrying seed for sowing, will certainly come again with joy, carrying his sheaves. ^6
