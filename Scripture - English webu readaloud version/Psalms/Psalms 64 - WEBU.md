# Psalms 64 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 63 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 65 - WEBU]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. A Psalm by David. Hear my voice, God, in my complaint. Preserve my life from fear of the enemy. ^1

**2** Hide me from the conspiracy of the wicked, from the noisy crowd of the ones doing evil; ^2

**3** who sharpen their tongue like a sword, and aim their arrows, deadly words, ^3

**4** to shoot innocent men from ambushes. They shoot at him suddenly and fearlessly. ^4

**5** They encourage themselves in evil plans. They talk about laying snares secretly. They say, “Who will see them?” ^5

**6** They plot injustice, saying, “We have made a perfect plan!” Surely man’s mind and heart are cunning. ^6

**7** But God will shoot at them. They will be suddenly struck down with an arrow. ^7

**8** Their own tongues shall ruin them. All who see them will shake their heads. ^8

**9** All mankind shall be afraid. They shall declare the work of God, and shall wisely ponder what he has done. ^9

**10** The righteous shall be glad in the LORD, and shall take refuge in him. All the upright in heart shall praise him! ^10
