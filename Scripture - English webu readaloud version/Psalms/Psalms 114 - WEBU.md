# Psalms 114 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 113 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 115 - WEBU]] →

**1** When Israel went out of Egypt, the house of Jacob from a people of foreign language, ^1

**2** Judah became his sanctuary, Israel his dominion. ^2

**3** The sea saw it, and fled. The Jordan was driven back. ^3

**4** The mountains skipped like rams, the little hills like lambs. ^4

**5** What was it, you sea, that you fled? You Jordan, that you turned back? ^5

**6** You mountains, that you skipped like rams? You little hills, like lambs? ^6

**7** Tremble, you earth, at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the God of Jacob, ^7

**8** who turned the rock into a pool of water, the flint into a spring of waters. ^8
