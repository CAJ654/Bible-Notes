# Psalms 146 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 145 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 147 - WEBU]] →

**1** Praise the LORD! Praise the LORD, my soul. ^1

**2** While I live, I will praise the LORD. I will sing praises to my God as long as I exist. ^2

**3** Don’t put your trust in princes, in a son of man in whom there is no help. ^3

**4** His spirit departs, and he returns to the earth. In that very day, his thoughts perish. ^4

**5** Happy is he who has the God of Jacob for his help, whose hope is in the LORD, his God, ^5

**6** who made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that is in them; who keeps truth forever; ^6

**7** who executes justice for the oppressed; who gives food to the hungry. The LORD frees the prisoners. ^7

**8** The LORD opens the eyes of the blind. The LORD raises up those who are bowed down. The LORD loves the righteous. ^8

**9** The LORD preserves the foreigners. He upholds the fatherless and widow, but he turns the way of the wicked upside down. ^9

**10** The LORD will reign forever; your God, O Zion, to all generations. Praise the LORD! ^10
