# Psalms 33 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 32 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 34 - WEBU]] →

**1** Rejoice in the LORD, you righteous! Praise is fitting for the upright. ^1

**2** Give thanks to the LORD with the lyre. Sing praises to him with the harp of ten strings. ^2

**3** Sing to him a new song. Play skillfully with a shout of joy! ^3

**4** For the LORD’s word is right. All his work is done in faithfulness. ^4

**5** He loves righteousness and justice. The earth is full of the loving kindness of the LORD. ^5

**6** By the LORD’s word, the heavens were made: all their army by the breath of his mouth. ^6

**7** He gathers the waters of the sea together as a heap. He lays up the deeps in storehouses. ^7

**8** Let all the earth fear the LORD. Let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of him. ^8

**9** For he spoke, and it was done. He commanded, and it stood firm. ^9

**10** The LORD brings the counsel of the nations to nothing. He makes the thoughts of the peoples to be of no effect. ^10

**11** The counsel of the LORD stands fast forever, the thoughts of his heart to all generations. ^11

**12** Blessed is the nation whose God is the LORD, the people whom he has chosen for his own inheritance. ^12

**13** The LORD looks from heaven. He sees all the sons of men. ^13

**14** From the place of his habitation he looks out on all the inhabitants of the earth, ^14

**15** he who fashions all of their hearts; and he considers all of their works. ^15

**16** There is no king saved by the multitude of an army. A mighty man is not delivered by great strength. ^16

**17** A horse is a vain thing for safety, neither does he deliver any by his great power. ^17

**18** Behold, the LORD’s eye is on those who fear him, on those who hope in his loving kindness, ^18

**19** to deliver their soul from death, to keep them alive in famine. ^19

**20** Our soul has waited for the LORD. He is our help and our shield. ^20

**21** For our heart rejoices in him, because we have trusted in his holy name. ^21

**22** Let your loving kindness be on us, LORD, since we have hoped in you. ^22
