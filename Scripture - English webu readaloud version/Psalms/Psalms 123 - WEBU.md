# Psalms 123 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 122 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 124 - WEBU]] →

**1** A Song of Ascents. I lift up my eyes to you, you who sit in the heavens. ^1

**2** Behold, as the eyes of servants look to the hand of their master, as the eyes of a maid to the hand of her mistress, so our eyes look to the LORD, our God, until he has mercy on us. ^2

**3** Have mercy on us, LORD, have mercy on us, for we have endured much contempt. ^3

**4** Our soul is exceedingly filled with the scoffing of those who are at ease, with the contempt of the proud. ^4
