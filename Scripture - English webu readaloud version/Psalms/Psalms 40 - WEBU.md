# Psalms 40 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 39 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 41 - WEBU]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. A Psalm by David. I waited patiently for the LORD. He turned to me, and heard my cry. ^1

**2** He brought me up also out of a horrible pit, out of the miry clay. He set my feet on a rock, and gave me a firm place to stand. ^2

**3** He has put a new song in my mouth, even praise to our God. Many shall see it, and fear, and shall trust in the LORD. ^3

**4** Blessed is the man who makes the LORD his trust, and doesn’t respect the proud, nor such as turn away to lies. ^4

**5** Many, LORD, my God, are the wonderful works which you have done, and your thoughts which are toward us. They can’t be declared back to you. If I would declare and speak of them, they are more than can be counted. ^5

**6** Sacrifice and offering you didn’t desire. You have opened my ears. You have not required burnt offering and sin offering. ^6

**7** Then I said, “Behold, I have come. It is written about me in the book in the scroll. ^7

**8** I delight to do your will, my God. Yes, your law is within my heart.” ^8

**9** I have proclaimed glad news of righteousness in the great assembly. Behold, I will not seal my lips, LORD, you know. ^9

**10** I have not hidden your righteousness within my heart. I have declared your faithfulness and your salvation. I have not concealed your loving kindness and your truth from the great assembly. ^10

**11** Don’t withhold your tender mercies from me, LORD. Let your loving kindness and your truth continually preserve me. ^11

**12** For innumerable evils have surrounded me. My iniquities have overtaken me, so that I am not able to look up. They are more than the hairs of my head. My heart has failed me. ^12

**13** Be pleased, LORD, to deliver me. Hurry to help me, LORD. ^13

**14** Let them be disappointed and confounded together who seek after my soul to destroy it. Let them be turned backward and brought to dishonor who delight in my hurt. ^14

**15** Let them be desolate by reason of their shame that tell me, “Aha! Aha!” ^15

**16** Let all those who seek you rejoice and be glad in you. Let such as love your salvation say continually, “Let the LORD be exalted!” ^16

**17** But I am poor and needy. May the Lord think about me. You are my help and my deliverer. Don’t delay, my God. ^17
