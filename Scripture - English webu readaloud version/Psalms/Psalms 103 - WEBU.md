# Psalms 103 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 102 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 104 - WEBU]] →

**1** By David. Praise the LORD, my soul! All that is within me, praise his holy name! ^1

**2** Praise the LORD, my soul, and don’t forget all his benefits, ^2

**3** who forgives all your sins, who heals all your diseases, ^3

**4** who redeems your life from destruction, who crowns you with loving kindness and tender mercies, ^4

**5** who satisfies your desire with good things, so that your youth is renewed like the eagle’s. ^5

**6** The LORD executes righteous acts, and justice for all who are oppressed. ^6

**7** He made known his ways to Moses, his deeds to the children of Israel. ^7

**8** The LORD is merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and abundant in loving kindness. ^8

**9** He will not always accuse; neither will he stay angry forever. ^9

**10** He has not dealt with us according to our sins, nor repaid us for our iniquities. ^10

**11** For as the heavens are high above the earth, so great is his loving kindness toward those who fear him. ^11

**12** As far as the east is from the west, so far has he removed our transgressions from us. ^12

**13** Like a father has compassion on his children, so the LORD has compassion on those who fear him. ^13

**14** For he knows how we are made. He remembers that we are dust. ^14

**15** As for man, his days are like grass. As a flower of the field, so he flourishes. ^15

**16** For the wind passes over it, and it is gone. Its place remembers it no more. ^16

**17** But the LORD’s loving kindness is from everlasting to everlasting with those who fear him, his righteousness to children’s children, ^17

**18** to those who keep his covenant, to those who remember to obey his precepts. ^18

**19** The LORD has established his throne in the heavens. His kingdom rules over all. ^19

**20** Praise the LORD, you angels of his, who are mighty in strength, who fulfill his word, obeying the voice of his word. ^20

**21** Praise the LORD, all you armies of his, you servants of his, who do his pleasure. ^21

**22** Praise the LORD, all you works of his, in all places of his dominion. Praise the LORD, my soul! ^22
