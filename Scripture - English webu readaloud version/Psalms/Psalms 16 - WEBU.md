# Psalms 16 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 15 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 17 - WEBU]] →

**1** A Poem by David. Preserve me, God, for I take refuge in you. ^1

**2** My soul, you have said to the LORD, “You are my Lord. Apart from you I have no good thing.” ^2

**3** As for the saints who are on the earth, they are the excellent ones in whom is all my delight. ^3

**4** Their sorrows shall be multiplied who give gifts to another god. Their drink offerings of blood I will not offer, nor take their names on my lips. ^4

**5** The LORD assigned my portion and my cup. You made my lot secure. ^5

**6** The lines have fallen to me in pleasant places. Yes, I have a good inheritance. ^6

**7** I will bless the LORD, who has given me counsel. Yes, my heart instructs me in the night seasons. ^7

**8** I have set the LORD always before me. Because he is at my right hand, I shall not be moved. ^8

**9** Therefore my heart is glad, and my tongue rejoices. My body shall also dwell in safety. ^9

**10** For you will not leave my soul in Sheol, neither will you allow your holy one to see corruption. ^10

**11** You will show me the path of life. In your presence is fullness of joy. In your right hand there are pleasures forever more. ^11
