# Psalms 150 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 149 - WEBU]]

**1** Praise the LORD! Praise God in his sanctuary! Praise him in his heavens for his acts of power! ^1

**2** Praise him for his mighty acts! Praise him according to his excellent greatness! ^2

**3** Praise him with the sounding of the trumpet! Praise him with harp and lyre! ^3

**4** Praise him with tambourine and dancing! Praise him with stringed instruments and flute! ^4

**5** Praise him with loud cymbals! Praise him with resounding cymbals! ^5

**6** Let everything that has breath praise the LORD! Praise the LORD! ^6
