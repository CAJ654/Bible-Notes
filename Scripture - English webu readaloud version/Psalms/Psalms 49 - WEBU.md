# Psalms 49 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 48 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 50 - WEBU]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. A Psalm by the sons of Korah. Hear this, all you peoples. Listen, all you inhabitants of the world, ^1

**2** both low and high, rich and poor together. ^2

**3** My mouth will speak words of wisdom. My heart will utter understanding. ^3

**4** I will incline my ear to a proverb. I will solve my riddle on the harp. ^4

**5** Why should I fear in the days of evil, when iniquity at my heels surrounds me? ^5

**6** Those who trust in their wealth, and boast in the multitude of their riches— ^6

**7** none of them can by any means redeem his brother, nor give God a ransom for him. ^7

**8** For the redemption of their life is costly, no payment is ever enough, ^8

**9** that he should live on forever, that he should not see corruption. ^9

**10** For he sees that wise men die; likewise the fool and the senseless perish, and leave their wealth to others. ^10

**11** Their inward thought is that their houses will endure forever, and their dwelling places to all generations. They name their lands after themselves. ^11

**12** But man, despite his riches, doesn’t endure. He is like the animals that perish. ^12

**13** This is the destiny of those who are foolish, and of those who approve their sayings. Selah. ^13

**14** They are appointed as a flock for Sheol. Death shall be their shepherd. The upright shall have dominion over them in the morning. Their beauty shall decay in Sheol, far from their mansion. ^14

**15** But God will redeem my soul from the power of Sheol, for he will receive me. Selah. ^15

**16** Don’t be afraid when a man is made rich, when the glory of his house is increased; ^16

**17** for when he dies he will carry nothing away. His glory won’t descend after him. ^17

**18** Though while he lived he blessed his soul— and men praise you when you do well for yourself— ^18

**19** he shall go to the generation of his fathers. They shall never see the light. ^19

**20** A man who has riches without understanding, is like the animals that perish. ^20
