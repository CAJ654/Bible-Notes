# Psalms 13 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 12 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 14 - WEBU]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. A Psalm by David. How long, LORD? Will you forget me forever? How long will you hide your face from me? ^1

**2** How long shall I take counsel in my soul, having sorrow in my heart every day? How long shall my enemy triumph over me? ^2

**3** Behold, and answer me, LORD, my God. Give light to my eyes, lest I sleep in death; ^3

**4** lest my enemy say, “I have prevailed against him;” lest my adversaries rejoice when I fall. ^4

**5** But I trust in your loving kindness. My heart rejoices in your salvation. ^5

**6** I will sing to the LORD, because he has been good to me. ^6
