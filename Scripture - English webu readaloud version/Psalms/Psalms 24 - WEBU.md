# Psalms 24 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 23 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 25 - WEBU]] →

**1** A Psalm by David. The earth is the LORD’s, with its fullness; the world, and those who dwell in it. ^1

**2** For he has founded it on the seas, and established it on the floods. ^2

**3** Who may ascend to the LORD’s hill? Who may stand in his holy place? ^3

**4** He who has clean hands and a pure heart; who has not lifted up his soul to falsehood, and has not sworn deceitfully. ^4

**5** He shall receive a blessing from the LORD, righteousness from the God of his salvation. ^5

**6** This is the generation of those who seek Him, who seek your face—even Jacob. Selah. ^6

**7** Lift up your heads, you gates! Be lifted up, you everlasting doors, and the King of glory will come in. ^7

**8** Who is the King of glory? The LORD strong and mighty, the LORD mighty in battle. ^8

**9** Lift up your heads, you gates; yes, lift them up, you everlasting doors, and the King of glory will come in. ^9

**10** Who is this King of glory? The LORD of Armies is the King of glory! Selah. ^10
