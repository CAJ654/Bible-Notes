# Psalms 32 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 31 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 33 - WEBU]] →

**1** By David. A contemplative psalm. Blessed is he whose disobedience is forgiven, whose sin is covered. ^1

**2** Blessed is the man to whom the LORD doesn’t impute iniquity, in whose spirit there is no deceit. ^2

**3** When I kept silence, my bones wasted away through my groaning all day long. ^3

**4** For day and night your hand was heavy on me. My strength was sapped in the heat of summer. Selah. ^4

**5** I acknowledged my sin to you. I didn’t hide my iniquity. I said, I will confess my transgressions to the LORD, and you forgave the iniquity of my sin. Selah. ^5

**6** For this, let everyone who is godly pray to you in a time when you may be found. Surely when the great waters overflow, they shall not reach to him. ^6

**7** You are my hiding place. You will preserve me from trouble. You will surround me with songs of deliverance. Selah. ^7

**8** I will instruct you and teach you in the way which you shall go. I will counsel you with my eye on you. ^8

**9** Don’t be like the horse, or like the mule, which have no understanding, who are controlled by bit and bridle, or else they will not come near to you. ^9

**10** Many sorrows come to the wicked, but loving kindness shall surround him who trusts in the LORD. ^10

**11** Be glad in the LORD, and rejoice, you righteous! Shout for joy, all you who are upright in heart! ^11
