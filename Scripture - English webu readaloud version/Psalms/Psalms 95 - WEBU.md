# Psalms 95 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 94 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 96 - WEBU]] →

**1** Oh come, let’s sing to the LORD. Let’s shout aloud to the rock of our salvation! ^1

**2** Let’s come before his presence with thanksgiving. Let’s extol him with songs! ^2

**3** For the LORD is a great God, a great King above all gods. ^3

**4** In his hand are the deep places of the earth. The heights of the mountains are also his. ^4

**5** The sea is his, and he made it. His hands formed the dry land. ^5

**6** Oh come, let’s worship and bow down. Let’s kneel before the LORD, our Maker, ^6

**7** for he is our God. We are the people of his pasture, and the sheep in his care. Today, oh that you would hear his voice! ^7

**8** Don’t harden your heart, as at Meribah, as in the day of Massah in the wilderness, ^8

**9** when your fathers tempted me, tested me, and saw my work. ^9

**10** Forty long years I was grieved with that generation, and said, “They are a people who err in their heart. They have not known my ways.” ^10

**11** Therefore I swore in my wrath, “They won’t enter into my rest.” ^11
