# Psalms 106 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 105 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 107 - WEBU]] →

**1** Praise the LORD! Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, for his loving kindness endures forever. ^1

**2** Who can utter the mighty acts of the LORD, or fully declare all his praise? ^2

**3** Blessed are those who keep justice. Blessed is one who does what is right at all times. ^3

**4** Remember me, LORD, with the favor that you show to your people. Visit me with your salvation, ^4

**5** that I may see the prosperity of your chosen, that I may rejoice in the gladness of your nation, that I may glory with your inheritance. ^5

**6** We have sinned with our fathers. We have committed iniquity. We have done wickedly. ^6

**7** Our fathers didn’t understand your wonders in Egypt. They didn’t remember the multitude of your loving kindnesses, but were rebellious at the sea, even at the Red Sea. ^7

**8** Nevertheless he saved them for his name’s sake, that he might make his mighty power known. ^8

**9** He rebuked the Red Sea also, and it was dried up; so he led them through the depths, as through a desert. ^9

**10** He saved them from the hand of him who hated them, and redeemed them from the hand of the enemy. ^10

**11** The waters covered their adversaries. There was not one of them left. ^11

**12** Then they believed his words. They sang his praise. ^12

**13** They soon forgot his works. They didn’t wait for his counsel, ^13

**14** but gave in to craving in the desert, and tested God in the wasteland. ^14

**15** He gave them their request, but sent leanness into their soul. ^15

**16** They envied Moses also in the camp, and Aaron, the LORD’s saint. ^16

**17** The earth opened and swallowed up Dathan, and covered the company of Abiram. ^17

**18** A fire was kindled in their company. The flame burned up the wicked. ^18

**19** They made a calf in Horeb, and worshiped a molten image. ^19

**20** Thus they exchanged their glory for an image of a bull that eats grass. ^20

**21** They forgot God, their Savior, who had done great things in Egypt, ^21

**22** wondrous works in the land of Ham, and awesome things by the Red Sea. ^22

**23** Therefore he said that he would destroy them, had Moses, his chosen, not stood before him in the breach, to turn away his wrath, so that he wouldn’t destroy them. ^23

**24** Yes, they despised the pleasant land. They didn’t believe his word, ^24

**25** but murmured in their tents, and didn’t listen to the LORD’s voice. ^25

**26** Therefore he swore to them that he would overthrow them in the wilderness, ^26

**27** that he would overthrow their offspring among the nations, and scatter them in the lands. ^27

**28** They joined themselves also to Baal Peor, and ate the sacrifices of the dead. ^28

**29** Thus they provoked him to anger with their deeds. The plague broke in on them. ^29

**30** Then Phinehas stood up and executed judgment, so the plague was stopped. ^30

**31** That was credited to him for righteousness, for all generations to come. ^31

**32** They angered him also at the waters of Meribah, so that Moses was troubled for their sakes; ^32

**33** because they were rebellious against his spirit, he spoke rashly with his lips. ^33

**34** They didn’t destroy the peoples, as the LORD commanded them, ^34

**35** but mixed themselves with the nations, and learned their works. ^35

**36** They served their idols, which became a snare to them. ^36

**37** Yes, they sacrificed their sons and their daughters to demons. ^37

**38** They shed innocent blood, even the blood of their sons and of their daughters, whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan. The land was polluted with blood. ^38

**39** Thus they were defiled with their works, and prostituted themselves in their deeds. ^39

**40** Therefore the LORD burned with anger against his people. He abhorred his inheritance. ^40

**41** He gave them into the hand of the nations. Those who hated them ruled over them. ^41

**42** Their enemies also oppressed them. They were brought into subjection under their hand. ^42

**43** He rescued them many times, but they were rebellious in their counsel, and were brought low in their iniquity. ^43

**44** Nevertheless he regarded their distress, when he heard their cry. ^44

**45** He remembered for them his covenant, and repented according to the multitude of his loving kindnesses. ^45

**46** He made them also to be pitied by all those who carried them captive. ^46

**47** Save us, LORD, our God, gather us from among the nations, to give thanks to your holy name, to triumph in your praise! ^47

**48** Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting even to everlasting! Let all the people say, “Amen.” Praise the LORD! ^48
