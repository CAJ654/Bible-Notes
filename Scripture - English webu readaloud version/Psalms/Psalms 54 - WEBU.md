# Psalms 54 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 53 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 55 - WEBU]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. On stringed instruments. A contemplation by David, when the Ziphites came and said to Saul, “Isn’t David hiding himself among us?” Save me, God, by your name. Vindicate me in your might. ^1

**2** Hear my prayer, God. Listen to the words of my mouth. ^2

**3** For strangers have risen up against me. Violent men have sought after my soul. They haven’t set God before them. Selah. ^3

**4** Behold, God is my helper. The Lord is the one who sustains my soul. ^4

**5** He will repay the evil to my enemies. Destroy them in your truth. ^5

**6** With a free will offering, I will sacrifice to you. I will give thanks to your name, LORD, for it is good. ^6

**7** For he has delivered me out of all trouble. My eye has seen triumph over my enemies. ^7
