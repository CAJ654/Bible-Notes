# Psalms 147 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 146 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 148 - WEBU]] →

**1** Praise the LORD, for it is good to sing praises to our God; for it is pleasant and fitting to praise him. ^1

**2** The LORD builds up Jerusalem. He gathers together the outcasts of Israel. ^2

**3** He heals the broken in heart, and binds up their wounds. ^3

**4** He counts the number of the stars. He calls them all by their names. ^4

**5** Great is our Lord, and mighty in power. His understanding is infinite. ^5

**6** The LORD upholds the humble. He brings the wicked down to the ground. ^6

**7** Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving. Sing praises on the harp to our God, ^7

**8** who covers the sky with clouds, who prepares rain for the earth, who makes grass grow on the mountains. ^8

**9** He provides food for the livestock, and for the young ravens when they call. ^9

**10** He doesn’t delight in the strength of the horse. He takes no pleasure in the legs of a man. ^10

**11** The LORD takes pleasure in those who fear him, in those who hope in his loving kindness. ^11

**12** Praise the LORD, Jerusalem! Praise your God, Zion! ^12

**13** For he has strengthened the bars of your gates. He has blessed your children within you. ^13

**14** He makes peace in your borders. He fills you with the finest of the wheat. ^14

**15** He sends out his commandment to the earth. His word runs very swiftly. ^15

**16** He gives snow like wool, and scatters frost like ashes. ^16

**17** He hurls down his hail like pebbles. Who can stand before his cold? ^17

**18** He sends out his word, and melts them. He causes his wind to blow, and the waters flow. ^18

**19** He shows his word to Jacob, his statutes and his ordinances to Israel. ^19

**20** He has not done this for just any nation. They don’t know his ordinances. Praise the LORD! ^20
