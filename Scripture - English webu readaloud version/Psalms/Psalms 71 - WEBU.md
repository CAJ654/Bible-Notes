# Psalms 71 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 70 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 72 - WEBU]] →

**1** In you, LORD, I take refuge. Never let me be disappointed. ^1

**2** Deliver me in your righteousness, and rescue me. Turn your ear to me, and save me. ^2

**3** Be to me a rock of refuge to which I may always go. Give the command to save me, for you are my rock and my fortress. ^3

**4** Rescue me, my God, from the hand of the wicked, from the hand of the unrighteous and cruel man. ^4

**5** For you are my hope, Lord GOD, my confidence from my youth. ^5

**6** I have relied on you from the womb. You are he who took me out of my mother’s womb. I will always praise you. ^6

**7** I am a marvel to many, but you are my strong refuge. ^7

**8** My mouth shall be filled with your praise, with your honor all day long. ^8

**9** Don’t reject me in my old age. Don’t forsake me when my strength fails. ^9

**10** For my enemies talk about me. Those who watch for my soul conspire together, ^10

**11** saying, “God has forsaken him. Pursue and take him, for no one will rescue him.” ^11

**12** God, don’t be far from me. My God, hurry to help me. ^12

**13** Let my accusers be disappointed and consumed. Let them be covered with disgrace and scorn who want to harm me. ^13

**14** But I will always hope, and will add to all of your praise. ^14

**15** My mouth will tell about your righteousness, and of your salvation all day, though I don’t know its full measure. ^15

**16** I will come with the mighty acts of the Lord GOD. I will make mention of your righteousness, even of yours alone. ^16

**17** God, you have taught me from my youth. Until now, I have declared your wondrous works. ^17

**18** Yes, even when I am old and gray-haired, God, don’t forsake me, until I have declared your strength to the next generation, your might to everyone who is to come. ^18

**19** God, your righteousness also reaches to the heavens. You have done great things. God, who is like you? ^19

**20** You, who have shown us many and bitter troubles, you will let me live. You will bring us up again from the depths of the earth. ^20

**21** Increase my honor and comfort me again. ^21

**22** I will also praise you with the harp for your faithfulness, my God. I sing praises to you with the lyre, Holy One of Israel. ^22

**23** My lips shall shout for joy! My soul, which you have redeemed, sings praises to you! ^23

**24** My tongue will also talk about your righteousness all day long, for they are disappointed, and they are confounded, who want to harm me. ^24
