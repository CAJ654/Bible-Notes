# Psalms 96 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 95 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 97 - WEBU]] →

**1** Sing to the LORD a new song! Sing to the LORD, all the earth. ^1

**2** Sing to the LORD! Bless his name! Proclaim his salvation from day to day! ^2

**3** Declare his glory among the nations, his marvelous works among all the peoples. ^3

**4** For the LORD is great, and greatly to be praised! He is to be feared above all gods. ^4

**5** For all the gods of the peoples are idols, but the LORD made the heavens. ^5

**6** Honor and majesty are before him. Strength and beauty are in his sanctuary. ^6

**7** Ascribe to the LORD, you families of nations, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength. ^7

**8** Ascribe to the LORD the glory due to his name. Bring an offering, and come into his courts. ^8

**9** Worship the LORD in holy array. Tremble before him, all the earth. ^9

**10** Say among the nations, “The LORD reigns.” The world is also established. It can’t be moved. He will judge the peoples with equity. ^10

**11** Let the heavens be glad, and let the earth rejoice. Let the sea roar, and its fullness! ^11

**12** Let the field and all that is in it exult! Then all the trees of the woods shall sing for joy ^12

**13** before the LORD; for he comes, for he comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world with righteousness, the peoples with his truth. ^13
