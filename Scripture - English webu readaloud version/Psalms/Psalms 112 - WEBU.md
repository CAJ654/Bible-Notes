# Psalms 112 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 111 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 113 - WEBU]] →

**1** Praise the LORD! Blessed is the man who fears the LORD, who delights greatly in his commandments. ^1

**2** His offspring will be mighty in the land. The generation of the upright will be blessed. ^2

**3** Wealth and riches are in his house. His righteousness endures forever. ^3

**4** Light dawns in the darkness for the upright, gracious, merciful, and righteous. ^4

**5** It is well with the man who deals graciously and lends. He will maintain his cause in judgment. ^5

**6** For he will never be shaken. The righteous will be remembered forever. ^6

**7** He will not be afraid of evil news. His heart is steadfast, trusting in the LORD. ^7

**8** His heart is established. He will not be afraid in the end when he sees his adversaries. ^8

**9** He has dispersed, he has given to the poor. His righteousness endures forever. His horn will be exalted with honor. ^9

**10** The wicked will see it, and be grieved. He shall gnash with his teeth, and melt away. The desire of the wicked will perish. ^10
