# Psalms 130 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 129 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 131 - WEBU]] →

**1** A Song of Ascents. Out of the depths I have cried to you, LORD. ^1

**2** Lord, hear my voice. Let your ears be attentive to the voice of my petitions. ^2

**3** If you, LORD, kept a record of sins, Lord, who could stand? ^3

**4** But there is forgiveness with you, therefore you are feared. ^4

**5** I wait for the LORD. My soul waits. I hope in his word. ^5

**6** My soul longs for the Lord more than watchmen long for the morning, more than watchmen for the morning. ^6

**7** Israel, hope in the LORD, for there is loving kindness with the LORD. Abundant redemption is with him. ^7

**8** He will redeem Israel from all their sins. ^8
