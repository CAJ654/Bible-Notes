# Psalms 82 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 81 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 83 - WEBU]] →

**1** A Psalm by Asaph. God presides in the great assembly. He judges among the gods. ^1

**2** “How long will you judge unjustly, and show partiality to the wicked?” Selah. ^2

**3** “Defend the weak, the poor, and the fatherless. Maintain the rights of the poor and oppressed. ^3

**4** Rescue the weak and needy. Deliver them out of the hand of the wicked.” ^4

**5** They don’t know, neither do they understand. They walk back and forth in darkness. All the foundations of the earth are shaken. ^5

**6** I said, “You are gods, all of you are sons of the Most High. ^6

**7** Nevertheless you shall die like men, and fall like one of the rulers.” ^7

**8** Arise, God, judge the earth, for you inherit all of the nations. ^8
