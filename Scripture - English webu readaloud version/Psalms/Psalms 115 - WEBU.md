# Psalms 115 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 114 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 116 - WEBU]] →

**1** Not to us, LORD, not to us, but to your name give glory, for your loving kindness, and for your truth’s sake. ^1

**2** Why should the nations say, “Where is their God, now?” ^2

**3** But our God is in the heavens. He does whatever he pleases. ^3

**4** Their idols are silver and gold, the work of men’s hands. ^4

**5** They have mouths, but they don’t speak. They have eyes, but they don’t see. ^5

**6** They have ears, but they don’t hear. They have noses, but they don’t smell. ^6

**7** They have hands, but they don’t feel. They have feet, but they don’t walk, neither do they speak through their throat. ^7

**8** Those who make them will be like them; yes, everyone who trusts in them. ^8

**9** Israel, trust in the LORD! He is their help and their shield. ^9

**10** House of Aaron, trust in the LORD! He is their help and their shield. ^10

**11** You who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD! He is their help and their shield. ^11

**12** The LORD remembers us. He will bless us. He will bless the house of Israel. He will bless the house of Aaron. ^12

**13** He will bless those who fear the LORD, both small and great. ^13

**14** May the LORD increase you more and more, you and your children. ^14

**15** Blessed are you by the LORD, who made heaven and earth. ^15

**16** The heavens are the LORD’s heavens, but he has given the earth to the children of men. ^16

**17** The dead don’t praise the LORD, nor any who go down into silence, ^17

**18** but we will bless the LORD, from this time forward and forever more. Praise the LORD! ^18
