# Psalms 15 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 14 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 16 - WEBU]] →

**1** A Psalm by David. LORD, who shall dwell in your sanctuary? Who shall live on your holy hill? ^1

**2** He who walks blamelessly and does what is right, and speaks truth in his heart; ^2

**3** he who doesn’t slander with his tongue, nor does evil to his friend, nor casts slurs against his fellow man; ^3

**4** in whose eyes a vile man is despised, but who honors those who fear the LORD; he who keeps an oath even when it hurts, and doesn’t change; ^4

**5** he who doesn’t lend out his money for usury, nor take a bribe against the innocent. He who does these things shall never be shaken. ^5
