# Psalms 107 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 106 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 108 - WEBU]] →

**1** Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, for his loving kindness endures forever. ^1

**2** Let the redeemed by the LORD say so, whom he has redeemed from the hand of the adversary, ^2

**3** and gathered out of the lands, from the east and from the west, from the north and from the south. ^3

**4** They wandered in the wilderness in a desert way. They found no city to live in. ^4

**5** Hungry and thirsty, their soul fainted in them. ^5

**6** Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, and he delivered them out of their distresses. ^6

**7** He led them also by a straight way, that they might go to a city to live in. ^7

**8** Let them praise the LORD for his loving kindness, for his wonderful deeds to the children of men! ^8

**9** For he satisfies the longing soul. He fills the hungry soul with good. ^9

**10** Some sat in darkness and in the shadow of death, being bound in affliction and iron, ^10

**11** because they rebelled against the words of God, and condemned the counsel of the Most High. ^11

**12** Therefore he brought down their heart with labor. They fell down, and there was no one to help. ^12

**13** Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, and he saved them out of their distresses. ^13

**14** He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death, and broke away their chains. ^14

**15** Let them praise the LORD for his loving kindness, for his wonderful deeds to the children of men! ^15

**16** For he has broken the gates of bronze, and cut through bars of iron. ^16

**17** Fools are afflicted because of their disobedience, and because of their iniquities. ^17

**18** Their soul abhors all kinds of food. They draw near to the gates of death. ^18

**19** Then they cry to the LORD in their trouble, and he saves them out of their distresses. ^19

**20** He sends his word, and heals them, and delivers them from their graves. ^20

**21** Let them praise the LORD for his loving kindness, for his wonderful deeds to the children of men! ^21

**22** Let them offer the sacrifices of thanksgiving, and declare his deeds with singing. ^22

**23** Those who go down to the sea in ships, who do business in great waters, ^23

**24** these see the LORD’s deeds, and his wonders in the deep. ^24

**25** For he commands, and raises the stormy wind, which lifts up its waves. ^25

**26** They mount up to the sky; they go down again to the depths. Their soul melts away because of trouble. ^26

**27** They reel back and forth, and stagger like a drunken man, and are at their wits’ end. ^27

**28** Then they cry to the LORD in their trouble, and he brings them out of their distress. ^28

**29** He makes the storm a calm, so that its waves are still. ^29

**30** Then they are glad because it is calm, so he brings them to their desired haven. ^30

**31** Let them praise the LORD for his loving kindness, for his wonderful deeds for the children of men! ^31

**32** Let them exalt him also in the assembly of the people, and praise him in the seat of the elders. ^32

**33** He turns rivers into a desert, water springs into a thirsty ground, ^33

**34** and a fruitful land into a salt waste, for the wickedness of those who dwell in it. ^34

**35** He turns a desert into a pool of water, and a dry land into water springs. ^35

**36** There he makes the hungry live, that they may prepare a city to live in, ^36

**37** sow fields, plant vineyards, and reap the fruits of increase. ^37

**38** He blesses them also, so that they are multiplied greatly. He doesn’t allow their livestock to decrease. ^38

**39** Again, they are diminished and bowed down through oppression, trouble, and sorrow. ^39

**40** He pours contempt on princes, and causes them to wander in a trackless waste. ^40

**41** Yet he lifts the needy out of their affliction, and increases their families like a flock. ^41

**42** The upright will see it, and be glad. All the wicked will shut their mouths. ^42

**43** Whoever is wise will pay attention to these things. They will consider the loving kindnesses of the LORD. ^43
