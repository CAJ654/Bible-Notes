# Psalms 148 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 147 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 149 - WEBU]] →

**1** Praise the LORD! Praise the LORD from the heavens! Praise him in the heights! ^1

**2** Praise him, all his angels! Praise him, all his army! ^2

**3** Praise him, sun and moon! Praise him, all you shining stars! ^3

**4** Praise him, you heavens of heavens, you waters that are above the heavens. ^4

**5** Let them praise the LORD’s name, for he commanded, and they were created. ^5

**6** He has also established them forever and ever. He has made a decree which will not pass away. ^6

**7** Praise the LORD from the earth, you great sea creatures, and all depths, ^7

**8** lightning and hail, snow and clouds, stormy wind, fulfilling his word, ^8

**9** mountains and all hills, fruit trees and all cedars, ^9

**10** wild animals and all livestock, small creatures and flying birds, ^10

**11** kings of the earth and all peoples, princes and all judges of the earth, ^11

**12** both young men and maidens, old men and children. ^12

**13** Let them praise the LORD’s name, for his name alone is exalted. His glory is above the earth and the heavens. ^13

**14** He has lifted up the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints, even of the children of Israel, a people near to him. Praise the LORD! ^14
