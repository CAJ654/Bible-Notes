# Psalms 119 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 118 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 120 - WEBU]] →

**1** ALEPH Blessed are those whose ways are blameless, who walk according to the LORD’s law. ^1

**2** Blessed are those who keep his statutes, who seek him with their whole heart. ^2

**3** Yes, they do nothing wrong. They walk in his ways. ^3

**4** You have commanded your precepts, that we should fully obey them. ^4

**5** Oh that my ways were steadfast to obey your statutes! ^5

**6** Then I wouldn’t be disappointed, when I consider all of your commandments. ^6

**7** I will give thanks to you with uprightness of heart, when I learn your righteous judgments. ^7

**8** I will observe your statutes. Don’t utterly forsake me. BETH ^8

**9** How can a young man keep his way pure? By living according to your word. ^9

**10** With my whole heart I have sought you. Don’t let me wander from your commandments. ^10

**11** I have hidden your word in my heart, that I might not sin against you. ^11

**12** Blessed are you, LORD. Teach me your statutes. ^12

**13** With my lips, I have declared all the ordinances of your mouth. ^13

**14** I have rejoiced in the way of your testimonies, as much as in all riches. ^14

**15** I will meditate on your precepts, and consider your ways. ^15

**16** I will delight myself in your statutes. I will not forget your word. GIMEL ^16

**17** Do good to your servant. I will live and I will obey your word. ^17

**18** Open my eyes, that I may see wondrous things out of your law. ^18

**19** I am a stranger on the earth. Don’t hide your commandments from me. ^19

**20** My soul is consumed with longing for your ordinances at all times. ^20

**21** You have rebuked the proud who are cursed, who wander from your commandments. ^21

**22** Take reproach and contempt away from me, for I have kept your statutes. ^22

**23** Though princes sit and slander me, your servant will meditate on your statutes. ^23

**24** Indeed your statutes are my delight, and my counselors. DALETH ^24

**25** My soul is laid low in the dust. Revive me according to your word! ^25

**26** I declared my ways, and you answered me. Teach me your statutes. ^26

**27** Let me understand the teaching of your precepts! Then I will meditate on your wondrous works. ^27

**28** My soul is weary with sorrow; strengthen me according to your word. ^28

**29** Keep me from the way of deceit. Grant me your law graciously! ^29

**30** I have chosen the way of truth. I have set your ordinances before me. ^30

**31** I cling to your statutes, LORD. Don’t let me be disappointed. ^31

**32** I run in the path of your commandments, for you have set my heart free. HE ^32

**33** Teach me, LORD, the way of your statutes. I will keep them to the end. ^33

**34** Give me understanding, and I will keep your law. Yes, I will obey it with my whole heart. ^34

**35** Direct me in the path of your commandments, for I delight in them. ^35

**36** Turn my heart toward your statutes, not toward selfish gain. ^36

**37** Turn my eyes away from looking at worthless things. Revive me in your ways. ^37

**38** Fulfill your promise to your servant, that you may be feared. ^38

**39** Take away my disgrace that I dread, for your ordinances are good. ^39

**40** Behold, I long for your precepts! Revive me in your righteousness. VAV ^40

**41** Let your loving kindness also come to me, LORD, your salvation, according to your word. ^41

**42** So I will have an answer for him who reproaches me, for I trust in your word. ^42

**43** Don’t snatch the word of truth out of my mouth, for I put my hope in your ordinances. ^43

**44** So I will obey your law continually, forever and ever. ^44

**45** I will walk in liberty, for I have sought your precepts. ^45

**46** I will also speak of your statutes before kings, and will not be disappointed. ^46

**47** I will delight myself in your commandments, because I love them. ^47

**48** I reach out my hands for your commandments, which I love. I will meditate on your statutes. ZAYIN ^48

**49** Remember your word to your servant, because you gave me hope. ^49

**50** This is my comfort in my affliction, for your word has revived me. ^50

**51** The arrogant mock me excessively, but I don’t swerve from your law. ^51

**52** I remember your ordinances of old, LORD, and have comforted myself. ^52

**53** Indignation has taken hold on me, because of the wicked who forsake your law. ^53

**54** Your statutes have been my songs in the house where I live. ^54

**55** I have remembered your name, LORD, in the night, and I obey your law. ^55

**56** This is my way, that I keep your precepts. HETH ^56

**57** The LORD is my portion. I promised to obey your words. ^57

**58** I sought your favor with my whole heart. Be merciful to me according to your word. ^58

**59** I considered my ways, and turned my steps to your statutes. ^59

**60** I will hurry, and not delay, to obey your commandments. ^60

**61** The ropes of the wicked bind me, but I won’t forget your law. ^61

**62** At midnight I will rise to give thanks to you, because of your righteous ordinances. ^62

**63** I am a friend of all those who fear you, of those who observe your precepts. ^63

**64** The earth is full of your loving kindness, LORD. Teach me your statutes. TETH ^64

**65** You have treated your servant well, according to your word, LORD. ^65

**66** Teach me good judgment and knowledge, for I believe in your commandments. ^66

**67** Before I was afflicted, I went astray; but now I observe your word. ^67

**68** You are good, and do good. Teach me your statutes. ^68

**69** The proud have smeared a lie upon me. With my whole heart, I will keep your precepts. ^69

**70** Their heart is as callous as the fat, but I delight in your law. ^70

**71** It is good for me that I have been afflicted, that I may learn your statutes. ^71

**72** The law of your mouth is better to me than thousands of pieces of gold and silver. YODH ^72

**73** Your hands have made me and formed me. Give me understanding, that I may learn your commandments. ^73

**74** Those who fear you will see me and be glad, because I have put my hope in your word. ^74

**75** LORD, I know that your judgments are righteous, that in faithfulness you have afflicted me. ^75

**76** Please let your loving kindness be for my comfort, according to your word to your servant. ^76

**77** Let your tender mercies come to me, that I may live; for your law is my delight. ^77

**78** Let the proud be disappointed, for they have overthrown me wrongfully. I will meditate on your precepts. ^78

**79** Let those who fear you turn to me. They will know your statutes. ^79

**80** Let my heart be blameless toward your decrees, that I may not be disappointed. KAPF ^80

**81** My soul faints for your salvation. I hope in your word. ^81

**82** My eyes fail for your word. I say, “When will you comfort me?” ^82

**83** For I have become like a wineskin in the smoke. I don’t forget your statutes. ^83

**84** How many are the days of your servant? When will you execute judgment on those who persecute me? ^84

**85** The proud have dug pits for me, contrary to your law. ^85

**86** All of your commandments are faithful. They persecute me wrongfully. Help me! ^86

**87** They had almost wiped me from the earth, but I didn’t forsake your precepts. ^87

**88** Preserve my life according to your loving kindness, so I will obey the statutes of your mouth. LAMEDH ^88

**89** LORD, your word is settled in heaven forever. ^89

**90** Your faithfulness is to all generations. You have established the earth, and it remains. ^90

**91** Your laws remain to this day, for all things serve you. ^91

**92** Unless your law had been my delight, I would have perished in my affliction. ^92

**93** I will never forget your precepts, for with them, you have revived me. ^93

**94** I am yours. Save me, for I have sought your precepts. ^94

**95** The wicked have waited for me, to destroy me. I will consider your statutes. ^95

**96** I have seen a limit to all perfection, but your commands are boundless. MEM ^96

**97** How I love your law! It is my meditation all day. ^97

**98** Your commandments make me wiser than my enemies, for your commandments are always with me. ^98

**99** I have more understanding than all my teachers, for your testimonies are my meditation. ^99

**100** I understand more than the aged, because I have kept your precepts. ^100

**101** I have kept my feet from every evil way, that I might observe your word. ^101

**102** I have not turned away from your ordinances, for you have taught me. ^102

**103** How sweet are your promises to my taste, more than honey to my mouth! ^103

**104** Through your precepts, I get understanding; therefore I hate every false way. NUN ^104

**105** Your word is a lamp to my feet, and a light for my path. ^105

**106** I have sworn, and have confirmed it, that I will obey your righteous ordinances. ^106

**107** I am afflicted very much. Revive me, LORD, according to your word. ^107

**108** Accept, I beg you, the willing offerings of my mouth. LORD, teach me your ordinances. ^108

**109** My soul is continually in my hand, yet I won’t forget your law. ^109

**110** The wicked have laid a snare for me, yet I haven’t gone astray from your precepts. ^110

**111** I have taken your testimonies as a heritage forever, for they are the joy of my heart. ^111

**112** I have set my heart to perform your statutes forever, even to the end. SAMEKH ^112

**113** I hate double-minded men, but I love your law. ^113

**114** You are my hiding place and my shield. I hope in your word. ^114

**115** Depart from me, you evildoers, that I may keep the commandments of my God. ^115

**116** Uphold me according to your word, that I may live. Let me not be ashamed of my hope. ^116

**117** Hold me up, and I will be safe, and will have respect for your statutes continually. ^117

**118** You reject all those who stray from your statutes, for their deceit is in vain. ^118

**119** You put away all the wicked of the earth like dross. Therefore I love your testimonies. ^119

**120** My flesh trembles for fear of you. I am afraid of your judgments. AYIN ^120

**121** I have done what is just and righteous. Don’t leave me to my oppressors. ^121

**122** Ensure your servant’s well-being. Don’t let the proud oppress me. ^122

**123** My eyes fail looking for your salvation, for your righteous word. ^123

**124** Deal with your servant according to your loving kindness. Teach me your statutes. ^124

**125** I am your servant. Give me understanding, that I may know your testimonies. ^125

**126** It is time to act, LORD, for they break your law. ^126

**127** Therefore I love your commandments more than gold, yes, more than pure gold. ^127

**128** Therefore I consider all of your precepts to be right. I hate every false way. PE ^128

**129** Your testimonies are wonderful, therefore my soul keeps them. ^129

**130** The entrance of your words gives light. It gives understanding to the simple. ^130

**131** I opened my mouth wide and panted, for I longed for your commandments. ^131

**132** Turn to me, and have mercy on me, as you always do to those who love your name. ^132

**133** Establish my footsteps in your word. Don’t let any iniquity have dominion over me. ^133

**134** Redeem me from the oppression of man, so I will observe your precepts. ^134

**135** Make your face shine on your servant. Teach me your statutes. ^135

**136** Streams of tears run down my eyes, because they don’t observe your law. TZADHE ^136

**137** You are righteous, LORD. Your judgments are upright. ^137

**138** You have commanded your statutes in righteousness. They are fully trustworthy. ^138

**139** My zeal wears me out, because my enemies ignore your words. ^139

**140** Your promises have been thoroughly tested, and your servant loves them. ^140

**141** I am small and despised. I don’t forget your precepts. ^141

**142** Your righteousness is an everlasting righteousness. Your law is truth. ^142

**143** Trouble and anguish have taken hold of me. Your commandments are my delight. ^143

**144** Your testimonies are righteous forever. Give me understanding, that I may live. QOPH ^144

**145** I have called with my whole heart. Answer me, LORD! I will keep your statutes. ^145

**146** I have called to you. Save me! I will obey your statutes. ^146

**147** I rise before dawn and cry for help. I put my hope in your words. ^147

**148** My eyes stay open through the night watches, that I might meditate on your word. ^148

**149** Hear my voice according to your loving kindness. Revive me, LORD, according to your ordinances. ^149

**150** They draw near who follow after wickedness. They are far from your law. ^150

**151** You are near, LORD. All your commandments are truth. ^151

**152** Of old I have known from your testimonies, that you have founded them forever. RESH ^152

**153** Consider my affliction, and deliver me, for I don’t forget your law. ^153

**154** Plead my cause, and redeem me! Revive me according to your promise. ^154

**155** Salvation is far from the wicked, for they don’t seek your statutes. ^155

**156** Great are your tender mercies, LORD. Revive me according to your ordinances. ^156

**157** Many are my persecutors and my adversaries. I haven’t swerved from your testimonies. ^157

**158** I look at the faithless with loathing, because they don’t observe your word. ^158

**159** Consider how I love your precepts. Revive me, LORD, according to your loving kindness. ^159

**160** All of your words are truth. Every one of your righteous ordinances endures forever. SIN AND SHIN ^160

**161** Princes have persecuted me without a cause, but my heart stands in awe of your words. ^161

**162** I rejoice at your word, as one who finds great plunder. ^162

**163** I hate and abhor falsehood. I love your law. ^163

**164** Seven times a day, I praise you, because of your righteous ordinances. ^164

**165** Those who love your law have great peace. Nothing causes them to stumble. ^165

**166** I have hoped for your salvation, LORD. I have done your commandments. ^166

**167** My soul has observed your testimonies. I love them exceedingly. ^167

**168** I have obeyed your precepts and your testimonies, for all my ways are before you. TAV ^168

**169** Let my cry come before you, LORD. Give me understanding according to your word. ^169

**170** Let my supplication come before you. Deliver me according to your word. ^170

**171** Let my lips utter praise, for you teach me your statutes. ^171

**172** Let my tongue sing of your word, for all your commandments are righteousness. ^172

**173** Let your hand be ready to help me, for I have chosen your precepts. ^173

**174** I have longed for your salvation, LORD. Your law is my delight. ^174

**175** Let my soul live, that I may praise you. Let your ordinances help me. ^175

**176** I have gone astray like a lost sheep. Seek your servant, for I don’t forget your commandments. ^176
