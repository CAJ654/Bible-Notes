# Psalms 72 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 71 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 73 - WEBU]] →

**1** By Solomon. God, give the king your justice; your righteousness to the royal son. ^1

**2** He will judge your people with righteousness, and your poor with justice. ^2

**3** The mountains shall bring prosperity to the people. The hills bring the fruit of righteousness. ^3

**4** He will judge the poor of the people. He will save the children of the needy, and will break the oppressor in pieces. ^4

**5** They shall fear you while the sun endures; and as long as the moon, throughout all generations. ^5

**6** He will come down like rain on the mown grass, as showers that water the earth. ^6

**7** In his days, the righteous shall flourish, and abundance of peace, until the moon is no more. ^7

**8** He shall have dominion also from sea to sea, from the River to the ends of the earth. ^8

**9** Those who dwell in the wilderness shall bow before him. His enemies shall lick the dust. ^9

**10** The kings of Tarshish and of the islands will bring tribute. The kings of Sheba and Seba shall offer gifts. ^10

**11** Yes, all kings shall fall down before him. All nations shall serve him. ^11

**12** For he will deliver the needy when he cries; the poor, who has no helper. ^12

**13** He will have pity on the poor and needy. He will save the souls of the needy. ^13

**14** He will redeem their soul from oppression and violence. Their blood will be precious in his sight. ^14

**15** He will live; and Sheba’s gold will be given to him. Men will pray for him continually. They will bless him all day long. ^15

**16** Abundance of grain shall be throughout the land. Its fruit sways like Lebanon. Let it flourish, thriving like the grass of the field. ^16

**17** His name endures forever. His name continues as long as the sun. Men shall be blessed by him. All nations will call him blessed. ^17

**18** Praise be to the LORD God, the God of Israel, who alone does marvelous deeds. ^18

**19** Blessed be his glorious name forever! Let the whole earth be filled with his glory! Amen and amen. ^19

**20** This ends the prayers by David, the son of Jesse. ^20
