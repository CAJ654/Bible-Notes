# Psalms 11 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 10 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 12 - WEBU]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. By David. In the LORD, I take refuge. How can you say to my soul, “Flee as a bird to your mountain”? ^1

**2** For, behold, the wicked bend their bows. They set their arrows on the strings, that they may shoot in darkness at the upright in heart. ^2

**3** If the foundations are destroyed, what can the righteous do? ^3

**4** The LORD is in his holy temple. The LORD is on his throne in heaven. His eyes observe. His eyes examine the children of men. ^4

**5** The LORD examines the righteous, but his soul hates the wicked and him who loves violence. ^5

**6** On the wicked he will rain blazing coals; fire, sulfur, and scorching wind shall be the portion of their cup. ^6

**7** For the LORD is righteous. He loves righteousness. The upright shall see his face. ^7
