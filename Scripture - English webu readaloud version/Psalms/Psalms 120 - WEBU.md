# Psalms 120 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 119 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 121 - WEBU]] →

**1** A Song of Ascents. In my distress, I cried to the LORD. He answered me. ^1

**2** Deliver my soul, LORD, from lying lips, from a deceitful tongue. ^2

**3** What will be given to you, and what will be done more to you, you deceitful tongue? ^3

**4** Sharp arrows of the mighty, with coals of juniper. ^4

**5** Woe is me, that I live in Meshech, that I dwell among the tents of Kedar! ^5

**6** My soul has had her dwelling too long with him who hates peace. ^6

**7** I am for peace, but when I speak, they are for war. ^7
