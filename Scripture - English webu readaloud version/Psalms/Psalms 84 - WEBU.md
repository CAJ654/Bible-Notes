# Psalms 84 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 83 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 85 - WEBU]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. On an instrument of Gath. A Psalm by the sons of Korah. How lovely are your dwellings, LORD of Armies! ^1

**2** My soul longs, and even faints for the courts of the LORD. My heart and my flesh cry out for the living God. ^2

**3** Yes, the sparrow has found a home, and the swallow a nest for herself, where she may have her young, near your altars, LORD of Armies, my King, and my God. ^3

**4** Blessed are those who dwell in your house. They are always praising you. Selah. ^4

**5** Blessed are those whose strength is in you, who have set their hearts on a pilgrimage. ^5

**6** Passing through the valley of Weeping, they make it a place of springs. Yes, the autumn rain covers it with blessings. ^6

**7** They go from strength to strength. Every one of them appears before God in Zion. ^7

**8** LORD, God of Armies, hear my prayer. Listen, God of Jacob. Selah. ^8

**9** Behold, God our shield, look at the face of your anointed. ^9

**10** For a day in your courts is better than a thousand. I would rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God, than to dwell in the tents of wickedness. ^10

**11** For the LORD God is a sun and a shield. The LORD will give grace and glory. He withholds no good thing from those who walk blamelessly. ^11

**12** LORD of Armies, blessed is the man who trusts in you. ^12
