# Psalms 93 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 92 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 94 - WEBU]] →

**1** The LORD reigns! He is clothed with majesty! The LORD is armed with strength. The world also is established. It can’t be moved. ^1

**2** Your throne is established from long ago. You are from everlasting. ^2

**3** The floods have lifted up, LORD, the floods have lifted up their voice. The floods lift up their waves. ^3

**4** Above the voices of many waters, the mighty breakers of the sea, the LORD on high is mighty. ^4

**5** Your statutes stand firm. Holiness adorns your house, LORD, forever more. ^5
