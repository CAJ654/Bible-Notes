# Psalms 6 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 5 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 7 - WEBU]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician; on stringed instruments, upon the eight-stringed lyre. A Psalm by David. LORD, don’t rebuke me in your anger, neither discipline me in your wrath. ^1

**2** Have mercy on me, LORD, for I am faint. LORD, heal me, for my bones are troubled. ^2

**3** My soul is also in great anguish. But you, LORD—how long? ^3

**4** Return, LORD. Deliver my soul, and save me for your loving kindness’ sake. ^4

**5** For in death there is no memory of you. In Sheol, who shall give you thanks? ^5

**6** I am weary with my groaning. Every night I flood my bed. I drench my couch with my tears. ^6

**7** My eye wastes away because of grief. It grows old because of all my adversaries. ^7

**8** Depart from me, all you workers of iniquity, for the LORD has heard the voice of my weeping. ^8

**9** The LORD has heard my supplication. The LORD accepts my prayer. ^9

**10** May all my enemies be ashamed and dismayed. They shall turn back, they shall be disgraced suddenly. ^10
