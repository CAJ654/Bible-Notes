# Psalms 105 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 104 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 106 - WEBU]] →

**1** Give thanks to the LORD! Call on his name! Make his doings known among the peoples. ^1

**2** Sing to him, sing praises to him! Tell of all his marvelous works. ^2

**3** Glory in his holy name. Let the heart of those who seek the LORD rejoice. ^3

**4** Seek the LORD and his strength. Seek his face forever more. ^4

**5** Remember his marvelous works that he has done: his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth, ^5

**6** you offspring of Abraham, his servant, you children of Jacob, his chosen ones. ^6

**7** He is the LORD, our God. His judgments are in all the earth. ^7

**8** He has remembered his covenant forever, the word which he commanded to a thousand generations, ^8

**9** the covenant which he made with Abraham, his oath to Isaac, ^9

**10** and confirmed it to Jacob for a statute; to Israel for an everlasting covenant, ^10

**11** saying, “To you I will give the land of Canaan, the lot of your inheritance,” ^11

**12** when they were but a few men in number, yes, very few, and foreigners in it. ^12

**13** They went about from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another people. ^13

**14** He allowed no one to do them wrong. Yes, he reproved kings for their sakes, ^14

**15** “Don’t touch my anointed ones! Do my prophets no harm!” ^15

**16** He called for a famine on the land. He destroyed the food supplies. ^16

**17** He sent a man before them. Joseph was sold for a slave. ^17

**18** They bruised his feet with shackles. His neck was locked in irons, ^18

**19** until the time that his word happened, and the LORD’s word proved him true. ^19

**20** The king sent and freed him, even the ruler of peoples, and let him go free. ^20

**21** He made him lord of his house, and ruler of all of his possessions, ^21

**22** to discipline his princes at his pleasure, and to teach his elders wisdom. ^22

**23** Israel also came into Egypt. Jacob lived in the land of Ham. ^23

**24** He increased his people greatly, and made them stronger than their adversaries. ^24

**25** He turned their heart to hate his people, to conspire against his servants. ^25

**26** He sent Moses, his servant, and Aaron, whom he had chosen. ^26

**27** They performed miracles among them, and wonders in the land of Ham. ^27

**28** He sent darkness, and made it dark. They didn’t rebel against his words. ^28

**29** He turned their waters into blood, and killed their fish. ^29

**30** Their land swarmed with frogs, even in the rooms of their kings. ^30

**31** He spoke, and swarms of flies came, and lice in all their borders. ^31

**32** He gave them hail for rain, with lightning in their land. ^32

**33** He struck their vines and also their fig trees, and shattered the trees of their country. ^33

**34** He spoke, and the locusts came with the grasshoppers, without number. ^34

**35** They ate up every plant in their land, and ate up the fruit of their ground. ^35

**36** He struck also all the firstborn in their land, the first fruits of all their manhood. ^36

**37** He brought them out with silver and gold. There was not one feeble person among his tribes. ^37

**38** Egypt was glad when they departed, for the fear of them had fallen on them. ^38

**39** He spread a cloud for a covering, fire to give light in the night. ^39

**40** They asked, and he brought quails, and satisfied them with the bread of the sky. ^40

**41** He opened the rock, and waters gushed out. They ran as a river in the dry places. ^41

**42** For he remembered his holy word, and Abraham, his servant. ^42

**43** He brought his people out with joy, his chosen with singing. ^43

**44** He gave them the lands of the nations. They took the labor of the peoples in possession, ^44

**45** that they might keep his statutes, and observe his laws. Praise the LORD! ^45
