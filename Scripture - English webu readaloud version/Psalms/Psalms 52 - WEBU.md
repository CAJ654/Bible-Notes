# Psalms 52 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 51 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 53 - WEBU]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. A contemplation by David, when Doeg the Edomite came and told Saul, “David has come to Ahimelech’s house.” Why do you boast of mischief, mighty man? God’s loving kindness endures continually. ^1

**2** Your tongue plots destruction, like a sharp razor, working deceitfully. ^2

**3** You love evil more than good, lying rather than speaking the truth. Selah. ^3

**4** You love all devouring words, you deceitful tongue. ^4

**5** God will likewise destroy you forever. He will take you up, and pluck you out of your tent, and root you out of the land of the living. Selah. ^5

**6** The righteous also will see it, and fear, and laugh at him, saying, ^6

**7** “Behold, this is the man who didn’t make God his strength, but trusted in the abundance of his riches, and strengthened himself in his wickedness.” ^7

**8** But as for me, I am like a green olive tree in God’s house. I trust in God’s loving kindness forever and ever. ^8

**9** I will give you thanks forever, because you have done it. I will hope in your name, for it is good, in the presence of your saints. ^9
