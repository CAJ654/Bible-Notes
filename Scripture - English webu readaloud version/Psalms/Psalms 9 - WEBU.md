# Psalms 9 (WEBU)

← [[Psalms 8 - WEBU]] | [[Psalms 10 - WEBU]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. Set to “The Death of the Son.” A Psalm by David. I will give thanks to the LORD with my whole heart. I will tell of all your marvelous works. ^1

**2** I will be glad and rejoice in you. I will sing praise to your name, O Most High. ^2

**3** When my enemies turn back, they stumble and perish in your presence. ^3

**4** For you have maintained my just cause. You sit on the throne judging righteously. ^4

**5** You have rebuked the nations. You have destroyed the wicked. You have blotted out their name forever and ever. ^5

**6** The enemy is overtaken by endless ruin. The very memory of the cities which you have overthrown has perished. ^6

**7** But the LORD reigns forever. He has prepared his throne for judgment. ^7

**8** He will judge the world in righteousness. He will administer judgment to the peoples in uprightness. ^8

**9** The LORD will also be a high tower for the oppressed; a high tower in times of trouble. ^9

**10** Those who know your name will put their trust in you, for you, LORD, have not forsaken those who seek you. ^10

**11** Sing praises to the LORD, who dwells in Zion, and declare among the people what he has done. ^11

**12** For he who avenges blood remembers them. He doesn’t forget the cry of the afflicted. ^12

**13** Have mercy on me, LORD. See my affliction by those who hate me, and lift me up from the gates of death, ^13

**14** that I may show all of your praise. I will rejoice in your salvation in the gates of the daughter of Zion. ^14

**15** The nations have sunk down in the pit that they made. In the net which they hid, their own foot is taken. ^15

**16** The LORD has made himself known. He has executed judgment. The wicked is snared by the work of his own hands. Meditation. Selah. ^16

**17** The wicked shall be turned back to Sheol, even all the nations that forget God. ^17

**18** For the needy shall not always be forgotten, nor the hope of the poor perish forever. ^18

**19** Arise, LORD! Don’t let man prevail. Let the nations be judged in your sight. ^19

**20** Put them in fear, LORD. Let the nations know that they are only men. Selah. ^20
