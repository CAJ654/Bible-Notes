# Job 18 (WEBU)

← [[Job 17 - WEBU]] | [[Job 19 - WEBU]] →

**1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered, ^1

**2** “How long will you hunt for words? Consider, and afterwards we will speak. ^2

**3** Why are we counted as animals, which have become unclean in your sight? ^3

**4** You who tear yourself in your anger, will the earth be forsaken for you? Or will the rock be removed out of its place? ^4

**5** “Yes, the light of the wicked will be put out. The spark of his fire won’t shine. ^5

**6** The light will be dark in his tent. His lamp above him will be put out. ^6

**7** The steps of his strength will be shortened. His own counsel will cast him down. ^7

**8** For he is cast into a net by his own feet, and he wanders into its mesh. ^8

**9** A snare will take him by the heel. A trap will catch him. ^9

**10** A noose is hidden for him in the ground, a trap for him on the path. ^10

**11** Terrors will make him afraid on every side, and will chase him at his heels. ^11

**12** His strength will be famished. Calamity will be ready at his side. ^12

**13** The members of his body will be devoured. The firstborn of death will devour his members. ^13

**14** He will be rooted out of the security of his tent. He will be brought to the king of terrors. ^14

**15** There will dwell in his tent that which is none of his. Sulfur will be scattered on his habitation. ^15

**16** His roots will be dried up beneath. His branch will be cut off above. ^16

**17** His memory will perish from the earth. He will have no name in the street. ^17

**18** He will be driven from light into darkness, and chased out of the world. ^18

**19** He will have neither son nor grandson among his people, nor any remaining where he lived. ^19

**20** Those who come after will be astonished at his day, as those who went before were frightened. ^20

**21** Surely such are the dwellings of the unrighteous. This is the place of him who doesn’t know God.” ^21
