# Job 38 (WEBU)

← [[Job 37 - WEBU]] | [[Job 39 - WEBU]] →

**1** Then the LORD answered Job out of the whirlwind, ^1

**2** “Who is this who darkens counsel by words without knowledge? ^2

**3** Brace yourself like a man, for I will question you, then you answer me! ^3

**4** “Where were you when I laid the foundations of the earth? Declare, if you have understanding. ^4

**5** Who determined its measures, if you know? Or who stretched the line on it? ^5

**6** What were its foundations fastened on? Or who laid its cornerstone, ^6

**7** when the morning stars sang together, and all the sons of God shouted for joy? ^7

**8** “Or who shut up the sea with doors, when it broke out of the womb, ^8

**9** when I made clouds its garment, and wrapped it in thick darkness, ^9

**10** marked out for it my bound, set bars and doors, ^10

**11** and said, ‘You may come here, but no further. Your proud waves shall be stopped here’? ^11

**12** “Have you commanded the morning in your days, and caused the dawn to know its place, ^12

**13** that it might take hold of the ends of the earth, and shake the wicked out of it? ^13

**14** It is changed as clay under the seal, and presented as a garment. ^14

**15** From the wicked, their light is withheld. The high arm is broken. ^15

**16** “Have you entered into the springs of the sea? Or have you walked in the recesses of the deep? ^16

**17** Have the gates of death been revealed to you? Or have you seen the gates of the shadow of death? ^17

**18** Have you comprehended the earth in its width? Declare, if you know it all. ^18

**19** “What is the way to the dwelling of light? As for darkness, where is its place, ^19

**20** that you should take it to its bound, that you should discern the paths to its house? ^20

**21** Surely you know, for you were born then, and the number of your days is great! ^21

**22** Have you entered the storehouses of the snow, or have you seen the storehouses of the hail, ^22

**23** which I have reserved against the time of trouble, against the day of battle and war? ^23

**24** By what way is the lightning distributed, or the east wind scattered on the earth? ^24

**25** Who has cut a channel for the flood water, or the path for the thunderstorm, ^25

**26** to cause it to rain on a land where there is no man, on the wilderness, in which there is no man, ^26

**27** to satisfy the waste and desolate ground, to cause the tender grass to grow? ^27

**28** Does the rain have a father? Or who fathers the drops of dew? ^28

**29** Whose womb did the ice come out of? Who has given birth to the gray frost of the sky? ^29

**30** The waters become hard like stone, when the surface of the deep is frozen. ^30

**31** “Can you bind the cluster of the Pleiades, or loosen the cords of Orion? ^31

**32** Can you lead the constellations out in their season? Or can you guide the Bear with her cubs? ^32

**33** Do you know the laws of the heavens? Can you establish its dominion over the earth? ^33

**34** “Can you lift up your voice to the clouds, that abundance of waters may cover you? ^34

**35** Can you send out lightnings, that they may go? Do they report to you, ‘Here we are’? ^35

**36** Who has put wisdom in the inward parts? Or who has given understanding to the mind? ^36

**37** Who can count the clouds by wisdom? Or who can pour out the containers of the sky, ^37

**38** when the dust runs into a mass, and the clods of earth stick together? ^38

**39** “Can you hunt the prey for the lioness, or satisfy the appetite of the young lions, ^39

**40** when they crouch in their dens, and lie in wait in the thicket? ^40

**41** Who provides for the raven his prey, when his young ones cry to God, and wander for lack of food? ^41
