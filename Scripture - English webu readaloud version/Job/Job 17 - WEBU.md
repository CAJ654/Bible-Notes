# Job 17 (WEBU)

← [[Job 16 - WEBU]] | [[Job 18 - WEBU]] →

**1** “My spirit is consumed. My days are extinct and the grave is ready for me. ^1

**2** Surely there are mockers with me. My eye dwells on their provocation. ^2

**3** “Now give a pledge. Be collateral for me with yourself. Who is there who will strike hands with me? ^3

**4** For you have hidden their heart from understanding, therefore you will not exalt them. ^4

**5** He who denounces his friends for plunder, even the eyes of his children will fail. ^5

**6** “But he has made me a byword of the people. They spit in my face. ^6

**7** My eye also is dim by reason of sorrow. All my members are as a shadow. ^7

**8** Upright men will be astonished at this. The innocent will stir himself up against the godless. ^8

**9** Yet the righteous will hold to his way. He who has clean hands will grow stronger and stronger. ^9

**10** But as for you all, come back. I will not find a wise man among you. ^10

**11** My days are past. My plans are broken off, as are the thoughts of my heart. ^11

**12** They change the night into day, saying ‘The light is near’ in the presence of darkness. ^12

**13** If I look for Sheol as my house, if I have spread my couch in the darkness, ^13

**14** if I have said to corruption, ‘You are my father,’ and to the worm, ‘My mother,’ and ‘My sister,’ ^14

**15** where then is my hope? As for my hope, who will see it? ^15

**16** Shall it go down with me to the gates of Sheol, or descend together into the dust?” ^16
