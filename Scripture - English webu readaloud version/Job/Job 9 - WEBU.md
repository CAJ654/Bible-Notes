# Job 9 (WEBU)

← [[Job 8 - WEBU]] | [[Job 10 - WEBU]] →

**1** Then Job answered, ^1

**2** “Truly I know that it is so, but how can man be just with God? ^2

**3** If he is pleased to contend with him, he can’t answer him one time in a thousand. ^3

**4** God is wise in heart, and mighty in strength. Who has hardened himself against him and prospered? ^4

**5** He removes the mountains, and they don’t know it, when he overturns them in his anger. ^5

**6** He shakes the earth out of its place. Its pillars tremble. ^6

**7** He commands the sun and it doesn’t rise, and seals up the stars. ^7

**8** He alone stretches out the heavens, and treads on the waves of the sea. ^8

**9** He makes the Bear, Orion, and the Pleiades, and the rooms of the south. ^9

**10** He does great things past finding out; yes, marvelous things without number. ^10

**11** Behold, he goes by me, and I don’t see him. He passes on also, but I don’t perceive him. ^11

**12** Behold, he snatches away. Who can hinder him? Who will ask him, ‘What are you doing?’ ^12

**13** “God will not withdraw his anger. The helpers of Rahab stoop under him. ^13

**14** How much less will I answer him, and choose my words to argue with him? ^14

**15** Though I were righteous, yet I wouldn’t answer him. I would make supplication to my judge. ^15

**16** If I had called, and he had answered me, yet I wouldn’t believe that he listened to my voice. ^16

**17** For he breaks me with a storm, and multiplies my wounds without cause. ^17

**18** He will not allow me to catch my breath, but fills me with bitterness. ^18

**19** If it is a matter of strength, behold, he is mighty! If of justice, ‘Who,’ says he, ‘will summon me?’ ^19

**20** Though I am righteous, my own mouth will condemn me. Though I am blameless, it will prove me perverse. ^20

**21** I am blameless. I don’t respect myself. I despise my life. ^21

**22** “It is all the same. Therefore I say he destroys the blameless and the wicked. ^22

**23** If the scourge kills suddenly, he will mock at the trial of the innocent. ^23

**24** The earth is given into the hand of the wicked. He covers the faces of its judges. If not he, then who is it? ^24

**25** “Now my days are swifter than a runner. They flee away. They see no good. ^25

**26** They have passed away as the swift ships, as the eagle that swoops on the prey. ^26

**27** If I say, ‘I will forget my complaint, I will put off my sad face, and cheer up,’ ^27

**28** I am afraid of all my sorrows. I know that you will not hold me innocent. ^28

**29** I will be condemned. Why then do I labor in vain? ^29

**30** If I wash myself with snow, and cleanse my hands with lye, ^30

**31** yet you will plunge me in the ditch. My own clothes will abhor me. ^31

**32** For he is not a man, as I am, that I should answer him, that we should come together in judgment. ^32

**33** There is no umpire between us, that might lay his hand on us both. ^33

**34** Let him take his rod away from me. Let his terror not make me afraid; ^34

**35** then I would speak, and not fear him, for I am not so in myself. ^35
