# Job 13 (WEBU)

← [[Job 12 - WEBU]] | [[Job 14 - WEBU]] →

**1** “Behold, my eye has seen all this. My ear has heard and understood it. ^1

**2** What you know, I know also. I am not inferior to you. ^2

**3** “Surely I would speak to the Almighty. I desire to reason with God. ^3

**4** But you are forgers of lies. You are all physicians of no value. ^4

**5** Oh that you would be completely silent! Then you would be wise. ^5

**6** Hear now my reasoning. Listen to the pleadings of my lips. ^6

**7** Will you speak unrighteously for God, and talk deceitfully for him? ^7

**8** Will you show partiality to him? Will you contend for God? ^8

**9** Is it good that he should search you out? Or as one deceives a man, will you deceive him? ^9

**10** He will surely reprove you if you secretly show partiality. ^10

**11** Won’t his majesty make you afraid and his dread fall on you? ^11

**12** Your memorable sayings are proverbs of ashes. Your defenses are defenses of clay. ^12

**13** “Be silent! Leave me alone, that I may speak. Let come on me what will. ^13

**14** Why should I take my flesh in my teeth, and put my life in my hand? ^14

**15** Behold, he will kill me. I have no hope. Nevertheless, I will maintain my ways before him. ^15

**16** This also will be my salvation, that a godless man will not come before him. ^16

**17** Listen carefully to my speech. Let my declaration be in your ears. ^17

**18** See now, I have set my cause in order. I know that I am righteous. ^18

**19** Who is he who will contend with me? For then I would hold my peace and give up the spirit. ^19

**20** “Only don’t do two things to me, then I will not hide myself from your face: ^20

**21** withdraw your hand far from me, and don’t let your terror make me afraid. ^21

**22** Then call, and I will answer, or let me speak, and you answer me. ^22

**23** How many are my iniquities and sins? Make me know my disobedience and my sin. ^23

**24** Why do you hide your face, and consider me your enemy? ^24

**25** Will you harass a driven leaf? Will you pursue the dry stubble? ^25

**26** For you write bitter things against me, and make me inherit the iniquities of my youth. ^26

**27** You also put my feet in the stocks, and mark all my paths. You set a bound to the soles of my feet, ^27

**28** though I am decaying like a rotten thing, like a garment that is moth-eaten. ^28
