# Job 11 (WEBU)

← [[Job 10 - WEBU]] | [[Job 12 - WEBU]] →

**1** Then Zophar, the Naamathite, answered, ^1

**2** “Shouldn’t the multitude of words be answered? Should a man full of talk be justified? ^2

**3** Should your boastings make men hold their peace? When you mock, will no man make you ashamed? ^3

**4** For you say, ‘My doctrine is pure. I am clean in your eyes.’ ^4

**5** But oh that God would speak, and open his lips against you, ^5

**6** that he would show you the secrets of wisdom! For true wisdom has two sides. Know therefore that God exacts of you less than your iniquity deserves. ^6

**7** “Can you fathom the mystery of God? Or can you probe the limits of the Almighty? ^7

**8** They are high as heaven. What can you do? They are deeper than Sheol. What can you know? ^8

**9** Its measure is longer than the earth, and broader than the sea. ^9

**10** If he passes by, or confines, or convenes a court, then who can oppose him? ^10

**11** For he knows false men. He sees iniquity also, even though he doesn’t consider it. ^11

**12** An empty-headed man becomes wise when a man is born as a wild donkey’s colt. ^12

**13** “If you set your heart aright, stretch out your hands toward him. ^13

**14** If iniquity is in your hand, put it far away. Don’t let unrighteousness dwell in your tents. ^14

**15** Surely then you will lift up your face without spot. Yes, you will be steadfast, and will not fear, ^15

**16** for you will forget your misery. You will remember it like waters that have passed away. ^16

**17** Life will be clearer than the noonday. Though there is darkness, it will be as the morning. ^17

**18** You will be secure, because there is hope. Yes, you will search, and will take your rest in safety. ^18

**19** Also you will lie down, and no one will make you afraid. Yes, many will court your favor. ^19

**20** But the eyes of the wicked will fail. They will have no way to flee. Their hope will be the giving up of the spirit.” ^20
