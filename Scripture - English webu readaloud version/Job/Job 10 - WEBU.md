# Job 10 (WEBU)

← [[Job 9 - WEBU]] | [[Job 11 - WEBU]] →

**1** “My soul is weary of my life. I will give free course to my complaint. I will speak in the bitterness of my soul. ^1

**2** I will tell God, ‘Do not condemn me. Show me why you contend with me. ^2

**3** Is it good to you that you should oppress, that you should despise the work of your hands, and smile on the counsel of the wicked? ^3

**4** Do you have eyes of flesh? Or do you see as man sees? ^4

**5** Are your days as the days of mortals, or your years as man’s years, ^5

**6** that you inquire after my iniquity, and search after my sin? ^6

**7** Although you know that I am not wicked, there is no one who can deliver out of your hand. ^7

**8** “‘Your hands have framed me and fashioned me altogether, yet you destroy me. ^8

**9** Remember, I beg you, that you have fashioned me as clay. Will you bring me into dust again? ^9

**10** Haven’t you poured me out like milk, and curdled me like cheese? ^10

**11** You have clothed me with skin and flesh, and knit me together with bones and sinews. ^11

**12** You have granted me life and loving kindness. Your visitation has preserved my spirit. ^12

**13** Yet you hid these things in your heart. I know that this is with you: ^13

**14** if I sin, then you mark me. You will not acquit me from my iniquity. ^14

**15** If I am wicked, woe to me. If I am righteous, I still will not lift up my head, being filled with disgrace, and conscious of my affliction. ^15

**16** If my head is held high, you hunt me like a lion. Again you show yourself powerful to me. ^16

**17** You renew your witnesses against me, and increase your indignation on me. Changes and warfare are with me. ^17

**18** “‘Why, then, have you brought me out of the womb? I wish I had given up the spirit, and no eye had seen me. ^18

**19** I should have been as though I had not been. I should have been carried from the womb to the grave. ^19

**20** Aren’t my days few? Stop! Leave me alone, that I may find a little comfort, ^20

**21** before I go where I will not return from, to the land of darkness and of the shadow of death; ^21

**22** the land dark as midnight, of the shadow of death, without any order, where the light is as midnight.’” ^22
