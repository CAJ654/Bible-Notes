# Job 30 (WEBU)

← [[Job 29 - WEBU]] | [[Job 31 - WEBU]] →

**1** “But now those who are younger than I have me in derision, whose fathers I considered unworthy to put with my sheep dogs. ^1

**2** Of what use is the strength of their hands to me, men in whom ripe age has perished? ^2

**3** They are gaunt from lack and famine. They gnaw the dry ground, in the gloom of waste and desolation. ^3

**4** They pluck salt herbs by the bushes. The roots of the broom tree are their food. ^4

**5** They are driven out from among men. They cry after them as after a thief, ^5

**6** so that they live in frightful valleys, and in holes of the earth and of the rocks. ^6

**7** They bray among the bushes. They are gathered together under the nettles. ^7

**8** They are children of fools, yes, children of wicked men. They were flogged out of the land. ^8

**9** “Now I have become their song. Yes, I am a byword to them. ^9

**10** They abhor me, they stand aloof from me, and don’t hesitate to spit in my face. ^10

**11** For he has untied his cord, and afflicted me; and they have thrown off restraint before me. ^11

**12** On my right hand rise the rabble. They thrust aside my feet. They cast their ways of destruction up against me. ^12

**13** They mar my path. They promote my destruction without anyone’s help. ^13

**14** As through a wide breach they come. They roll themselves in amid the ruin. ^14

**15** Terrors have turned on me. They chase my honor as the wind. My welfare has passed away as a cloud. ^15

**16** “Now my soul is poured out within me. Days of affliction have taken hold of me. ^16

**17** In the night season my bones are pierced in me, and the pains that gnaw me take no rest. ^17

**18** My garment is disfigured by great force. It binds me about as the collar of my tunic. ^18

**19** He has cast me into the mire. I have become like dust and ashes. ^19

**20** I cry to you, and you do not answer me. I stand up, and you gaze at me. ^20

**21** You have turned to be cruel to me. With the might of your hand you persecute me. ^21

**22** You lift me up to the wind, and drive me with it. You dissolve me in the storm. ^22

**23** For I know that you will bring me to death, to the house appointed for all living. ^23

**24** “However doesn’t one stretch out a hand in his fall? Or in his calamity therefore cry for help? ^24

**25** Didn’t I weep for him who was in trouble? Wasn’t my soul grieved for the needy? ^25

**26** When I looked for good, then evil came. When I waited for light, darkness came. ^26

**27** My heart is troubled, and doesn’t rest. Days of affliction have come on me. ^27

**28** I go mourning without the sun. I stand up in the assembly, and cry for help. ^28

**29** I am a brother to jackals, and a companion to ostriches. ^29

**30** My skin grows black and peels from me. My bones are burned with heat. ^30

**31** Therefore my harp has turned to mourning, and my pipe into the voice of those who weep. ^31
