# Job 23 (WEBU)

← [[Job 22 - WEBU]] | [[Job 24 - WEBU]] →

**1** Then Job answered, ^1

**2** “Even today my complaint is rebellious. His hand is heavy in spite of my groaning. ^2

**3** Oh that I knew where I might find him! That I might come even to his seat! ^3

**4** I would set my cause in order before him, and fill my mouth with arguments. ^4

**5** I would know the words which he would answer me, and understand what he would tell me. ^5

**6** Would he contend with me in the greatness of his power? No, but he would listen to me. ^6

**7** There the upright might reason with him, so I should be delivered forever from my judge. ^7

**8** “If I go east, he is not there. If I go west, I can’t find him. ^8

**9** He works to the north, but I can’t see him. He turns south, but I can’t catch a glimpse of him. ^9

**10** But he knows the way that I take. When he has tried me, I will come out like gold. ^10

**11** My foot has held fast to his steps. I have kept his way, and not turned away. ^11

**12** I haven’t gone back from the commandment of his lips. I have treasured up the words of his mouth more than my necessary food. ^12

**13** But he stands alone, and who can oppose him? What his soul desires, even that he does. ^13

**14** For he performs that which is appointed for me. Many such things are with him. ^14

**15** Therefore I am terrified at his presence. When I consider, I am afraid of him. ^15

**16** For God has made my heart faint. The Almighty has terrified me. ^16

**17** Because I was not cut off before the darkness, neither did he cover the thick darkness from my face. ^17
