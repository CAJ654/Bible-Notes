# Job 15 (WEBU)

← [[Job 14 - WEBU]] | [[Job 16 - WEBU]] →

**1** Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered, ^1

**2** “Should a wise man answer with vain knowledge, and fill himself with the east wind? ^2

**3** Should he reason with unprofitable talk, or with speeches with which he can do no good? ^3

**4** Yes, you do away with fear, and hinder devotion before God. ^4

**5** For your iniquity teaches your mouth, and you choose the language of the crafty. ^5

**6** Your own mouth condemns you, and not I. Yes, your own lips testify against you. ^6

**7** “Are you the first man who was born? Or were you brought out before the hills? ^7

**8** Have you heard the secret counsel of God? Do you limit wisdom to yourself? ^8

**9** What do you know that we don’t know? What do you understand which is not in us? ^9

**10** With us are both the gray-headed and the very aged men, much older than your father. ^10

**11** Are the consolations of God too small for you, even the word that is gentle toward you? ^11

**12** Why does your heart carry you away? Why do your eyes flash, ^12

**13** that you turn your spirit against God, and let such words go out of your mouth? ^13

**14** What is man, that he should be clean? What is he who is born of a woman, that he should be righteous? ^14

**15** Behold, he puts no trust in his holy ones. Yes, the heavens are not clean in his sight; ^15

**16** how much less one who is abominable and corrupt, a man who drinks iniquity like water! ^16

**17** “I will show you, listen to me; that which I have seen I will declare ^17

**18** (which wise men have told by their fathers, and have not hidden it; ^18

**19** to whom alone the land was given, and no stranger passed among them): ^19

**20** the wicked man writhes in pain all his days, even the number of years that are laid up for the oppressor. ^20

**21** A sound of terrors is in his ears. In prosperity the destroyer will come on him. ^21

**22** He doesn’t believe that he will return out of darkness. He is waited for by the sword. ^22

**23** He wanders abroad for bread, saying, ‘Where is it?’ He knows that the day of darkness is ready at his hand. ^23

**24** Distress and anguish make him afraid. They prevail against him, as a king ready to the battle. ^24

**25** Because he has stretched out his hand against God, and behaves himself proudly against the Almighty, ^25

**26** he runs at him with a stiff neck, with the thick shields of his bucklers, ^26

**27** because he has covered his face with his fatness, and gathered fat on his thighs. ^27

**28** He has lived in desolate cities, in houses which no one inhabited, which were ready to become heaps. ^28

**29** He will not be rich, neither will his substance continue, neither will their possessions be extended on the earth. ^29

**30** He will not depart out of darkness. The flame will dry up his branches. He will go away by the breath of God’s mouth. ^30

**31** Let him not trust in emptiness, deceiving himself, for emptiness will be his reward. ^31

**32** It will be accomplished before his time. His branch will not be green. ^32

**33** He will shake off his unripe grape as the vine, and will cast off his flower as the olive tree. ^33

**34** For the company of the godless will be barren, and fire will consume the tents of bribery. ^34

**35** They conceive mischief and produce iniquity. Their heart prepares deceit.” ^35
