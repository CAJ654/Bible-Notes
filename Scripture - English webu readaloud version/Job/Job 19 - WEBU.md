# Job 19 (WEBU)

← [[Job 18 - WEBU]] | [[Job 20 - WEBU]] →

**1** Then Job answered, ^1

**2** “How long will you torment me, and crush me with words? ^2

**3** You have reproached me ten times. You aren’t ashamed that you attack me. ^3

**4** If it is true that I have erred, my error remains with myself. ^4

**5** If indeed you will magnify yourselves against me, and plead against me my reproach, ^5

**6** know now that God has subverted me, and has surrounded me with his net. ^6

**7** “Behold, I cry out of wrong, but I am not heard. I cry for help, but there is no justice. ^7

**8** He has walled up my way so that I can’t pass, and has set darkness in my paths. ^8

**9** He has stripped me of my glory, and taken the crown from my head. ^9

**10** He has broken me down on every side, and I am gone. He has plucked my hope up like a tree. ^10

**11** He has also kindled his wrath against me. He counts me among his adversaries. ^11

**12** His troops come on together, build a siege ramp against me, and encamp around my tent. ^12

**13** “He has put my brothers far from me. My acquaintances are wholly estranged from me. ^13

**14** My relatives have gone away. My familiar friends have forgotten me. ^14

**15** Those who dwell in my house and my maids consider me a stranger. I am an alien in their sight. ^15

**16** I call to my servant, and he gives me no answer. I beg him with my mouth. ^16

**17** My breath is offensive to my wife. I am loathsome to the children of my own mother. ^17

**18** Even young children despise me. If I arise, they speak against me. ^18

**19** All my familiar friends abhor me. They whom I loved have turned against me. ^19

**20** My bones stick to my skin and to my flesh. I have escaped by the skin of my teeth. ^20

**21** “Have pity on me. Have pity on me, you my friends, for the hand of God has touched me. ^21

**22** Why do you persecute me as God, and are not satisfied with my flesh? ^22

**23** “Oh that my words were now written! Oh that they were inscribed in a book! ^23

**24** That with an iron pen and lead they were engraved in the rock forever! ^24

**25** But as for me, I know that my Redeemer lives. In the end, he will stand upon the earth. ^25

**26** After my skin is destroyed, then I will see God in my flesh, ^26

**27** whom I, even I, will see on my side. My eyes will see, and not as a stranger. “My heart is consumed within me. ^27

**28** If you say, ‘How we will persecute him!’ because the root of the matter is found in me, ^28

**29** be afraid of the sword, for wrath brings the punishments of the sword, that you may know there is a judgment.” ^29
