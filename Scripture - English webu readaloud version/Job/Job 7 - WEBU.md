# Job 7 (WEBU)

← [[Job 6 - WEBU]] | [[Job 8 - WEBU]] →

**1** “Isn’t a man forced to labor on earth? Aren’t his days like the days of a hired hand? ^1

**2** As a servant who earnestly desires the shadow, as a hireling who looks for his wages, ^2

**3** so I am made to possess months of misery, wearisome nights are appointed to me. ^3

**4** When I lie down, I say, ‘When will I arise, and the night be gone?’ I toss and turn until the dawning of the day. ^4

**5** My flesh is clothed with worms and clods of dust. My skin closes up, and breaks out afresh. ^5

**6** My days are swifter than a weaver’s shuttle, and are spent without hope. ^6

**7** Oh remember that my life is a breath. My eye will no more see good. ^7

**8** The eye of him who sees me will see me no more. Your eyes will be on me, but I will not be. ^8

**9** As the cloud is consumed and vanishes away, so he who goes down to Sheol will come up no more. ^9

**10** He will return no more to his house, neither will his place know him any more. ^10

**11** “Therefore I will not keep silent. I will speak in the anguish of my spirit. I will complain in the bitterness of my soul. ^11

**12** Am I a sea, or a sea monster, that you put a guard over me? ^12

**13** When I say, ‘My bed will comfort me. My couch will ease my complaint,’ ^13

**14** then you scare me with dreams and terrify me through visions, ^14

**15** so that my soul chooses strangling, death rather than my bones. ^15

**16** I loathe my life. I don’t want to live forever. Leave me alone, for my days are but a breath. ^16

**17** What is man, that you should magnify him, that you should set your mind on him, ^17

**18** that you should visit him every morning, and test him every moment? ^18

**19** How long will you not look away from me, nor leave me alone until I swallow down my spittle? ^19

**20** If I have sinned, what do I do to you, you watcher of men? Why have you set me as a mark for you, so that I am a burden to myself? ^20

**21** Why do you not pardon my disobedience, and take away my iniquity? For now will I lie down in the dust. You will seek me diligently, but I will not be.” ^21
