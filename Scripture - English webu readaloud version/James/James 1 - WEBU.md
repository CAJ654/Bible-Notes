# James 1 (WEBU)

[[James 2 - WEBU]] →

**1** James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, to the twelve tribes which are in the Dispersion: Greetings. ^1

**2** Count it all joy, my brothers, when you fall into various temptations, ^2

**3** knowing that the testing of your faith produces endurance. ^3

**4** Let endurance have its perfect work, that you may be perfect and complete, lacking in nothing. ^4

**5** But if any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask of God, who gives to all liberally and without reproach, and it will be given to him. ^5

**6** But let him ask in faith, without any doubting, for he who doubts is like a wave of the sea, driven by the wind and tossed. ^6

**7** For that man shouldn’t think that he will receive anything from the Lord. ^7

**8** He is a double-minded man, unstable in all his ways. ^8

**9** Let the brother in humble circumstances glory in his high position; ^9

**10** and the rich, in that he is made humble, because like the flower in the grass, he will pass away. ^10

**11** For the sun arises with the scorching wind and withers the grass; and the flower in it falls, and the beauty of its appearance perishes. So the rich man will also fade away in his pursuits. ^11

**12** Blessed is a person who endures temptation, for when he has been approved, he will receive the crown of life which the Lord promised to those who love him. ^12

**13** Let no man say when he is tempted, “I am tempted by God,” for God can’t be tempted by evil, and he himself tempts no one. ^13

**14** But each one is tempted when he is drawn away by his own lust and enticed. ^14

**15** Then the lust, when it has conceived, bears sin. The sin, when it is full grown, produces death. ^15

**16** Don’t be deceived, my beloved brothers. ^16

**17** Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of lights, with whom can be no variation nor turning shadow. ^17

**18** Of his own will he gave birth to us by the word of truth, that we should be a kind of first fruits of his creatures. ^18

**19** So, then, my beloved brothers, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, and slow to anger; ^19

**20** for the anger of man doesn’t produce the righteousness of God. ^20

**21** Therefore, putting away all filthiness and overflowing of wickedness, receive with humility the implanted word, which is able to save your souls. ^21

**22** But be doers of the word, and not only hearers, deluding your own selves. ^22

**23** For if anyone is a hearer of the word and not a doer, he is like a man looking at his natural face in a mirror; ^23

**24** for he sees himself, and goes away, and immediately forgets what kind of man he was. ^24

**25** But he who looks into the perfect law of freedom and continues, not being a hearer who forgets but a doer of the work, this man will be blessed in what he does. ^25

**26** If anyone among you thinks himself to be religious while he doesn’t bridle his tongue, but deceives his heart, this man’s religion is worthless. ^26

**27** Pure religion and undefiled before our God and Father is this: to visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction, and to keep oneself unstained by the world. ^27
