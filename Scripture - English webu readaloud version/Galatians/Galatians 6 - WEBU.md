# Galatians 6 (WEBU)

← [[Galatians 5 - WEBU]]

**1** Brothers, even if a man is caught in some fault, you who are spiritual must restore such a one in a spirit of gentleness, looking to yourself so that you also aren’t tempted. ^1

**2** Bear one another’s burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ. ^2

**3** For if a man thinks himself to be something when he is nothing, he deceives himself. ^3

**4** But let each man examine his own work, and then he will have reason to boast in himself, and not in someone else. ^4

**5** For each man will bear his own burden. ^5

**6** But let him who is taught in the word share all good things with him who teaches. ^6

**7** Don’t be deceived. God is not mocked, for whatever a man sows, that he will also reap. ^7

**8** For he who sows to his own flesh will from the flesh reap corruption. But he who sows to the Spirit will from the Spirit reap eternal life. ^8

**9** Let’s not be weary in doing good, for we will reap in due season if we don’t give up. ^9

**10** So then, as we have opportunity, let’s do what is good toward all men, and especially toward those who are of the household of the faith. ^10

**11** See with what large letters I write to you with my own hand. ^11

**12** As many as desire to make a good impression in the flesh compel you to be circumcised, just so they may not be persecuted for the cross of Christ. ^12

**13** For even they who receive circumcision don’t keep the law themselves, but they desire to have you circumcised, so that they may boast in your flesh. ^13

**14** But far be it from me to boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world. ^14

**15** For in Christ Jesus neither is circumcision anything, nor uncircumcision, but a new creation. ^15

**16** As many as walk by this rule, peace and mercy be on them, and on God’s Israel. ^16

**17** From now on, let no one cause me any trouble, for I bear the marks of the Lord Jesus branded on my body. ^17

**18** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brothers. Amen. ^18
