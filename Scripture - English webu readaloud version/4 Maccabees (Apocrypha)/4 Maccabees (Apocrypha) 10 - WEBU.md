# 4 Maccabees (Apocrypha) 10 (WEBU)

← [[4 Maccabees (Apocrypha) 9 - WEBU]] | [[4 Maccabees (Apocrypha) 11 - WEBU]] →

**1** Now this one endured this praiseworthy death. The third was brought along, and exhorted by many to taste and save his life. ^1

**2** But he cried out and said, “Don’t you know that the father of those who are dead is my father also, and that the same mother bore me, and that I was brought up in the same way? ^2

**3** I don’t renounce the noble relationship of my kindred. ^3

**4** Now then, whatever instrument of vengeance you have, apply it to my body, for you aren’t able to touch my soul, even if you want to.” ^4

**5** But they, highly incensed at his boldness of speech, dislocated his hands and feet with racking engines, and wrenching them from their sockets, dismembered him. ^5

**6** They dragged around his fingers, his arms, his legs, and his ankles. ^6

**7** Not being able by any means to strangle him, they tore off his skin, together with the extreme tips of his fingers, and then dragged him to the wheel, ^7

**8** around which his vertebral joints were loosened, and he saw his own flesh torn to shreds, and streams of blood flowing from his entrails. ^8

**9** When about to die, he said, ^9

**10** “We, O accursed tyrant, suffer this for the sake of Divine education and virtue. ^10

**11** But you, for your impiety and blood shedding, will endure unceasing torments.” ^11

**12** Thus having died worthily of his kindred, they dragged forward the fourth, saying, ^12

**13** “Don’t share the madness of your kindred, but respect the king and save yourself.” ^13

**14** But he said to them, “You don’t have a fire so scorching as to make me play the coward. ^14

**15** By the blessed death of my kindred, and the eternal punishment of the tyrant, and the glorious life of the pious, I will not repudiate the noble brotherhood. ^15

**16** Invent, O tyrant, tortures, that you may learn, even through them, that I am the brother of those tormented before.” ^16

**17** When he had said this, the blood-thirsty, murderous, and unholy Antiochus ordered his tongue to be cut out. ^17

**18** But he said, “Even if you take away the organ of speech, God still hears the silent. ^18

**19** Behold, my tongue is extended, cut it off; for in spite of that you won’t silence our reasoning. ^19

**20** We gladly lose our limbs on behalf of God. ^20

**21** But God will speedily find you, since you cut off the tongue, the instrument of divine melody.” ^21
