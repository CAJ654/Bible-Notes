# Proverbs 6 (WEBU)

← [[Proverbs 5 - WEBU]] | [[Proverbs 7 - WEBU]] →

**1** My son, if you have become collateral for your neighbor, if you have struck your hands in pledge for a stranger, ^1

**2** you are trapped by the words of your mouth; you are ensnared with the words of your mouth. ^2

**3** Do this now, my son, and deliver yourself, since you have come into the hand of your neighbor. Go, humble yourself. Press your plea with your neighbor. ^3

**4** Give no sleep to your eyes, nor slumber to your eyelids. ^4

**5** Free yourself, like a gazelle from the hand of the hunter, like a bird from the snare of the fowler. ^5

**6** Go to the ant, you sluggard. Consider her ways, and be wise; ^6

**7** which having no chief, overseer, or ruler, ^7

**8** provides her bread in the summer, and gathers her food in the harvest. ^8

**9** How long will you sleep, sluggard? When will you arise out of your sleep? ^9

**10** A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep— ^10

**11** so your poverty will come as a robber, and your scarcity as an armed man. ^11

**12** A worthless person, a man of iniquity, is he who walks with a perverse mouth, ^12

**13** who winks with his eyes, who signals with his feet, who motions with his fingers, ^13

**14** in whose heart is perverseness, who devises evil continually, who always sows discord. ^14

**15** Therefore his calamity will come suddenly. He will be broken suddenly, and that without remedy. ^15

**16** There are six things which the LORD hates; yes, seven which are an abomination to him: ^16

**17** arrogant eyes, a lying tongue, hands that shed innocent blood, ^17

**18** a heart that devises wicked schemes, feet that are swift in running to mischief, ^18

**19** a false witness who utters lies, and he who sows discord among brothers. ^19

**20** My son, keep your father’s commandment, and don’t forsake your mother’s teaching. ^20

**21** Bind them continually on your heart. Tie them around your neck. ^21

**22** When you walk, it will lead you. When you sleep, it will watch over you. When you awake, it will talk with you. ^22

**23** For the commandment is a lamp, and the law is light. Reproofs of instruction are the way of life, ^23

**24** to keep you from the immoral woman, from the flattery of the wayward wife’s tongue. ^24

**25** Don’t lust after her beauty in your heart, neither let her captivate you with her eyelids. ^25

**26** For a prostitute reduces you to a piece of bread. The adulteress hunts for your precious life. ^26

**27** Can a man scoop fire into his lap, and his clothes not be burned? ^27

**28** Or can one walk on hot coals, and his feet not be scorched? ^28

**29** So is he who goes in to his neighbor’s wife. Whoever touches her will not be unpunished. ^29

**30** Men don’t despise a thief if he steals to satisfy himself when he is hungry, ^30

**31** but if he is found, he shall restore seven times. He shall give all the wealth of his house. ^31

**32** He who commits adultery with a woman is void of understanding. He who does it destroys his own soul. ^32

**33** He will get wounds and dishonor. His reproach will not be wiped away. ^33

**34** For jealousy arouses the fury of the husband. He won’t spare in the day of vengeance. ^34

**35** He won’t regard any ransom, neither will he rest content, though you give many gifts. ^35
