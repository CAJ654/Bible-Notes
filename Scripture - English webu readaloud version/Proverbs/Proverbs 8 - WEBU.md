# Proverbs 8 (WEBU)

← [[Proverbs 7 - WEBU]] | [[Proverbs 9 - WEBU]] →

**1** Doesn’t wisdom cry out? Doesn’t understanding raise her voice? ^1

**2** On the top of high places by the way, where the paths meet, she stands. ^2

**3** Beside the gates, at the entry of the city, at the entry doors, she cries aloud: ^3

**4** “I call to you men! I send my voice to the sons of mankind. ^4

**5** You simple, understand prudence! You fools, be of an understanding heart! ^5

**6** Hear, for I will speak excellent things. The opening of my lips is for right things. ^6

**7** For my mouth speaks truth. Wickedness is an abomination to my lips. ^7

**8** All the words of my mouth are in righteousness. There is nothing crooked or perverse in them. ^8

**9** They are all plain to him who understands, right to those who find knowledge. ^9

**10** Receive my instruction rather than silver, knowledge rather than choice gold. ^10

**11** For wisdom is better than rubies. All the things that may be desired can’t be compared to it. ^11

**12** “I, wisdom, have made prudence my dwelling. Find out knowledge and discretion. ^12

**13** The fear of the LORD is to hate evil. I hate pride, arrogance, the evil way, and the perverse mouth. ^13

**14** Counsel and sound knowledge are mine. I have understanding and power. ^14

**15** By me kings reign, and princes decree justice. ^15

**16** By me princes rule, nobles, and all the righteous rulers of the earth. ^16

**17** I love those who love me. Those who seek me diligently will find me. ^17

**18** With me are riches, honor, enduring wealth, and prosperity. ^18

**19** My fruit is better than gold, yes, than fine gold, my yield than choice silver. ^19

**20** I walk in the way of righteousness, in the middle of the paths of justice, ^20

**21** that I may give wealth to those who love me. I fill their treasuries. ^21

**22** “The LORD possessed me in the beginning of his work, before his deeds of old. ^22

**23** I was set up from everlasting, from the beginning, before the earth existed. ^23

**24** When there were no depths, I was born, when there were no springs abounding with water. ^24

**25** Before the mountains were settled in place, before the hills, I was born; ^25

**26** while as yet he had not made the earth, nor the fields, nor the beginning of the dust of the world. ^26

**27** When he established the heavens, I was there. When he set a circle on the surface of the deep, ^27

**28** when he established the clouds above, when the springs of the deep became strong, ^28

**29** when he gave to the sea its boundary, that the waters should not violate his commandment, when he marked out the foundations of the earth, ^29

**30** then I was the craftsman by his side. I was a delight day by day, always rejoicing before him, ^30

**31** rejoicing in his whole world. My delight was with the sons of men. ^31

**32** “Now therefore, my sons, listen to me, for blessed are those who keep my ways. ^32

**33** Hear instruction, and be wise. Don’t refuse it. ^33

**34** Blessed is the man who hears me, watching daily at my gates, waiting at my door posts. ^34

**35** For whoever finds me finds life, and will obtain favor from the LORD. ^35

**36** But he who sins against me wrongs his own soul. All those who hate me love death.” ^36
