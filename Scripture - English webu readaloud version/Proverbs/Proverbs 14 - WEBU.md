# Proverbs 14 (WEBU)

← [[Proverbs 13 - WEBU]] | [[Proverbs 15 - WEBU]] →

**1** Every wise woman builds her house, but the foolish one tears it down with her own hands. ^1

**2** He who walks in his uprightness fears the LORD, but he who is perverse in his ways despises him. ^2

**3** The fool’s talk brings a rod to his back, but the lips of the wise protect them. ^3

**4** Where no oxen are, the crib is clean, but much increase is by the strength of the ox. ^4

**5** A truthful witness will not lie, but a false witness pours out lies. ^5

**6** A scoffer seeks wisdom, and doesn’t find it, but knowledge comes easily to a discerning person. ^6

**7** Stay away from a foolish man, for you won’t find knowledge on his lips. ^7

**8** The wisdom of the prudent is to think about his way, but the folly of fools is deceit. ^8

**9** Fools mock at making atonement for sins, but among the upright there is good will. ^9

**10** The heart knows its own bitterness and joy; he will not share these with a stranger. ^10

**11** The house of the wicked will be overthrown, but the tent of the upright will flourish. ^11

**12** There is a way which seems right to a man, but in the end it leads to death. ^12

**13** Even in laughter the heart may be sorrowful, and mirth may end in heaviness. ^13

**14** The unfaithful will be repaid for his own ways; likewise a good man will be rewarded for his ways. ^14

**15** A simple man believes everything, but the prudent man carefully considers his ways. ^15

**16** A wise man fears and shuns evil, but the fool is hot headed and reckless. ^16

**17** He who is quick to become angry will commit folly, and a crafty man is hated. ^17

**18** The simple inherit folly, but the prudent are crowned with knowledge. ^18

**19** The evil bow down before the good, and the wicked at the gates of the righteous. ^19

**20** The poor person is shunned even by his own neighbor, but the rich person has many friends. ^20

**21** He who despises his neighbor sins, but he who has pity on the poor is blessed. ^21

**22** Don’t they go astray who plot evil? But love and faithfulness belong to those who plan good. ^22

**23** In all hard work there is profit, but the talk of the lips leads only to poverty. ^23

**24** The crown of the wise is their riches, but the folly of fools crowns them with folly. ^24

**25** A truthful witness saves souls, but a false witness is deceitful. ^25

**26** In the fear of the LORD is a secure fortress, and he will be a refuge for his children. ^26

**27** The fear of the LORD is a fountain of life, turning people from the snares of death. ^27

**28** In the multitude of people is the king’s glory, but in the lack of people is the destruction of the prince. ^28

**29** He who is slow to anger has great understanding, but he who has a quick temper displays folly. ^29

**30** The life of the body is a heart at peace, but envy rots the bones. ^30

**31** He who oppresses the poor shows contempt for his Maker, but he who is kind to the needy honors him. ^31

**32** The wicked is brought down in his calamity, but in death, the righteous has a refuge. ^32

**33** Wisdom rests in the heart of one who has understanding, and is even made known in the inward part of fools. ^33

**34** Righteousness exalts a nation, but sin is a disgrace to any people. ^34

**35** The king’s favor is toward a servant who deals wisely, but his wrath is toward one who causes shame. ^35
