# Proverbs 9 (WEBU)

← [[Proverbs 8 - WEBU]] | [[Proverbs 10 - WEBU]] →

**1** Wisdom has built her house. She has carved out her seven pillars. ^1

**2** She has prepared her meat. She has mixed her wine. She has also set her table. ^2

**3** She has sent out her maidens. She cries from the highest places of the city: ^3

**4** “Whoever is simple, let him turn in here!” As for him who is void of understanding, she says to him, ^4

**5** “Come, eat some of my bread, Drink some of the wine which I have mixed! ^5

**6** Leave your simple ways, and live. Walk in the way of understanding.” ^6

**7** One who corrects a mocker invites insult. One who reproves a wicked man invites abuse. ^7

**8** Don’t reprove a scoffer, lest he hate you. Reprove a wise person, and he will love you. ^8

**9** Instruct a wise person, and he will be still wiser. Teach a righteous person, and he will increase in learning. ^9

**10** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom. The knowledge of the Holy One is understanding. ^10

**11** For by me your days will be multiplied. The years of your life will be increased. ^11

**12** If you are wise, you are wise for yourself. If you mock, you alone will bear it. ^12

**13** The foolish woman is loud, undisciplined, and knows nothing. ^13

**14** She sits at the door of her house, on a seat in the high places of the city, ^14

**15** to call to those who pass by, who go straight on their ways, ^15

**16** “Whoever is simple, let him turn in here.” As for him who is void of understanding, she says to him, ^16

**17** “Stolen water is sweet. Food eaten in secret is pleasant.” ^17

**18** But he doesn’t know that the departed spirits are there, that her guests are in the depths of Sheol. ^18
