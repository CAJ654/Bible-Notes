# Proverbs 7 (WEBU)

← [[Proverbs 6 - WEBU]] | [[Proverbs 8 - WEBU]] →

**1** My son, keep my words. Lay up my commandments within you. ^1

**2** Keep my commandments and live! Guard my teaching as the apple of your eye. ^2

**3** Bind them on your fingers. Write them on the tablet of your heart. ^3

**4** Tell wisdom, “You are my sister.” Call understanding your relative, ^4

**5** that they may keep you from the strange woman, from the foreigner who flatters with her words. ^5

**6** For at the window of my house, I looked out through my lattice. ^6

**7** I saw among the simple ones. I discerned among the youths a young man void of understanding, ^7

**8** passing through the street near her corner, he went the way to her house, ^8

**9** in the twilight, in the evening of the day, in the middle of the night and in the darkness. ^9

**10** Behold, there a woman met him with the attire of a prostitute, and with crafty intent. ^10

**11** She is loud and defiant. Her feet don’t stay in her house. ^11

**12** Now she is in the streets, now in the squares, and lurking at every corner. ^12

**13** So she caught him, and kissed him. With an impudent face she said to him: ^13

**14** “Sacrifices of peace offerings are with me. Today I have paid my vows. ^14

**15** Therefore I came out to meet you, to diligently seek your face, and I have found you. ^15

**16** I have spread my couch with carpets of tapestry, with striped cloths of the yarn of Egypt. ^16

**17** I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon. ^17

**18** Come, let’s take our fill of loving until the morning. Let’s solace ourselves with loving. ^18

**19** For my husband isn’t at home. He has gone on a long journey. ^19

**20** He has taken a bag of money with him. He will come home at the full moon.” ^20

**21** With persuasive words, she led him astray. With the flattering of her lips, she seduced him. ^21

**22** He followed her immediately, as an ox goes to the slaughter, as a fool stepping into a noose. ^22

**23** Until an arrow strikes through his liver, as a bird hurries to the snare, and doesn’t know that it will cost his life. ^23

**24** Now therefore, sons, listen to me. Pay attention to the words of my mouth. ^24

**25** Don’t let your heart turn to her ways. Don’t go astray in her paths, ^25

**26** for she has thrown down many wounded. Yes, all her slain are a mighty army. ^26

**27** Her house is the way to Sheol, going down to the rooms of death. ^27
