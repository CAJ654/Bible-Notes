# Proverbs 19 (WEBU)

← [[Proverbs 18 - WEBU]] | [[Proverbs 20 - WEBU]] →

**1** Better is the poor who walks in his integrity than he who is perverse in his lips and is a fool. ^1

**2** It isn’t good to have zeal without knowledge, nor to be hasty with one’s feet and miss the way. ^2

**3** The foolishness of man subverts his way; his heart rages against the LORD. ^3

**4** Wealth adds many friends, but the poor is separated from his friend. ^4

**5** A false witness shall not be unpunished. He who pours out lies shall not go free. ^5

**6** Many will entreat the favor of a ruler, and everyone is a friend to a man who gives gifts. ^6

**7** All the relatives of the poor shun him; how much more do his friends avoid him! He pursues them with pleas, but they are gone. ^7

**8** He who gets wisdom loves his own soul. He who keeps understanding shall find good. ^8

**9** A false witness shall not be unpunished. He who utters lies shall perish. ^9

**10** Delicate living is not appropriate for a fool, much less for a servant to have rule over princes. ^10

**11** The discretion of a man makes him slow to anger. It is his glory to overlook an offense. ^11

**12** The king’s wrath is like the roaring of a lion, but his favor is like dew on the grass. ^12

**13** A foolish son is the calamity of his father. A wife’s quarrels are a continual dripping. ^13

**14** House and riches are an inheritance from fathers, but a prudent wife is from the LORD. ^14

**15** Slothfulness casts into a deep sleep. The idle soul shall suffer hunger. ^15

**16** He who keeps the commandment keeps his soul, but he who is contemptuous in his ways shall die. ^16

**17** He who has pity on the poor lends to the LORD; he will reward him. ^17

**18** Discipline your son, for there is hope; don’t be a willing party to his death. ^18

**19** A hot-tempered man must pay the penalty, for if you rescue him, you must do it again. ^19

**20** Listen to counsel and receive instruction, that you may be wise in your latter end. ^20

**21** There are many plans in a man’s heart, but the LORD’s counsel will prevail. ^21

**22** That which makes a man to be desired is his kindness. A poor man is better than a liar. ^22

**23** The fear of the LORD leads to life, then contentment; he rests and will not be touched by trouble. ^23

**24** The sluggard buries his hand in the dish; he will not so much as bring it to his mouth again. ^24

**25** Flog a scoffer, and the simple will learn prudence; rebuke one who has understanding, and he will gain knowledge. ^25

**26** He who robs his father and drives away his mother is a son who causes shame and brings reproach. ^26

**27** If you stop listening to instruction, my son, you will stray from the words of knowledge. ^27

**28** A corrupt witness mocks justice, and the mouth of the wicked gulps down iniquity. ^28

**29** Penalties are prepared for scoffers, and beatings for the backs of fools. ^29
