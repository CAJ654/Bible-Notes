# Proverbs 1 (WEBU)

[[Proverbs 2 - WEBU]] →

**1** The proverbs of Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel: ^1

**2** to know wisdom and instruction; to discern the words of understanding; ^2

**3** to receive instruction in wise dealing, in righteousness, justice, and equity; ^3

**4** to give prudence to the simple, knowledge and discretion to the young man— ^4

**5** that the wise man may hear, and increase in learning; that the man of understanding may attain to sound counsel; ^5

**6** to understand a proverb and parables, the words and riddles of the wise. ^6

**7** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge, but the foolish despise wisdom and instruction. ^7

**8** My son, listen to your father’s instruction, and don’t forsake your mother’s teaching; ^8

**9** for they will be a garland to grace your head, and chains around your neck. ^9

**10** My son, if sinners entice you, don’t consent. ^10

**11** If they say, “Come with us. Let’s lie in wait for blood. Let’s lurk secretly for the innocent without cause. ^11

**12** Let’s swallow them up alive like Sheol, and whole, like those who go down into the pit. ^12

**13** We’ll find all valuable wealth. We’ll fill our houses with plunder. ^13

**14** You shall cast your lot among us. We’ll all have one purse”— ^14

**15** my son, don’t walk on the path with them. Keep your foot from their path, ^15

**16** for their feet run to evil. They hurry to shed blood. ^16

**17** For the net is spread in vain in the sight of any bird; ^17

**18** but these lay in wait for their own blood. They lurk secretly for their own lives. ^18

**19** So are the ways of everyone who is greedy for gain. It takes away the life of its owners. ^19

**20** Wisdom calls aloud in the street. She utters her voice in the public squares. ^20

**21** She calls at the head of noisy places. At the entrance of the city gates, she utters her words: ^21

**22** “How long, you simple ones, will you love simplicity? How long will mockers delight themselves in mockery, and fools hate knowledge? ^22

**23** Turn at my reproof. Behold, I will pour out my spirit on you. I will make known my words to you. ^23

**24** Because I have called, and you have refused; I have stretched out my hand, and no one has paid attention; ^24

**25** but you have ignored all my counsel, and wanted none of my reproof; ^25

**26** I also will laugh at your disaster. I will mock when calamity overtakes you, ^26

**27** when calamity overtakes you like a storm, when your disaster comes on like a whirlwind, when distress and anguish come on you. ^27

**28** Then they will call on me, but I will not answer. They will seek me diligently, but they will not find me, ^28

**29** because they hated knowledge, and didn’t choose the fear of the LORD. ^29

**30** They wanted none of my counsel. They despised all my reproof. ^30

**31** Therefore they will eat of the fruit of their own way, and be filled with their own schemes. ^31

**32** For the backsliding of the simple will kill them. The careless ease of fools will destroy them. ^32

**33** But whoever listens to me will dwell securely, and will be at ease, without fear of harm.” ^33
