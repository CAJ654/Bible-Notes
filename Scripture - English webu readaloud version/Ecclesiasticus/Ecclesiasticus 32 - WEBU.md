# Ecclesiasticus 32 (WEBU)

← [[Ecclesiasticus 31 - WEBU]] | [[Ecclesiasticus 33 - WEBU]] →

**1** Have they made you ruler of a feast? Don’t be lifted up. Be among them as one of them. Take care of them first, and then sit down. ^1

**2** And when you have done all your duties, take your place, that you may be gladdened on their account, and receive a wreath for your good service. ^2

**3** Speak, you who are older, for it’s your right, but with sound knowledge; and don’t interrupt the music. ^3

**4** Don’t pour out talk where there is a performance of music. Don’t display your wisdom at the wrong time. ^4

**5** As a ruby signet in a setting of gold, so is a music concert at a wine banquet. ^5

**6** As an emerald signet in a work of gold, so is musical melody with pleasant wine. ^6

**7** Speak, young man, if you are obliged to, but no more than twice, and only if asked. ^7

**8** Sum up your speech, many things in few words. Be as one who knows and yet holds his tongue. ^8

**9** When among great men, don’t behave as their equal. When another is speaking, don’t babble. ^9

**10** Lightning speeds before thunder. Approval goes before one who is modest. ^10

**11** Rise up in good time, and don’t be last. Go home quickly and don’t loiter ^11

**12** Amuse yourself there and do what is in your heart. Don’t sin by proud speech. ^12

**13** For these things bless your Maker, who gives you to drink freely of his good things. ^13

**14** He who fears the Lord will receive discipline. Those who seek him early will find favor. ^14

**15** He who seeks the law shall be filled with it, but the hypocrite will stumble at it. ^15

**16** Those who fear the Lord will find true judgment, and will kindle righteous acts like a light. ^16

**17** A sinful man shuns reproof, and will find a judgment according to his will. ^17

**18** A sensible person won’t neglect a thought. An insolent and proud man won’t crouch in fear, even after he has done a thing by himself without counsel. ^18

**19** Do nothing without counsel, but when you have acted, don’t regret it. ^19

**20** Don’t go in a way of conflict. Don’t stumble in stony places. ^20

**21** Don’t be overconfident on a smooth road. ^21

**22** Beware of your own children. ^22

**23** In every work guard your own soul, for this is the keeping of the commandments. ^23

**24** He who believes the law gives heed to the commandment. He who trusts in the Lord will suffer no loss. ^24
