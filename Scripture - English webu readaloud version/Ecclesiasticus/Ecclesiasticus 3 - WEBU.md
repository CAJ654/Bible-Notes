# Ecclesiasticus 3 (WEBU)

← [[Ecclesiasticus 2 - WEBU]] | [[Ecclesiasticus 4 - WEBU]] →

**1** Hear me, your father, O my children, and do what you hear, that you all may be safe. ^1

**2** For the Lord honors the father over the children, and has confirmed the judgment of the mother over her sons. ^2

**3** He who honors his father will make atonement for sins. ^3

**4** He who gives glory to his mother is as one who lays up treasure. ^4

**5** Whoever honors his father will have joy in his own children. He will be heard in the day of his prayer. ^5

**6** He who gives glory to his father will have length of days. He who listens to the Lord will bring rest to his mother, ^6

**7** and will serve under his parents, as to masters. ^7

**8** Honor your father in deed and word, that a blessing may come upon you from him. ^8

**9** For the blessing of the father establishes the houses of children, but the curse of the mother roots out the foundations. ^9

**10** Don’t glorify yourself in the dishonor of your father, for your father’s dishonor is no glory to you. ^10

**11** For the glory of a man is from the honor of his father, and a mother in dishonor is a reproach to her children. ^11

**12** My son, help your father in his old age, and don’t grieve him as long as he lives. ^12

**13** If he fails in understanding, have patience with him. Don’t dishonor him in your full strength. ^13

**14** For the kindness to your father will not be forgotten. Instead of sins it will be added to build you up. ^14

**15** In the day of your affliction it will be remembered for you, as fair weather upon ice, so your sins will also melt away. ^15

**16** He who forsakes his father is as a blasphemer. He who provokes his mother is cursed by the Lord. ^16

**17** My son, go on with your business in humility; so you will be loved by an acceptable man. ^17

**18** The greater you are, humble yourself the more, and you will find favor before the Lord. ^18

**19** For the power of the Lord is great, and he is glorified by those who are lowly. ^19

**20** Don’t seek things that are too hard for you, and don’t search out things that are above your strength. ^20

**21** Think about the things that have been commanded you, for you have no need of the things that are secret. ^21

**22** Don’t be overly busy in tasks that are beyond you, for more things are shown to you than men can understand. ^22

**23** For the conceit of many has led them astray. Evil opinion has caused their judgment to slip. ^23

**24** There is no light without eyes. There is no wisdom without knowledge. ^24

**25** A stubborn heart will do badly at the end. He who loves danger will perish in it. ^25

**26** A stubborn heart will be burdened with troubles. The sinner will heap sin upon sins. ^26

**27** The calamity of the proud has no healing, for a weed of wickedness has taken root in him. ^27

**28** The heart of the prudent will understand a proverb. A wise man desires the ear of a listener. ^28

**29** Water will quench a flaming fire; almsgiving will make atonement for sins. ^29

**30** He who repays good turns is mindful of that which comes afterward. In the time of his falling he will find a support. ^30
