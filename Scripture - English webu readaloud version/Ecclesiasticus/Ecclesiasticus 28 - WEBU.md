# Ecclesiasticus 28 (WEBU)

← [[Ecclesiasticus 27 - WEBU]] | [[Ecclesiasticus 29 - WEBU]] →

**1** He who takes vengeance will find vengeance from the Lord, and he will surely make his sins firm. ^1

**2** Forgive your neighbor the hurt that he has done, and then your sins will be pardoned when you pray. ^2

**3** Does anyone harbor anger against another and expect healing from the Lord? ^3

**4** Upon a man like himself he has no mercy, and does he make supplication for his own sins? ^4

**5** He himself, being flesh, nourishes wrath. Who will make atonement for his sins? ^5

**6** Remember your last end, and stop enmity. Remember corruption and death, and be true to the commandments. ^6

**7** Remember the commandments, and don’t be angry with your neighbor. Remember the covenant of the Highest, and overlook ignorance. ^7

**8** Abstain from strife, and you will diminish your sins, for a passionate man will kindle strife. ^8

**9** A man who is a sinner will trouble friends and sow discord among those who are at peace. ^9

**10** As is the fuel of the fire, so it will burn; and as the stoutness of the strife is, so it will burn. As is the strength of the man, so will be his wrath; and as is his wealth, so he will exalt his anger. ^10

**11** A contention begun in haste kindles a fire; and hasty fighting sheds blood. ^11

**12** If you blow on a spark, it will burn; and if you spit upon it, it will be quenched. Both of these come out of your mouth. ^12

**13** Curse the whisperer and double-tongued, for he has destroyed many who were at peace. ^13

**14** A slanderer has shaken many, and dispersed them from nation to nation. It has pulled down strong cities and overthrown the houses of great men. ^14

**15** A slanderer has cast out brave women and deprived them of their labors. ^15

**16** He who listens to it will not find rest, nor will he live quietly. ^16

**17** The stroke of a whip makes a mark in the flesh, but the stroke of a tongue will break bones. ^17

**18** Many have fallen by the edge of the sword, yet not so many as those who have fallen because of the tongue. ^18

**19** Happy is he who is sheltered from it, who has not passed through its wrath, who has not drawn its yoke, and has not been bound with its bands. ^19

**20** For its yoke is a yoke of iron, and its bands are bands of brass. ^20

**21** Its death is an evil death, and Hades is better than it. ^21

**22** It will not have rule over godly men. They will not be burned in its flame. ^22

**23** Those who forsake the Lord will fall into it. It will burn among them, and won’t be quenched. It will be sent against them like a lion. It will destroy them like a leopard. ^23

**24** As you hedge your possession about with thorns, and secure your silver and your gold, ^24

**25** so make a balance and a weight for your words, and make a door and a bar for your mouth. ^25

**26** Take heed lest you slip with it, lest you fall before one who lies in wait. ^26
