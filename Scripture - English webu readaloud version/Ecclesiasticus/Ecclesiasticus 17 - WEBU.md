# Ecclesiasticus 17 (WEBU)

← [[Ecclesiasticus 16 - WEBU]] | [[Ecclesiasticus 18 - WEBU]] →

**1** The Lord created mankind out of earth, and turned them back to it again. ^1

**2** He gave them days by number, and a set time, and gave them authority over the things that are on it. ^2

**3** He endowed them with strength proper to them, and made them according to his own image. ^3

**4** He put the fear of man upon all flesh, and gave him dominion over beasts and birds. ^4

**5** He gave them counsel, tongue, eyes, ears, and heart to have understanding. ^5

**6** He filled them with the knowledge of wisdom, and showed them good and evil. ^6

**7** He set his eye upon their hearts, to show them the majesty of his works. ^7

**8** And they will praise his holy name, that they may declare the majesty of his works. ^8

**9** He added to them knowledge, and gave them a law of life for a heritage. ^9

**10** He made an everlasting covenant with them, and showed them his decrees. ^10

**11** Their eyes saw the majesty of his glory. Their ears heard the glory of his voice. ^11

**12** He said to them, “Beware of all unrighteousness.” So he gave them commandment, each man concerning his neighbor. ^12

**13** Their ways are ever before him. They will not be hidden from his eyes. ^13

**14** For every nation he appointed a ruler, but Israel is the Lord’s portion. ^14

**15** All their works are as clear as the sun before him. His eyes are continually upon their ways. ^15

**16** Their iniquities are not hidden from him. All their sins are before the Lord. ^16

**17** With him the alms of a man is as a signet. He will keep a man’s kindness as the pupil of the eye. ^17

**18** Afterwards he will rise up and repay them, and give their repayment upon their head. ^18

**19** However to those who repent he grants a return. He comforts those who are losing hope. ^19

**20** Return to the Lord, and forsake sins. Make your prayer before his face offend less. ^20

**21** Turn again to the Most High, and turn away from iniquity. Greatly hate the abominable thing. ^21

**22** Who will give praise to the Most High in Hades, in place of the living who return thanks? ^22

**23** Thanksgiving perishes from the dead, as from one who doesn’t exist. He who is in life and health will praise the Lord. ^23

**24** How great is the mercy of the Lord, and his forgiveness to those who turn to him! ^24

**25** For humans are not capable of everything, because the son of man is not immortal. ^25

**26** What is brighter than the sun? Yet even this can be eclipsed. So flesh and blood devise evil. ^26

**27** He looks upon the power of the height of heaven, while all men are earth and ashes. ^27
