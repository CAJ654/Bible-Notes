# Ecclesiasticus 7 (WEBU)

← [[Ecclesiasticus 6 - WEBU]] | [[Ecclesiasticus 8 - WEBU]] →

**1** Do no evil, so no evil will overtake you. ^1

**2** Depart from wrong, and it will turn away from you. ^2

**3** My son, don’t sow upon the furrows of unrighteousness, and you won’t reap them sevenfold. ^3

**4** Don’t seek preeminence from the Lord, nor the seat of honor from the king. ^4

**5** Don’t justify yourself in the presence of the Lord, and don’t display your wisdom before the king. ^5

**6** Don’t seek to be a judge, lest you not be able to take away iniquities, lest perhaps you fear the person of a mighty man, and lay a stumbling block in the way of your uprightness. ^6

**7** Don’t sin against the multitude of the city. Don’t disgrace yourself in the crowd. ^7

**8** Don’t commit a sin twice, for even in one you will not be unpunished. ^8

**9** Don’t say, “He will look upon the multitude of my gifts. When I make an offering to the Most High God, he will accept it.” ^9

**10** Don’t be faint-hearted in your prayer. Don’t neglect to give alms. ^10

**11** Don’t laugh a man to scorn when he is in the bitterness of his soul, for there is one who humbles and exalts. ^11

**12** Don’t devise a lie against your brother, or do the same to a friend. ^12

**13** Refuse to utter a lie, for that habit results in no good. ^13

**14** Don’t babble in the assembly of elders. Don’t repeat your words in your prayer. ^14

**15** Don’t hate hard labor or farm work, which the Most High has created. ^15

**16** Don’t number yourself among the multitude of sinners. Remember that wrath will not wait. ^16

**17** Humble your soul greatly, for the punishment of the ungodly man is fire and the worm. ^17

**18** Don’t exchange a friend for something, neither a true brother for the gold of Ophir. ^18

**19** Don’t deprive yourself of a wise and good wife, for her grace is worth more than gold. ^19

**20** Don’t abuse a servant who works faithfully, or a hireling who gives you his life. ^20

**21** Let your soul love a wise servant. Don’t defraud him of liberty. ^21

**22** Do you have cattle? Look after them. If they are profitable to you, let them stay by you. ^22

**23** Do you have children? Correct them, and make them obedient from their youth. ^23

**24** Do you have daughters? Take care of their bodies, and don’t be overly indulgent toward them. ^24

**25** Give your daughter in marriage, and you will have accomplished a great matter. Give her to a man of understanding. ^25

**26** Do you have a wife who pleases you? Don’t cast her out. But don’t trust yourself to one who is hateful. ^26

**27** Honor your father with your whole heart, and don’t forget the birth pangs of your mother. ^27

**28** Remember that you were born of them. What will you repay them for the things that they have done for you? ^28

**29** Fear the Lord with all your soul; and revere his priests. ^29

**30** With all your strength love him who made you. Don’t forsake his ministers. ^30

**31** Fear the Lord and honor the priest. Give him his portion, even as it is commanded you: the first fruits, the trespass offering, the gift of the shoulders, the sacrifice of sanctification, and the first fruits of holy things. ^31

**32** Also stretch out your hand to the poor man, that your blessing may be complete. ^32

**33** A gift has grace in the sight of every living man. Don’t withhold grace for a dead man. ^33

**34** Don’t avoid those who weep, and mourn with those who mourn. ^34

**35** Don’t be slow to visit a sick man, for by such things you will gain love. ^35

**36** In all your words, remember eternity, and you will never sin. ^36
