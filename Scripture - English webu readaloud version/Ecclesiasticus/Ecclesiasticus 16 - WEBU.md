# Ecclesiasticus 16 (WEBU)

← [[Ecclesiasticus 15 - WEBU]] | [[Ecclesiasticus 17 - WEBU]] →

**1** Don’t desire a multitude of unprofitable children, neither delight in ungodly sons. ^1

**2** If they multiply, don’t delight in them unless the fear of the Lord is in them. ^2

**3** Don’t trust in their life. Don’t rely on their numbers; for one can be better than a thousand, and to die childless than to have ungodly children. ^3

**4** For from one who has understanding, a city will be populated, but a race of wicked men will be made desolate. ^4

**5** I have seen many such things with my eyes. My ear has heard mightier things than these. ^5

**6** In a congregation of sinners, a fire will be kindled. In a disobedient nation, wrath is kindled. ^6

**7** He was not pacified toward the giants of old time, who revolted in their strength. ^7

**8** He didn’t spare Lot’s neighbors, whom he abhorred for their pride. ^8

**9** He didn’t pity the people of perdition who were taken away in their sins, ^9

**10** or in like manner, the six hundred thousand footmen who were gathered together in the hardness of their hearts. ^10

**11** Even if there is one stiff-necked person, it is a marvel if he will be unpunished; for mercy and wrath are both with him who is mighty to forgive, and he pours out wrath. ^11

**12** As his mercy is great, so is his correction also. He judges a man according to his works. ^12

**13** The sinner will not escape with plunder. The perseverance of the godly will not be frustrated. ^13

**14** He will make room for every work of mercy. Each man will receive according to his works. ^14

**15** Don’t say, “I will be hidden from the Lord,” and “Who will remember me from on high?” I will not be known among so many people, for what is my soul in a boundless creation? ^15

**16** Behold, heaven, the heaven of heavens, the deep, and the earth, will be moved when he visits. ^16

**17** The mountains and the foundations of the earth together are shaken with trembling when he looks at them. ^17

**18** No heart will think about these things. Who could comprehend his ways? ^18

**19** Like a tempest which no man can see, so, the majority of his works are hidden. ^19

**20** Who will declare his works of righteousness? Who will wait for them? For his covenant is afar off. ^20

**21** He who is lacking in understanding thinks about these things. An unwise and erring man thinks foolishly. ^21

**22** My son, listen to me, learn knowledge, and heed my words with your heart. ^22

**23** I will impart instruction with precision, and declare knowledge exactly. ^23

**24** In the judgment of the Lord are his works from the beginning. From the making of them he determined their boundaries. ^24

**25** He arranged his works for all time, and their beginnings to their generations. They aren’t hungry or weary, and they don’t cease from their works. ^25

**26** No one pushes aside his neighbor. They will never disobey his word. ^26

**27** After this also the Lord looked at the earth and filled it with his blessings. ^27

**28** All manner of living things covered its surface, and they return into it. ^28
