# Zechariah 2 (WEBU)

← [[Zechariah 1 - WEBU]] | [[Zechariah 3 - WEBU]] →

**1** I lifted up my eyes, and saw, and behold, a man with a measuring line in his hand. ^1

**2** Then I asked, “Where are you going?” He said to me, “To measure Jerusalem, to see what is its width and what is its length.” ^2

**3** Behold, the angel who talked with me went out, and another angel went out to meet him, ^3

**4** and said to him, “Run, speak to this young man, saying, ‘Jerusalem will be inhabited as villages without walls, because of the multitude of men and livestock in it. ^4

**5** For I,’ says the LORD, ‘will be to her a wall of fire around it, and I will be the glory in the middle of her. ^5

**6** Come! Come! Flee from the land of the north,’ says the LORD; ‘for I have spread you abroad as the four winds of the sky,’ says the LORD. ^6

**7** ‘Come, Zion! Escape, you who dwell with the daughter of Babylon.’ ^7

**8** For the LORD of Armies says: ‘For honor he has sent me to the nations which plundered you; for he who touches you touches the apple of his eye. ^8

**9** For, behold, I will shake my hand over them, and they will be a plunder to those who served them; and you will know that the LORD of Armies has sent me. ^9

**10** Sing and rejoice, daughter of Zion! For behold, I come and I will dwell within you,’ says the LORD. ^10

**11** Many nations shall join themselves to the LORD in that day, and shall be my people; and I will dwell among you, and you shall know that the LORD of Armies has sent me to you. ^11

**12** The LORD will inherit Judah as his portion in the holy land, and will again choose Jerusalem. ^12

**13** Be silent, all flesh, before the LORD; for he has roused himself from his holy habitation!” ^13
