# Philemon 1 (WEBU)



**1** Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother, to Philemon, our beloved fellow worker, ^1

**2** to the beloved Apphia, to Archippus our fellow soldier, and to the assembly in your house: ^2

**3** Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. ^3

**4** I thank my God always, making mention of you in my prayers, ^4

**5** hearing of your love and of the faith which you have toward the Lord Jesus and toward all the saints, ^5

**6** that the fellowship of your faith may become effective in the knowledge of every good thing which is in us in Christ Jesus. ^6

**7** For we have much joy and comfort in your love, because the hearts of the saints have been refreshed through you, brother. ^7

**8** Therefore though I have all boldness in Christ to command you that which is appropriate, ^8

**9** yet for love’s sake I rather appeal to you, being such a one as Paul, the aged, but also a prisoner of Jesus Christ. ^9

**10** I appeal to you for my child Onesimus, whom I have become the father of in my chains, ^10

**11** who once was useless to you, but now is useful to you and to me. ^11

**12** I am sending him back. Therefore receive him, that is, my own heart, ^12

**13** whom I desired to keep with me, that on your behalf he might serve me in my chains for the Good News. ^13

**14** But I was willing to do nothing without your consent, that your goodness would not be as of necessity, but of free will. ^14

**15** For perhaps he was therefore separated from you for a while that you would have him forever, ^15

**16** no longer as a slave, but more than a slave, a beloved brother—especially to me, but how much rather to you, both in the flesh and in the Lord. ^16

**17** If then you count me a partner, receive him as you would receive me. ^17

**18** But if he has wronged you at all or owes you anything, put that to my account. ^18

**19** I, Paul, write this with my own hand: I will repay it (not to mention to you that you owe to me even your own self besides). ^19

**20** Yes, brother, let me have joy from you in the Lord. Refresh my heart in the Lord. ^20

**21** Having confidence in your obedience, I write to you, knowing that you will do even beyond what I say. ^21

**22** Also, prepare a guest room for me, for I hope that through your prayers I will be restored to you. ^22

**23** Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, greets you, ^23

**24** as do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, my fellow workers. ^24

**25** The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen. ^25
