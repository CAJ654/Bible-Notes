# Tobit (Apocrypha) 9 (WEBU)

← [[Tobit (Apocrypha) 8 - WEBU]] | [[Tobit (Apocrypha) 10 - WEBU]] →

**1** And Tobias called Raphael, and said to him, ^1

**2** “Brother Azarias, take with you a servant and two camels, and go to Rages of Media to Gabael, and receive the money for me, and bring him to the wedding feast, ^2

**3** because Raguel has sworn that I must not depart. ^3

**4** My father counts the days; and if I wait long, he will be very grieved. ^4

**5** So Raphael went on his way, and lodged with Gabael, and gave him the handwriting; so he brought forth the bags with their seals, and gave them to him. ^5

**6** Then they rose up early in the morning together, and came to the wedding feast. Tobias blessed his wife. ^6
