# Romans 12 (WMB)

← [[Romans 11 - WMB]] | [[Romans 13 - WMB]] →

**1** Therefore I urge you, brothers, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable to God, which is your spiritual service. ^1

**2** Don’t be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, so that you may prove what is the good, well-pleasing, and perfect will of God. ^2

**3** For I say through the grace that was given me, to everyone who is among you, not to think of yourself more highly than you ought to think; but to think reasonably, as God has apportioned to each person a measure of faith. ^3

**4** For even as we have many members in one body, and all the members don’t have the same function, ^4

**5** so we, who are many, are one body in Messiah, and individually members of one another, ^5

**6** having gifts differing according to the grace that was given to us: if prophecy, let’s prophesy according to the proportion of our faith; ^6

**7** or service, let’s give ourselves to service; or he who teaches, to his teaching; ^7

**8** or he who exhorts, to his exhorting; he who gives, let him do it with generosity; he who rules, with diligence; he who shows mercy, with cheerfulness. ^8

**9** Let love be without hypocrisy. Abhor that which is evil. Cling to that which is good. ^9

**10** In love of the brothers be tenderly affectionate to one another; in honor prefer one another, ^10

**11** not lagging in diligence, fervent in spirit, serving the Lord, ^11

**12** rejoicing in hope, enduring in troubles, continuing steadfastly in prayer, ^12

**13** contributing to the needs of the holy ones, and given to hospitality. ^13

**14** Bless those who persecute you; bless, and don’t curse. ^14

**15** Rejoice with those who rejoice. Weep with those who weep. ^15

**16** Be of the same mind one toward another. Don’t set your mind on high things, but associate with the humble. Don’t be wise in your own conceits. ^16

**17** Repay no one evil for evil. Respect what is honorable in the sight of all men. ^17

**18** If it is possible, as much as it is up to you, be at peace with all men. ^18

**19** Don’t seek revenge yourselves, beloved, but give place to God’s wrath. For it is written, “Vengeance belongs to me; I will repay, says the Lord.” ^19

**20** Therefore “If your enemy is hungry, feed him. If he is thirsty, give him a drink; for in doing so, you will heap coals of fire on his head.” ^20

**21** Don’t be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good. ^21
