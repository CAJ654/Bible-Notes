# Proverbs 31 (WMB)

← [[Proverbs 30 - WMB]]

**1** The words of King Lemuel—the revelation which his mother taught him: ^1

**2** “Oh, my son! Oh, son of my womb! Oh, son of my vows! ^2

**3** Don’t give your strength to women, nor your ways to that which destroys kings. ^3

**4** It is not for kings, Lemuel, it is not for kings to drink wine, nor for princes to say, ‘Where is strong drink?’ ^4

**5** lest they drink, and forget the decree, and pervert the justice due to anyone who is afflicted. ^5

**6** Give strong drink to him who is ready to perish, and wine to the bitter in soul. ^6

**7** Let him drink, and forget his poverty, and remember his misery no more. ^7

**8** Open your mouth for the mute, in the cause of all who are left desolate. ^8

**9** Open your mouth, judge righteously, and serve justice to the poor and needy.” ^9

**10** Who can find a worthy woman? For her value is far above rubies. ^10

**11** The heart of her husband trusts in her. He shall have no lack of gain. ^11

**12** She does him good, and not harm, all the days of her life. ^12

**13** She seeks wool and flax, and works eagerly with her hands. ^13

**14** She is like the merchant ships. She brings her bread from afar. ^14

**15** She rises also while it is yet night, gives food to her household, and portions for her servant girls. ^15

**16** She considers a field, and buys it. With the fruit of her hands, she plants a vineyard. ^16

**17** She arms her waist with strength, and makes her arms strong. ^17

**18** She perceives that her merchandise is profitable. Her lamp doesn’t go out by night. ^18

**19** She lays her hands to the distaff, and her hands hold the spindle. ^19

**20** She opens her arms to the poor; yes, she extends her hands to the needy. ^20

**21** She is not afraid of the snow for her household, for all her household are clothed with scarlet. ^21

**22** She makes for herself carpets of tapestry. Her clothing is fine linen and purple. ^22

**23** Her husband is respected in the gates, when he sits among the elders of the land. ^23

**24** She makes linen garments and sells them, and delivers sashes to the merchant. ^24

**25** Strength and dignity are her clothing. She laughs at the time to come. ^25

**26** She opens her mouth with wisdom. Kind instruction is on her tongue. ^26

**27** She looks well to the ways of her household, and doesn’t eat the bread of idleness. ^27

**28** Her children rise up and call her blessed. Her husband also praises her: ^28

**29** “Many women do noble things, but you excel them all.” ^29

**30** Charm is deceitful, and beauty is vain; but a woman who fears the LORD, she shall be praised. ^30

**31** Give her of the fruit of her hands! Let her works praise her in the gates! ^31
