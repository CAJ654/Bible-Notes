# Proverbs 3 (WMB)

← [[Proverbs 2 - WMB]] | [[Proverbs 4 - WMB]] →

**1** My son, don’t forget my teaching, but let your heart keep my commandments, ^1

**2** for they will add to you length of days, years of life, and peace. ^2

**3** Don’t let kindness and truth forsake you. Bind them around your neck. Write them on the tablet of your heart. ^3

**4** So you will find favor, and good understanding in the sight of God and man. ^4

**5** Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and don’t lean on your own understanding. ^5

**6** In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make your paths straight. ^6

**7** Don’t be wise in your own eyes. Fear the LORD, and depart from evil. ^7

**8** It will be health to your body, and nourishment to your bones. ^8

**9** Honor the LORD with your substance, with the first fruits of all your increase; ^9

**10** so your barns will be filled with plenty, and your vats will overflow with new wine. ^10

**11** My son, don’t despise the LORD’s discipline, neither be weary of his correction; ^11

**12** for whom the LORD loves, he corrects, even as a father reproves the son in whom he delights. ^12

**13** Happy is the man who finds wisdom, the man who gets understanding. ^13

**14** For her good profit is better than getting silver, and her return is better than fine gold. ^14

**15** She is more precious than rubies. None of the things you can desire are to be compared to her. ^15

**16** Length of days is in her right hand. In her left hand are riches and honor. ^16

**17** Her ways are ways of pleasantness. All her paths are peace. ^17

**18** She is a tree of life to those who lay hold of her. Happy is everyone who retains her. ^18

**19** By wisdom the LORD founded the earth. By understanding, he established the heavens. ^19

**20** By his knowledge, the depths were broken up, and the skies drop down the dew. ^20

**21** My son, let them not depart from your eyes. Keep sound wisdom and discretion, ^21

**22** so they will be life to your soul, and grace for your neck. ^22

**23** Then you shall walk in your way securely. Your foot won’t stumble. ^23

**24** When you lie down, you will not be afraid. Yes, you will lie down, and your sleep will be sweet. ^24

**25** Don’t be afraid of sudden fear, neither of the desolation of the wicked, when it comes; ^25

**26** for the LORD will be your confidence, and will keep your foot from being taken. ^26

**27** Don’t withhold good from those to whom it is due, when it is in the power of your hand to do it. ^27

**28** Don’t say to your neighbor, “Go, and come again; tomorrow I will give it to you,” when you have it by you. ^28

**29** Don’t devise evil against your neighbor, since he dwells securely by you. ^29

**30** Don’t strive with a man without cause, if he has done you no harm. ^30

**31** Don’t envy the man of violence. Choose none of his ways. ^31

**32** For the perverse is an abomination to the LORD, but his friendship is with the upright. ^32

**33** The LORD’s curse is in the house of the wicked, but he blesses the habitation of the righteous. ^33

**34** Surely he mocks the mockers, but he gives grace to the humble. ^34

**35** The wise will inherit glory, but shame will be the promotion of fools. ^35
