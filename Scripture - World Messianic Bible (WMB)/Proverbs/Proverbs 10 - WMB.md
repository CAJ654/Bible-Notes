# Proverbs 10 (WMB)

← [[Proverbs 9 - WMB]] | [[Proverbs 11 - WMB]] →

**1** The proverbs of Solomon. A wise son makes a glad father; but a foolish son brings grief to his mother. ^1

**2** Treasures of wickedness profit nothing, but righteousness delivers from death. ^2

**3** The LORD will not allow the soul of the righteous to go hungry, but he thrusts away the desire of the wicked. ^3

**4** He becomes poor who works with a lazy hand, but the hand of the diligent brings wealth. ^4

**5** He who gathers in summer is a wise son, but he who sleeps during the harvest is a son who causes shame. ^5

**6** Blessings are on the head of the righteous, but violence covers the mouth of the wicked. ^6

**7** The memory of the righteous is blessed, but the name of the wicked will rot. ^7

**8** The wise in heart accept commandments, but a chattering fool will fall. ^8

**9** He who walks blamelessly walks surely, but he who perverts his ways will be found out. ^9

**10** One who winks with the eye causes sorrow, but a chattering fool will fall. ^10

**11** The mouth of the righteous is a spring of life, but violence covers the mouth of the wicked. ^11

**12** Hatred stirs up strife, but love covers all wrongs. ^12

**13** Wisdom is found on the lips of him who has discernment, but a rod is for the back of him who is void of understanding. ^13

**14** Wise men lay up knowledge, but the mouth of the foolish is near ruin. ^14

**15** The rich man’s wealth is his strong city. The destruction of the poor is their poverty. ^15

**16** The labor of the righteous leads to life. The increase of the wicked leads to sin. ^16

**17** He is in the way of life who heeds correction, but he who forsakes reproof leads others astray. ^17

**18** He who hides hatred has lying lips. He who utters a slander is a fool. ^18

**19** In the multitude of words there is no lack of disobedience, but he who restrains his lips does wisely. ^19

**20** The tongue of the righteous is like choice silver. The heart of the wicked is of little worth. ^20

**21** The lips of the righteous feed many, but the foolish die for lack of understanding. ^21

**22** The LORD’s blessing brings wealth, and he adds no trouble to it. ^22

**23** It is a fool’s pleasure to do wickedness, but wisdom is a man of understanding’s pleasure. ^23

**24** What the wicked fear will overtake them, but the desire of the righteous will be granted. ^24

**25** When the whirlwind passes, the wicked is no more; but the righteous stand firm forever. ^25

**26** As vinegar to the teeth, and as smoke to the eyes, so is the sluggard to those who send him. ^26

**27** The fear of the LORD prolongs days, but the years of the wicked shall be shortened. ^27

**28** The prospect of the righteous is joy, but the hope of the wicked will perish. ^28

**29** The way of the LORD is a stronghold to the upright, but it is a destruction to the workers of iniquity. ^29

**30** The righteous will never be removed, but the wicked will not dwell in the land. ^30

**31** The mouth of the righteous produces wisdom, but the perverse tongue will be cut off. ^31

**32** The lips of the righteous know what is acceptable, but the mouth of the wicked is perverse. ^32
