# Proverbs 16 (WMB)

← [[Proverbs 15 - WMB]] | [[Proverbs 17 - WMB]] →

**1** The plans of the heart belong to man, but the answer of the tongue is from the LORD. ^1

**2** All the ways of a man are clean in his own eyes, but the LORD weighs the motives. ^2

**3** Commit your deeds to the LORD, and your plans shall succeed. ^3

**4** The LORD has made everything for its own end— yes, even the wicked for the day of evil. ^4

**5** Everyone who is proud in heart is an abomination to the LORD; they shall certainly not be unpunished. ^5

**6** By mercy and truth iniquity is atoned for. By the fear of the LORD men depart from evil. ^6

**7** When a man’s ways please the LORD, he makes even his enemies to be at peace with him. ^7

**8** Better is a little with righteousness, than great revenues with injustice. ^8

**9** A man’s heart plans his course, but the LORD directs his steps. ^9

**10** Inspired judgments are on the lips of the king. He shall not betray his mouth. ^10

**11** Honest balances and scales are the LORD’s; all the weights in the bag are his work. ^11

**12** It is an abomination for kings to do wrong, for the throne is established by righteousness. ^12

**13** Righteous lips are the delight of kings. They value one who speaks the truth. ^13

**14** The king’s wrath is a messenger of death, but a wise man will pacify it. ^14

**15** In the light of the king’s face is life. His favor is like a cloud of the spring rain. ^15

**16** How much better it is to get wisdom than gold! Yes, to get understanding is to be chosen rather than silver. ^16

**17** The highway of the upright is to depart from evil. He who keeps his way preserves his soul. ^17

**18** Pride goes before destruction, and an arrogant spirit before a fall. ^18

**19** It is better to be of a lowly spirit with the poor, than to divide the plunder with the proud. ^19

**20** He who heeds the Word finds prosperity. Whoever trusts in the LORD is blessed. ^20

**21** The wise in heart shall be called prudent. Pleasantness of the lips promotes instruction. ^21

**22** Understanding is a fountain of life to one who has it, but the punishment of fools is their folly. ^22

**23** The heart of the wise instructs his mouth, and adds learning to his lips. ^23

**24** Pleasant words are a honeycomb, sweet to the soul, and health to the bones. ^24

**25** There is a way which seems right to a man, but in the end it leads to death. ^25

**26** The appetite of the laboring man labors for him, for his mouth urges him on. ^26

**27** A worthless man devises mischief. His speech is like a scorching fire. ^27

**28** A perverse man stirs up strife. A whisperer separates close friends. ^28

**29** A man of violence entices his neighbor, and leads him in a way that is not good. ^29

**30** One who winks his eyes to plot perversities, one who compresses his lips, is bent on evil. ^30

**31** Gray hair is a crown of glory. It is attained by a life of righteousness. ^31

**32** One who is slow to anger is better than the mighty; one who rules his spirit, than he who takes a city. ^32

**33** The lot is cast into the lap, but its every decision is from the LORD. ^33
