# Proverbs 2 (WMB)

← [[Proverbs 1 - WMB]] | [[Proverbs 3 - WMB]] →

**1** My son, if you will receive my words, and store up my commandments within you, ^1

**2** so as to turn your ear to wisdom, and apply your heart to understanding; ^2

**3** yes, if you call out for discernment, and lift up your voice for understanding; ^3

**4** if you seek her as silver, and search for her as for hidden treasures; ^4

**5** then you will understand the fear of the LORD, and find the knowledge of God. ^5

**6** For the LORD gives wisdom. Out of his mouth comes knowledge and understanding. ^6

**7** He lays up sound wisdom for the upright. He is a shield to those who walk in integrity, ^7

**8** that he may guard the paths of justice, and preserve the way of his holy ones. ^8

**9** Then you will understand righteousness and justice, equity and every good path. ^9

**10** For wisdom will enter into your heart. Knowledge will be pleasant to your soul. ^10

**11** Discretion will watch over you. Understanding will keep you, ^11

**12** to deliver you from the way of evil, from the men who speak perverse things, ^12

**13** who forsake the paths of uprightness, to walk in the ways of darkness, ^13

**14** who rejoice to do evil, and delight in the perverseness of evil, ^14

**15** who are crooked in their ways, and wayward in their paths, ^15

**16** to deliver you from the strange woman, even from the foreigner who flatters with her words, ^16

**17** who forsakes the friend of her youth, and forgets the covenant of her God; ^17

**18** for her house leads down to death, her paths to the departed spirits. ^18

**19** None who go to her return again, neither do they attain to the paths of life. ^19

**20** Therefore walk in the way of good men, and keep the paths of the righteous. ^20

**21** For the upright will dwell in the land. The perfect will remain in it. ^21

**22** But the wicked will be cut off from the land. The treacherous will be rooted out of it. ^22
