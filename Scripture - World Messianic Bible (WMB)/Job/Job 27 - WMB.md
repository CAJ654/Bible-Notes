# Job 27 (WMB)

← [[Job 26 - WMB]] | [[Job 28 - WMB]] →

**1** Job again took up his parable, and said, ^1

**2** “As God lives, who has taken away my right, the Almighty, who has made my soul bitter ^2

**3** (for the length of my life is still in me, and the spirit of God is in my nostrils); ^3

**4** surely my lips will not speak unrighteousness, neither will my tongue utter deceit. ^4

**5** Far be it from me that I should justify you. Until I die I will not put away my integrity from me. ^5

**6** I hold fast to my righteousness, and will not let it go. My heart will not reproach me so long as I live. ^6

**7** “Let my enemy be as the wicked. Let him who rises up against me be as the unrighteous. ^7

**8** For what is the hope of the godless, when he is cut off, when God takes away his life? ^8

**9** Will God hear his cry when trouble comes on him? ^9

**10** Will he delight himself in the Almighty, and call on God at all times? ^10

**11** I will teach you about the hand of God. I will not conceal that which is with the Almighty. ^11

**12** Behold, all of you have seen it yourselves; why then have you become altogether vain? ^12

**13** “This is the portion of a wicked man with God, the heritage of oppressors, which they receive from the Almighty. ^13

**14** If his children are multiplied, it is for the sword. His offspring will not be satisfied with bread. ^14

**15** Those who remain of him will be buried in death. His widows will make no lamentation. ^15

**16** Though he heap up silver as the dust, and prepare clothing as the clay; ^16

**17** he may prepare it, but the just will put it on, and the innocent will divide the silver. ^17

**18** He builds his house as the moth, as a booth which the watchman makes. ^18

**19** He lies down rich, but he will not do so again. He opens his eyes, and he is not. ^19

**20** Terrors overtake him like waters. A storm steals him away in the night. ^20

**21** The east wind carries him away, and he departs. It sweeps him out of his place. ^21

**22** For it hurls at him, and does not spare, as he flees away from his hand. ^22

**23** Men will clap their hands at him, and will hiss him out of his place. ^23
