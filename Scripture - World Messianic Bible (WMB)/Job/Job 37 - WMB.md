# Job 37 (WMB)

← [[Job 36 - WMB]] | [[Job 38 - WMB]] →

**1** “Yes, at this my heart trembles, and is moved out of its place. ^1

**2** Hear, oh, hear the noise of his voice, the sound that goes out of his mouth. ^2

**3** He sends it out under the whole sky, and his lightning to the ends of the earth. ^3

**4** After it a voice roars. He thunders with the voice of his majesty. He doesn’t hold back anything when his voice is heard. ^4

**5** God thunders marvelously with his voice. He does great things, which we can’t comprehend. ^5

**6** For he says to the snow, ‘Fall on the earth,’ likewise to the shower of rain, and to the showers of his mighty rain. ^6

**7** He seals up the hand of every man, that all men whom he has made may know it. ^7

**8** Then the animals take cover, and remain in their dens. ^8

**9** Out of its room comes the storm, and cold out of the north. ^9

**10** By the breath of God, ice is given, and the width of the waters is frozen. ^10

**11** Yes, he loads the thick cloud with moisture. He spreads abroad the cloud of his lightning. ^11

**12** It is turned around by his guidance, that they may do whatever he commands them on the surface of the habitable world, ^12

**13** whether it is for correction, or for his land, or for loving kindness, that he causes it to come. ^13

**14** “Listen to this, Job. Stand still, and consider the wondrous works of God. ^14

**15** Do you know how God controls them, and causes the lightning of his cloud to shine? ^15

**16** Do you know the workings of the clouds, the wondrous works of him who is perfect in knowledge? ^16

**17** You whose clothing is warm when the earth is still by reason of the south wind? ^17

**18** Can you, with him, spread out the sky, which is strong as a cast metal mirror? ^18

**19** Teach us what we will tell him, for we can’t make our case by reason of darkness. ^19

**20** Will it be told him that I would speak? Or should a man wish that he were swallowed up? ^20

**21** Now men don’t see the light which is bright in the skies, but the wind passes, and clears them. ^21

**22** Out of the north comes golden splendor. With God is awesome majesty. ^22

**23** We can’t reach the Almighty. He is exalted in power. In justice and great righteousness, he will not oppress. ^23

**24** Therefore men revere him. He doesn’t regard any who are wise of heart.” ^24
