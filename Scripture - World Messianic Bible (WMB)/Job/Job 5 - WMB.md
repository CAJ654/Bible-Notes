# Job 5 (WMB)

← [[Job 4 - WMB]] | [[Job 6 - WMB]] →

**1** “Call now; is there any who will answer you? To which of the holy ones will you turn? ^1

**2** For resentment kills the foolish man, and jealousy kills the simple. ^2

**3** I have seen the foolish taking root, but suddenly I cursed his habitation. ^3

**4** His children are far from safety. They are crushed in the gate. Neither is there any to deliver them, ^4

**5** whose harvest the hungry eat up, and take it even out of the thorns. The snare gapes for their substance. ^5

**6** For affliction doesn’t come out of the dust, neither does trouble spring out of the ground; ^6

**7** but man is born to trouble, as the sparks fly upward. ^7

**8** “But as for me, I would seek God. I would commit my cause to God, ^8

**9** who does great things that can’t be fathomed, marvelous things without number; ^9

**10** who gives rain on the earth, and sends waters on the fields; ^10

**11** so that he sets up on high those who are low, those who mourn are exalted to safety. ^11

**12** He frustrates the plans of the crafty, so that their hands can’t perform their enterprise. ^12

**13** He takes the wise in their own craftiness; the counsel of the cunning is carried headlong. ^13

**14** They meet with darkness in the day time, and grope at noonday as in the night. ^14

**15** But he saves from the sword of their mouth, even the needy from the hand of the mighty. ^15

**16** So the poor has hope, and injustice shuts her mouth. ^16

**17** “Behold, happy is the man whom God corrects. Therefore do not despise the chastening of the Almighty. ^17

**18** For he wounds and binds up. He injures and his hands make whole. ^18

**19** He will deliver you in six troubles; yes, in seven no evil will touch you. ^19

**20** In famine he will redeem you from death; in war, from the power of the sword. ^20

**21** You will be hidden from the scourge of the tongue, neither will you be afraid of destruction when it comes. ^21

**22** You will laugh at destruction and famine, neither will you be afraid of the animals of the earth. ^22

**23** For you will be allied with the stones of the field. The animals of the field will be at peace with you. ^23

**24** You will know that your tent is in peace. You will visit your fold, and will miss nothing. ^24

**25** You will know also that your offspring will be great, your offspring as the grass of the earth. ^25

**26** You will come to your grave in a full age, like a shock of grain comes in its season. ^26

**27** Behold, we have researched it. It is so. Hear it, and know it for your good.” ^27
