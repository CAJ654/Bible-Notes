# Job 25 (WMB)

← [[Job 24 - WMB]] | [[Job 26 - WMB]] →

**1** Then Bildad the Shuhite answered, ^1

**2** “Dominion and fear are with him. He makes peace in his high places. ^2

**3** Can his armies be counted? On whom does his light not arise? ^3

**4** How then can man be just with God? Or how can he who is born of a woman be clean? ^4

**5** Behold, even the moon has no brightness, and the stars are not pure in his sight; ^5

**6** How much less man, who is a worm, and the son of man, who is a worm!” ^6
