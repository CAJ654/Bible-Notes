# Job 20 (WMB)

← [[Job 19 - WMB]] | [[Job 21 - WMB]] →

**1** Then Zophar the Naamathite answered, ^1

**2** “Therefore my thoughts answer me, even by reason of my haste that is in me. ^2

**3** I have heard the reproof which puts me to shame. The spirit of my understanding answers me. ^3

**4** Don’t you know this from old time, since man was placed on earth, ^4

**5** that the triumphing of the wicked is short, the joy of the godless but for a moment? ^5

**6** Though his height mount up to the heavens, and his head reach to the clouds, ^6

**7** yet he will perish forever like his own dung. Those who have seen him will say, ‘Where is he?’ ^7

**8** He will fly away as a dream, and will not be found. Yes, he will be chased away like a vision of the night. ^8

**9** The eye which saw him will see him no more, neither will his place see him any more. ^9

**10** His children will seek the favor of the poor. His hands will give back his wealth. ^10

**11** His bones are full of his youth, but youth will lie down with him in the dust. ^11

**12** “Though wickedness is sweet in his mouth, though he hide it under his tongue, ^12

**13** though he spare it, and will not let it go, but keep it still within his mouth, ^13

**14** yet his food in his bowels is turned. It is cobra venom within him. ^14

**15** He has swallowed down riches, and he will vomit them up again. God will cast them out of his belly. ^15

**16** He will suck cobra venom. The viper’s tongue will kill him. ^16

**17** He will not look at the rivers, the flowing streams of honey and butter. ^17

**18** He will restore that for which he labored, and will not swallow it down. He will not rejoice according to the substance that he has gotten. ^18

**19** For he has oppressed and forsaken the poor. He has violently taken away a house, and he will not build it up. ^19

**20** “Because he knew no quietness within him, he will not save anything of that in which he delights. ^20

**21** There was nothing left that he didn’t devour, therefore his prosperity will not endure. ^21

**22** In the fullness of his sufficiency, distress will overtake him. The hand of everyone who is in misery will come on him. ^22

**23** When he is about to fill his belly, God will cast the fierceness of his wrath on him. It will rain on him while he is eating. ^23

**24** He will flee from the iron weapon. The bronze arrow will strike him through. ^24

**25** He draws it out, and it comes out of his body. Yes, the glittering point comes out of his liver. Terrors are on him. ^25

**26** All darkness is laid up for his treasures. An unfanned fire will devour him. It will consume that which is left in his tent. ^26

**27** The heavens will reveal his iniquity. The earth will rise up against him. ^27

**28** The increase of his house will depart. They will rush away in the day of his wrath. ^28

**29** This is the portion of a wicked man from God, the heritage appointed to him by God.” ^29
