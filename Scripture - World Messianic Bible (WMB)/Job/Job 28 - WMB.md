# Job 28 (WMB)

← [[Job 27 - WMB]] | [[Job 29 - WMB]] →

**1** “Surely there is a mine for silver, and a place for gold which they refine. ^1

**2** Iron is taken out of the earth, and copper is smelted out of the ore. ^2

**3** Man sets an end to darkness, and searches out, to the furthest bound, the stones of obscurity and of thick darkness. ^3

**4** He breaks open a shaft away from where people live. They are forgotten by the foot. They hang far from men, they swing back and forth. ^4

**5** As for the earth, out of it comes bread. Underneath it is turned up as it were by fire. ^5

**6** Sapphires come from its rocks. It has dust of gold. ^6

**7** That path no bird of prey knows, neither has the falcon’s eye seen it. ^7

**8** The proud animals have not trodden it, nor has the fierce lion passed by there. ^8

**9** He puts his hand on the flinty rock, and he overturns the mountains by the roots. ^9

**10** He cuts out channels among the rocks. His eye sees every precious thing. ^10

**11** He binds the streams that they don’t trickle. The thing that is hidden he brings out to light. ^11

**12** “But where will wisdom be found? Where is the place of understanding? ^12

**13** Man doesn’t know its price, and it isn’t found in the land of the living. ^13

**14** The deep says, ‘It isn’t in me.’ The sea says, ‘It isn’t with me.’ ^14

**15** It can’t be gotten for gold, neither will silver be weighed for its price. ^15

**16** It can’t be valued with the gold of Ophir, with the precious onyx, or the sapphire. ^16

**17** Gold and glass can’t equal it, neither will it be exchanged for jewels of fine gold. ^17

**18** No mention will be made of coral or of crystal. Yes, the price of wisdom is above rubies. ^18

**19** The topaz of Ethiopia will not equal it. It won’t be valued with pure gold. ^19

**20** Where then does wisdom come from? Where is the place of understanding? ^20

**21** Seeing it is hidden from the eyes of all living, and kept close from the birds of the sky. ^21

**22** Destruction and Death say, ‘We have heard a rumor of it with our ears.’ ^22

**23** “God understands its way, and he knows its place. ^23

**24** For he looks to the ends of the earth, and sees under the whole sky. ^24

**25** He establishes the force of the wind. Yes, he measures out the waters by measure. ^25

**26** When he made a decree for the rain, and a way for the lightning of the thunder, ^26

**27** then he saw it, and declared it. He established it, yes, and searched it out. ^27

**28** To man he said, ‘Behold, the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom. To depart from evil is understanding.’” ^28
