# Job 35 (WMB)

← [[Job 34 - WMB]] | [[Job 36 - WMB]] →

**1** Moreover Elihu answered, ^1

**2** “Do you think this to be your right, or do you say, ‘My righteousness is more than God’s,’ ^2

**3** that you ask, ‘What advantage will it be to you? What profit will I have, more than if I had sinned?’ ^3

**4** I will answer you, and your companions with you. ^4

**5** Look to the skies, and see. See the skies, which are higher than you. ^5

**6** If you have sinned, what effect do you have against him? If your transgressions are multiplied, what do you do to him? ^6

**7** If you are righteous, what do you give him? Or what does he receive from your hand? ^7

**8** Your wickedness may hurt a man as you are, and your righteousness may profit a son of man. ^8

**9** “By reason of the multitude of oppressions they cry out. They cry for help by reason of the arm of the mighty. ^9

**10** But no one says, ‘Where is God my Maker, who gives songs in the night, ^10

**11** who teaches us more than the animals of the earth, and makes us wiser than the birds of the sky?’ ^11

**12** There they cry, but no one answers, because of the pride of evil men. ^12

**13** Surely God will not hear an empty cry, neither will the Almighty regard it. ^13

**14** How much less when you say you don’t see him. The cause is before him, and you wait for him! ^14

**15** But now, because he has not visited in his anger, neither does he greatly regard arrogance, ^15

**16** therefore Job opens his mouth with empty talk, and he multiplies words without knowledge.” ^16
