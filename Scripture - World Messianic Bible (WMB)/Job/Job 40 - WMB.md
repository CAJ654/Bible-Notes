# Job 40 (WMB)

← [[Job 39 - WMB]] | [[Job 41 - WMB]] →

**1** Moreover the LORD answered Job, ^1

**2** “Shall he who argues contend with the Almighty? He who argues with God, let him answer it.” ^2

**3** Then Job answered the LORD, ^3

**4** “Behold, I am of small account. What will I answer you? I lay my hand on my mouth. ^4

**5** I have spoken once, and I will not answer; Yes, twice, but I will proceed no further.” ^5

**6** Then the LORD answered Job out of the whirlwind: ^6

**7** “Now brace yourself like a man. I will question you, and you will answer me. ^7

**8** Will you even annul my judgment? Will you condemn me, that you may be justified? ^8

**9** Or do you have an arm like God? Can you thunder with a voice like him? ^9

**10** “Now deck yourself with excellency and dignity. Array yourself with honor and majesty. ^10

**11** Pour out the fury of your anger. Look at everyone who is proud, and bring him low. ^11

**12** Look at everyone who is proud, and humble him. Crush the wicked in their place. ^12

**13** Hide them in the dust together. Bind their faces in the hidden place. ^13

**14** Then I will also admit to you that your own right hand can save you. ^14

**15** “See now behemoth, which I made as well as you. He eats grass as an ox. ^15

**16** Look now, his strength is in his thighs. His force is in the muscles of his belly. ^16

**17** He moves his tail like a cedar. The sinews of his thighs are knit together. ^17

**18** His bones are like tubes of bronze. His limbs are like bars of iron. ^18

**19** He is the chief of the ways of God. He who made him gives him his sword. ^19

**20** Surely the mountains produce food for him, where all the animals of the field play. ^20

**21** He lies under the lotus trees, in the covert of the reed, and the marsh. ^21

**22** The lotuses cover him with their shade. The willows of the brook surround him. ^22

**23** Behold, if a river overflows, he doesn’t tremble. He is confident, though the Jordan swells even to his mouth. ^23

**24** Shall any take him when he is on the watch, or pierce through his nose with a snare? ^24
