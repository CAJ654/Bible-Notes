# 1 Chronicles 3 (WMB)

← [[1 Chronicles 2 - WMB]] | [[1 Chronicles 4 - WMB]] →

**1** Now these were the sons of David, who were born to him in Hebron: the firstborn, Amnon, of Ahinoam the Jezreelitess; the second, Daniel, of Abigail the Carmelitess; ^1

**2** the third, Absalom the son of Maacah the daughter of Talmai king of Geshur; the fourth, Adonijah the son of Haggith; ^2

**3** the fifth, Shephatiah of Abital; the sixth, Ithream by Eglah his wife: ^3

**4** six were born to him in Hebron; and he reigned there seven years and six months. He reigned thirty-three years in Jerusalem; ^4

**5** and these were born to him in Jerusalem: Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon, four, by Bathshua the daughter of Ammiel; ^5

**6** and Ibhar, Elishama, Eliphelet, ^6

**7** Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia, ^7

**8** Elishama, Eliada, and Eliphelet, nine. ^8

**9** All these were the sons of David, in addition to the sons of the concubines; and Tamar was their sister. ^9

**10** Solomon’s son was Rehoboam, Abijah his son, Asa his son, Jehoshaphat his son, ^10

**11** Joram his son, Ahaziah his son, Joash his son, ^11

**12** Amaziah his son, Azariah his son, Jotham his son, ^12

**13** Ahaz his son, Hezekiah his son, Manasseh his son, ^13

**14** Amon his son, and Josiah his son. ^14

**15** The sons of Josiah: the firstborn Yochanan, the second Jehoiakim, the third Zedekiah, and the fourth Shallum. ^15

**16** The sons of Jehoiakim: Jeconiah his son, and Zedekiah his son. ^16

**17** The sons of Jeconiah, the captive: Shealtiel his son, ^17

**18** Malchiram, Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah. ^18

**19** The sons of Pedaiah: Zerubbabel and Shimei. The sons of Zerubbabel: Meshullam and Hananiah; and Shelomith was their sister; ^19

**20** and Hashubah, Ohel, Berechiah, Hasadiah, and Jushab Hesed, five. ^20

**21** The sons of Hananiah: Pelatiah and Jeshaiah; the sons of Rephaiah, the sons of Arnan, the sons of Obadiah, the sons of Shecaniah. ^21

**22** The son of Shecaniah: Shemaiah. The sons of Shemaiah: Hattush, Igal, Bariah, Neariah, and Shaphat, six. ^22

**23** The sons of Neariah: Elioenai, Hizkiah, and Azrikam, three. ^23

**24** The sons of Elioenai: Hodaviah, Eliashib, Pelaiah, Akkub, Yochanan, Delaiah, and Anani, seven. ^24
