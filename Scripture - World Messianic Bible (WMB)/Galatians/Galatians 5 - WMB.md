# Galatians 5 (WMB)

← [[Galatians 4 - WMB]] | [[Galatians 6 - WMB]] →

**1** Stand firm therefore in the liberty by which Messiah has made us free, and don’t be entangled again with a yoke of bondage. ^1

**2** Behold, I, Paul, tell you that if you receive circumcision, Messiah will profit you nothing. ^2

**3** Yes, I testify again to every man who receives circumcision that he is a debtor to do the whole law. ^3

**4** You are alienated from Messiah, you who desire to be justified by the law. You have fallen away from grace. ^4

**5** For we through the Spirit, by faith wait for the hope of righteousness. ^5

**6** For in Messiah Yeshua neither circumcision nor uncircumcision amounts to anything, but faith working through love. ^6

**7** You were running well! Who interfered with you that you should not obey the truth? ^7

**8** This persuasion is not from him who calls you. ^8

**9** A little yeast grows through the whole lump. ^9

**10** I have confidence toward you in the Lord that you will think no other way. But he who troubles you will bear his judgment, whoever he is. ^10

**11** But I, brothers, if I still proclaim circumcision, why am I still persecuted? Then the stumbling block of the cross has been removed. ^11

**12** I wish that those who disturb you would cut themselves off. ^12

**13** For you, brothers, were called for freedom. Only don’t use your freedom as an opportunity for the flesh, but through love be servants to one another. ^13

**14** For the whole law is fulfilled in one word, in this: “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.” ^14

**15** But if you bite and devour one another, be careful that you don’t consume one another. ^15

**16** But I say, walk by the Spirit, and you won’t fulfill the lust of the flesh. ^16

**17** For the flesh lusts against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh; and these are contrary to one another, that you may not do the things that you desire. ^17

**18** But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the law. ^18

**19** Now the deeds of the flesh are obvious, which are: adultery, sexual immorality, uncleanness, lustfulness, ^19

**20** idolatry, sorcery, hatred, strife, jealousies, outbursts of anger, rivalries, divisions, heresies, ^20

**21** envy, murders, drunkenness, orgies, and things like these; of which I forewarn you, even as I also forewarned you, that those who practice such things will not inherit God’s Kingdom. ^21

**22** But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faith, ^22

**23** gentleness, and self-control. Against such things there is no law. ^23

**24** Those who belong to Messiah have crucified the flesh with its passions and lusts. ^24

**25** If we live by the Spirit, let’s also walk by the Spirit. ^25

**26** Let’s not become conceited, provoking one another, and envying one another. ^26
