# Psalms 69 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 68 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 70 - WMB]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. To the tune of “Lilies.” By David. Save me, God, for the waters have come up to my neck! ^1

**2** I sink in deep mire, where there is no foothold. I have come into deep waters, where the floods overflow me. ^2

**3** I am weary with my crying. My throat is dry. My eyes fail looking for my God. ^3

**4** Those who hate me without a cause are more than the hairs of my head. Those who want to cut me off, being my enemies wrongfully, are mighty. I have to restore what I didn’t take away. ^4

**5** God, you know my foolishness. My sins aren’t hidden from you. ^5

**6** Don’t let those who wait for you be shamed through me, Lord GOD of Hosts. Don’t let those who seek you be brought to dishonor through me, God of Israel. ^6

**7** Because for your sake, I have borne reproach. Shame has covered my face. ^7

**8** I have become a stranger to my brothers, an alien to my mother’s children. ^8

**9** For the zeal of your house consumes me. The reproaches of those who reproach you have fallen on me. ^9

**10** When I wept and I fasted, that was to my reproach. ^10

**11** When I made sackcloth my clothing, I became a byword to them. ^11

**12** Those who sit in the gate talk about me. I am the song of the drunkards. ^12

**13** But as for me, my prayer is to you, LORD, in an acceptable time. God, in the abundance of your loving kindness, answer me in the truth of your salvation. ^13

**14** Deliver me out of the mire, and don’t let me sink. Let me be delivered from those who hate me, and out of the deep waters. ^14

**15** Don’t let the flood waters overwhelm me, neither let the deep swallow me up. Don’t let the pit shut its mouth on me. ^15

**16** Answer me, LORD, for your loving kindness is good. According to the multitude of your tender mercies, turn to me. ^16

**17** Don’t hide your face from your servant, for I am in distress. Answer me speedily! ^17

**18** Draw near to my soul and redeem it. Ransom me because of my enemies. ^18

**19** You know my reproach, my shame, and my dishonor. My adversaries are all before you. ^19

**20** Reproach has broken my heart, and I am full of heaviness. I looked for some to take pity, but there was none; for comforters, but I found none. ^20

**21** They also gave me poison for my food. In my thirst, they gave me vinegar to drink. ^21

**22** Let their table before them become a snare. May it become a retribution and a trap. ^22

**23** Let their eyes be darkened, so that they can’t see. Let their backs be continually bent. ^23

**24** Pour out your indignation on them. Let the fierceness of your anger overtake them. ^24

**25** Let their habitation be desolate. Let no one dwell in their tents. ^25

**26** For they persecute him whom you have wounded. They tell of the sorrow of those whom you have hurt. ^26

**27** Charge them with crime upon crime. Don’t let them come into your righteousness. ^27

**28** Let them be blotted out of the book of life, and not be written with the righteous. ^28

**29** But I am in pain and distress. Let your salvation, God, protect me. ^29

**30** I will praise the name of God with a song, and will magnify him with thanksgiving. ^30

**31** It will please the LORD better than an ox, or a bull that has horns and hoofs. ^31

**32** The humble have seen it, and are glad. You who seek after God, let your heart live. ^32

**33** For the LORD hears the needy, and doesn’t despise his captive people. ^33

**34** Let heaven and earth praise him; the seas, and everything that moves therein! ^34

**35** For God will save Zion, and build the cities of Judah. They shall settle there, and own it. ^35

**36** The children also of his servants shall inherit it. Those who love his name shall dwell therein. ^36
