# Psalms 25 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 24 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 26 - WMB]] →

**1** By David. To you, LORD, I lift up my soul. ^1

**2** My God, I have trusted in you. Don’t let me be shamed. Don’t let my enemies triumph over me. ^2

**3** Yes, no one who waits for you will be shamed. They will be shamed who deal treacherously without cause. ^3

**4** Show me your ways, LORD. Teach me your paths. ^4

**5** Guide me in your truth, and teach me, for you are the God of my salvation. I wait for you all day long. ^5

**6** LORD, remember your tender mercies and your loving kindness, for they are from old times. ^6

**7** Don’t remember the sins of my youth, nor my transgressions. Remember me according to your loving kindness, for your goodness’ sake, LORD. ^7

**8** Good and upright is the LORD, therefore he will instruct sinners in the way. ^8

**9** He will guide the humble in justice. He will teach the humble his way. ^9

**10** All the paths of the LORD are loving kindness and truth to such as keep his covenant and his testimonies. ^10

**11** For your name’s sake, LORD, pardon my iniquity, for it is great. ^11

**12** What man is he who fears the LORD? He shall instruct him in the way that he shall choose. ^12

**13** His soul will dwell at ease. His offspring will inherit the land. ^13

**14** The friendship of the LORD is with those who fear him. He will show them his covenant. ^14

**15** My eyes are ever on the LORD, for he will pluck my feet out of the net. ^15

**16** Turn to me, and have mercy on me, for I am desolate and afflicted. ^16

**17** The troubles of my heart are enlarged. Oh bring me out of my distresses. ^17

**18** Consider my affliction and my travail. Forgive all my sins. ^18

**19** Consider my enemies, for they are many. They hate me with cruel hatred. ^19

**20** Oh keep my soul, and deliver me. Let me not be disappointed, for I take refuge in you. ^20

**21** Let integrity and uprightness preserve me, for I wait for you. ^21

**22** God, redeem Israel out of all his troubles. ^22
