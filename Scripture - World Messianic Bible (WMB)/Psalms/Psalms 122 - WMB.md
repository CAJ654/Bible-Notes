# Psalms 122 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 121 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 123 - WMB]] →

**1** A Song of Ascents. By David. I was glad when they said to me, “Let’s go to the LORD’s house!” ^1

**2** Our feet are standing within your gates, Jerusalem! ^2

**3** Jerusalem is built as a city that is compact together, ^3

**4** where the tribes go up, even the LORD’s tribes, according to an ordinance for Israel, to give thanks to the LORD’s name. ^4

**5** For there are set thrones for judgment, the thrones of David’s house. ^5

**6** Pray for the peace of Jerusalem. Those who love you will prosper. ^6

**7** Peace be within your walls, and prosperity within your palaces. ^7

**8** For my brothers’ and companions’ sakes, I will now say, “Peace be within you.” ^8

**9** For the sake of the house of the LORD our God, I will seek your good. ^9
