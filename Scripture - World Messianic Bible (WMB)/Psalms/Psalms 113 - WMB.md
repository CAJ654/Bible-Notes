# Psalms 113 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 112 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 114 - WMB]] →

**1** Praise the LORD! Praise, you servants of the LORD, praise the LORD’s name. ^1

**2** Blessed be the LORD’s name, from this time forward and forever more. ^2

**3** From the rising of the sun to its going down, the LORD’s name is to be praised. ^3

**4** The LORD is high above all nations, his glory above the heavens. ^4

**5** Who is like the LORD, our God, who has his seat on high, ^5

**6** who stoops down to see in heaven and in the earth? ^6

**7** He raises up the poor out of the dust, and lifts up the needy from the ash heap, ^7

**8** that he may set him with princes, even with the princes of his people. ^8

**9** He settles the barren woman in her home as a joyful mother of children. Praise the LORD! ^9
