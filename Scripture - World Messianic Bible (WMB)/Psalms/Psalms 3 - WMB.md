# Psalms 3 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 2 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 4 - WMB]] →

**1** A Psalm by David, when he fled from Absalom his son. LORD, how my adversaries have increased! Many are those who rise up against me. ^1

**2** Many there are who say of my soul, “There is no help for him in God.” Selah. ^2

**3** But you, LORD, are a shield around me, my glory, and the one who lifts up my head. ^3

**4** I cry to the LORD with my voice, and he answers me out of his holy hill. Selah. ^4

**5** I laid myself down and slept. I awakened, for the LORD sustains me. ^5

**6** I will not be afraid of tens of thousands of people who have set themselves against me on every side. ^6

**7** Arise, LORD! Save me, my God! For you have struck all of my enemies on the cheek bone. You have broken the teeth of the wicked. ^7

**8** Salvation belongs to the LORD. May your blessing be on your people. Selah. ^8
