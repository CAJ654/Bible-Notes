# Psalms 94 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 93 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 95 - WMB]] →

**1** LORD, you God to whom vengeance belongs, you God to whom vengeance belongs, shine out. ^1

**2** Rise up, you judge of the earth. Pay back the proud what they deserve. ^2

**3** LORD, how long will the wicked, how long will the wicked triumph? ^3

**4** They pour out arrogant words. All the evildoers boast. ^4

**5** They break your people in pieces, LORD, and afflict your heritage. ^5

**6** They kill the widow and the alien, and murder the fatherless. ^6

**7** They say, “The LORD will not see, neither will Jacob’s God consider.” ^7

**8** Consider, you senseless among the people; you fools, when will you be wise? ^8

**9** He who implanted the ear, won’t he hear? He who formed the eye, won’t he see? ^9

**10** He who disciplines the nations, won’t he punish? He who teaches man knows. ^10

**11** The LORD knows the thoughts of man, that they are futile. ^11

**12** Blessed is the man whom you discipline, LORD, and teach out of your Torah, ^12

**13** that you may give him rest from the days of adversity, until the pit is dug for the wicked. ^13

**14** For the LORD won’t reject his people, neither will he forsake his inheritance. ^14

**15** For judgment will return to righteousness. All the upright in heart shall follow it. ^15

**16** Who will rise up for me against the wicked? Who will stand up for me against the evildoers? ^16

**17** Unless the LORD had been my help, my soul would have soon lived in silence. ^17

**18** When I said, “My foot is slipping!” Your loving kindness, LORD, held me up. ^18

**19** In the multitude of my thoughts within me, your comforts delight my soul. ^19

**20** Shall the throne of wickedness have fellowship with you, which brings about mischief by statute? ^20

**21** They gather themselves together against the soul of the righteous, and condemn the innocent blood. ^21

**22** But the LORD has been my high tower, my God, the rock of my refuge. ^22

**23** He has brought on them their own iniquity, and will cut them off in their own wickedness. The LORD, our God, will cut them off. ^23
