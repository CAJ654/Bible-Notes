# Psalms 76 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 75 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 77 - WMB]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. On stringed instruments. A Psalm by Asaph. A song. In Judah, God is known. His name is great in Israel. ^1

**2** His tabernacle is also in Salem. His dwelling place in Zion. ^2

**3** There he broke the flaming arrows of the bow, the shield, and the sword, and the weapons of war. Selah. ^3

**4** Glorious are you, and excellent, more than mountains of game. ^4

**5** Valiant men lie plundered, they have slept their last sleep. None of the men of war can lift their hands. ^5

**6** At your rebuke, God of Jacob, both chariot and horse are cast into a dead sleep. ^6

**7** You, even you, are to be feared. Who can stand in your sight when you are angry? ^7

**8** You pronounced judgment from heaven. The earth feared, and was silent, ^8

**9** when God arose to judgment, to save all the afflicted ones of the earth. Selah. ^9

**10** Surely the wrath of man praises you. The survivors of your wrath are restrained. ^10

**11** Make vows to the LORD your God, and fulfill them! Let all of his neighbors bring presents to him who is to be feared. ^11

**12** He will cut off the spirit of princes. He is feared by the kings of the earth. ^12
