# Psalms 83 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 82 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 84 - WMB]] →

**1** A song. A Psalm by Asaph. God, don’t keep silent. Don’t keep silent, and don’t be still, God. ^1

**2** For, behold, your enemies are stirred up. Those who hate you have lifted up their heads. ^2

**3** They conspire with cunning against your people. They plot against your cherished ones. ^3

**4** “Come,” they say, “let’s destroy them as a nation, that the name of Israel may be remembered no more.” ^4

**5** For they have conspired together with one mind. They form an alliance against you. ^5

**6** The tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites; Moab, and the Hagrites; ^6

**7** Gebal, Ammon, and Amalek; Philistia with the inhabitants of Tyre; ^7

**8** Assyria also is joined with them. They have helped the children of Lot. Selah. ^8

**9** Do to them as you did to Midian, as to Sisera, as to Jabin, at the river Kishon; ^9

**10** who perished at Endor, who became as dung for the earth. ^10

**11** Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb, yes, all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna, ^11

**12** who said, “Let’s take possession of God’s pasture lands.” ^12

**13** My God, make them like tumbleweed, like chaff before the wind. ^13

**14** As the fire that burns the forest, as the flame that sets the mountains on fire, ^14

**15** so pursue them with your tempest, and terrify them with your storm. ^15

**16** Fill their faces with confusion, that they may seek your name, LORD. ^16

**17** Let them be disappointed and dismayed forever. Yes, let them be confounded and perish; ^17

**18** that they may know that you alone, whose name is the LORD, are the Most High over all the earth. ^18
