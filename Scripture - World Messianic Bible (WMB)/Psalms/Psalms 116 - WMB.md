# Psalms 116 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 115 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 117 - WMB]] →

**1** I love the LORD, because he listens to my voice, and my cries for mercy. ^1

**2** Because he has turned his ear to me, therefore I will call on him as long as I live. ^2

**3** The cords of death surrounded me, the pains of Sheol got a hold of me. I found trouble and sorrow. ^3

**4** Then I called on the LORD’s name: “LORD, I beg you, deliver my soul.” ^4

**5** The LORD is gracious and righteous. Yes, our God is merciful. ^5

**6** The LORD preserves the simple. I was brought low, and he saved me. ^6

**7** Return to your rest, my soul, for the LORD has dealt bountifully with you. ^7

**8** For you have delivered my soul from death, my eyes from tears, and my feet from falling. ^8

**9** I will walk before the LORD in the land of the living. ^9

**10** I believed, therefore I said, “I was greatly afflicted.” ^10

**11** I said in my haste, “All people are liars.” ^11

**12** What will I give to the LORD for all his benefits toward me? ^12

**13** I will take the cup of salvation, and call on the LORD’s name. ^13

**14** I will pay my vows to the LORD, yes, in the presence of all his people. ^14

**15** Precious in the LORD’s sight is the death of his holy ones. ^15

**16** LORD, truly I am your servant. I am your servant, the son of your servant girl. You have freed me from my chains. ^16

**17** I will offer to you the sacrifice of thanksgiving, and will call on the LORD’s name. ^17

**18** I will pay my vows to the LORD, yes, in the presence of all his people, ^18

**19** in the courts of the LORD’s house, in the middle of you, Jerusalem. Praise the LORD! ^19
