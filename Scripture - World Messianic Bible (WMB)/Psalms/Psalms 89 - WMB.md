# Psalms 89 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 88 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 90 - WMB]] →

**1** A contemplation by Ethan, the Ezrahite. I will sing of the loving kindness of the LORD forever. With my mouth, I will make known your faithfulness to all generations. ^1

**2** I indeed declare, “Love stands firm forever. You established the heavens. Your faithfulness is in them.” ^2

**3** “I have made a covenant with my chosen one, I have sworn to David, my servant, ^3

**4** ‘I will establish your offspring forever, and build up your throne to all generations.’” Selah. ^4

**5** The heavens will praise your wonders, LORD, your faithfulness also in the assembly of the holy ones. ^5

**6** For who in the skies can be compared to the LORD? Who among the sons of the heavenly beings is like the LORD, ^6

**7** a very awesome God in the council of the holy ones, to be feared above all those who are around him? ^7

**8** LORD, God of Hosts, who is a mighty one, like you? The LORD, your faithfulness is around you. ^8

**9** You rule the pride of the sea. When its waves rise up, you calm them. ^9

**10** You have broken Rahab in pieces, like one of the slain. You have scattered your enemies with your mighty arm. ^10

**11** The heavens are yours. The earth also is yours, the world and its fullness. You have founded them. ^11

**12** You have created the north and the south. Tabor and Hermon rejoice in your name. ^12

**13** You have a mighty arm. Your hand is strong, and your right hand is exalted. ^13

**14** Righteousness and justice are the foundation of your throne. Loving kindness and truth go before your face. ^14

**15** Blessed are the people who learn to acclaim you. They walk in the light of your presence, LORD. ^15

**16** In your name they rejoice all day. In your righteousness, they are exalted. ^16

**17** For you are the glory of their strength. In your favor, our horn will be exalted. ^17

**18** For our shield belongs to the LORD, our king to the Holy One of Israel. ^18

**19** Then you spoke in vision to your holy ones, and said, “I have given strength to the warrior. I have exalted a young man from the people. ^19

**20** I have found David, my servant. I have anointed him with my holy oil, ^20

**21** with whom my hand shall be established. My arm will also strengthen him. ^21

**22** No enemy will tax him. No wicked man will oppress him. ^22

**23** I will beat down his adversaries before him, and strike those who hate him. ^23

**24** But my faithfulness and my loving kindness will be with him. In my name, his horn will be exalted. ^24

**25** I will set his hand also on the sea, and his right hand on the rivers. ^25

**26** He will call to me, ‘You are my Father, my God, and the rock of my salvation!’ ^26

**27** I will also appoint him my firstborn, the highest of the kings of the earth. ^27

**28** I will keep my loving kindness for him forever more. My covenant will stand firm with him. ^28

**29** I will also make his offspring endure forever, and his throne as the days of heaven. ^29

**30** If his children forsake my Torah, and don’t walk in my ordinances; ^30

**31** if they break my statutes, and don’t keep my commandments; ^31

**32** then I will punish their sin with the rod, and their iniquity with stripes. ^32

**33** But I will not completely take my loving kindness from him, nor allow my faithfulness to fail. ^33

**34** I will not break my covenant, nor alter what my lips have uttered. ^34

**35** Once I have sworn by my holiness, I will not lie to David. ^35

**36** His offspring will endure forever, his throne like the sun before me. ^36

**37** It will be established forever like the moon, the faithful witness in the sky.” Selah. ^37

**38** But you have rejected and spurned. You have been angry with your anointed. ^38

**39** You have renounced the covenant of your servant. You have defiled his crown in the dust. ^39

**40** You have broken down all his hedges. You have brought his strongholds to ruin. ^40

**41** All who pass by the way rob him. He has become a reproach to his neighbors. ^41

**42** You have exalted the right hand of his adversaries. You have made all of his enemies rejoice. ^42

**43** Yes, you turn back the edge of his sword, and haven’t supported him in battle. ^43

**44** You have ended his splendor, and thrown his throne down to the ground. ^44

**45** You have shortened the days of his youth. You have covered him with shame. Selah. ^45

**46** How long, LORD? Will you hide yourself forever? Will your wrath burn like fire? ^46

**47** Remember how short my time is, for what vanity you have created all the children of men! ^47

**48** What man is he who shall live and not see death, who shall deliver his soul from the power of Sheol? Selah. ^48

**49** Lord, where are your former loving kindnesses, which you swore to David in your faithfulness? ^49

**50** Remember, Lord, the reproach of your servants, how I bear in my heart the taunts of all the mighty peoples, ^50

**51** With which your enemies have mocked, LORD, with which they have mocked the footsteps of your anointed one. ^51

**52** Blessed be the LORD forever more. Amen, and Amen. ^52
