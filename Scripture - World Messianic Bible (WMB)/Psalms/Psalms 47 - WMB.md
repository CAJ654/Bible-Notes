# Psalms 47 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 46 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 48 - WMB]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. A Psalm by the sons of Korah. Oh clap your hands, all you nations. Shout to God with the voice of triumph! ^1

**2** For the LORD Most High is awesome. He is a great King over all the earth. ^2

**3** He subdues nations under us, and peoples under our feet. ^3

**4** He chooses our inheritance for us, the glory of Jacob whom he loved. Selah. ^4

**5** God has gone up with a shout, the LORD with the sound of a shofar. ^5

**6** Sing praises to God! Sing praises! Sing praises to our King! Sing praises! ^6

**7** For God is the King of all the earth. Sing praises with understanding. ^7

**8** God reigns over the nations. God sits on his holy throne. ^8

**9** The princes of the peoples are gathered together, the people of the God of Abraham. For the shields of the earth belong to God. He is greatly exalted! ^9
