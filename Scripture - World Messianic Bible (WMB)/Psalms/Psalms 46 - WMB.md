# Psalms 46 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 45 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 47 - WMB]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. By the sons of Korah. According to Alamoth. God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. ^1

**2** Therefore we won’t be afraid, though the earth changes, though the mountains are shaken into the heart of the seas; ^2

**3** though its waters roar and are troubled, though the mountains tremble with their swelling. Selah. ^3

**4** There is a river, the streams of which make the city of God glad, the holy place of the tents of the Most High. ^4

**5** God is within her. She shall not be moved. God will help her at dawn. ^5

**6** The nations raged. The kingdoms were moved. He lifted his voice and the earth melted. ^6

**7** The LORD of Hosts is with us. The God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah. ^7

**8** Come, see the LORD’s works, what desolations he has made in the earth. ^8

**9** He makes wars cease to the end of the earth. He breaks the bow, and shatters the spear. He burns the chariots in the fire. ^9

**10** “Be still, and know that I am God. I will be exalted among the nations. I will be exalted in the earth.” ^10

**11** The LORD of Hosts is with us. The God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah. ^11
