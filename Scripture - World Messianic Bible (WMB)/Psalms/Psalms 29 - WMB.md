# Psalms 29 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 28 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 30 - WMB]] →

**1** A Psalm by David. Ascribe to the LORD, you sons of the mighty, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength. ^1

**2** Ascribe to the LORD the glory due to his name. Worship the LORD in holy array. ^2

**3** The LORD’s voice is on the waters. The God of glory thunders, even the LORD on many waters. ^3

**4** The LORD’s voice is powerful. The LORD’s voice is full of majesty. ^4

**5** The LORD’s voice breaks the cedars. Yes, the LORD breaks in pieces the cedars of Lebanon. ^5

**6** He makes them also to skip like a calf; Lebanon and Sirion like a young, wild ox. ^6

**7** The LORD’s voice strikes with flashes of lightning. ^7

**8** The LORD’s voice shakes the wilderness. The LORD shakes the wilderness of Kadesh. ^8

**9** The LORD’s voice makes the deer calve, and strips the forests bare. In his temple everything says, “Glory!” ^9

**10** The LORD sat enthroned at the Flood. Yes, the LORD sits as King forever. ^10

**11** The LORD will give strength to his people. The LORD will bless his people with peace. ^11
