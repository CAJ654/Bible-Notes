# Psalms 149 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 148 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 150 - WMB]] →

**1** Praise the LORD! Sing to the LORD a new song, his praise in the assembly of the holy ones. ^1

**2** Let Israel rejoice in him who made them. Let the children of Zion be joyful in their King. ^2

**3** Let them praise his name in the dance! Let them sing praises to him with tambourine and harp! ^3

**4** For the LORD takes pleasure in his people. He crowns the humble with salvation yeshu`ah. ^4

**5** Let the holy ones rejoice in honor. Let them sing for joy on their beds. ^5

**6** May the high praises of God be in their mouths, and a two-edged sword in their hand, ^6

**7** to execute vengeance on the nations, and punishments on the peoples; ^7

**8** to bind their kings with chains, and their nobles with fetters of iron; ^8

**9** to execute on them the written judgment. All his holy ones have this honor. Praise the LORD! ^9
