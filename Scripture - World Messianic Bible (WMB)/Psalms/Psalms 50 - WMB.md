# Psalms 50 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 49 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 51 - WMB]] →

**1** A Psalm by Asaph. The Mighty One, God, the LORD, speaks, and calls the earth from sunrise to sunset. ^1

**2** Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God shines out. ^2

**3** Our God comes, and does not keep silent. A fire devours before him. It is very stormy around him. ^3

**4** He calls to the heavens above, to the earth, that he may judge his people: ^4

**5** “Gather my holy ones together to me, those who have made a covenant with me by sacrifice.” ^5

**6** The heavens shall declare his righteousness, for God himself is judge. Selah. ^6

**7** “Hear, my people, and I will speak. Israel, I will testify against you. I am God, your God. ^7

**8** I don’t rebuke you for your sacrifices. Your burnt offerings are continually before me. ^8

**9** I have no need for a bull from your stall, nor male goats from your pens. ^9

**10** For every animal of the forest is mine, and the livestock on a thousand hills. ^10

**11** I know all the birds of the mountains. The wild animals of the field are mine. ^11

**12** If I were hungry, I would not tell you, for the world is mine, and all that is in it. ^12

**13** Will I eat the meat of bulls, or drink the blood of goats? ^13

**14** Offer to God the sacrifice of thanksgiving. Pay your vows to the Most High. ^14

**15** Call on me in the day of trouble. I will deliver you, and you will honor me.” ^15

**16** But to the wicked God says, “What right do you have to declare my statutes, that you have taken my covenant on your lips, ^16

**17** since you hate instruction, and throw my words behind you? ^17

**18** When you saw a thief, you consented with him, and have participated with adulterers. ^18

**19** “You give your mouth to evil. Your tongue frames deceit. ^19

**20** You sit and speak against your brother. You slander your own mother’s son. ^20

**21** You have done these things, and I kept silent. You thought that I was just like you. I will rebuke you, and accuse you in front of your eyes. ^21

**22** “Now consider this, you who forget God, lest I tear you into pieces, and there be no one to deliver. ^22

**23** Whoever offers the sacrifice of thanksgiving glorifies me, and prepares his way so that I will show God’s salvation to him.” ^23
