# Psalms 20 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 19 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 21 - WMB]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. A Psalm by David. May the LORD answer you in the day of trouble. May the name of the God of Jacob set you up on high, ^1

**2** send you help from the sanctuary, grant you support from Zion, ^2

**3** remember all your offerings, and accept your burned sacrifice. Selah. ^3

**4** May he grant you your heart’s desire, and fulfill all your counsel. ^4

**5** We will triumph in your salvation. In the name of our God, we will set up our banners. May the LORD grant all your requests. ^5

**6** Now I know that the LORD saves his anointed. He will answer him from his holy heaven, with the saving strength of his right hand. ^6

**7** Some trust in chariots, and some in horses, but we trust in the name of the LORD our God. ^7

**8** They are bowed down and fallen, but we rise up, and stand upright. ^8

**9** Save, LORD! Let the King answer us when we call! ^9
