# Psalms 62 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 61 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 63 - WMB]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. To Jeduthun. A Psalm by David. My soul rests in God alone. My salvation yeshuah-ti is from him. ^1

**2** He alone is my rock, my salvation, and my fortress. I will never be greatly shaken. ^2

**3** How long will you assault a man? Would all of you throw him down, like a leaning wall, like a tottering fence? ^3

**4** They fully intend to throw him down from his lofty place. They delight in lies. They bless with their mouth, but they curse inwardly. Selah. ^4

**5** My soul, wait in silence for God alone, for my expectation is from him. ^5

**6** He alone is my rock and my salvation yeshuah-ti, my fortress. I will not be shaken. ^6

**7** My salvation and my honor is with God. The rock of my strength, and my refuge, is in God. ^7

**8** Trust in him at all times, you people. Pour out your heart before him. God is a refuge for us. Selah. ^8

**9** Surely men of low degree are just a breath, and men of high degree are a lie. In the balances they will go up. They are together lighter than a breath. ^9

**10** Don’t trust in oppression. Don’t become vain in robbery. If riches increase, don’t set your heart on them. ^10

**11** God has spoken once; twice I have heard this, that power belongs to God. ^11

**12** Also to you, Lord, belongs loving kindness, for you reward every man according to his work. ^12
