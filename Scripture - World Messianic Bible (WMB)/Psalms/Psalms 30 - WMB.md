# Psalms 30 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 29 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 31 - WMB]] →

**1** A Psalm. A Song for the Dedication of the Temple. By David. I will extol you, LORD, for you have raised me up, and have not made my foes to rejoice over me. ^1

**2** LORD my God, I cried to you, and you have healed me. ^2

**3** LORD, you have brought up my soul from Sheol. You have kept me alive, that I should not go down to the pit. ^3

**4** Sing praise to the LORD, you holy ones of his. Give thanks to his holy name. ^4

**5** For his anger is but for a moment. His favor is for a lifetime. Weeping may stay for the night, but joy comes in the morning. ^5

**6** As for me, I said in my prosperity, “I shall never be moved.” ^6

**7** You, LORD, when you favored me, made my mountain stand strong; but when you hid your face, I was troubled. ^7

**8** I cried to you, LORD. I made supplication to the Lord: ^8

**9** “What profit is there in my destruction, if I go down to the pit? Shall the dust praise you? Shall it declare your truth? ^9

**10** Hear, LORD, and have mercy on me. LORD, be my helper.” ^10

**11** You have turned my mourning into dancing for me. You have removed my sackcloth, and clothed me with gladness, ^11

**12** to the end that my heart may sing praise to you, and not be silent. LORD my God, I will give thanks to you forever! ^12
