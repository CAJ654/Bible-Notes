# Psalms 77 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 76 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 78 - WMB]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. To Jeduthun. A Psalm by Asaph. My cry goes to God! Indeed, I cry to God for help, and for him to listen to me. ^1

**2** In the day of my trouble I sought the Lord. My hand was stretched out in the night, and didn’t get tired. My soul refused to be comforted. ^2

**3** I remember God, and I groan. I complain, and my spirit is overwhelmed. Selah. ^3

**4** You hold my eyelids open. I am so troubled that I can’t speak. ^4

**5** I have considered the days of old, the years of ancient times. ^5

**6** I remember my song in the night. I consider in my own heart; my spirit diligently inquires: ^6

**7** “Will the Lord reject us forever? Will he be favorable no more? ^7

**8** Has his loving kindness vanished forever? Does his promise fail for generations? ^8

**9** Has God forgotten to be gracious? Has he, in anger, withheld his compassion?” Selah. ^9

**10** Then I thought, “I will appeal to this: the years of the right hand of the Most High.” ^10

**11** I will remember the LORD’s deeds; for I will remember your wonders of old. ^11

**12** I will also meditate on all your work, and consider your doings. ^12

**13** Your way, God, is in the sanctuary. What god is great like God? ^13

**14** You are the God who does wonders. You have made your strength known among the peoples. ^14

**15** You have redeemed your people with your arm, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah. ^15

**16** The waters saw you, God. The waters saw you, and they writhed. The depths also convulsed. ^16

**17** The clouds poured out water. The skies resounded with thunder. Your arrows also flashed around. ^17

**18** The voice of your thunder was in the whirlwind. The lightnings lit up the world. The earth trembled and shook. ^18

**19** Your way was through the sea, your paths through the great waters. Your footsteps were not known. ^19

**20** You led your people like a flock, by the hand of Moses and Aaron. ^20
