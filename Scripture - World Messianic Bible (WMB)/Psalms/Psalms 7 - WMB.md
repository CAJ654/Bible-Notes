# Psalms 7 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 6 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 8 - WMB]] →

**1** A meditation by David, which he sang to the LORD, concerning the words of Cush, the Benjamite. LORD, my God, I take refuge in you. Save me from all those who pursue me, and deliver me, ^1

**2** lest they tear apart my soul like a lion, ripping it in pieces, while there is no one to deliver. ^2

**3** LORD, my God, if I have done this, if there is iniquity in my hands, ^3

**4** if I have rewarded evil to him who was at peace with me (yes, I have delivered him who without cause was my adversary), ^4

**5** let the enemy pursue my soul, and overtake it; yes, let him tread my life down to the earth, and lay my glory in the dust. Selah. ^5

**6** Arise, LORD, in your anger. Lift up yourself against the rage of my adversaries. Awake for me. You have commanded judgment. ^6

**7** Let the congregation of the peoples surround you. Rule over them on high. ^7

**8** The LORD administers judgment to the peoples. Judge me, LORD, according to my righteousness, and to my integrity that is in me. ^8

**9** Oh let the wickedness of the wicked come to an end, but establish the righteous; their minds and hearts are searched by the righteous God. ^9

**10** My shield is with God, who saves the upright in heart. ^10

**11** God is a righteous judge, yes, a God who has indignation every day. ^11

**12** If a man doesn’t repent, he will sharpen his sword; he has bent and strung his bow. ^12

**13** He has also prepared for himself the instruments of death. He makes ready his flaming arrows. ^13

**14** Behold, he travails with iniquity. Yes, he has conceived mischief, and brought out falsehood. ^14

**15** He has dug a hole, and has fallen into the pit which he made. ^15

**16** The trouble he causes shall return to his own head. His violence shall come down on the crown of his own head. ^16

**17** I will give thanks to the LORD according to his righteousness, and will sing praise to the name of the LORD Most High. ^17
