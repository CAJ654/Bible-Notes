# Psalms 79 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 78 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 80 - WMB]] →

**1** A Psalm by Asaph. God, the nations have come into your inheritance. They have defiled your holy temple. They have laid Jerusalem in heaps. ^1

**2** They have given the dead bodies of your servants to be food for the birds of the sky, the flesh of your holy ones to the animals of the earth. ^2

**3** They have shed their blood like water around Jerusalem. There was no one to bury them. ^3

**4** We have become a reproach to our neighbors, a scoffing and derision to those who are around us. ^4

**5** How long, LORD? Will you be angry forever? Will your jealousy burn like fire? ^5

**6** Pour out your wrath on the nations that don’t know you, on the kingdoms that don’t call on your name, ^6

**7** for they have devoured Jacob, and destroyed his homeland. ^7

**8** Don’t hold the iniquities of our forefathers against us. Let your tender mercies speedily meet us, for we are in desperate need. ^8

**9** Help us, God of our salvation, for the glory of your name. Deliver us, and forgive our sins, for your name’s sake. ^9

**10** Why should the nations say, “Where is their God?” Let it be known among the nations, before our eyes, that vengeance for your servants’ blood is being poured out. ^10

**11** Let the sighing of the prisoner come before you. According to the greatness of your power, preserve those who are sentenced to death. ^11

**12** Pay back to our neighbors seven times into their bosom their reproach with which they have reproached you, Lord. ^12

**13** So we, your people and sheep of your pasture, will give you thanks forever. We will praise you forever, to all generations. ^13
