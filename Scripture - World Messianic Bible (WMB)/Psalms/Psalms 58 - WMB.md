# Psalms 58 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 57 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 59 - WMB]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A poem by David. Do you indeed speak righteousness, silent ones? Do you judge blamelessly, you sons of men? ^1

**2** No, in your heart you plot injustice. You measure out the violence of your hands in the earth. ^2

**3** The wicked go astray from the womb. They are wayward as soon as they are born, speaking lies. ^3

**4** Their poison is like the poison of a snake, like a deaf cobra that stops its ear, ^4

**5** which doesn’t listen to the voice of charmers, no matter how skillful the charmer may be. ^5

**6** Break their teeth, God, in their mouth. Break out the great teeth of the young lions, LORD. ^6

**7** Let them vanish like water that flows away. When they draw the bow, let their arrows be made blunt. ^7

**8** Let them be like a snail which melts and passes away, like the stillborn child, who has not seen the sun. ^8

**9** Before your pots can feel the heat of the thorns, he will sweep away the green and the burning alike. ^9

**10** The righteous shall rejoice when he sees the vengeance. He shall wash his feet in the blood of the wicked, ^10

**11** so that men shall say, “Most certainly there is a reward for the righteous. Most certainly there is a God who judges the earth.” ^11
