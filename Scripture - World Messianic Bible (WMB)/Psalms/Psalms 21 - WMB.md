# Psalms 21 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 20 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 22 - WMB]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. A Psalm by David. The king rejoices in your strength, LORD! How greatly he rejoices in your salvation! ^1

**2** You have given him his heart’s desire, and have not withheld the request of his lips. Selah. ^2

**3** For you meet him with the blessings of goodness. You set a crown of fine gold on his head. ^3

**4** He asked life of you and you gave it to him, even length of days forever and ever. ^4

**5** His glory is great in your salvation. You lay honor and majesty on him. ^5

**6** For you make him most blessed forever. You make him glad with joy in your presence. ^6

**7** For the king trusts in the LORD. Through the loving kindness of the Most High, he shall not be moved. ^7

**8** Your hand will find out all of your enemies. Your right hand will find out those who hate you. ^8

**9** You will make them as a fiery furnace in the time of your anger. The LORD will swallow them up in his wrath. The fire shall devour them. ^9

**10** You will destroy their descendants from the earth, their posterity from among the children of men. ^10

**11** For they intended evil against you. They plotted evil against you which cannot succeed. ^11

**12** For you will make them turn their back, when you aim drawn bows at their face. ^12

**13** Be exalted, LORD, in your strength, so we will sing and praise your power. ^13
