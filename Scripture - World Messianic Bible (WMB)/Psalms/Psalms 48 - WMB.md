# Psalms 48 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 47 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 49 - WMB]] →

**1** A Song. A Psalm by the sons of Korah. Great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised, in the city of our God, in his holy mountain. ^1

**2** Beautiful in elevation, the joy of the whole earth, is Mount Zion, on the north sides, the city of the great King. ^2

**3** God has shown himself in her citadels as a refuge. ^3

**4** For, behold, the kings assembled themselves, they passed by together. ^4

**5** They saw it, then they were amazed. They were dismayed. They hurried away. ^5

**6** Trembling took hold of them there, pain, as of a woman in travail. ^6

**7** With the east wind, you break the ships of Tarshish. ^7

**8** As we have heard, so we have seen, in the city of the LORD of Hosts, in the city of our God. God will establish it forever. Selah. ^8

**9** We have thought about your loving kindness, God, in the middle of your temple. ^9

**10** As is your name, God, so is your praise to the ends of the earth. Your right hand is full of righteousness. ^10

**11** Let Mount Zion be glad! Let the daughters of Judah rejoice because of your judgments. ^11

**12** Walk about Zion, and go around her. Number its towers. ^12

**13** Notice her bulwarks. Consider her palaces, that you may tell it to the next generation. ^13

**14** For this God is our God forever and ever. He will be our guide even to death. ^14
