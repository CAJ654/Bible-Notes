# Psalms 38 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 37 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 39 - WMB]] →

**1** A Psalm by David, for a memorial. LORD, don’t rebuke me in your wrath, neither chasten me in your hot displeasure. ^1

**2** For your arrows have pierced me, your hand presses hard on me. ^2

**3** There is no soundness in my flesh because of your indignation, neither is there any health in my bones because of my sin. ^3

**4** For my iniquities have gone over my head. As a heavy burden, they are too heavy for me. ^4

**5** My wounds are loathsome and corrupt because of my foolishness. ^5

**6** I am in pain and bowed down greatly. I go mourning all day long. ^6

**7** For my waist is filled with burning. There is no soundness in my flesh. ^7

**8** I am faint and severely bruised. I have groaned by reason of the anguish of my heart. ^8

**9** Lord, all my desire is before you. My groaning is not hidden from you. ^9

**10** My heart throbs. My strength fails me. As for the light of my eyes, it has also left me. ^10

**11** My lovers and my friends stand aloof from my plague. My kinsmen stand far away. ^11

**12** They also who seek after my life lay snares. Those who seek my hurt speak mischievous things, and meditate deceits all day long. ^12

**13** But I, as a deaf man, don’t hear. I am as a mute man who doesn’t open his mouth. ^13

**14** Yes, I am as a man who doesn’t hear, in whose mouth are no reproofs. ^14

**15** For I hope in you, LORD. You will answer, Lord my God. ^15

**16** For I said, “Don’t let them gloat over me, or exalt themselves over me when my foot slips.” ^16

**17** For I am ready to fall. My pain is continually before me. ^17

**18** For I will declare my iniquity. I will be sorry for my sin. ^18

**19** But my enemies are vigorous and many. Those who hate me without reason are numerous. ^19

**20** They who render evil for good are also adversaries to me, because I follow what is good. ^20

**21** Don’t forsake me, LORD. My God, don’t be far from me. ^21

**22** Hurry to help me, Lord, my salvation. ^22
