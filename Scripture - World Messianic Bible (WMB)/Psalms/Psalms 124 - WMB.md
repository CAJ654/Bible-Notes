# Psalms 124 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 123 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 125 - WMB]] →

**1** A Song of Ascents. By David. If it had not been the LORD who was on our side, let Israel now say, ^1

**2** if it had not been the LORD who was on our side, when men rose up against us, ^2

**3** then they would have swallowed us up alive, when their wrath was kindled against us, ^3

**4** then the waters would have overwhelmed us, the stream would have gone over our soul. ^4

**5** Then the proud waters would have gone over our soul. ^5

**6** Blessed be the LORD, who has not given us as a prey to their teeth. ^6

**7** Our soul has escaped like a bird out of the fowler’s snare. The snare is broken, and we have escaped. ^7

**8** Our help is in the LORD’s name, who made heaven and earth. ^8
