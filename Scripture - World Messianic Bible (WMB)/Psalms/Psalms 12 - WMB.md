# Psalms 12 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 11 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 13 - WMB]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician; upon an eight-stringed lyre. A Psalm of David. Help, LORD; for the godly man ceases. For the faithful fail from among the children of men. ^1

**2** Everyone lies to his neighbor. They speak with flattering lips, and with a double heart. ^2

**3** May the LORD cut off all flattering lips, and the tongue that boasts, ^3

**4** who have said, “With our tongue we will prevail. Our lips are our own. Who is lord over us?” ^4

**5** “Because of the oppression of the weak and because of the groaning of the needy, I will now arise,” says the LORD; “I will set him in safety from those who malign him.” ^5

**6** The LORD’s words are flawless words, as silver refined in a clay furnace, purified seven times. ^6

**7** You will keep them, LORD. You will preserve them from this generation forever. ^7

**8** The wicked walk on every side, when what is vile is exalted among the sons of men. ^8
