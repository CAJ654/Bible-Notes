# Psalms 97 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 96 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 98 - WMB]] →

**1** The LORD reigns! Let the earth rejoice! Let the multitude of islands be glad! ^1

**2** Clouds and darkness are around him. Righteousness and justice are the foundation of his throne. ^2

**3** A fire goes before him, and burns up his adversaries on every side. ^3

**4** His lightning lights up the world. The earth sees, and trembles. ^4

**5** The mountains melt like wax at the presence of the LORD, at the presence of the Lord of the whole earth. ^5

**6** The heavens declare his righteousness. All the peoples have seen his glory. ^6

**7** Let all them be shamed who serve engraved images, who boast in their idols. Worship him, all you gods! ^7

**8** Zion heard and was glad. The daughters of Judah rejoiced because of your judgments, LORD. ^8

**9** For you, LORD, are most high above all the earth. You are exalted far above all gods. ^9

**10** You who love the LORD, hate evil! He preserves the souls of his holy ones. He delivers them out of the hand of the wicked. ^10

**11** Light is sown for the righteous, and gladness for the upright in heart. ^11

**12** Be glad in the LORD, you righteous people! Give thanks to his holy Name. ^12
