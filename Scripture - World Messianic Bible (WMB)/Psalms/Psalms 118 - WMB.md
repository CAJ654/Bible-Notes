# Psalms 118 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 117 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 119 - WMB]] →

**1** Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, for his loving kindness endures forever. ^1

**2** Let Israel now say that his loving kindness endures forever. ^2

**3** Let the house of Aaron now say that his loving kindness endures forever. ^3

**4** Now let those who fear the LORD say that his loving kindness endures forever. ^4

**5** Out of my distress, I called on the LORD. The LORD answered me with freedom. ^5

**6** The LORD is on my side. I will not be afraid. What can man do to me? ^6

**7** The LORD is on my side among those who help me. Therefore I will look in triumph at those who hate me. ^7

**8** It is better to take refuge in the LORD, than to put confidence in man. ^8

**9** It is better to take refuge in the LORD, than to put confidence in princes. ^9

**10** All the nations surrounded me, but in the LORD’s name I cut them off. ^10

**11** They surrounded me, yes, they surrounded me. In the LORD’s name I indeed cut them off. ^11

**12** They surrounded me like bees. They are quenched like the burning thorns. In the LORD’s name I cut them off. ^12

**13** You pushed me back hard, to make me fall, but the LORD helped me. ^13

**14** The LORD is my strength and song. He has become my salvation yeshu`ah. ^14

**15** The voice of rejoicing and salvation yeshu`ah is in the tents of the righteous. “The right hand of the LORD does valiantly. ^15

**16** The right hand of the LORD is exalted! The right hand of the LORD does valiantly!” ^16

**17** I will not die, but live, and declare the LORD’s works. ^17

**18** The LORD has punished me severely, but he has not given me over to death. ^18

**19** Open to me the gates of righteousness. I will enter into them. I will give thanks to the LORD. ^19

**20** This is the gate of the LORD; the righteous will enter into it. ^20

**21** I will give thanks to you, for you have answered me, and have become my salvation yeshu`ah. ^21

**22** The stone which the builders rejected has become the cornerstone. ^22

**23** This is the LORD’s doing. It is marvelous in our eyes. ^23

**24** This is the day that the LORD has made. We will rejoice and be glad in it! ^24

**25** Save us now, we beg you, LORD! LORD, we beg you, send prosperity now. ^25

**26** Blessed is he who comes in the LORD’s name! We have blessed you out of the LORD’s house. ^26

**27** The LORD is God, and he has given us light. Bind the sacrifice with cords, even to the horns of the altar. ^27

**28** You are my God, and I will give thanks to you. You are my God, I will exalt you. ^28

**29** Oh give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, for his loving kindness endures forever. ^29
