# Psalms 74 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 73 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 75 - WMB]] →

**1** A contemplation by Asaph. God, why have you rejected us forever? Why does your anger smolder against the sheep of your pasture? ^1

**2** Remember your congregation, which you purchased of old, which you have redeemed to be the tribe of your inheritance: Mount Zion, in which you have lived. ^2

**3** Lift up your feet to the perpetual ruins, all the evil that the enemy has done in the sanctuary. ^3

**4** Your adversaries have roared in the middle of your assembly. They have set up their standards as signs. ^4

**5** They behaved like men wielding axes, cutting through a thicket of trees. ^5

**6** Now they break all its carved work down with hatchet and hammers. ^6

**7** They have burned your sanctuary to the ground. They have profaned the dwelling place of your Name. ^7

**8** They said in their heart, “We will crush them completely.” They have burned up all the places in the land where God was worshiped. ^8

**9** We see no miraculous signs. There is no longer any prophet, neither is there among us anyone who knows how long. ^9

**10** How long, God, shall the adversary reproach? Shall the enemy blaspheme your name forever? ^10

**11** Why do you draw back your hand, even your right hand? Take it from your chest and consume them! ^11

**12** Yet God is my King of old, working salvation throughout the earth. ^12

**13** You divided the sea by your strength. You broke the heads of the sea monsters in the waters. ^13

**14** You broke the heads of Leviathan in pieces. You gave him as food to people and desert creatures. ^14

**15** You opened up spring and stream. You dried up mighty rivers. ^15

**16** The day is yours, the night is also yours. You have prepared the light and the sun. ^16

**17** You have set all the boundaries of the earth. You have made summer and winter. ^17

**18** Remember this, that the enemy has mocked you, LORD. Foolish people have blasphemed your name. ^18

**19** Don’t deliver the soul of your dove to wild beasts. Don’t forget the life of your poor forever. ^19

**20** Honor your covenant, for haunts of violence fill the dark places of the earth. ^20

**21** Don’t let the oppressed return ashamed. Let the poor and needy praise your name. ^21

**22** Arise, God! Plead your own cause. Remember how the foolish man mocks you all day. ^22

**23** Don’t forget the voice of your adversaries. The tumult of those who rise up against you ascends continually. ^23
