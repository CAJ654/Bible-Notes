# Psalms 1 (WMB)

[[Psalms 2 - WMB]] →

**1** Blessed is the man who doesn’t walk in the counsel of the wicked, nor stand on the path of sinners, nor sit in the seat of scoffers; ^1

**2** but his delight is in the LORD’s law. On his Torah he meditates day and night. ^2

**3** He will be like a tree planted by the streams of water, that produces its fruit in its season, whose leaf also does not wither. Whatever he does shall prosper. ^3

**4** The wicked are not so, but are like the chaff which the wind drives away. ^4

**5** Therefore the wicked shall not stand in the judgment, nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous. ^5

**6** For the LORD knows the way of the righteous, but the way of the wicked shall perish. ^6
