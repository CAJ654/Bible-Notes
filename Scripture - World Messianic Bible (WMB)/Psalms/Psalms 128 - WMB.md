# Psalms 128 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 127 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 129 - WMB]] →

**1** A Song of Ascents. Blessed is everyone who fears the LORD, who walks in his ways. ^1

**2** For you will eat the labor of your hands. You will be happy, and it will be well with you. ^2

**3** Your wife will be as a fruitful vine in the innermost parts of your house, your children like olive shoots around your table. ^3

**4** Behold, this is how the man who fears the LORD is blessed. ^4

**5** May the LORD bless you out of Zion, and may you see the good of Jerusalem all the days of your life. ^5

**6** Yes, may you see your children’s children. Peace be upon Israel. ^6
