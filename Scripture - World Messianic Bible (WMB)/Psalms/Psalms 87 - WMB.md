# Psalms 87 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 86 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 88 - WMB]] →

**1** A Psalm by the sons of Korah; a Song. His foundation is in the holy mountains. ^1

**2** The LORD loves the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob. ^2

**3** Glorious things are spoken about you, city of God. Selah. ^3

**4** I will record Rahab and Babylon among those who acknowledge me. Behold, Philistia, Tyre, and also Ethiopia: “This one was born there.” ^4

**5** Yes, of Zion it will be said, “This one and that one was born in her;” the Most High himself will establish her. ^5

**6** The LORD will count, when he writes up the peoples, “This one was born there.” Selah. ^6

**7** Those who sing as well as those who dance say, “All my springs are in you.” ^7
