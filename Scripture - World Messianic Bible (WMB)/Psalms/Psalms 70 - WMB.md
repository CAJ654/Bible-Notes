# Psalms 70 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 69 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 71 - WMB]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. By David. A reminder. Hurry, God, to deliver me. Come quickly to help me, LORD. ^1

**2** Let them be disappointed and confounded who seek my soul. Let those who desire my ruin be turned back in disgrace. ^2

**3** Let them be turned because of their shame who say, “Aha! Aha!” ^3

**4** Let all those who seek you rejoice and be glad in you. Let those who love your salvation continually say, “Let God be exalted!” ^4

**5** But I am poor and needy. Come to me quickly, God. You are my help and my deliverer. LORD, don’t delay. ^5
