# Psalms 137 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 136 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 138 - WMB]] →

**1** By the rivers of Babylon, there we sat down. Yes, we wept, when we remembered Zion. ^1

**2** On the willows in that land, we hung up our harps. ^2

**3** For there, those who led us captive asked us for songs. Those who tormented us demanded songs of joy: “Sing us one of the songs of Zion!” ^3

**4** How can we sing the LORD’s song in a foreign land? ^4

**5** If I forget you, Jerusalem, let my right hand forget its skill. ^5

**6** Let my tongue stick to the roof of my mouth if I don’t remember you, if I don’t prefer Jerusalem above my chief joy. ^6

**7** Remember, LORD, against the children of Edom in the day of Jerusalem, who said, “Raze it! Raze it even to its foundation!” ^7

**8** Daughter of Babylon, doomed to destruction, he will be happy who repays you, as you have done to us. ^8

**9** Happy shall he be, who takes and dashes your little ones against the rock. ^9
