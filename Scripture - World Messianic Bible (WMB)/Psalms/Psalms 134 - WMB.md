# Psalms 134 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 133 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 135 - WMB]] →

**1** A Song of Ascents. Look! Praise the LORD, all you servants of the LORD, who stand by night in the LORD’s house! ^1

**2** Lift up your hands in the sanctuary. Praise the LORD! ^2

**3** May the LORD bless you from Zion, even he who made heaven and earth. ^3
