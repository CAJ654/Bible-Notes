# Psalms 108 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 107 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 109 - WMB]] →

**1** A Song. A Psalm by David. My heart is steadfast, God. I will sing and I will make music with my soul. ^1

**2** Wake up, harp and lyre! I will wake up the dawn. ^2

**3** I will give thanks to you, LORD, among the nations. I will sing praises to you among the peoples. ^3

**4** For your loving kindness is great above the heavens. Your faithfulness reaches to the skies. ^4

**5** Be exalted, God, above the heavens! Let your glory be over all the earth. ^5

**6** That your beloved may be delivered, save with your right hand, and answer us. ^6

**7** God has spoken from his sanctuary: “In triumph, I will divide Shechem, and measure out the valley of Succoth. ^7

**8** Gilead is mine. Manasseh is mine. Ephraim also is my helmet. Judah is my scepter. ^8

**9** Moab is my wash pot. I will toss my sandal on Edom. I will shout over Philistia.” ^9

**10** Who will bring me into the fortified city? Who will lead me to Edom? ^10

**11** Haven’t you rejected us, God? You don’t go out, God, with our armies. ^11

**12** Give us help against the enemy, for the help of man is vain. ^12

**13** Through God, we will do valiantly, for it is he who will tread down our enemies. ^13
