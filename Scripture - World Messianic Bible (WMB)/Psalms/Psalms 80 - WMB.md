# Psalms 80 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 79 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 81 - WMB]] →

**1** For the Chief Musician. To the tune of “The Lilies of the Covenant.” A Psalm by Asaph. Hear us, Shepherd of Israel, you who lead Joseph like a flock, you who sit above the cherubim, shine out. ^1

**2** Before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh, stir up your might! Come to save us! ^2

**3** Turn us again, God. Cause your face to shine, and we will be saved. ^3

**4** LORD God of Hosts, how long will you be angry against the prayer of your people? ^4

**5** You have fed them with the bread of tears, and given them tears to drink in large measure. ^5

**6** You make us a source of contention to our neighbors. Our enemies laugh among themselves. ^6

**7** Turn us again, God of Hosts. Cause your face to shine, and we will be saved. ^7

**8** You brought a vine out of Egypt. You drove out the nations, and planted it. ^8

**9** You cleared the ground for it. It took deep root, and filled the land. ^9

**10** The mountains were covered with its shadow. Its boughs were like God’s cedars. ^10

**11** It sent out its branches to the sea, its shoots to the River. ^11

**12** Why have you broken down its walls, so that all those who pass by the way pluck it? ^12

**13** The boar out of the wood ravages it. The wild animals of the field feed on it. ^13

**14** Turn again, we beg you, God of Hosts. Look down from heaven, and see, and visit this vine, ^14

**15** the stock which your right hand planted, the branch that you made strong for yourself. ^15

**16** It’s burned with fire. It’s cut down. They perish at your rebuke. ^16

**17** Let your hand be on the man of your right hand, on the son of man whom you made strong for yourself. ^17

**18** So we will not turn away from you. Revive us, and we will call on your name. ^18

**19** Turn us again, LORD God of Hosts. Cause your face to shine, and we will be saved. ^19
