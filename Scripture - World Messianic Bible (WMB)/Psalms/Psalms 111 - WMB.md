# Psalms 111 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 110 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 112 - WMB]] →

**1** Praise the LORD! I will give thanks to the LORD with my whole heart, in the council of the upright, and in the congregation. ^1

**2** The LORD’s works are great, pondered by all those who delight in them. ^2

**3** His work is honor and majesty. His righteousness endures forever. ^3

**4** He has caused his wonderful works to be remembered. The LORD is gracious and merciful. ^4

**5** He has given food to those who fear him. He always remembers his covenant. ^5

**6** He has shown his people the power of his works, in giving them the heritage of the nations. ^6

**7** The works of his hands are truth and justice. All his precepts are sure. ^7

**8** They are established forever and ever. They are done in truth and uprightness. ^8

**9** He has sent redemption to his people. He has ordained his covenant forever. His name is holy and awesome! ^9

**10** The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom. All those who do his work have a good understanding. His praise endures forever! ^10
