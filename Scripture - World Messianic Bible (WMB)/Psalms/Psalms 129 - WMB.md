# Psalms 129 (WMB)

← [[Psalms 128 - WMB]] | [[Psalms 130 - WMB]] →

**1** A Song of Ascents. Many times they have afflicted me from my youth up. Let Israel now say: ^1

**2** many times they have afflicted me from my youth up, yet they have not prevailed against me. ^2

**3** The plowers plowed on my back. They made their furrows long. ^3

**4** The LORD is righteous. He has cut apart the cords of the wicked. ^4

**5** Let them be disappointed and turned backward, all those who hate Zion. ^5

**6** Let them be as the grass on the housetops, which withers before it grows up, ^6

**7** with which the reaper doesn’t fill his hand, nor he who binds sheaves, his bosom. ^7

**8** Neither do those who go by say, “The blessing of the LORD be on you. We bless you in the LORD’s name.” ^8
