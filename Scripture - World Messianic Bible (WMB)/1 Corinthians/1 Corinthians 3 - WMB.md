# 1 Corinthians 3 (WMB)

← [[1 Corinthians 2 - WMB]] | [[1 Corinthians 4 - WMB]] →

**1** Brothers, I couldn’t speak to you as to spiritual, but as to fleshly, as to babies in Messiah. ^1

**2** I fed you with milk, not with solid food, for you weren’t yet ready. Indeed, you aren’t ready even now, ^2

**3** for you are still fleshly. For insofar as there is jealousy, strife, and factions among you, aren’t you fleshly, and don’t you walk in the ways of men? ^3

**4** For when one says, “I follow Paul,” and another, “I follow Apollos,” aren’t you fleshly? ^4

**5** Who then is Apollos, and who is Paul, but servants through whom you believed, and each as the Lord gave to him? ^5

**6** I planted. Apollos watered. But God gave the increase. ^6

**7** So then neither he who plants is anything, nor he who waters, but God who gives the increase. ^7

**8** Now he who plants and he who waters are the same, but each will receive his own reward according to his own labor. ^8

**9** For we are God’s fellow workers. You are God’s farming, God’s building. ^9

**10** According to the grace of God which was given to me, as a wise master builder I laid a foundation, and another builds on it. But let each man be careful how he builds on it. ^10

**11** For no one can lay any other foundation than that which has been laid, which is Yeshua the Messiah. ^11

**12** But if anyone builds on the foundation with gold, silver, costly stones, wood, hay, or straw, ^12

**13** each man’s work will be revealed. For the Day will declare it, because it is revealed in fire; and the fire itself will test what sort of work each man’s work is. ^13

**14** If any man’s work remains which he built on it, he will receive a reward. ^14

**15** If any man’s work is burned, he will suffer loss, but he himself will be saved, but as through fire. ^15

**16** Don’t you know that you are God’s temple and that God’s Spirit lives in you? ^16

**17** If anyone destroys God’s temple, God will destroy him; for God’s temple is holy, which you are. ^17

**18** Let no one deceive himself. If anyone thinks that he is wise among you in this world, let him become a fool that he may become wise. ^18

**19** For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God. For it is written, “He has taken the wise in their craftiness.” ^19

**20** And again, “The Lord knows the reasoning of the wise, that it is worthless.” ^20

**21** Therefore let no one boast in men. For all things are yours, ^21

**22** whether Paul, or Apollos, or Kefa, or the world, or life, or death, or things present, or things to come. All are yours, ^22

**23** and you are Messiah’s, and Messiah is God’s. ^23
