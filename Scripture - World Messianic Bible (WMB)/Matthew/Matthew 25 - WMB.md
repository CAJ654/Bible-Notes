# Matthew 25 (WMB)

← [[Matthew 24 - WMB]] | [[Matthew 26 - WMB]] →

**1** “Then the Kingdom of Heaven will be like ten virgins who took their lamps and went out to meet the bridegroom. ^1

**2** Five of them were foolish, and five were wise. ^2

**3** Those who were foolish, when they took their lamps, took no oil with them, ^3

**4** but the wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps. ^4

**5** Now while the bridegroom delayed, they all slumbered and slept. ^5

**6** But at midnight there was a cry, ‘Behold! The bridegroom is coming! Come out to meet him!’ ^6

**7** Then all those virgins arose, and trimmed their lamps. ^7

**8** The foolish said to the wise, ‘Give us some of your oil, for our lamps are going out.’ ^8

**9** But the wise answered, saying, ‘What if there isn’t enough for us and you? You go rather to those who sell, and buy for yourselves.’ ^9

**10** While they went away to buy, the bridegroom came, and those who were ready went in with him to the wedding feast, and the door was shut. ^10

**11** Afterward the other virgins also came, saying, ‘Lord, Lord, open to us.’ ^11

**12** But he answered, ‘Most certainly I tell you, I don’t know you.’ ^12

**13** Watch therefore, for you don’t know the day nor the hour in which the Son of Man is coming. ^13

**14** “For it is like a man going into another country, who called his own servants and entrusted his goods to them. ^14

**15** To one he gave five talents, to another two, to another one, to each according to his own ability. Then he went on his journey. ^15

**16** Immediately he who received the five talents went and traded with them, and made another five talents. ^16

**17** In the same way, he also who got the two gained another two. ^17

**18** But he who received the one talent went away and dug in the earth and hid his lord’s money. ^18

**19** “Now after a long time the lord of those servants came, and settled accounts with them. ^19

**20** He who received the five talents came and brought another five talents, saying, ‘Lord, you delivered to me five talents. Behold, I have gained another five talents in addition to them.’ ^20

**21** “His lord said to him, ‘Well done, good and faithful servant. You have been faithful over a few things, I will set you over many things. Enter into the joy of your lord.’ ^21

**22** “He also who got the two talents came and said, ‘Lord, you delivered to me two talents. Behold, I have gained another two talents in addition to them.’ ^22

**23** “His lord said to him, ‘Well done, good and faithful servant. You have been faithful over a few things. I will set you over many things. Enter into the joy of your lord.’ ^23

**24** “He also who had received the one talent came and said, ‘Lord, I knew you that you are a hard man, reaping where you didn’t sow, and gathering where you didn’t scatter. ^24

**25** I was afraid, and went away and hid your talent in the earth. Behold, you have what is yours.’ ^25

**26** “But his lord answered him, ‘You wicked and slothful servant. You knew that I reap where I didn’t sow, and gather where I didn’t scatter. ^26

**27** You ought therefore to have deposited my money with the bankers, and at my coming I should have received back my own with interest. ^27

**28** Take away therefore the talent from him and give it to him who has the ten talents. ^28

**29** For to everyone who has will be given, and he will have abundance, but from him who doesn’t have, even that which he has will be taken away. ^29

**30** Throw out the unprofitable servant into the outer darkness, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.’ ^30

**31** “But when the Son of Man comes in his glory, and all the holy angels with him, then he will sit on the throne of his glory. ^31

**32** Before him all the nations will be gathered, and he will separate them one from another, as a shepherd separates the sheep from the goats. ^32

**33** He will set the sheep on his right hand, but the goats on the left. ^33

**34** Then the King will tell those on his right hand, ‘Come, blessed of my Father, inherit the Kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world; ^34

**35** for I was hungry and you gave me food to eat. I was thirsty and you gave me drink. I was a stranger and you took me in. ^35

**36** I was naked and you clothed me. I was sick and you visited me. I was in prison and you came to me.’ ^36

**37** “Then the righteous will answer him, saying, ‘Lord, when did we see you hungry and feed you, or thirsty and give you a drink? ^37

**38** When did we see you as a stranger and take you in, or naked and clothe you? ^38

**39** When did we see you sick or in prison and come to you?’ ^39

**40** “The King will answer them, ‘Most certainly I tell you, because you did it to one of the least of these my brothers, you did it to me.’ ^40

**41** Then he will say also to those on the left hand, ‘Depart from me, you cursed, into the eternal fire which is prepared for the devil and his angels; ^41

**42** for I was hungry, and you didn’t give me food to eat; I was thirsty, and you gave me no drink; ^42

**43** I was a stranger, and you didn’t take me in; naked, and you didn’t clothe me; sick, and in prison, and you didn’t visit me.’ ^43

**44** “Then they will also answer, saying, ‘Lord, when did we see you hungry, or thirsty, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and didn’t help you?’ ^44

**45** “Then he will answer them, saying, ‘Most certainly I tell you, because you didn’t do it to one of the least of these, you didn’t do it to me.’ ^45

**46** These will go away into eternal punishment, but the righteous into eternal life.” ^46
