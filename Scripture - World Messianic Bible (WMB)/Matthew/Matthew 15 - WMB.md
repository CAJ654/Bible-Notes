# Matthew 15 (WMB)

← [[Matthew 14 - WMB]] | [[Matthew 16 - WMB]] →

**1** Then Pharisees and scribes came to Yeshua from Jerusalem, saying, ^1

**2** “Why do your disciples disobey the tradition of the elders? For they don’t wash their hands when they eat bread.” ^2

**3** He answered them, “Why do you also disobey the commandment of God because of your tradition? ^3

**4** For God commanded, ‘Honor your father and your mother,’ and, ‘He who speaks evil of father or mother, let him be put to death.’ ^4

**5** But you say, ‘Whoever may tell his father or his mother, “Whatever help you might otherwise have gotten from me is a gift devoted to God,” ^5

**6** he shall not honor his father or mother.’ You have made the commandment of God void because of your tradition. ^6

**7** You hypocrites! Well did Isaiah prophesy of you, saying, ^7

**8** ‘These people draw near to me with their mouth, and honor me with their lips; but their heart is far from me. ^8

**9** And they worship me in vain, teaching as doctrine rules made by men.’” ^9

**10** He summoned the multitude, and said to them, “Hear, and understand. ^10

**11** That which enters into the mouth doesn’t defile the man; but that which proceeds out of the mouth, this defiles the man.” ^11

**12** Then the disciples came and said to him, “Do you know that the Pharisees were offended when they heard this saying?” ^12

**13** But he answered, “Every plant which my heavenly Father didn’t plant will be uprooted. ^13

**14** Leave them alone. They are blind guides of the blind. If the blind guide the blind, both will fall into a pit.” ^14

**15** Peter answered him, “Explain the parable to us.” ^15

**16** So Yeshua said, “Do you also still not understand? ^16

**17** Don’t you understand that whatever goes into the mouth passes into the belly and then out of the body? ^17

**18** But the things which proceed out of the mouth come out of the heart, and they defile the man. ^18

**19** For out of the heart come evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, sexual sins, thefts, false testimony, and blasphemies. ^19

**20** These are the things which defile the man; but to eat with unwashed hands doesn’t defile the man.” ^20

**21** Yeshua went out from there and withdrew into the region of Tyre and Sidon. ^21

**22** Behold, a Canaanite woman came out from those borders and cried, saying, “Have mercy on me, Lord, you son of David! My daughter is severely possessed by a demon!” ^22

**23** But he answered her not a word. His disciples came and begged him, saying, “Send her away; for she cries after us.” ^23

**24** But he answered, “I wasn’t sent to anyone but the lost sheep of the house of Israel.” ^24

**25** But she came and worshiped him, saying, “Lord, help me.” ^25

**26** But he answered, “It is not appropriate to take the children’s bread and throw it to the dogs.” ^26

**27** But she said, “Yes, Lord, but even the dogs eat the crumbs which fall from their masters’ table.” ^27

**28** Then Yeshua answered her, “Woman, great is your faith! Be it done to you even as you desire.” And her daughter was healed from that hour. ^28

**29** Yeshua departed from there and came near to the sea of Galilee; and he went up on the mountain and sat there. ^29

**30** Great multitudes came to him, having with them the lame, blind, mute, maimed, and many others, and they put them down at his feet. He healed them, ^30

**31** so that the multitude wondered when they saw the mute speaking, the injured healed, the lame walking, and the blind seeing—and they glorified the God of Israel. ^31

**32** Yeshua summoned his disciples and said, “I have compassion on the multitude, because they have continued with me now three days and have nothing to eat. I don’t want to send them away fasting, or they might faint on the way.” ^32

**33** The disciples said to him, “Where could we get so many loaves in a deserted place as to satisfy so great a multitude?” ^33

**34** Yeshua said to them, “How many loaves do you have?” They said, “Seven, and a few small fish.” ^34

**35** He commanded the multitude to sit down on the ground; ^35

**36** and he took the seven loaves and the fish. He gave thanks and broke them, and gave to the disciples, and the disciples to the multitudes. ^36

**37** They all ate and were filled. They took up seven baskets full of the broken pieces that were left over. ^37

**38** Those who ate were four thousand men, in addition to women and children. ^38

**39** Then he sent away the multitudes, got into the boat, and came into the borders of Magdala. ^39
