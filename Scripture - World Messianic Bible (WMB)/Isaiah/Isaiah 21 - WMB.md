# Isaiah 21 (WMB)

← [[Isaiah 20 - WMB]] | [[Isaiah 22 - WMB]] →

**1** The burden of the wilderness of the sea. As whirlwinds in the South sweep through, it comes from the wilderness, from an awesome land. ^1

**2** A grievous vision is declared to me. The treacherous man deals treacherously, and the destroyer destroys. Go up, Elam; attack! I have stopped all of Media’s sighing. ^2

**3** Therefore my thighs are filled with anguish. Pains have seized me, like the pains of a woman in labor. I am in so much pain that I can’t hear. I am so dismayed that I can’t see. ^3

**4** My heart flutters. Horror has frightened me. The twilight that I desired has been turned into trembling for me. ^4

**5** They prepare the table. They set the watch. They eat. They drink. Rise up, you princes, oil the shield! ^5

**6** For the Lord said to me, “Go, set a watchman. Let him declare what he sees. ^6

**7** When he sees a troop, horsemen in pairs, a troop of donkeys, a troop of camels, he shall listen diligently with great attentiveness.” ^7

**8** He cried like a lion: “Lord, I stand continually on the watchtower in the daytime, and every night I stay at my post. ^8

**9** Behold, here comes a troop of men, horsemen in pairs.” He answered, “Fallen, fallen is Babylon; and all the engraved images of her gods are broken to the ground. ^9

**10** You are my threshing, and the grain of my floor!” That which I have heard from the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, I have declared to you. ^10

**11** The burden of Dumah. One calls to me out of Seir, “Watchman, what of the night? Watchman, what of the night?” ^11

**12** The watchman said, “The morning comes, and also the night. If you will inquire, inquire. Come back again.” ^12

**13** The burden on Arabia. You will lodge in the thickets in Arabia, you caravans of Dedanites. ^13

**14** They brought water to him who was thirsty. The inhabitants of the land of Tema met the fugitives with their bread. ^14

**15** For they fled away from the swords, from the drawn sword, from the bent bow, and from the heat of battle. ^15

**16** For the Lord said to me, “Within a year, as a worker bound by contract would count it, all the glory of Kedar will fail, ^16

**17** and the residue of the number of the archers, the mighty men of the children of Kedar, will be few; for the LORD, the God of Israel, has spoken it.” ^17
