# 1 Timothy 2 (WMB)

← [[1 Timothy 1 - WMB]] | [[1 Timothy 3 - WMB]] →

**1** I exhort therefore, first of all, that petitions, prayers, intercessions, and givings of thanks be made for all men, ^1

**2** for kings and all who are in high places, that we may lead a tranquil and quiet life in all godliness and reverence. ^2

**3** For this is good and acceptable in the sight of God our Savior, ^3

**4** who desires all people to be saved and come to full knowledge of the truth. ^4

**5** For there is one God and one mediator between God and men, the man Messiah Yeshua, ^5

**6** who gave himself as a ransom for all, the testimony at the proper time, ^6

**7** to which I was appointed a proclaimer and an emissary—I am telling the truth in Messiah, not lying—a teacher of the Gentiles in faith and truth. ^7

**8** I desire therefore that the men in every place pray, lifting up holy hands without anger and doubting. ^8

**9** In the same way, that women also adorn themselves in decent clothing, with modesty and propriety, not with braided hair, gold, pearls, or expensive clothing, ^9

**10** but with good works, which is appropriate for women professing godliness. ^10

**11** Let a woman learn in quietness with full submission. ^11

**12** But I don’t permit a woman to teach, nor to exercise authority over a man, but to be in quietness. ^12

**13** For Adam was formed first, then Havah. ^13

**14** Adam wasn’t deceived, but the woman, being deceived, has fallen into disobedience; ^14

**15** but she will be saved through her childbearing, if they continue in faith, love, and holiness with sobriety. ^15
