# Genesis 4 (WMB)

← [[Genesis 3 - WMB]] | [[Genesis 5 - WMB]] →

**1** The man knew Havah his wife. She conceived, and gave birth to Cain, and said, “I have gotten a man with the LORD’s help.” ^1

**2** Again she gave birth, to Cain’s brother Abel. Abel was a keeper of sheep, but Cain was a tiller of the ground. ^2

**3** As time passed, Cain brought an offering to the LORD from the fruit of the ground. ^3

**4** Abel also brought some of the firstborn of his flock and of its fat. The LORD respected Abel and his offering, ^4

**5** but he didn’t respect Cain and his offering. Cain was very angry, and the expression on his face fell. ^5

**6** The LORD said to Cain, “Why are you angry? Why has the expression of your face fallen? ^6

**7** If you do well, won’t it be lifted up? If you don’t do well, sin crouches at the door. Its desire is for you, but you are to rule over it.” ^7

**8** Cain said to Abel, his brother, “Let’s go into the field.” While they were in the field, Cain rose up against Abel, his brother, and killed him. ^8

**9** The LORD said to Cain, “Where is Abel, your brother?” He said, “I don’t know. Am I my brother’s keeper?” ^9

**10** The LORD said, “What have you done? The voice of your brother’s blood cries to me from the ground. ^10

**11** Now you are cursed because of the ground, which has opened its mouth to receive your brother’s blood from your hand. ^11

**12** From now on, when you till the ground, it won’t yield its strength to you. You will be a fugitive and a wanderer in the earth.” ^12

**13** Cain said to the LORD, “My punishment is greater than I can bear. ^13

**14** Behold, you have driven me out today from the surface of the ground. I will be hidden from your face, and I will be a fugitive and a wanderer in the earth. Whoever finds me will kill me.” ^14

**15** The LORD said to him, “Therefore whoever slays Cain, vengeance will be taken on him sevenfold.” The LORD appointed a sign for Cain, so that anyone finding him would not strike him. ^15

**16** Cain left the LORD’s presence, and lived in the land of Nod, east of Eden. ^16

**17** Cain knew his wife. She conceived, and gave birth to Enoch. He built a city, and named the city after the name of his son, Enoch. ^17

**18** Irad was born to Enoch. Irad became the father of Mehujael. Mehujael became the father of Methushael. Methushael became the father of Lamech. ^18

**19** Lamech took two wives: the name of the first one was Adah, and the name of the second one was Zillah. ^19

**20** Adah gave birth to Jabal, who was the father of those who dwell in tents and have livestock. ^20

**21** His brother’s name was Jubal, who was the father of all who handle the harp and pipe. ^21

**22** Zillah also gave birth to Tubal Cain, the forger of every cutting instrument of bronze and iron. Tubal Cain’s sister was Naamah. ^22

**23** Lamech said to his wives, “Adah and Zillah, hear my voice. You wives of Lamech, listen to my speech, for I have slain a man for wounding me, a young man for bruising me. ^23

**24** If Cain will be avenged seven times, truly Lamech seventy-seven times.” ^24

**25** Adam knew his wife again. She gave birth to a son, and named him Seth, saying, “for God has given me another child instead of Abel, for Cain killed him.” ^25

**26** A son was also born to Seth, and he named him Enosh. At that time men began to call on the LORD’s name. ^26
