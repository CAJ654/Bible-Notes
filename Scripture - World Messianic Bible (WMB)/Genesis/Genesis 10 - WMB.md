# Genesis 10 (WMB)

← [[Genesis 9 - WMB]] | [[Genesis 11 - WMB]] →

**1** Now this is the history of the generations of the sons of Noah and of Shem, Ham, and Japheth. Sons were born to them after the flood. ^1

**2** The sons of Japheth were: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. ^2

**3** The sons of Gomer were: Ashkenaz, Riphath, and Togarmah. ^3

**4** The sons of Javan were: Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim. ^4

**5** Of these were the islands of the nations divided in their lands, everyone after his language, after their families, in their nations. ^5

**6** The sons of Ham were: Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan. ^6

**7** The sons of Cush were: Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca. The sons of Raamah were: Sheba and Dedan. ^7

**8** Cush became the father of Nimrod. He began to be a mighty one in the earth. ^8

**9** He was a mighty hunter before the LORD. Therefore it is said, “like Nimrod, a mighty hunter before the LORD”. ^9

**10** The beginning of his kingdom was Babel, Erech, Accad, and Calneh, in the land of Shinar. ^10

**11** Out of that land he went into Assyria, and built Nineveh, Rehoboth Ir, Calah, ^11

**12** and Resen between Nineveh and the great city Calah. ^12

**13** Mizraim became the father of Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, Naphtuhim, ^13

**14** Pathrusim, Casluhim (which the Philistines descended from), and Caphtorim. ^14

**15** Canaan became the father of Sidon (his firstborn), Heth, ^15

**16** the Jebusites, the Amorites, the Girgashites, ^16

**17** the Hivites, the Arkites, the Sinites, ^17

**18** the Arvadites, the Zemarites, and the Hamathites. Afterward the families of the Canaanites were spread abroad. ^18

**19** The border of the Canaanites was from Sidon—as you go toward Gerar—to Gaza—as you go toward Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, and Zeboiim—to Lasha. ^19

**20** These are the sons of Ham, after their families, according to their languages, in their lands and their nations. ^20

**21** Children were also born to Shem (the elder brother of Japheth), the father of all the children of Eber. ^21

**22** The sons of Shem were: Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, and Aram. ^22

**23** The sons of Aram were: Uz, Hul, Gether, and Mash. ^23

**24** Arpachshad became the father of Shelah. Shelah became the father of Eber. ^24

**25** To Eber were born two sons. The name of the one was Peleg, for in his days the earth was divided. His brother’s name was Joktan. ^25

**26** Joktan became the father of Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah, ^26

**27** Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah, ^27

**28** Obal, Abimael, Sheba, ^28

**29** Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan. ^29

**30** Their dwelling extended from Mesha, as you go toward Sephar, the mountain of the east. ^30

**31** These are the sons of Shem, by their families, according to their languages, lands, and nations. ^31

**32** These are the families of the sons of Noah, by their generations, according to their nations. The nations divided from these in the earth after the flood. ^32
