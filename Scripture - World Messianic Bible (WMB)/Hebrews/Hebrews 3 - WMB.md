# Hebrews 3 (WMB)

← [[Hebrews 2 - WMB]] | [[Hebrews 4 - WMB]] →

**1** Therefore, holy brothers, partakers of a heavenly calling, consider the Emissary and High Priest of our confession: Yeshua, ^1

**2** who was faithful to him who appointed him, as also Moses was in all his house. ^2

**3** For he has been counted worthy of more glory than Moses, because he who built the house has more honor than the house. ^3

**4** For every house is built by someone; but he who built all things is God. ^4

**5** Moses indeed was faithful in all his house as a servant, for a testimony of those things which were afterward to be spoken, ^5

**6** but Messiah is faithful as a Son over his house. We are his house, if we hold fast our confidence and the glorying of our hope firm to the end. ^6

**7** Therefore, even as the Holy Spirit says, “Today if you will hear his voice, ^7

**8** don’t harden your hearts as in the rebellion, in the day of the trial in the wilderness, ^8

**9** where your fathers tested me and tried me, and saw my deeds for forty years. ^9

**10** Therefore I was displeased with that generation, and said, ‘They always err in their heart, but they didn’t know my ways.’ ^10

**11** As I swore in my wrath, ‘They will not enter into my rest.’” ^11

**12** Beware, brothers, lest perhaps there might be in any one of you an evil heart of unbelief, in falling away from the living God; ^12

**13** but exhort one another day by day, so long as it is called “today”, lest any one of you be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin. ^13

**14** For we have become partakers of Messiah, if we hold the beginning of our confidence firm to the end, ^14

**15** while it is said, “Today if you will hear his voice, don’t harden your hearts, as in the rebellion.” ^15

**16** For who, when they heard, rebelled? Wasn’t it all those who came out of Egypt led by Moses? ^16

**17** With whom was he displeased forty years? Wasn’t it with those who sinned, whose bodies fell in the wilderness? ^17

**18** To whom did he swear that they wouldn’t enter into his rest, but to those who were disobedient? ^18

**19** We see that they weren’t able to enter in because of unbelief. ^19
