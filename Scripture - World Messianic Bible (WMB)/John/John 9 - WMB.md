# John 9 (WMB)

← [[John 8 - WMB]] | [[John 10 - WMB]] →

**1** As he passed by, he saw a man blind from birth. ^1

**2** His disciples asked him, “Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, that he was born blind?” ^2

**3** Yeshua answered, “This man didn’t sin, nor did his parents, but that the works of God might be revealed in him. ^3

**4** I must work the works of him who sent me while it is day. The night is coming, when no one can work. ^4

**5** While I am in the world, I am the light of the world.” ^5

**6** When he had said this, he spat on the ground, made mud with the saliva, anointed the blind man’s eyes with the mud, ^6

**7** and said to him, “Go, wash in the pool of Siloam” (which means “Sent”). So he went away, washed, and came back seeing. ^7

**8** Therefore the neighbors and those who saw that he was blind before said, “Isn’t this he who sat and begged?” ^8

**9** Others were saying, “It is he.” Still others were saying, “He looks like him.” He said, “I am he.” ^9

**10** They therefore were asking him, “How were your eyes opened?” ^10

**11** He answered, “A man called Yeshua made mud, anointed my eyes, and said to me, ‘Go to the pool of Siloam and wash.’ So I went away and washed, and I received sight.” ^11

**12** Then they asked him, “Where is he?” He said, “I don’t know.” ^12

**13** They brought him who had been blind to the Pharisees. ^13

**14** It was a Sabbath when Yeshua made the mud and opened his eyes. ^14

**15** Again therefore the Pharisees also asked him how he received his sight. He said to them, “He put mud on my eyes, I washed, and I see.” ^15

**16** Some therefore of the Pharisees said, “This man is not from God, because he doesn’t keep the Sabbath.” Others said, “How can a man who is a sinner do such signs?” So there was division among them. ^16

**17** Therefore they asked the blind man again, “What do you say about him, because he opened your eyes?” He said, “He is a prophet.” ^17

**18** The Judeans therefore didn’t believe concerning him, that he had been blind and had received his sight, until they called the parents of him who had received his sight, ^18

**19** and asked them, “Is this your son, whom you say was born blind? How then does he now see?” ^19

**20** His parents answered them, “We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind; ^20

**21** but how he now sees, we don’t know; or who opened his eyes, we don’t know. He is of age. Ask him. He will speak for himself.” ^21

**22** His parents said these things because they feared the Judeans; for the Judeans had already agreed that if any man would confess him as Messiah, he would be put out of the synagogue. ^22

**23** Therefore his parents said, “He is of age. Ask him.” ^23

**24** So they called the man who was blind a second time, and said to him, “Give glory to God. We know that this man is a sinner.” ^24

**25** He therefore answered, “I don’t know if he is a sinner. One thing I do know: that though I was blind, now I see.” ^25

**26** They said to him again, “What did he do to you? How did he open your eyes?” ^26

**27** He answered them, “I told you already, and you didn’t listen. Why do you want to hear it again? You don’t also want to become his disciples, do you?” ^27

**28** They insulted him and said, “You are his disciple, but we are disciples of Moses. ^28

**29** We know that God has spoken to Moses. But as for this man, we don’t know where he comes from.” ^29

**30** The man answered them, “How amazing! You don’t know where he comes from, yet he opened my eyes. ^30

**31** We know that God doesn’t listen to sinners, but if anyone is a worshiper of God and does his will, he listens to him. ^31

**32** Since the world began it has never been heard of that anyone opened the eyes of someone born blind. ^32

**33** If this man were not from God, he could do nothing.” ^33

**34** They answered him, “You were altogether born in sins, and do you teach us?” Then they threw him out. ^34

**35** Yeshua heard that they had thrown him out, and finding him, he said, “Do you believe in the Son of God?” ^35

**36** He answered, “Who is he, Lord, that I may believe in him?” ^36

**37** Yeshua said to him, “You have both seen him, and it is he who speaks with you.” ^37

**38** He said, “Lord, I believe!” and he worshiped him. ^38

**39** Yeshua said, “I came into this world for judgment, that those who don’t see may see; and that those who see may become blind.” ^39

**40** Those of the Pharisees who were with him heard these things, and said to him, “Are we also blind?” ^40

**41** Yeshua said to them, “If you were blind, you would have no sin; but now you say, ‘We see.’ Therefore your sin remains. ^41
