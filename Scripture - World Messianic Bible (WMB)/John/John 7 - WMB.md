# John 7 (WMB)

← [[John 6 - WMB]] | [[John 8 - WMB]] →

**1** After these things, Yeshua was walking in Galilee, for he wouldn’t walk in Judea, because the Judeans sought to kill him. ^1

**2** Now the Jewish festival, the Feast of Sukkot, was at hand. ^2

**3** His brothers therefore said to him, “Depart from here and go into Judea, that your disciples also may see your works which you do. ^3

**4** For no one does anything in secret while he seeks to be known openly. If you do these things, reveal yourself to the world.” ^4

**5** For even his brothers didn’t believe in him. ^5

**6** Yeshua therefore said to them, “My time has not yet come, but your time is always ready. ^6

**7** The world can’t hate you, but it hates me, because I testify about it, that its works are evil. ^7

**8** You go up to the feast. I am not yet going up to this feast, because my time is not yet fulfilled.” ^8

**9** Having said these things to them, he stayed in Galilee. ^9

**10** But when his brothers had gone up to the feast, then he also went up, not publicly, but as it were in secret. ^10

**11** The Judeans therefore sought him at the feast, and said, “Where is he?” ^11

**12** There was much murmuring among the multitudes concerning him. Some said, “He is a good man.” Others said, “Not so, but he leads the multitude astray.” ^12

**13** Yet no one spoke openly of him for fear of the Judeans. ^13

**14** But when it was now the middle of the feast, Yeshua went up into the temple and taught. ^14

**15** The Judeans therefore marveled, saying, “How does this man know letters, having never been educated?” ^15

**16** Yeshua therefore answered them, “My teaching is not mine, but his who sent me. ^16

**17** If anyone desires to do his will, he will know about the teaching, whether it is from God or if I am speaking from myself. ^17

**18** He who speaks from himself seeks his own glory, but he who seeks the glory of him who sent him is true, and no unrighteousness is in him. ^18

**19** Didn’t Moses give you the Torah, and yet none of you keeps the Torah? Why do you seek to kill me?” ^19

**20** The multitude answered, “You have a demon! Who seeks to kill you?” ^20

**21** Yeshua answered them, “I did one work and you all marvel because of it. ^21

**22** Moses has given you circumcision (not that it is of Moses, but of the fathers), and on the Sabbath you circumcise a boy. ^22

**23** If a boy receives circumcision on the Sabbath, that the Torah of Moses may not be broken, are you angry with me because I made a man completely healthy on the Sabbath? ^23

**24** Don’t judge according to appearance, but judge righteous judgment.” ^24

**25** Therefore some of them of Jerusalem said, “Isn’t this he whom they seek to kill? ^25

**26** Behold, he speaks openly, and they say nothing to him. Can it be that the rulers indeed know that this is truly the Messiah? ^26

**27** However, we know where this man comes from, but when the Messiah comes, no one will know where he comes from.” ^27

**28** Yeshua therefore cried out in the temple, teaching and saying, “You both know me, and know where I am from. I have not come of myself, but he who sent me is true, whom you don’t know. ^28

**29** I know him, because I am from him, and he sent me.” ^29

**30** They sought therefore to take him; but no one laid a hand on him, because his hour had not yet come. ^30

**31** But of the multitude, many believed in him. They said, “When the Messiah comes, he won’t do more signs than those which this man has done, will he?” ^31

**32** The Pharisees heard the multitude murmuring these things concerning him, and the chief priests and the Pharisees sent officers to arrest him. ^32

**33** Then Yeshua said, “I will be with you a little while longer, then I go to him who sent me. ^33

**34** You will seek me and won’t find me. You can’t come where I am.” ^34

**35** The Judeans therefore said among themselves, “Where will this man go that we won’t find him? Will he go to the Diaspora among the Greeks and teach the Greeks? ^35

**36** What is this word that he said, ‘You will seek me, and won’t find me;’ and ‘Where I am, you can’t come’?” ^36

**37** Now on the last and greatest day of the feast, Yeshua stood and cried out, “If anyone is thirsty, let him come to me and drink! ^37

**38** He who believes in me, as the Scripture has said, from within him will flow rivers of living water.” ^38

**39** But he said this about the Spirit, which those believing in him were to receive. For the Holy Spirit was not yet given, because Yeshua wasn’t yet glorified. ^39

**40** Many of the multitude therefore, when they heard these words, said, “This is truly the Prophet.” ^40

**41** Others said, “This is the Messiah.” But some said, “What, does the Messiah come out of Galilee? ^41

**42** Hasn’t the Scripture said that the Messiah comes of the offspring of David, and from Bethlehem, the village where David was?” ^42

**43** So a division arose in the multitude because of him. ^43

**44** Some of them would have arrested him, but no one laid hands on him. ^44

**45** The officers therefore came to the chief priests and Pharisees; and they said to them, “Why didn’t you bring him?” ^45

**46** The officers answered, “No man ever spoke like this man!” ^46

**47** The Pharisees therefore answered them, “You aren’t also led astray, are you? ^47

**48** Have any of the rulers or any of the Pharisees believed in him? ^48

**49** But this multitude that doesn’t know the Torah is cursed.” ^49

**50** Nicodemus (he who came to him by night, being one of them) said to them, ^50

**51** “Does our Torah judge a man unless it first hears from him personally and knows what he does?” ^51

**52** They answered him, “Are you also from Galilee? Search and see that no prophet has arisen out of Galilee.” ^52

**53** Everyone went to his own house, ^53
