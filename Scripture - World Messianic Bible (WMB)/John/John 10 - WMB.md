# John 10 (WMB)

← [[John 9 - WMB]] | [[John 11 - WMB]] →

**1** “Most certainly, I tell you, one who doesn’t enter by the door into the sheep fold, but climbs up some other way, is a thief and a robber. ^1

**2** But one who enters in by the door is the shepherd of the sheep. ^2

**3** The gatekeeper opens the gate for him, and the sheep listen to his voice. He calls his own sheep by name and leads them out. ^3

**4** Whenever he brings out his own sheep, he goes before them; and the sheep follow him, for they know his voice. ^4

**5** They will by no means follow a stranger, but will flee from him; for they don’t know the voice of strangers.” ^5

**6** Yeshua spoke this parable to them, but they didn’t understand what he was telling them. ^6

**7** Yeshua therefore said to them again, “Most certainly, I tell you, I am the sheep’s door. ^7

**8** All who came before me are thieves and robbers, but the sheep didn’t listen to them. ^8

**9** I am the door. If anyone enters in by me, he will be saved, and will go in and go out and will find pasture. ^9

**10** The thief only comes to steal, kill, and destroy. I came that they may have life, and may have it abundantly. ^10

**11** “I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep. ^11

**12** He who is a hired hand, and not a shepherd, who doesn’t own the sheep, sees the wolf coming, leaves the sheep, and flees. The wolf snatches the sheep and scatters them. ^12

**13** The hired hand flees because he is a hired hand and doesn’t care for the sheep. ^13

**14** I am the good shepherd. I know my own, and I’m known by my own; ^14

**15** even as the Father knows me, and I know the Father. I lay down my life for the sheep. ^15

**16** I have other sheep which are not of this fold. I must bring them also, and they will hear my voice. They will become one flock with one shepherd. ^16

**17** Therefore the Father loves me, because I lay down my life, that I may take it again. ^17

**18** No one takes it away from me, but I lay it down by myself. I have power to lay it down, and I have power to take it again. I received this commandment from my Father.” ^18

**19** Therefore a division arose again among the Judeans because of these words. ^19

**20** Many of them said, “He has a demon and is insane! Why do you listen to him?” ^20

**21** Others said, “These are not the sayings of one possessed by a demon. It isn’t possible for a demon to open the eyes of the blind, is it?” ^21

**22** It was the Feast of Hanukkah at Jerusalem. ^22

**23** It was winter, and Yeshua was walking in the temple, in Solomon’s porch. ^23

**24** The Judeans therefore came around him and said to him, “How long will you hold us in suspense? If you are the Messiah, tell us plainly.” ^24

**25** Yeshua answered them, “I told you, and you don’t believe. The works that I do in my Father’s name, these testify about me. ^25

**26** But you don’t believe, because you are not of my sheep, as I told you. ^26

**27** My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me. ^27

**28** I give eternal life to them. They will never perish, and no one will snatch them out of my hand. ^28

**29** My Father who has given them to me is greater than all. No one is able to snatch them out of my Father’s hand. ^29

**30** I and the Father are one.” ^30

**31** Therefore the Judeans took up stones again to stone him. ^31

**32** Yeshua answered them, “I have shown you many good works from my Father. For which of those works do you stone me?” ^32

**33** The Judeans answered him, “We don’t stone you for a good work, but for blasphemy, because you, being a man, make yourself God.” ^33

**34** Yeshua answered them, “Isn’t it written in your law, ‘I said, you are gods’? ^34

**35** If he called them gods, to whom the word of God came (and the Scripture can’t be broken), ^35

**36** do you say of him whom the Father sanctified and sent into the world, ‘You blaspheme,’ because I said, ‘I am the Son of God’? ^36

**37** If I don’t do the works of my Father, don’t believe me. ^37

**38** But if I do them, though you don’t believe me, believe the works, that you may know and believe that the Father is in me, and I in the Father.” ^38

**39** They sought again to seize him, and he went out of their hand. ^39

**40** He went away again beyond the Jordan into the place where Yochanan was immersing at first, and he stayed there. ^40

**41** Many came to him. They said, “Yochanan indeed did no sign, but everything that Yochanan said about this man is true.” ^41

**42** Many believed in him there. ^42
