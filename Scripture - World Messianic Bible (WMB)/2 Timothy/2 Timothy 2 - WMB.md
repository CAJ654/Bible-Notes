# 2 Timothy 2 (WMB)

← [[2 Timothy 1 - WMB]] | [[2 Timothy 3 - WMB]] →

**1** You therefore, my child, be strengthened in the grace that is in Messiah Yeshua. ^1

**2** The things which you have heard from me among many witnesses, commit the same things to faithful men who will be able to teach others also. ^2

**3** You therefore must endure hardship as a good soldier of Messiah Yeshua. ^3

**4** No soldier on duty entangles himself in the affairs of life, that he may please him who enrolled him as a soldier. ^4

**5** Also, if anyone competes in athletics, he isn’t crowned unless he has competed by the rules. ^5

**6** The farmer who labors must be the first to get a share of the crops. ^6

**7** Consider what I say, and may the Lord give you understanding in all things. ^7

**8** Remember Yeshua the Messiah, risen from the dead, of the offspring of David, according to my Good News, ^8

**9** in which I suffer hardship to the point of chains as a criminal. But God’s word isn’t chained. ^9

**10** Therefore I endure all things for the chosen ones’ sake, that they also may obtain the salvation which is in Messiah Yeshua with eternal glory. ^10

**11** This saying is trustworthy: “For if we died with him, we will also live with him. ^11

**12** If we endure, we will also reign with him. If we deny him, he also will deny us. ^12

**13** If we are faithless, he remains faithful; for he can’t deny himself.” ^13

**14** Remind them of these things, charging them in the sight of the Lord that they don’t argue about words to no profit, to the subverting of those who hear. ^14

**15** Give diligence to present yourself approved by God, a workman who doesn’t need to be ashamed, properly handling the Word of Truth. ^15

**16** But shun empty chatter, for it will go further in ungodliness, ^16

**17** and those words will consume like gangrene, of whom is Hymenaeus and Philetus: ^17

**18** men who have erred concerning the truth, saying that the resurrection is already past, and overthrowing the faith of some. ^18

**19** However, God’s firm foundation stands, having this seal: “The Lord knows those who are his,” and, “Let every one who names the name of the Lord depart from unrighteousness.” ^19

**20** Now in a large house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of clay. Some are for honor and some for dishonor. ^20

**21** If anyone therefore purges himself from these, he will be a vessel for honor, sanctified, and suitable for the master’s use, prepared for every good work. ^21

**22** Flee from youthful lusts; but pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart. ^22

**23** But refuse foolish and ignorant questionings, knowing that they generate strife. ^23

**24** The Lord’s servant must not quarrel, but be gentle toward all, able to teach, patient, ^24

**25** in gentleness correcting those who oppose him. Perhaps God may give them repentance leading to a full knowledge of the truth, ^25

**26** and they may recover themselves out of the devil’s snare, having been taken captive by him to do his will. ^26
