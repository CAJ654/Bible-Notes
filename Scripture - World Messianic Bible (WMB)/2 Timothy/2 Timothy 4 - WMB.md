# 2 Timothy 4 (WMB)

← [[2 Timothy 3 - WMB]]

**1** I command you therefore before God and the Lord Yeshua the Messiah, who will judge the living and the dead at his appearing and his Kingdom: ^1

**2** proclaim the word; be urgent in season and out of season; reprove, rebuke, and exhort with all patience and teaching. ^2

**3** For the time will come when they will not listen to the sound doctrine, but having itching ears, will heap up for themselves teachers after their own lusts, ^3

**4** and will turn away their ears from the truth, and turn away to fables. ^4

**5** But you be sober in all things, suffer hardship, do the work of an evangelist, and fulfill your ministry. ^5

**6** For I am already being offered, and the time of my departure has come. ^6

**7** I have fought the good fight. I have finished the course. I have kept the faith. ^7

**8** From now on, the crown of righteousness is stored up for me, which the Lord, the righteous judge, will give to me on that day; and not to me only, but also to all those who have loved his appearing. ^8

**9** Be diligent to come to me soon, ^9

**10** for Demas left me, having loved this present world, and went to Thessalonica; Crescens to Galatia; and Titus to Dalmatia. ^10

**11** Only Luke is with me. Take Mark and bring him with you, for he is useful to me for service. ^11

**12** But I sent Tychicus to Ephesus. ^12

**13** Bring the cloak that I left at Troas with Carpus when you come—and the books, especially the parchments. ^13

**14** Alexander the coppersmith did much evil to me. The Lord will repay him according to his deeds. ^14

**15** Beware of him, for he greatly opposed our words. ^15

**16** At my first defense, no one came to help me, but all left me. May it not be held against them. ^16

**17** But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me, that through me the message might be fully proclaimed, and that all the Gentiles might hear. So I was delivered out of the mouth of the lion. ^17

**18** And the Lord will deliver me from every evil work and will preserve me for his heavenly Kingdom. To him be the glory forever and ever. Amen. ^18

**19** Greet Prisca and Aquila, and the house of Onesiphorus. ^19

**20** Erastus remained at Corinth, but I left Trophimus at Miletus sick. ^20

**21** Be diligent to come before winter. Eubulus salutes you, as do Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brothers. ^21

**22** The Lord Yeshua the Messiah be with your spirit. Grace be with you. Amen. ^22
