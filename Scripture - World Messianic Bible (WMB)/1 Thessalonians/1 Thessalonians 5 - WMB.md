# 1 Thessalonians 5 (WMB)

← [[1 Thessalonians 4 - WMB]]

**1** But concerning the times and the seasons, brothers, you have no need that anything be written to you. ^1

**2** For you yourselves know well that the day of the Lord comes like a thief in the night. ^2

**3** For when they are saying, “Peace and safety,” then sudden destruction will come on them, like birth pains on a pregnant woman. Then they will in no way escape. ^3

**4** But you, brothers, aren’t in darkness, that the day should overtake you like a thief. ^4

**5** You are all children of light and children of the day. We don’t belong to the night, nor to darkness, ^5

**6** so then let’s not sleep, as the rest do, but let’s watch and be sober. ^6

**7** For those who sleep, sleep in the night; and those who are drunk are drunk in the night. ^7

**8** But since we belong to the day, let’s be sober, putting on the breastplate of faith and love, and for a helmet, the hope of salvation. ^8

**9** For God didn’t appoint us to wrath, but to the obtaining of salvation through our Lord Yeshua the Messiah, ^9

**10** who died for us, that, whether we wake or sleep, we should live together with him. ^10

**11** Therefore exhort one another, and build each other up, even as you also do. ^11

**12** But we beg you, brothers, to know those who labor among you, and are over you in the Lord and admonish you, ^12

**13** and to respect and honor them in love for their work’s sake. Be at peace among yourselves. ^13

**14** We exhort you, brothers: Admonish the disorderly; encourage the faint-hearted; support the weak; be patient toward all. ^14

**15** See that no one returns evil for evil to anyone, but always follow after that which is good for one another and for all. ^15

**16** Always rejoice. ^16

**17** Pray without ceasing. ^17

**18** In everything give thanks, for this is the will of God in Messiah Yeshua toward you. ^18

**19** Don’t quench the Spirit. ^19

**20** Don’t despise prophecies. ^20

**21** Test all things, and hold firmly that which is good. ^21

**22** Abstain from every form of evil. ^22

**23** May the God of peace himself sanctify you completely. May your whole spirit, soul, and body be preserved blameless at the coming of our Lord Yeshua the Messiah. ^23

**24** He who calls you is faithful, who will also do it. ^24

**25** Brothers, pray for us. ^25

**26** Greet all the brothers with a holy kiss. ^26

**27** I solemnly command you by the Lord that this letter be read to all the holy brothers. ^27

**28** The grace of our Lord Yeshua the Messiah be with you. Amen. ^28
