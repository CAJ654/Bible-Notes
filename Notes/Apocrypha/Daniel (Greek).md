## Why it's considered apocrypha

The Greek version of Daniel is longer than the Hebrew/Aramaic text in the Tanakh. It adds the Prayer of Azariah and Song of the Three Young Men, Susanna, and Bel and the Dragon. These additions survive only in Greek (Septuagint), not in the Hebrew Bible, so Protestant and Jewish canons exclude them, while Catholic and Orthodox canons keep them as deuterocanonical.
