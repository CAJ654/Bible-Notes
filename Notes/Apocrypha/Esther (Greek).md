## Why it's considered apocrypha

The Greek version of Esther is longer than the Hebrew text in the Tanakh. It adds prayers of Mordecai and Esther, the texts of the royal decrees, and Mordecai's dream, and it mentions God by name, which the Hebrew does not. These additions survive only in Greek (Septuagint), not in the Hebrew Bible, so Protestant and Jewish canons exclude them, while Catholic and Orthodox canons keep them as deuterocanonical.
